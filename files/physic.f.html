Explanation

Purpose. Recomputes the auxiliary physics at every shell of a model: equation of state, opacity, temperature gradients and convection, mean molecular weight, then the thermal diffusivity and viscosity, and the largest angular velocity gradient that is stable against the shear instability. Its comment: "find actual and adiabatic temperature gradients, opacity, and mean molecular weight for all radiative shells" (the loop in fact covers all shells).

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the model (HCOMP, HD, HG (gravity), HL, HP, HR, HS, HT, all logarithmic except HL, HG and HCOMP), the rotational distortion factors FP, FT, M and TEFFL; the envelope composition from /COMP/ and /COMP2/; LMHD (/MHD/) and LDH (/DEBHU/).
  • Who calls it: GETW, HPOINT and STARIN.
  • What it calls: MEQOS (MHD tables) or EQSTAT (the general equation of state), GETOPAC, TPGRAD (gradients and convective velocity), VISCOS, and INTRP2.
  • Outputs (all through COMMON blocks, except LC):
  • LC, the convection flags (argument);
  • /SCRTCH/: SDEL(1..3,·) $= \nabla_\mathrm{rad}, \nabla, \nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, the opacity SO, the convective velocity SVEL;
  • /TEMP/: CP, HAMU (the mean molecular weight), SQDT ($(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T)_P$), and through VISCOS THDIF and VISC;
  • /DWMAX/: QWRMAX, the critical $d\Omega/d\ln r$;
  • /DPMIX/: IOVIM, the current shell, for TPGRAD; /OPTAB/: the starting indices of the opacity search (IDT = 15, IDD = 5). These feed the rotational mixing and transport routines (MIDMOD interpolates them in time), and MICRODIFF_MTE (for microscopic diffusion) reads SDEL(2,·).

The method.

  1. Per shell: the equation of state (with Debye–Hückel abundances set first when LDH), the opacity, and TPGRAD, without derivatives (LDERIV = .FALSE.).

  2. Mean molecular weight of a fully ionized gas. With $A$ = 1.007825, 4.002603, 12 and 3.01603 for H, ⁴He, the metals (as carbon) and ³He, $$\frac{1}{\mu_\mathrm{ion}} = \frac{1}{\mu_\mathrm{ion,env}} + \sum_k \frac{X_k - X_{k,\mathrm{env}}}{A_k},\qquad \frac{1}{\mu_e} = \frac{X}{A_\mathrm{H}} + 2\left(\frac{X_{^3\mathrm{He}}}{A_{^3\mathrm{He}}} + \frac{Y}{A_{^4\mathrm{He}}}\right) + \frac{Z}{2},\qquad \mu = \frac{\mu_\mathrm{ion}\,\mu_e}{\mu_\mathrm{ion}+\mu_e}.$$

  3. Shear stability (Endal & Sofia 1978, ApJ 220, 279, as cited). At each interface between shells, with $\rho$, $P$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ and $g$ interpolated to the midpoint in $\log m$ (four-point Lagrange), $$\left(\frac{d\Omega}{d\ln r}\right)_\mathrm{max} = 2\sqrt{\frac{\rho}{P}\,g^2\,(\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla)}$$ where the root is real, and 0 otherwise. QWRMAX(IM) belongs to the interface between shells IM-1 and IM; it is 0 where both shells are convective, and QWRMAX(1) is never set. $\rho$ and $P$ are interpolated in their logarithms (HD, HP), $g$ (HG, not logarithmic) and the gradients directly.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused argument LCZ removed, and unused arguments removed from the calls to MEQOS and VISCOS.

Questions for the experts

  1. LC overwritten. PHYSIC resets the caller's convection flags LC from TPGRAD. Do all three callers expect this?
  2. Unused or unset. /DWMAX/'s QWRMX0 and the flag LOCOND are not used here; KSAHA, which EQSTAT updates (a counter of Saha-equation calls), is never initialized here.
  3. The comment's "radiative shells". The loop covers all shells, convective ones included. Update the comment?
