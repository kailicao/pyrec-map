Explanation

Purpose. "Model to equal" for the older settling routine GRSETT: it interpolates the diffusion coefficients and the abundances from the model points to an equally spaced grid in radius, at the grid points and at the midpoints. MICRODIFF_MTE is its newer counterpart, for MICRODIFF.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the coefficients COD1, COD2 and their derivatives with respect to $X$, QCOD1X, QCOD2X, at the model points (from SETUP_GRSETT); the metal coefficients COD1Z, COD2Z, QCOD1Z, QCOD2Z in /GRAVSZ/; HCOMP, the radii HRU and masses HS1 in Bahcall–Loeb units; IBEGIN, IEND, M.
  • Who calls it: only GRSETT.
  • What it calls: INTERP (four-point Lagrange weights).
  • Outputs: NPT, DR, the grid radii ER, and on the points and midpoints: masses (EM, EM_H), coefficients (ECOD1, ECOD1_H, ECOD2_H, EQCOD1X_H, EQCOD2X_H) and hydrogen (EX, EX_H). For metals (LDIFZ), the same in /GRAVEZ/, which LAX_WENDROF1, LAX_WENDROF2, GRSETT and EQUAL_TO_MODEL read.
  • COMMON blocks: /GRAVS3/ (LDIFZ), /GRAVSZ/ (read; shared with SETUP_GRSETT), /GRAVEZ/ (written).

The method. As in MICRODIFF_MTE: the number of points $N = \lceil (r_\mathrm{end}-r_\mathrm{begin})/\Delta r_\mathrm{min}\rceil$ (at least 2, at most JSON), here with $\Delta r_\mathrm{min}$ the smallest spacing over the whole region; the first midpoint by linear interpolation, the others by four-point Lagrange interpolation, $f(r) = \sum_{k=1}^4 w_k(r)\,f_{k_0+k-1}$; the end points take the model's values.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused arguments HQPR and HTU removed.

Questions for the experts

  1. The metals come from row 8. EZ and EZ_H are interpolated from HCOMP(8,·), while EQUAL_TO_MODEL applies the change to HCOMP(3,·) ($Z$), and MIX names row 8 ¹⁵N (XN15). Is row 8 a mistake for row 3? If so, the old routine's metal settling has been computed from the ¹⁵N profile.
  2. Unused derivative factors. The "center derivative" block computes FAC1, FAC2 and DELR, which are never used.
  3. The first midpoint's search. As in MICRODIFF_MTE, the search runs over model indices 2..NPT (bounded by the number of grid points) rather than IBEGIN+1..IEND.
  4. Midpoint radii lost. ER first holds the midpoint radii and is then overwritten with the grid points' radii, so the caller never sees the midpoints' radii. Intended?
  5. A debugging line. IF(I-1 .EQ. 0) PRINT*, 'mte line 47' cannot fire, and names the other file.
