Explanation

Purpose. Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the surface composition (MHP 7/98: "get OPAL95 opacity from table at surface X,Z"): a two-dimensional interpolation in $\log T$ and $\log R$, where $R = \rho/T_6^3$. It is the fastest of three routines; OP953D adds $X$ and OP954D adds $X$ and $Z$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the surface table CAPPAE(70,19) of $\log\kappa$ in /LLOT95E/, built by OP95XTAB; the indices and the Lagrange weights prepared by GETOPAL95 in /OP95INDX/, /OP95FACT/ and /OP95EXT/.
  • Who calls it: GETOPAL95, when the point's $Z$ matches the table's ZTAB (to a relative $10^{-4}$) and its $X$ the surface table's XTAB (to $10^{-4}$), and the acoustic depth is not being computed (LADON and LACOUT, which force OP954D).
  • Outputs: $\kappa$ (O), $\log\kappa$ (OL), QOD $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log\rho$ and QOT $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$.

The method. At each of the four nearest temperatures $T_i$, four-point Lagrange interpolation in $\log R$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its slope: $$C_i = \sum_k b^{(D)}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k},\qquad C'_i = \sum_k b^{(D')}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k}.$$ If $\log R$ lies below the table (LEXTL) or above it at some $T_i$ (LEXH(I)), the value at the edge is extrapolated linearly, $C_i \to C_i + (\log R - \log R_\mathrm{edge})\,C'_i$. Then, in $\log T$, $\log\kappa = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C_i$, $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T|_R = \sum_i b^{(T')}_i C_i$ and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log R|_T = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C'_i$. Since $\log R = \log\rho - 3\log T_6$, $$\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T,\qquad \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_\rho = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_R - 3\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T .$$

Design. The table dimensions are fixed as parameters (70 temperatures, 19 values of $\log R$, 10 of $X$, 13 of $Z$, 126 compositions), repeated in each of the five OP95… files.

8      IMPLICIT REAL*8 (A-H,O-Z)
9      IMPLICIT LOGICAL*4(L)
10      PARAMETER (NUMT=70)
11      PARAMETER (NUMD=19)
12      PARAMETER (NUMX=10)
13      PARAMETER (NUMZ=13)
14      PARAMETER (NUMXZ=126)
15C AS ABOVE FOR DESIRED SURFACE VALUE OF X.
16      COMMON /LLOT95E/CAPPAE(NUMT,NUMD),XTAB
17C INDICES FOR INTERPOLATION IN Z,X,T, AND R
18      COMMON/OP95INDX/JZ,JX(4,4),JT,JD(4)
19C INTERPOLATION FACTORS FOR Z,X,T, AND R, AS WELL AS DERIVATIVE
20C FACTORS FOR T AND RHO.
21      COMMON/OP95FACT/BZ(4),BX(4,4),BT(4),BTT(4),BD(4,4),BDD(4,4)
22C DATA FOR LINEAR EXTRAPOLATION WHEN OUTSIDE TABLE IN RHO
23      COMMON/OP95EXT/RL,RLL,RLH(4),LEXTL,LEXTH,LEXH(4)
24      DIMENSION C(4),QC(4)
25      SAVE
26C FIND OPACITY AT EACH OF THE 4 NEARBY VALUES OF T.
27      DO I = 1,4
28         IT = JT + I - 1
29C FOR EACH T, INTERPOLATE IN 4 VALUES OF R
30         ID = JD(I)
31C LOG CAPPA AT DESIRED RHO FOR EACH OF THE 4 DESIRED T.
32         C(I) = BD(I,1)*CAPPAE(IT,ID) + BD(I,2)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+1)
33     *         + BD(I,3)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+2) + BD(I,4)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+3)
34C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG R FOR EACH OF THE 4 DESIRED T.
35         QC(I) = BDD(I,1)*CAPPAE(IT,ID) + BDD(I,2)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+1)
36     *         + BDD(I,3)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+2) + BDD(I,4)*CAPPAE(IT,ID+3)
37      END DO
38C CHECK ON WHETHER RHO IS OUTSIDE OF THE TABLE AND NEEDS EXTRAPOLATION
39      IF(LEXTL)THEN
40C FACTOR IN R
41         DELTAR = RL-RLL
42C CORRECT CAPPA BY USING THE DERIVATIVE AT THE BOUNDARY.
43         DO I = 1,4
44            C(I) = C(I)+DELTAR*QC(I)
45         END DO
46      ELSE IF(LEXTH)THEN
47         DO I = 1,4
48            IF(LEXH(I))THEN
49               DELTAR = RL - RLH(I)
50               C(I) = C(I)+DELTAR*QC(I)
51            ENDIF
52         END DO
53      ENDIF
54C INTERPOLATE FOR LOG CAPPA IN T.
55      OL = BT(1)*C(1) + BT(2)*C(2) + BT(3)*C(3) + BT(4)*C(4)
56C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG T
57      QOT = BTT(1)*C(1) + BTT(2)*C(2) + BTT(3)*C(3) + BTT(4)*C(4)
58C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG R
59      QOD = BT(1)*QC(1) + BT(2)*QC(2) + BT(3)*QC(3) + BT(4)*QC(4)
60C CORRECT FROM DERIVATE AT FIXED R TO DERIVATIVE AT FIXED RHO.
61      QOT = QOT - 3.0D0*QOD
62      O = 1.0D1**OL
63      RETURN
64      END
