Explanation

Purpose. FPFT computes, for every shell of a rotating model, the form factors $f_P$ and $f_T$ that correct the stellar structure equations for rotational distortion, together with the mean effective gravity on each equipotential. The equipotentials' shapes come from SHAPE; the angular averages from a Gauss–Legendre quadrature.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: the main program (main.f), CRRECT (the structure correction, iterated), HPOINT and STARIN.
  • Inputs: HD, HR, HS ($\log\rho$, $\log r$, $\log m$ in cgs), OMEGA ($\Omega$ per shell, rad s$^{-1}$), M.
  • What it calls: SHAPE (the run of $r_0$ and $\eta_2$), TRAPZD (trapezoid rule for the distortion integral), POLINT (polynomial extrapolation) and QGAUSS (the angular quadrature, which calls FUNC).
  • Outputs: FP, FT (the form factors), HG (mean gravity $\langle g\rangle$), ETA2 ($\eta_2$, from Radau's equation as computed in SHAPE), R0. It also fills /QUADD/ with the spherical, rotational and distortion potentials PHISP, PHIROT, PHIDIS, read by CHECKJ, VCIRC and WRTMOD.
  • COMMON blocks: /QUADD/ (above); /ROT/ and /CCOUT/ are declared but not used here; constants from /CONST1/, /CONST2/.

The method. For each shell, outward:

  1. The distortion amplitude $a = \dfrac{5}{3}\dfrac{\Omega^2 r_0^3}{Gm\,(2+\eta_2)}$.
  2. The integral $I(r_0) = \int_0^{r_0}(\ldots)\,dr_0'$ (its integrand, per TRAPZD's comment, is $(\rho/m)\,r_0'^7\,\Omega^2(5+\eta_2)/(2+\eta_2)$) is added shell by shell: the trapezoid rule on the step from the previous shell, refined once and extrapolated to zero step with POLINT (a Romberg step; JMAX = 2, tolerance $10^{-6}$).
  3. QGAUSS integrates over $0 \le \theta \le \pi/2$ the surface element $S$, $gS$ and $S/g$, giving $S_\Phi$, $S_\Phi\langle g\rangle$ and $S_\Phi\langle g^{-1}\rangle$.
  4. With $r_\Phi$ the shell's radius (HR), $$f_P = \frac{4\pi r_\Phi^4}{Gm\,S_\Phi\langle g^{-1}\rangle},\qquad f_T = \frac{(4\pi r_\Phi^2)^2}{S_\Phi\langle g\rangle\;S_\Phi\langle g^{-1}\rangle},$$ which is the usual Kippenhahn–Thomas form, $f_T = (4\pi r_\Phi^2/S_\Phi)^2/(\langle g\rangle\langle g^{-1}\rangle)$, provided QGAUSS's outputs are the surface integrals named above (read from its code). HG = $\langle g\rangle$.

KC's changes. The unused argument I was removed from the call to TRAPZD (2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. Romberg with two levels. With JMAX = 2 the refinement stops after one halving whether or not the tolerance is met; the result is then used without a warning. Is two levels enough?
  2. Unused COMMON blocks. /ROT/ and /CCOUT/ are declared but not used here. Remove?
