Explanation

Purpose. GMASS06 is the version of GMASS for the 2006 OPAL equation of state. Its code is identical to GMASS01 apart from its name: the mean mass per nucleus (without electrons), the number fractions of H, He, C, N, O, Ne, the particles per nucleus and the ground-state energy.

How it fits. Called only by ESAC06. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

The method. See gmass.f for the equations; here $\mathrm{GMASS06} = \sum_i A_i\,\mathrm{FRAC}_i$, without the electrons' mass.

Questions for the experts

  1. Same as GMASS01. The two files are identical apart from the name, including the ion energies. Were the 2006 tables meant to use other values?
