Explanation

Purpose. MHDTBL opens the eight binary tables of the MHD equation of state, has MHDST read them, and closes them again. Its comment says the MHD equation of state "uses 3 ZAMS-type tables and optionally 5 center-type tables".

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the eight file names FMHD1–FMHD8, passed as arguments (since MHP 8/25; earlier they came through the COMMON block LUFNM, now commented out). The unit numbers IOMHD1–IOMHD8 come from /MHD/; PARMIN sets them to 40–47.
  • Who calls it: SETUPS, at start-up, only when the flag LMHD (in /MHD/) is true.
  • What it calls: MHDST, which reads the tables into memory.
  • COMMON blocks: /MHD/ (the flag and unit numbers; shared with PARMIN, SETUPS, PHYSIC, QATM, QENV, STARIN, COEFFT, ENVINT, WRTOUT) and /CCOUT2/ (debug flags, not used here).

Design. All eight files are opened with STATUS='OLD', so all eight must exist, even though the comment calls five of them optional.

Questions for the experts

  1. Optional tables. The comment says five of the eight tables are optional, but MHDTBL opens all eight with STATUS='OLD', so a missing file stops the run. Are the "center-type" tables in fact required now?
