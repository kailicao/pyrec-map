Explanation

Purpose. MDOT applies a mass change (loss or accretion) to the model over a step: it limits the step so that not too much mass changes, removes mass from the surface convection zone or adds it at the surface, rescales the mass coordinates, the total mass and the shell masses, removes angular momentum with lost mass, and for accretion mixes the accreted composition into the convection zone. Sign convention: DMDT > 0 is a mass gain.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only MASSLOSS (but with a shifted argument list: question 1).
  • Inputs and outputs (arguments): DELTS (s, may be shortened), the model (HCOMP, HD, HJM, HP, HR, HS, HS1, HS2, HT, OMEGA, M, JENV), HSTOT and SMASS (updated), DMDT ($M_\odot$/yr), RTOT, SUMDM (the convection zone's mass, from MASSLOSS); EACC, ETHAV and RMU have no effect (see step 4); outputs LNEW (= true: recompute the surface boundary condition) and SENVOLD (the old HS(M) − HSTOT, for GETNEWENV).
  • COMMON blocks: MASSCHG (FCZDMDT, FTOTDMDT, COMPACC, LMDOT), MASSCHG2 (SCEN, SMASS0, DLOGP, DLOGT; with MASSLOSS and COEFFT, which adds DLOGT, DLOGP to $\log T$, $\log P$ at and above JENV in its entropy terms when LMDOT and DMDT0 > 0), MASSCHG3 (the scaled solar wind), DISK (disk lifetime: SAGE, TDISK, LDISK), COMP (STOTAL, SENV), DEUTER (FMASSACC, read by LIBURN, DBURN and DBURNM), ROT (LROT, WALPCZ), FLAG, the constants.

The method. Two masses of the convection zone appear: $M_\mathrm{cz}$ = DMCZ = HS1(M) − HS1(JENV) (or HS1(M) if JENV = 1), and $M'_\mathrm{cz}$ = DMCZT = SUMDM.

  1. Optionally (LSOLWIND with rotation) $\dot M = \dot M_\odot\,\min\big((\Omega/\Omega_\odot)^2, (\Omega_\mathrm{max}/\Omega_\odot)^2\big)$, with $\dot M_\odot$ = DMSUN, $\Omega_\odot$ = DMWSUN, $\Omega_\mathrm{max}$ = DMWMAX.
  2. Rate in g/s: $|\dot M|\,M_\odot/\mathrm{yr}$. Step limits: $\Delta t \le f_\mathrm{tot}M/|\dot M|$, $\Delta t \le f_\mathrm{cz}\,M_\mathrm{cz}/|\dot M|$ ($f_\mathrm{tot}$ = FTOTDMDT, $f_\mathrm{cz}$ = FCZDMDT), and at most half the mass outside the fitting point, $\tfrac12(10^{\texttt{HSTOT}} - 10^{\texttt{HS(M)}})/|\dot M|$. The comment speaks of adding mass, but this limit applies to loss as well. With a disk (LDISK) and accretion, the step ends at the disk's lifetime TDISK (Gyr), and LMDOT is then turned off.
  3. $\Delta M = \dot M\,\Delta t$ (g).
  4. Accretion ($\Delta M > 0$):
  5. the new mean thermal energy $\bar e = (e_\mathrm{acc}\Delta M + \bar e_\mathrm{th}M'_\mathrm{cz})/(\Delta M + M'_\mathrm{cz})$ and FAC = $\log_{10}(\bar e/\bar e_\mathrm{th})$ are computed but not used (their use is commented out);
  6. RMU = $P\beta/(\rho T)$ (the gas constant over $\mu$) is recomputed at JENV, and a scale factor $\Delta\ln E = (\Delta M/M_\mathrm{cz})\,s_\mathrm{cen}/(\mathcal{R}/\mu)$, with $s_\mathrm{cen}$ = SCEN, gives $\Delta\log r = \tfrac23\Delta\ln E/\ln 10$, $\Delta\log T = -\Delta\log r$, $\Delta\log P = -4\Delta\log r$ (the mass term DLNM is set to zero);
  7. if JENV = 1 (fully convective), $\log r$, $\log P$, $\log T$ of every point change by these amounts; otherwise only $P$ and $T$ at JENV, and the radii above it are stretched about $r_\mathrm{b} = r_\mathrm{JENV}$: $r \leftarrow r_\mathrm{b} + 10^{\Delta\log r}(r - r_\mathrm{b})$.

MASSLOSS sets SCEN = 0, so these changes are zero in practice. 5. Angular momentum (rotation, loss): the lost matter carries the surface's specific angular momentum, $\Delta J = \tfrac23 R^2\,\Omega_s\,\Delta M$ (solid-body zone, WALPCZ ≥ 0), or $j_s\Delta M$ (WALPCZ ≤ −2), or the same with $\Omega_s = \Omega_M (r_M/R)^{\texttt{WALPCZ}}$; the zone's specific angular momentum HJM is scaled by $(J_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta J)/J_\mathrm{cz}$, with $J_\mathrm{cz} = \sum$ HJM·HS2 from JENV to M. 6. Mass coordinates (loss): points in the zone are compressed toward its base, $m_i \leftarrow m_\mathrm{b} + \frac{M_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta M}{M_\mathrm{cz}}(m_i - m_\mathrm{b})$. For accretion HS1 is not changed: the gain goes to the outermost shell. 7. New total mass (SMASS, SMASS0, HSTOT, STOTAL), shell masses HS2 from JENV to M − 1 as half the distance between neighbours, HS2(M) = $10^{\texttt{HSTOT}}$ − (HS1(M) + HS1(M−1))/2, and SENV. 8. Accretion: elements 1–11 of the zone are mixed with the accreted composition COMPACC, $\bar x = (x_\mathrm{JENV}\,M'_\mathrm{cz} + x_\mathrm{acc}\Delta M)/(M'_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta M)$; FMASSACC = $\Delta M$.

Design. Several unconditional WRITE(*,…) lines report the step reduction, the angular momentum and the mass change.

KC's changes. 2025-05-30: the variables of COMMON MASSCHG3 reordered (the logical LSOLWIND moved to the end).

Questions for the experts

  1. The call does not match the interface. MASSLOSS calls MDOT(BL, DELTS, HCOMP, …, SENVOLD) with 24 arguments, but MDOT is declared MDOT(DELTS, HCOMP, …, SENVOLD) with 23: every argument is shifted by one (MDOT's DELTS receives BL, its HCOMP receives DELTS, and so on). MDOT would write the shortened step into MASSLOSS's BL, LNEW = true into the integer JENV, the new mass into RTOT and SENVOLD into SUMDM. This has been so since the first commit of the repository. Any run with LMDOT true would misbehave; has mass loss been used with this version?
  2. MASSCHG3 is set nowhere. No other unit declares it (in PARMIN its declaration is commented out, and LSOLWIND is a local namelist variable there), so LSOLWIND, DMSUN, DMWSUN, DMWMAX are never set. The scaled solar wind therefore cannot be switched on.
  3. Inert code. With SCEN = 0 and DLNM = 0, the structure changes of step 4 vanish, and ETHNEW and FAC are not used; so EACC, ETHAV and the RMU argument have no effect. Is the code kept for later work?
  4. Hard stop. IF(JENV.EQ.M) STOP911 (that is, STOP 911) ends the run with only the stop code 911 when there is no surface convection zone.
  5. Angular momentum of accreted matter. For accretion HJM is not changed while HS2(M) grows, so the accreted matter implicitly takes the outer point's specific angular momentum. Intended?
  6. Out-of-bounds read (apparent bug). The loop DO I = JENV,M-1: HS2(I) = 0.5D0*(HS1(I+1)-HS1(I-1)) (lines 185–186) reads HS1(0) when JENV = 1, a case the routine handles elsewhere (lines 54 and 110). Should it start at MAX(JENV,2), with the central shell treated apart?
  7. The sign of WALPCZ. Line 145 sets $\Omega_\mathrm{surf} \propto r^{-\alpha}$ with $\alpha$ = WALPCZ, the opposite sign to WCZ's $\Omega = C\,r^{\alpha}$ (wcz.f line 58); see GETNEWENV, question 7. Which sign is meant?

Review (round 1)
  1. Wrong. Step 2: "(accretion) at most half the mass outside the fitting point". The limit applies whenever RATE > 0, and RATE = $|\dot M|$, so it applies to loss as well (mdot.f:66–69), though its comment says "add". Fix.
  2. Wrong. Step 4 says the correction "changes $\log r$, $\log T$, $\log P$ of the zone". Only for a fully convective model (JENV = 1) are all points shifted; otherwise only $T$ and $P$ at JENV change, and the radii above JENV are stretched about $r_\mathrm{b}$ by $10^{\Delta\log r}$. Fix: say so; add that DLOGP, DLOGT go to COEFFT through MASSCHG2 (its entropy terms, for points at or above JENV, when LMDOT and DMDT0 > 0).
  3. Missing. In step 4 the code also computes the new mean thermal energy $\bar e = (e_\mathrm{acc}\Delta M + \bar e_\mathrm{th} M_\mathrm{cz})/(\Delta M + M_\mathrm{cz})$ and FAC = $\log(\bar e/\bar e_\mathrm{th})$, both unused (their use is commented out); the RMU argument is overwritten before use. Fix: add to step 4 and to question 3.
  4. Missing. For accretion the mass coordinates HS1 are not changed: the gain goes into the outer shell's mass HS2(M) = $10^{\texttt{HSTOT}}$ − midpoint. Fix: add to steps 6–7.
  5. Missing question (apparent bug). The shell-mass loop DO I = JENV,M-1: HS2(I) = 0.5*(HS1(I+1)-HS1(I-1)) reads HS1(0), out of bounds, when JENV = 1 (mdot.f:185–186), a case the routine handles elsewhere (lines 54, 110). Fix: add a question.
  6. Unclear. Step 8 writes $M_\mathrm{cz}$ for two different masses: the step limit uses DMCZ = HS1(M) − HS1(JENV), the mixing uses DMCZT = SUMDM (the sum of HS2 from JENV to M, from MASSLOSS). Fix: name them.
  7. Unclear (question 4). "without a message": STOP911 is STOP 911 (blanks do not count in fixed form), which prints the code 911. Fix: "with only the stop code 911".
  8. Wrong (question 5). "Accretion adds mass but no angular momentum." HJM (specific angular momentum) is kept while HS2(M) grows, so the accreted mass takes the outer point's specific angular momentum. Fix: rephrase as a question on that implicit choice.
  9. Missing. Question 1 should note that, through the shift, MDOT would write the shortened step into MASSLOSS's BL, LNEW = true into the integer JENV, the new mass into RTOT and SENVOLD into SUMDM. Fix: one sentence.

Revised: items 1–9 applied.
