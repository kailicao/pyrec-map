Explanation

Purpose. The secular transport of angular momentum, and of the composition it carries, by rotational instabilities, over one time step. Its comment: "SECULR CALCULATES THE TRANSFER OF ANGULAR MOMENTUM (AND THE ASSOCIATED TRANSPORT OF COMPOSITION) DUE TO SECULAR ROTATIONAL INSTABILITIES." The mechanisms considered are "secular and dynamical shear, Eddington circulation, and the GSF instability". It is the central routine of rotational mixing: it sets up, iterates and checks the diffusion of $\Omega$ and of the abundances.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: GETW, once per time step, for a rotating model.
  • Inputs (arguments): the step DT; the model (HD, HG, HI, HL, HP, HR, HS, HS1, HS2, HT, HCOMP, M); the rotation (OMEGA, specific angular momentum HJM and its saved copy HJMSAV, ETA2, QIW, R0); the convection flags LCZ and the range of radiative interfaces IMIN–IMAX; and for the wind, BL, HSTOT, SMASS, TEFFL, HSBOT, HSTOP, LCZSUR (the star has a surface convection zone).
  • Outputs: OMEGA, HJM, HCOMP updated; HV (the circulation velocities), COD2 (composition diffusion coefficients), HICZ, OMEGAS; the flags LOK (converged) and LREDO, IREDO (the step must be cut, set by the checks).
  • What it calls, in order: SETUPV (factors for the velocities), GETQUA, VCIRC (stability tests and velocities; sets LDO), GETFC (with LVFC), MIXCZ, the wind routines MWIND and MCOWIND, SOLID (solid-body rotation), CODIFF (diffusion coefficients), ROTGRID (the equally spaced grid), DCOEFT and TRIDIA (the diffusion equation for $\Omega$ and its tridiagonal solution) or DADCOEFT (diffusion plus advection, LDIFAD), EQ2MOD (back to the model's grid), NDIFCOM (composition diffusion), CHECKJ and CHECKC (convergence and step-cutting checks).
  • COMMON, main ones: /DIFUS/ (ITDIF2, the maximum number of iterations), /DISK/ (disk locking: LDISK, TDISK, SAGE), /SBROT/ (LSOLID, IMPJMOD), /VARFC/ (VFC, LVFC, LDIFAD), /CWIND/ (LJDOT0, the wind), /INTVAR/ (RM, the mid-shell radii), /OLDAB/ (HCOMPS, the composition at the start), /OLDROT2/ (WMST), /EGRID/ (NTOT), /NOTRAN/ (LNOJ), /FLAG/ (LEXCOM), /LUOUT/ (IMODPT).

The method.

  1. Disk locking: if LDISK and the age is below TDISK, the surface rate is held fixed (LOCK).
  2. Shear. At each interface the logarithmic gradient of $\Omega$ is centered on the mid-shell radius $r_m$: $$\frac{d\Omega}{d\ln r}\bigg|_i \approx \frac{\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1}}{4}\left[\frac{1}{\ln r_i - \ln r_m} + \frac{1}{\ln r_m - \ln r_{i-1}}\right],$$ which is $(\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1})/(\ln r_i - \ln r_{i-1})$ when $r_m$ is the geometric mean.
  3. Iteration IT = 1 … ITDIF2. From the second iteration on, $\Omega$ is replaced by the average of the start and the latest solution, $\Omega \leftarrow \tfrac12(\Omega + \Omega_\mathrm{start})$, the velocities are recomputed, $j$ and the first four abundances are reset to their starting values, and convection zones are remixed (MIXCZ).
  4. Wind. For an isolated surface convection zone (no diffusion across its base, HV(IMAX) = 0) the wind torque is applied to that zone (MWIND); for solid-body models (LSOLID or IMPJMOD = 1) to the whole star, followed by SOLID. Otherwise the wind enters the diffusion equation as a boundary term (MCOWIND, giving WIND1, WIND2), or not at all when locked.
  5. Unstable regions. If VCIRC found no instability (LDO false), the routine ends. Otherwise CODIFF gives the coefficients, and each run of interfaces with $v > 0$ (HV) is solved on its own: ROTGRID maps it to an equally spaced grid, the diffusion equation for $\Omega$ is solved implicitly (DCOEFT + TRIDIA, or DADCOEFT), EQ2MOD maps the change in $j$ back, and NDIFCOM diffuses species 1–4 ($X$, $Y$, $Z$, He3: those that change the mean molecular weight). A region that ROTGRID flags (LDUM2: a single radiative interface between two convection zones) is skipped. For solid-body models (LSOLID or IMPJMOD = 1) the solution for $\Omega$ is skipped ("MHP 3/09"), but NDIFCOM still diffuses the composition. The center and the surface shells are treated as convective during this.
  6. Checks. CHECKJ checks the solution and updates $\Omega$; CHECKC checks the composition. On convergence (LOK), or after ITDIF2 iterations, species 5–11 (or 5–15 with LEXCOM) are diffused with NDIFCOM.

Design. Printing is switched off (LPRT = .FALSE., "G Somers 11/14"), so the block that writes the composition changes to IMODPT never runs. Large commented-out blocks remain (the old EQUAL, EQUAL2, DIFCOM, BSCOMP routines, and a coupling-factor diagnostic).

KC's changes. Unused arguments were removed from this routine's signature (DELTS, LC, MODEL, LFIRST, SJTOT, MRZONE, MXZONE, NRZONE, NZONE) and from its calls to SETUPV, VCIRC, MWIND, CODIFF, TRIDIA, DADCOEFT, NDIFCOM and CHECKJ (KC 2025-05-31); the call to MIXCZ was reduced to its four used arguments (KC 2025-05-30/31).

Questions for the experts

  1. LCODM and CODM are never set here (apparent bug). The test IF(LCODM.AND.CODM.GT.0.0D0) ("MHP 8/13 treat entire domain as unstable if a constant diffusion coefficient is being added") uses LCODM and CODM, which live in /MAG/ in PARMIN and CODIFF, but SECULR does not declare /MAG/. Here they are uninitialized local variables, so the option probably never takes effect in this routine (or depends on the compiler). Add COMMON/MAG/CODM,LCODM?
  2. The heavy species use the last region only. The final NDIFCOM for species 5–15 is called once, after the loop over regions, with IBEG, IEND, ECOD2 and EM of the last region processed. When there are several unstable regions, the others get no diffusion of these species. And if no region had $v > 0$ in this call, the values are those saved from an earlier call. Intended?
  3. Non-convergence counts as success. If the loop ends after ITDIF2 iterations without LOK, the code sets LOK = .TRUE. and goes on. Should it warn, or cut the step?
  4. LIMIX unused. LIMIX = .FALSE. is set "so the correct grads are used", but after KC's change MIXCZ no longer takes it. Remove the line, or was the behavior it controlled lost?
  5. Comment versus species. The comment says only "H, HE3, HE4" are diffused before the final iteration; the call covers species 1–4, which include $Z$ (species 3). Harmless, but the comment could say so.
