Explanation

Purpose. GETTAU computes the convective overturn timescale $\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ one pressure scale height above the base of the surface convection zone ("G Somers, 3/17"; its comment calls it "a new, systematic method"). Depending on where that point lies, it uses the interior model, the envelope integration, or the two stitched together.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: GETW, STARIN and WRTOUT.
  • Inputs: the model arrays (HCOMP, HR, HP, HD, HS1, HT, LC, M), the form factors FP, FT, the surface TEFFL, BL, HSTOT; and from /SCRTCH/, the interior's convective velocities SVEL and gradients SDEL, left there by an earlier physics pass.
  • What it calls: ENVINT (the envelope, which also sets the photospheric pressure PPHOT and, in the old scheme, TAUCZ), then TAUINTNEW or TAUINT.
  • Outputs: TAUCZ and PPHOT in /OVRTRN/, read by about twenty routines (wind and magnetic braking among them: MWIND, COWIND, DADCOEFT, …); RBCZ (argument), the radius of the convection zone's base in solar radii, set by TAUINTNEW (0 if it finds none); the old method (TAUINT) leaves it unchanged.
  • COMMON blocks: /OVRTRN/ (LNEWTCZ selects the new method, LCALCENV tells ENVINT whether to store the envelope's structure); /ENVSTRUCT/ (the envelope solution); /SCRTCH/ (interior velocities and gradients); /SPOTS/; /INTENV/, /INTATM/, /ENVGEN/ (declared, not used here); constants.

The method.

  1. Find the base of the surface convection zone, IMAX, scanning inward from the surface.
  2. If $\log P_\mathrm{base} - \log P_\mathrm{surf} < 1$ (or the old method is chosen), the point one scale height up may be in the envelope, so LCALCENV is set and the envelope's structure kept.
  3. Integrate the envelope with ENVINT from the surface conditions (radius and gravity from $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$, as in GETNEWENV, with the starspot correction to $T_\mathrm{eff}$). In the old method, ENVINT sets TAUCZ itself and the routine ends there.
  4. Copy the interior into work arrays, with $g = Gm/r^2$, the velocities and gradients from /SCRTCH/: DELF1 = $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$ (SDEL(1,·)) and DELF2 = $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ (SDEL(3,·), as PHYSIC stores them). With the new method and LCALCENV, append the envelope points 2 to NUMENV (the first is the last interior point), taking the same two gradients from EDELS(1,·) and EDELS(2,·), which QENV stores in the order $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, $\nabla$.
  5. TAUINTNEW (new method) or TAUINT (old) integrates the overturn time over these arrays.
  6. LCALCENV is reset to true.

KC's changes. The unused arguments HG (here), HCOMPF, HS2 and HSTOT (in the calls to TAUINTNEW and TAUINT) were removed (2025-05-31); the elements of /ENVSTRUCT/ were reordered (2025-05-30).

Questions for the experts

  1. Composition from beyond the model. The envelope points copy their composition from HCOMP(J,M+1), one point past the model's last point. Should it be HCOMP(J,M)? (The composition is not passed on to TAUINT* since KC's change, so this may have no effect now.)
  2. "At least 3 pressure scale heights". The comment says a difference of 1 in $\log P$ means "at least 3 PSCAS", but one dex in $P$ is $\ln 10 \approx 2.3$ scale heights. Is the threshold of 1 meant to be safe for one scale height, or should the comment change?
  3. Stale interior velocities. SVEL and SDEL come from /SCRTCH/, written by whichever routine ran the physics last. Are they guaranteed to belong to the current model when GETTAU is called from STARIN and WRTOUT?
  4. Unused variable. LALLCZ (fully convective star) is set but never used.

Review (round 1)
  1. Wrong premise (question 1). "SDEL(3,I) for the interior points but EDELS(2,·) for the envelope points … one of them is wrong." The two arrays store the gradients in different orders: PHYSIC sets SDEL(1..3) = $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ (physic.f lines 80–82), QENV sets EDEL(1..3) = $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, $\nabla$ (qenv.f lines 64–66), copied into EDELS. Both give $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$. Fix: state it in step 4 and drop the question.
  2. Unclear. "RBCZ … presumably set by TAUINTNEW". TAUINTNEW sets it (tauintnew.f lines 136–144, in solar radii at line 247, 0 at line 250); TAUINT does not. Fix: say so.
  3. Unclear. Step 4 "if needed, append the envelope points": only with the new method and LCALCENV, points 2 to NUMENV. Fix: say so.

Otherwise checked: the base search, the threshold, the ENVINT call with the spot correction, the early exit on TAUCZ ≠ 0, the reset of LCALCENV, the callers and the COMMON blocks; questions 2–5 are real.

Revised: question 1 answered in step 4 and dropped (the others renumbered); RBCZ and the appending stated.
