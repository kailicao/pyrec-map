Explanation

Purpose. SPLINTD2 evaluates a cubic spline at one point; it is splint.f's algorithm and equation, with arrays of the fixed size JSON = 5000, for the second derivatives made by SPLINC. Its header comment still names it "SUBROUTINE SPLINTD".

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only HPOINT, after SPLINC.
  • COMMON blocks: LUOUT, for the unit of its error message ("ERROR IN SPLINT ROUTINE", then STOP).

Questions for the experts

  1. Duplicates. SPLINTD2 and SPLINT differ only in their array declarations (and the comment's name SPLINTD). Could one routine serve both callers?
