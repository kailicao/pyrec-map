Explanation

Purpose. SPLINT evaluates a cubic spline at one point $x$, from the table $(x_i, y_i)$ and the second derivatives $y''_i$ computed beforehand: by SPLINJ, or, for the opacity routines GTLAOL, GTLAOL2 and GTPURZ, by CSPLINE (computed there, or tabulated by SULAOL and ZSULAOL). The comments say it is taken from Numerical Recipes (Press et al., p. 89) and was changed from REAL*4, for the opacities, to REAL*8 (MHP, 10/02).

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: BOOLE, CALCAD, and the low-temperature opacity routines GTLAOL, GTLAOL2 and GTPURZ.
  • Outputs: the value Y, and the bracketing indices KLO, KHI.
  • COMMON blocks: LUOUT, for the unit ISHORT of its error message.

The method. A bisection finds $x_\mathrm{lo} \le x < x_\mathrm{hi}$ (KLO, KHI). With $h = x_\mathrm{hi} - x_\mathrm{lo}$, $A = (x_\mathrm{hi} - x)/h$ and $B = 1 - A$:

$$y = A\,y_\mathrm{lo} + B\,y_\mathrm{hi} + \frac{h^2}{6}\left[(A^3 - A)\,y''_\mathrm{lo} + (B^3 - B)\,y''_\mathrm{hi}\right].$$

If two abscissas coincide ($h = 0$) it writes an error and stops the run. Outside the table it extrapolates the end interval's cubic, without a warning.

Design. KLO and KHI are arguments but are reset on entry, so a caller's previous bracket is not used as a starting guess.

Questions for the experts

  1. The comment's reference. The header's "SPLINE ABOVE" is Numerical Recipes' text: its spline (with end slopes) is CSPLINE here, while spline.f is an unrelated quadratic spline. A comment to update?
