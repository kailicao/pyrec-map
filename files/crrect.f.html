Explanation

Purpose. This file runs the Henyey iterations that correct the interior model at one level of the time step: it sets the surface boundary condition from the envelope, mixes and burns the composition, builds the linearized structure equations, solves them for the corrections to $\log P$, $\log T$, $\log r$ and $L$, applies the corrections (damped), and decides whether the model has converged.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments: the model (HP, HT, HR, HL, HD, HS, HS1, HS2, HCOMP, HPP, HTT), DELTS (time step, s), NITER (the maximum number of iterations), ITLVL (the level of iteration within the step), the flags LNEW, LRESET, LSBC, the envelope triangle (TRIT, TRIL, PS, TS, RS, FTRI, ISTORE, ESTORE), rotation (FP, FT, OMEGA, HJM, …); from COMMON, the tolerances HTOLER and the damping FCORR0, FCORRI, FCORR (/CTOL/), the start-of-step composition HCOMPP (/OLDMOD/), XNEW, ZNEW (/COMP/), LKUTHE (/HEFLSH/, the helium flash), LCORR (/CCOUT2/, print corrections), LROT (/ROT/).
  • Who calls it: the main program (main.f), within the evolution loop.
  • What it calls: SURFBC (the surface boundary condition from envelope integrations, as fitting coefficients CFENV), MIX (burning and mixing over the step), COEFFT (the equations and their derivatives, reduced by REDUCE), HSOLVE (the back-substitution), and with rotation GETROT and FPFT.
  • Outputs: the corrected model, BL, TEFFL, LFINI (converged), LARGE (corrections too large: the caller then cuts the step, probably), ITDONE, HMAX/NHMAX (largest corrections and where), TLUMX (luminosity by source, renormalized), and with rotation FP, FT, OMEGA, HKEROT. With LCORR, a line per iteration to ISHORT.

The method.

  1. If LSBC, call SURFBC with the envelope composition. It returns the fit of the envelope's base as linear relations in $\log P$ and $\log T$ at the outermost interior point: $$\log r = c_1\log P + c_2\log T + c_3, \qquad \log L = c_4\log P + c_5\log T + c_6, \qquad \log T_\mathrm{eff} = c_7\log P + c_8\log T + c_9.$$ With LDH (/DEBHU/), the composition for the Debye–Hückel correction (XXDH, YYDH, ZZDH, ZDH) is first set from shell M.
  2. At ITLVL > 2 with $\Delta t > 0$: reset the composition to its start-of-step value and call MIX.
  3. If the boundary condition was recomputed, the triangle did not flip (LFLP from SURFBC) and the model had converged at the previous level (LFINI), return at once.
  4. Iterate (at most NITER times):
  5. COEFFT and REDUCE build the system. Renormalize TLUMX so that its sum equals $L$ at the surface (not during the helium flash).
  6. From the second iteration, if the largest residuals of the equations (HMAX from REDUCE) are below HTOLER(5,·), the model has converged.
  7. The surface rows from the fit above (HSRF), the $L$ relation written in $L$ with the factor $\ln 10\,L$; HSOLVE gives the corrections $\delta$.
  8. Converged if $\max|\delta\log P|$, $\max|\delta\log T|$, $\max|\delta\log r|$ and $\max \min(|\delta L|, |\delta L/L|)$ are all below HTOLER(·,1); stop (return with LARGE) if any exceeds HTOLER(·,2).
  9. Apply $x \leftarrow x + f\,\delta x$, with the damping $f$ = FCORR, raised by FCORRI per iteration up to 1 if FCORR0 > 0; during the helium flash $f = 0.8$ once $|\delta L| \le 0.5$ and 1 once $\le 5\times10^{-3}$. The changes of $\log P$, $\log T$ over the step (HPP, HTT) are updated too.
  10. New $\log L$ and $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ (from the fit); with rotation, new $\Omega$, $f_P$, $f_T$ and rotational kinetic energy $\tfrac12\Omega j\,\Delta m$.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused arguments MODEL, MRZONE, NRZONE, NZONE removed; LSBC dropped from the call to SURFBC; QDT, QDP dropped from the call to MIX; MODEL dropped from the call to COEFFT.

Questions for the experts

  1. Operator precedence. IF(LNEW.OR.LRESET .AND.ITLVL.EQ.2) LSBC = .TRUE. reads as LNEW .OR. (LRESET .AND. ITLVL.EQ.2) in Fortran. Was (LNEW.OR.LRESET) .AND. ITLVL.EQ.2 meant?
  2. The luminosity test. The convergence test on $L$ uses $\min(|\delta L|, |\delta L/L|)$, with HL in solar units: where $L > 1\,L_\odot$ this is the relative change, elsewhere the absolute one (in a star fainter than the Sun, the absolute one everywhere); the first shell enters with $|\delta L|$ alone. Is the mixed test intended?
  3. Residual test before solving. At iteration 2 and later, if the residuals are below HTOLER(5,·), the routine returns as converged without solving. The comment notes that boundary equations are not checked. Can the surface condition then be unmet? (The message prints ITDONE+1 as the iteration count, but ITDONE is not incremented on this path.)
  4. Commented-out OVROT. The call to OVROT for rotating models is commented out here. Is overshoot for rotating models handled elsewhere now?
  5. Local zone arrays. MXZON0, JCORE, JENV are local and set by MIX (only MXZONE is an argument, so the caller never sees the others). JENV is then passed to COEFFT, which uses it with entropy terms and mass gain (LMDOT, DMDT0 > 0) to choose the shells whose $\log P$, $\log T$ changes get extra terms. At levels 1 and 2 MIX is not called, so COEFFT gets the value saved from an earlier call, or an unset value at the first call. Should JENV come from the caller?
