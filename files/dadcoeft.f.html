Explanation

Purpose. This file solves the transport of angular momentum by rotational instabilities over one time step when advection by meridional circulation is included (the option LDIFAD, "combined diffusion/advection equation", MHP 06/02). Since the circulation velocity depends on $\Omega$ and its first three radial derivatives, it writes the problem as four first-order equations per shell and solves the resulting band system with BANDW. It also returns the coefficient of composition mixing that results.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments DR (the even grid's spacing), DT (time step), EI (moment of inertia of each grid point), EW ($\Omega$ at the start), NTOT, WIND1, WIND2 (the wind's change of $\Omega$ at the start and end of the step, from COWIND or MWIND via the caller), LFIX (hold the surface $\Omega$ fixed). From COMMON: the velocity coefficients of the circulation and instabilities (EV0, EV1A, EV1B, EV2A, EV2B, ECOD5, ECOD6, FGEOM, ESS, EGSF in /DIFAD3/; QCHIRE in /EGRIDCHI/; EQQCOD, EQQQCOD in /EGRIDDER/; EWST, EQWRST in /DIFADDT/), set up by SETUPV and VCIRC; the multipliers in /VMULT/, /VMULT2/; DJOK, ITDIF2 in /DIFUS/; WMAX, EXW in /CWIND/; TAUCZ in /OVRTRN/; REQ, FGEOMIX in /DIFAD4/.
  • Who calls it: SECULR, for each unstable region when LDIFAD is on (otherwise DCOEFT sets up a tridiagonal diffusion system).
  • What it calls: BANDW.
  • Outputs: DJ (the change of angular momentum of each point), SUMDJ (their sum), LOKAD (converged), ECOD2 (the composition diffusion coefficient); ECOD3, ECOD4 in /DIFAD/ (read by CHECKJ for printing); VESN in /DIFAD4/. Many lines to the screen.

The equation. In the comment's words, the diffusion part is $$\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{4\pi\rho r^2}\,\frac{1}{I/M}\,\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(D\,\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial r}\right),$$ and LDIFAD adds an advective term $\partial_r(\rho r^4 V\Omega)$, with $V$ a function of $\Omega$, $\Omega'$, $\Omega''$ and $\Omega'''$.

The method.

  1. Unknowns. Four per shell, stored in order $\Omega$, $\Omega''$, $\Omega'$, $\Omega'''$; $NM = 4N - 2$ unknowns in all (the surface has two). Each row couples to unknowns up to 4 places before and 5 after, a band that BANDW handles.
  2. Rows. For shell $i$: the $\Omega$ equation, implicit in time, with advective terms ($\tfrac12\Delta t/(I_i\,\Delta r)$ times ECOD3, ECOD5) and diffusive terms ($\Delta t/(I_i\Delta r)$ times ECOD4, ECOD6); then three rows that define the derivatives by finite differences (for example $\Omega'_i = (\Omega_{i+1} - \Omega_i)/\Delta r$). At the centre, no flux; at the surface, the same unless LFIX, which holds $\Omega$ fixed.
  3. Coefficients. From the current $\Omega$ (EWPREV), with $\bar\Omega$ the interface mean (corrected by a second difference): $V_1 = f_w E_0\bar\Omega^2(E_{1a} + \bar\Omega^2E_{1b})$, $V_2 = f_w E_0\bar\Omega^2(E_{2a} + E_{2b})$; ECOD3 $= 0.2\,V_1\,f_\mathrm{geom}$ and ECOD4 $= (0.2\,V_2 + E_{SS} + E_{GSF})\,f_\mathrm{geom}$, not below zero. The shear and GSF terms are rescaled by $(\partial\Omega/\partial r)^2$ relative to the start.
  4. The wind. The surface row's right-hand side is reduced by $\Delta J = -\tfrac12(W_1 + W_2)\,I_N\,(\Delta t_\mathrm{sub}/\Delta t)$. From the second outer iteration, WIND2 is rescaled to the current surface $\Omega$ with the saturation law, $W_2 = W_{2,0}\,(\Omega_n^\mathrm{sat}/\Omega_p^\mathrm{sat})^{n-1}\,\Omega_n/\Omega_p$.
  5. Iterations. Three nested loops: substeps (NSTEP), an outer iteration (NNN, meant for the $d\theta/dt$ term, now zero) and an inner iteration (NN) on the coefficients. Inner convergence: $\max|\Delta\Omega/\Omega| <$ DJOK. Each inner solution is rescaled so that total angular momentum is conserved exactly: $\Omega \leftarrow \Omega\,(J_0 + \Delta J)/J$.
  6. Step cutting. If a correction exceeds 10% ($|\Delta\Omega/\Omega| > 0.1$) or the iterations fail, double the number of substeps and restart; after 5 cuts, STOP 111.
  7. Composition mixing. From the final solution, the circulation velocity $V_\mathrm{tot}$ (VTOT2: the sum of the advective and diffusive terms, with the ECOD4 term replaced by its circulation part alone, V1E, so that shear and GSF are not counted twice) and $$D_\mathrm{mix} = \left(5\,|f_c f_{ES}\,V_\mathrm{tot}\,r_\mathrm{eq}| + f_c f_{SS}E_{SS} + f_c f_{GSF}E_{GSF}\right) f_\mathrm{geom,mix}.$$

Design. The header comments are those of DCOEFT ("DCOEFT sets up…", "a tridiagonal matrix"), not updated for the band system. A residual check of the band solution (C = AA·B) is computed but never used. The damping factor FX is always 1 (the alternative is commented out). The time-derivative terms of $\theta$ (VTHN, VTHP) are all set to zero. /DEUTER/ is included but not used.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused argument EJ removed; the call to BANDW no longer passes M1, M2 (the band widths 4 and 5, now fixed in BANDW).

Questions for the experts

  1. Undeclared Rossby scaling. The saturation update uses LROSSBY and PMMSOLTAU, which live in /PMMWIND/, but this routine does not include /PMMWIND/. Here they are uninitialized local variables (a logical and a real), so the Rossby scaling of $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ probably never applies in this path (or applies with garbage). Should /PMMWIND/ be added?
  2. Unused residual check. The array C (the product of the saved matrix and the solution) is computed "to check on the matrix inversion", then ignored. Should it be compared with D?
  3. The $d\theta/dt$ term. The outer iteration exists for it, but every version of the term is commented out or zero. Can the loop go, or is the term meant to come back?
  4. Size limit. NM = 4*NTOT-2 must not exceed NMAX = 8000, so NTOT $\le$ 2000; nothing checks it.
  5. Hard-coded factors. The factors 0.2 in ECOD3/ECOD4 and 5 in VESN, and the 10% limit on corrections. Where do the 0.2 and 5 come from (they are reciprocal)?
  6. Screen output. Several unconditional WRITE(*,*) per iteration (sums of $J$, iteration counters, the first and last 10 points). Debug leftovers?

SUBROUTINE DADCOEFT(DR,DT,EI,EW,NTOT,WIND1,WIND2,DJ,

Implicit advection–diffusion of angular momentum on the even grid: four unknowns per shell ($\Omega$ and three derivatives) in a band system solved by BANDW; iterates on $\Omega$-dependent coefficients, enforces angular-momentum conservation with the wind, cuts the substep on failure, and returns DJ, SUMDJ, LOKAD and the composition coefficient ECOD2.
