Explanation

Purpose. YTIME gives the time step allowed by helium burning. Its comment: stars with a helium luminosity have a time step limited by the time to burn ATIME(4) of $Y$ at the temperature maximum, or the fraction ATIME(5) of $Y$ there; a high central density is taken as the sign of a giant in the helium flash rather than a horizontal-branch star.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the model (arguments: $\log\rho$ HD, HCOMP, $L$, $m$, $\log T$, JCORE, M, JXBEG), work arrays for the rates, and through COMMON blocks the limits ATIME (CTLIM), the extended-composition switch LEXCOM (FLAG), and ENU, EALPCA (NEWEPS, set by ENGEB).
  • Who calls it: only HTIMER.
  • What it calls: ENGEB, for the energy rates at the temperature maximum.
  • Outputs: DELTSY, the step (in seconds, by its use in HTIMER), and EG(1..6), the energy rates at the temperature maximum.

The method.

  • Helium flash ($\log\rho_c \ge 5$): find the shell of maximum temperature, compute its energy rates with ENGEB, and take $$\Delta t = \frac{10^{15}}{\varepsilon_5},$$ with $\varepsilon_5$ = EG(5) (probably the triple-α rate, by the order in ENGEB), at least $10^{-22}$.
  • Otherwise: with $Y_\mathrm{core}$ at JCORE and $q = 5.85\times10^{17}$ erg/g (the commented-out Q3A), if $Y_\mathrm{core} \ge$ ATIME(1), $$\Delta Y = \min(\texttt{ATIME(4)},\ \texttt{ATIME(5)}\,Y_\mathrm{core}),\qquad \Delta t = \frac{q}{L_\odot}\,\frac{m}{L/L_\odot}\,\Delta Y$$ at JCORE; otherwise the same with $m$ and $L$ just below the hydrogen shell (JXBEG-1).

KC's changes. Removed the unused arguments QDP and QDT, and shortened the call to ENGEB to its new argument list (2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. $Y$ or $1 - Z$? YCORE = 1D0 - HCOMP(3,JCORE) is $X + Y$, not $Y$ (HCOMP(2,·)). In a helium core $X \approx 0$ and the two agree, but during hydrogen burning they do not. Intended?
  2. An undefined step. In the ELSE branch ($Y_\mathrm{core} <$ ATIME(1)), DELTSY is used on the right-hand side before it is set in this call; it holds the value SAVEd from an earlier call. A line like DELTSY = MIN(ATIME(4), ATIME(5)*Y) seems to be missing.
  3. The same threshold for $X$ and $Y$. ATIME(1) is the central-hydrogen threshold in XTIME, and here the threshold for $Y_\mathrm{core}$. Two parameters?
  4. Hard-coded values. The flash criterion $\log\rho_c \ge 5$, the constant $10^{15}$ (whose units are unclear) and the floor $10^{-22}$.
