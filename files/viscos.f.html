Explanation

Purpose. VISCOS computes, for every shell, the kinematic viscosity (radiative plus molecular) and the radiative thermal diffusivity, which the rotational mixing routines use (for example the Prandtl number $\nu/K$ in vcirc.f). It also computes the Endal & Sofia versions "for comparison only".

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the composition HCOMP (its first 11 species), $\log\rho$ (HD), $\log T$ (HT), and through COMMON blocks the opacity SO (SCRTCH) and $c_P$ CP (TEMP), filled by PHYSIC.
  • Who calls it: only PHYSIC.
  • Outputs: in COMMON TEMP, VISC (cm²/s) and THDIF (cm²/s), shared with GETW, HPOINT and MIDMOD.

The method. With HCOMP's species $i$ of mass $A_i$ (WEIGHT) and charge $Z_i$ (Z), $n_i = X_i/A_i$, the mean ion charge $\bar Z = \sum n_i Z_i/\sum n_i$ and ELECN = $\bar Z\rho/m_u$:

  1. Radiative viscosity (Ledoux 1958, electron scattering only, by the comment): $$\nu_\mathrm{rad} = 6.728\times10^{-26}\,\frac{T^4}{\kappa\,\rho^2}$$ (the constant is $4a/15c$).
  2. Molecular viscosity (Spitzer 1962). With the Coulomb logarithm $$\ln\Lambda = 9.4245 + \tfrac12\ln\!\left(\frac{T^3\,\min(1,\,4.2\times10^5/T)}{n_e\,\bar Z^2}\right),$$ each species contributes $$\eta_i = \frac{3.125\times10^{-15}\,T^{5/2}\,n_i\sqrt{A_i}}{(\ln\Lambda - \ln Z_i)\,Z_i^2\,\sum_j n_j Z_j^2\sqrt{(A_i + A_j)/A_j}},$$ and $\nu_\mathrm{mol} = \sum_i \eta_i/\rho$ (species 3, the total $Z$, has $Z_3 = 0$ and is skipped).
  3. VISC $= \nu_\mathrm{rad} + \nu_\mathrm{mol}$.
  4. Thermal diffusivity (radiation only; conduction neglected, by the comment): $$K = \frac{16\,\sigma\,T^3}{3\,\kappa\,\rho^2\,c_P},$$ with $\sigma = 5.669\times10^{-5}$ written in the code.

KC's changes. The unused argument LC was removed (2025-05-31), with it the only means to skip convective zones (see question 1).

Questions for the experts

  1. "Skip convective zones". The comment above the loop says so, but every shell is computed. Intended?
  2. The electron density. ELECN = ZF*RHO/AMU uses the mean charge per ion, $\bar Z\rho/m_u$; the electron density is $\rho/m_u \sum_i X_i Z_i/A_i$, smaller by the mean ion mass. Is the Coulomb logarithm meant to use this approximation?
  3. The total $Z$ as a species. Species 3 (the total metal fraction $Z$) has weight 1.0 and charge 0; it is skipped in the sums over $Z_i^2$, but it adds $Z/1.0$ to COMSUM, and so lowers $\bar Z$. Should it be left out of COMSUM?
  4. Comparison values never used. VISCR2, VISMO2, VISC2 (Endal & Sofia) are computed for every shell and discarded.
  5. Hard-coded constants. The atomic mass unit, $\sigma$ and isotope masses are written in the code, not taken from the constants in COMMON; some masses differ from standard values ($^{15}$N 15.0011, $^{17}$O 16.99491 and $^{18}$O 17.99491, against about 15.0001, 16.9991 and 17.9992).
