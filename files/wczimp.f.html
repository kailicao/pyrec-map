Explanation

Purpose. WCZIMP sets the rotation of a convection zone (shells ISTART–IEND) at fixed total angular momentum, as wcz.f did, and adds options to impose a rotation profile on the radiative interior below the surface convection zone (JNT, 2015/05/14, copied into this code 2025/09/03–04). IMPJMOD ("impose J model", PARMIN's comment) chooses: 0, no effect; 1, solid-body rotation; 2, a solid-body core decoupled from the surface convection zone; 3, a solid-body core coupled to it.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: GETROT and GETW (in place of WCZ since 09/25).
  • What it calls: SOLID, for the zone, single shells, or the interior.
  • Inputs and outputs: the arguments, as in solid.f: HJM, OMEGA, HI, QIW, ETA2, R0 are updated.
  • COMMON blocks: SBROT (LSOLID, IMPJMOD, set by PARMIN; shared with GETW, MIDMOD, SECULR), ROT (WALPCZ), CONST1.

The method. With $m_j$ = HS2, $j_j$ = HJM, $I_j$ = HI, $\alpha$ = WALPCZ, $r_j = 10^{\texttt{HR}(j)}$:

  • Solid body for the zone if $\alpha > 0$, LSOLID, IMPJMOD = 1, or the zone is not the surface one (IEND < M).
  • IMPJMOD = 2: the zone follows $\Omega_j = \Omega_0 r_j^{\alpha}$ with $\Omega_0 = \sum_\mathrm{cz} m_j j_j / \sum_\mathrm{cz} r_j^{\alpha} I_j$; the interior (shells 1 to ISTART-1) is then made a solid body on its own, keeping its own angular momentum.
  • IMPJMOD = 3: the interior and the zone share one angular momentum budget; the interior rotates at the rate of the zone's base: $$\Omega_0 = \frac{\sum_{j \le N} m_j j_j}{r_\mathrm{s}^{\alpha}\sum_{j \le \mathrm{s}} I_j + \sum_{\mathrm{s} < j \le N} r_j^{\alpha} I_j},$$ with s = ISTART and $N$ = IEND; then $\Omega_j = \Omega_0 r_j^{\alpha}$ in the zone, $\Omega_j = \Omega_\mathrm{s}$ below it.
  • Constant specific angular momentum if $\alpha \le -2$ (and IMPJMOD = 0), as in WCZ.
  • Otherwise the power law for the zone alone, as in WCZ. After each assignment, SOLID is called on single shells to make $\Omega$, $I$ and $j$ consistent.

Questions for the experts

  1. The interior's moments of inertia. In IMPJMOD = 2 and 3, the interior's $I_j$ are those in HI on entry (from the previous rotation), and only the final SOLID call updates them. Is one pass enough, or should the budget be iterated?
  2. IMPJMOD = 3 and SOLID. After the interior is set to $\Omega_\mathrm{s}$, SOLID is called on shells 1 to ISTART-1, which conserves their total angular momentum and may change $\Omega$ again. Does the interior stay at the base's rate?
  3. Dates in the comments. The header says "copy of 2025/05/14", the next comments "2015/05/14"; PARMIN says "05/15". Which is the original date?
  4. $\alpha = 0$. WALPCZ = 0 (with IMPJMOD = 0) reaches the power-law branch, while WCZ treated it as solid body; the result is the same rotation, computed shell by shell.
