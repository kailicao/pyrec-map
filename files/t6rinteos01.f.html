Explanation

Purpose. T6RINTEOS01 interpolates one equation-of-state quantity in temperature T6 and density, for the OPAL EOS tables read by READCOEOS01 (probably the 2001 release, from the name). It is the routine of t6rinterp.f, with the method and equations described there, for larger tables (NR = 169, NT = 191) and COMMON blocks of its own (AEOS, AAEOS, BBEOS, EEOS, EEEOS).

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only ESAC01, after the interpolation in $X$.
  • What it calls: QUADEOS01.
  • Output: ESACT in COMMON EEOS, shared with ESAC01, OEQOS01, RADSUB01, READCOEOS01 and RHOOFP01.

Design. Unlike T6RINTERP, the smoothing in temperature of the second set of quadratics (ESACTQ) is inside the IP = 3 block ("moved to loc a"), so the weight DIX is always defined when it is used.

Questions for the experts

  1. The density blend without IP = 3. When IQ = 3 but IP ≠ 3, ESACTQ is computed but not used (the final blend needs both). Is falling back to the lower quadratic in density intended there?
