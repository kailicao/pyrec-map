Explanation

Purpose. YLLOC prepares the OPAL92 opacity tables for interpolation in density ("spline coeff.", in yllo3d.f's list): for each composition and temperature, it fits a natural cubic spline to $\log\kappa$ against $\log R$ with YSPLIN and stores the coefficients. With L2Z it does the same for the second table at another $Z$ (DBG 5/94).

How it fits.

  • Inputs: COMMON LLOT (OPACITY, $\log\kappa$, with NUMXM compositions and NUMTM temperatures) and GLLOT (the grid of $\log R$, GRHOT3), and their second-table versions, filled by SETLLO.
  • Who calls it: only SETLLO.
  • What it calls: YSPLIN.
  • Outputs: COMMON LINTPL (CFORD, NDS, NDD) and LINTPL2, read by YLLO2D, YLLO2D2 and LL4TH.

The method. Entries at or below $-9.999$ mark missing values and are skipped; the run stops if the first valid density is not the table's first ("CHECK NDS"). Rows with one valid point or none get no spline.

Questions for the experts

  1. Few points. A row with 2 or 3 valid densities is passed to YSPLIN, which needs at least 4 (see ysplin.f). Can such rows occur at the edges of the table?
