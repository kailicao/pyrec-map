Explanation

Purpose. GRSETT computes the gravitational settling of helium (and, with LDIFZ, of the metals) over one time step, "using the formalism of Bahcall and Loeb 1989" (its comment). The diffusion equation has two terms: one in $d\ln P/dr \cdot dX/dr$ (settling) and one in $d^2X/dr^2$ (diffusion). GRSETT treats the first explicitly with a two-step Lax–Wendroff scheme and the second implicitly, iterating on the coefficients.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: MIX and ROTMIX, the mixing drivers of the evolution step.
  • Inputs: DT (time step, s), HCOMP (mass fractions; HCOMP(1) = $X$, HCOMP(2) = $Y$, HCOMP(3) = $Z$), HQPR = $d\ln P/dr$, HR = $\log r$, HD = $\log\rho$, HS1 = $m$ (g, not logged), HT = $\log T$, LC (convective or not), M, STOT.
  • Output: the new HCOMP(1) and HCOMP(2) (via EQUAL_TO_MODEL, which uses $X+Y+Z=1$).
  • Calls, in order: SETUP_GRSETT (the coefficients COD1, COD2 and their $X$-derivatives, the convection-zone edges, and whether the model is fully convective), MODEL_TO_EQUAL (to an equally spaced grid in radius), LAX_WENDROF1 and LAX_WENDROF2, then in each iteration GET_IMP_DIFFCO and TRIDIAG_GS, and at the end EQUAL_TO_MODEL.
  • COMMON blocks: GRAVST (GRTOL, NITER_GS; set by PARMIN, shared with the microdiffusion routines, MIX, ROTMIX, PUTSTORE, the output writers), GRAVS3 (LDIFZ), GRAVEZ (the metals on the equal grid, with the Lax–Wendroff pair and the regridding routines), CONST1, LUOUT.

The method.

  1. Set up the coefficients; return at once for a fully convective model.
  2. Move to $N$ (NPT) equally spaced radii $r_i$ (step $\Delta r$), with values at the points (E…) and at the midpoints (E…_H). Keep the starting $X_i^0$ (EX_ORIG).

  3. Explicit term. LAX_WENDROF1 gives the half-step changes $\Delta X_{i+1/2}$; the midpoint coefficients are linearized in them, $$F_{i+1/2} \leftarrow F_{i+1/2} + \Delta X_{i+1/2}\,\frac{\partial F}{\partial X}\Big|_{i+1/2},$$ and LAX_WENDROF2 applies the full step, giving $X'_i$ (EX_PRIME).

  4. Implicit term. With $\alpha_i = 4\pi\Delta t/(\Delta r\,\Delta m_i)$ (ALPHA; $\Delta m_i$ the mass between midpoints, $m_{1/2}$ at the centre and $M - m_{N-1/2}$ at the surface), iterate up to NITER_GS times: take the change at the midpoints $\tfrac12(X_i + X_{i-1} - X^p_i - X^p_{i-1})$ since the previous iterate $X^p$; let GET_IMP_DIFFCO build the tridiagonal coefficients $a_i, b_i, c_i$ from ECOD2_H corrected by that change; solve $$a_i X_{i-1} + b_i X_i + c_i X_{i+1} = X'_i$$ with TRIDIAG_GS. With $D_{i+1/2}$ = ECOD2_H(I) (the coefficient between points $i$ and $i+1$), the rows are those of an implicit (backward Euler) step: $$\bigl(1+\alpha_i(D_{i-1/2}+D_{i+1/2})\bigr)X_i - \alpha_i D_{i-1/2}X_{i-1} - \alpha_i D_{i+1/2}X_{i+1} = X'_i,$$ with $D_{1/2} = D_{N+1/2} = 0$ (no flux through the centre or the surface). Stop when $\max_i (X_i - X^p_i) <$ GRTOL; print each iteration's largest change.

  5. Return the change $X_i - X_i^0$ (and $Z_i - Z_i^0$), which EQUAL_TO_MODEL interpolates back to the model's points (the change rather than the abundance, "to minimize errors from the interpolation").

Design. Each iteration writes a line to the short output (ISHORT). The metals get the explicit term only; the implicit iteration is for $X$ alone.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the call to MODEL_TO_EQUAL no longer passes HQPR and HTU.

Questions for the experts

  1. The convergence test ignores negative changes. DXMAX is the largest signed $X_i - X^p_i$, not its absolute value, so an iteration whose changes are all negative is taken as converged at once. Should it be ABS(DX)?
  2. Stale comment. "Settling of metals will be added later": metal settling was added in 3/94 (LDIFZ), for the explicit term only. Should the metals also get the implicit term?
  3. Unused. ZZ1 and ZZ2 (the metals' changes at the two ends) are computed and not used; the argument HD is passed on but GRSETT itself does not use it.
  4. The coefficient correction is one midpoint off. In the iteration, EX_H(I) (I = 2..NPT) is the change at the midpoint between points I-1 and I, but GET_IMP_DIFFCO adds EQCOD2X_H(I)*EX_H(I) to ECOD2_H(I), the coefficient between points I and I+1 (its matrix uses ECOD2_H(I-1) on the left of point I and ECOD2_H(I) on the right). ECOD2_H(1) also gets the stale EX_H(1) left by LAX_WENDROF1. Should the loop set EX_H(I-1) instead?
  5. Failure is only reported. When the iteration does not converge, GRSETT writes a message and carries on with the last iterate.

Settling of He (and metals) in one step: regrid to equal radii, explicit Lax–Wendroff for the settling term, implicit iterated tridiagonal solve for the diffusion term, regrid the change back (above).
