Explanation

Purpose. Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the model's $Z$ (MHP 7/98: "at fixed Z"): a three-dimensional interpolation in $X$, $\log T$ and $\log R$ ($R = \rho/T_6^3$). The same as OP952D, with one more interpolation in $X$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the table CAPPA(10,70,19) of $\log\kappa$ at the model's $Z$ in /LLOT95/, built by OP95ZTAB; the indices and weights in /OP95INDX/, /OP95FACT/ (BX(1,·) for $X$) and /OP95EXT/, from GETOPAL95.
  • Who calls it: GETOPAL95, when the point's $Z$ matches the table's ZTAB (to a relative $10^{-4}$) but its $X$ does not match the surface table's, and the acoustic depth is not being computed.
  • Outputs: $\kappa$, $\log\kappa$, QOD and QOT, as in OP952D.

The method. For each of the four nearest $X$ tables, the interpolation of OP952D in $\log R$ (with linear extrapolation beyond the table) and in $\log T$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its two derivatives; these three are then interpolated in $X$ with four-point weights BX(1,J), and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ is converted to constant $\rho$ as in OP952D.

10      PARAMETER (NUMT=70)
11      PARAMETER (NUMD=19)
12      PARAMETER (NUMX=10)
13      PARAMETER (NUMZ=13)
14      PARAMETER (NUMXZ=126)
15C AS ABOVE FOR THE MODEL Z.
16      COMMON /LLOT95/CAPPA(NUMX,NUMT,NUMD),ZTAB
17C INDICES FOR INTERPOLATION IN Z,X,T, AND R
18      COMMON/OP95INDX/JZ,JX(4,4),JT,JD(4)
19C INTERPOLATION FACTORS FOR Z,X,T, AND R, AS WELL AS DERIVATIVE
20C FACTORS FOR T AND RHO.
21      COMMON/OP95FACT/BZ(4),BX(4,4),BT(4),BTT(4),BD(4,4),BDD(4,4)
22C DATA FOR LINEAR EXTRAPOLATION WHEN OUTSIDE TABLE IN RHO
23      COMMON/OP95EXT/RL,RLL,RLH(4),LEXTL,LEXTH,LEXH(4)
24      DIMENSION C(4),QC(4),CC(4),CCT(4),CCD(4)
25      SAVE
26C FIND OPACITY AT EACH OF THE 4 NEARBY VALUES OF X
27      DO J = 1,4
28         IX = JX(1,J)
29C FOR EACH X, GET CAPPA FOR 4 VALUES OF T
30         DO I = 1,4
31            IT = JT + I - 1
32C FOR EACH T, GET CAPPA FOR 4 VALUES OF R
33            ID = JD(I)
34C LOG CAPPA AT DESIRED RHO FOR EACH OF THE 4 DESIRED T.
35            C(I) = BD(I,1)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID)+ BD(I,2)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+1)
36     *         + BD(I,3)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+2) + BD(I,4)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+3)
37C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG R FOR EACH OF THE 4 DESIRED T.
38            QC(I)=BDD(I,1)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID)+BDD(I,2)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+1)
39     *          +BDD(I,3)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+2)+BDD(I,4)*CAPPA(IX,IT,ID+3)
40         END DO
41C CHECK ON WHETHER RHO IS OUTSIDE OF THE TABLE AND NEEDS EXTRAPOLATION
42         IF(LEXTL)THEN
43C FACTOR IN R
44            DELTAR = RL-RLL
45C CORRECT CAPPA BY USING THE DERIVATIVE AT THE BOUNDARY.
46            DO I = 1,4
47               C(I) = C(I)+DELTAR*QC(I)
48            END DO
49         ELSE IF(LEXTH)THEN
50            DO I = 1,4
51               IF(LEXH(I))THEN
52                  DELTAR = RL - RLH(I)
53                  C(I) = C(I)+DELTAR*QC(I)
54               ENDIF
55            END DO
56         ENDIF

58         CC(J) = BT(1)*C(1) + BT(2)*C(2) + BT(3)*C(3) + BT(4)*C(4)
59C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG T
60         CCT(J) = BTT(1)*C(1) + BTT(2)*C(2) + BTT(3)*C(3) + BTT(4)*C(4)
61C D LOG CAPPA/D LOG R
62         CCD(J) = BT(1)*QC(1) + BT(2)*QC(2) + BT(3)*QC(3) + BT(4)*QC(4)
63      END DO
64C INTERPOLATE FOR LOG CAPPA IN X.
65      OL = BX(1,1)*CC(1) + BX(1,2)*CC(2) + BX(1,3)*CC(3) +
66     *     BX(1,4)*CC(4)
67C INTERPOLATE FOR QOT IN X.
68      QOT = BX(1,1)*CCT(1) + BX(1,2)*CCT(2) + BX(1,3)*CCT(3) +
69     *      BX(1,4)*CCT(4)
70C INTERPOLATE FOR QOD IN X.
71      QOD = BX(1,1)*CCD(1) + BX(1,2)*CCD(2) + BX(1,3)*CCD(3) +
72     *      BX(1,4)*CCD(4)
73C CORRECT FROM DERIVATE AT FIXED R TO DERIVATIVE AT FIXED RHO.
74      QOT = QOT - 3.0D0*QOD
75      O = 1.0D1**OL
76      RETURN
77      END
