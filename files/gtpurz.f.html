Explanation

Purpose. GTPURZ returns the opacity from the Livermore table for pure metals ($Z = 1$), with its derivatives, at a given density and temperature.

How it fits. Called only by GETOPAC, when $Z > 0.1$ and $\log T > 7.7$ (its comment: "Z > 0.10, T > 5 × 10⁷ K"; it stops if no pure-$Z$ table was read, LPUREZ): GETOPAC then interpolates $\log\kappa$ and its derivatives linearly in $Z$ between a normal table (at ZIT) and this pure-$Z$ table ($Z = 1$). The table is in COMMON ZLAOL (read by RDZLAOL) and ZSLAOL (its density splines, from ZSULAOL); it also takes TOLLAOL from NWLAOL. Calls LOCATE, XRNG4, CSPLINE, SPLINT.

The method. As in GTLAOL but without the composition step: a spline in $\log\rho$ at each of four temperatures (with linear extrapolation within the margin $\ln$ TOLLAOL), then a natural spline in $\log T$; chord slopes for the derivatives; $\log\kappa$ capped at 35.

Questions for the experts

  1. Unused block. SLAOL is declared but not used.
  2. Same margin question as GTLAOL (LOG(TOLLAOL) is a natural log).
