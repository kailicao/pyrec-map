Explanation

Purpose. ZSULAOL ("spline pure Z LAOL89") prepares the pure-metal LAOL89 opacity table (DBG 12/95) for interpolation in density, as sulaol.f does for the main tables: for each temperature, a natural cubic spline of $\log\kappa$ against $\log\rho$, through the nonzero entries, if there are at least four.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: COMMON ZLAOL (ZOLAOL, the grids ZOT, ZORHO, sizes NUMRHOZ, NUMTZ), filled by RDZLAOL.
  • Who calls it: SETUPOPAC.
  • What it calls: CSPLINE (end slopes $10^{30}$, a natural spline).
  • Outputs: COMMON ZSLAOL ($\log\kappa$, $\log\rho$, second derivatives, points per temperature), read by GTPURZ; a temperature with fewer than four nonzero entries gets NUMRSZ = 0, and GTPURZ skips it. ZOT is converted to $\log_{10} T$ in place; this is safe because the routine runs once per job (main program → SETUPS → SETUPOPAC).

Questions for the experts

  1. Logarithm in place. As in SULAOL, ZOT becomes $\log_{10}T$ in place, so a second call would take the logarithm twice. It is called once per job today; should the new code keep $T$ and $\log T$ apart?
