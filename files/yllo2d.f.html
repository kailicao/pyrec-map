Explanation

Purpose. YLLO2D interpolates the OPAL (Livermore) opacity tables (the comment of yllo3d.f: "the interpolation facility for the Livermore opacity tables using cubic spline interpolation") in two dimensions, at one composition table: in density with the stored splines, then in temperature. It returns $\log\kappa$, $\kappa$ and the derivatives with respect to $\log R$ and $\log T_6$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: $\log T_6$ (T), $\log R$ (D), and the grid indices of composition (M1; 4 means the surface table) and temperature (M2); the spline coefficients CFORD (COMMON LINTPL, from YLLOC) or, for the surface table, CFORD4 (LLOT4, from LL4TH), whose density range (NDS, NDD) is taken from composition KIPM1, which LL4TH sets; the grids in GLLOT.
  • Who calls it: YLLO3D and LL4TH.
  • What it calls: FINDEX (the density interval) and INTPOL (piecewise interpolation in $T$ with its derivative).
  • Outputs: O, OL ($\log_{10}\kappa$), QODF = $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log R$, QOTF = $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ at fixed $R$; and M3, the density index.

The method. For up to six temperatures around M2 (from M2-2 to M2+3), the spline in density gives $$\log\kappa = c_1 + c_2\,\delta + c_3\,\delta^2 + c_4\,\delta^3,\qquad \frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R} = c_2 + 2c_3\,\delta + 3c_4\,\delta^2,$$ with $\delta$ the distance from the knot; outside the table's densities (FINDEX then returns a negative index), the linear part only ($c_1 + c_2\delta$), from the first or last knot. Then INTPOL interpolates both in $\log T$ and gives $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$. The run stops if $T$ lies outside the temperatures used ("EXTRAPOLATION FAILS").

yllo2d2.f is the same routine for the second table at another $Z$.

Questions for the experts

  1. The error message. The check NDSS .NE. 1 stops with "OPAL95 2D CHECK NDSS", but these are the OPAL92 tables of LL4TH and SETLLO (OPAL95 has its own routines). A message to correct?
  2. Unused values. LMORE, QODTI and COMMON LLOT's OPACITY are not used.

SUBROUTINE YLLO2D(T,D,M1,M2,M3,O,OL,QODF,QOTF)

Two-dimensional interpolation in one OPAL92 composition table: splines in $\log R$ at up to six temperatures, then piecewise interpolation in $\log T$; with linear extrapolation in density.
