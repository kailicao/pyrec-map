Explanation

Purpose. splnr is Numerical Recipes' spline in single precision, identical to splinnr.f (see there for the method): the second derivatives of a cubic spline, with given end slopes or natural ends.

How it fits. Nothing in src/ calls it, and it uses no COMMON block.
