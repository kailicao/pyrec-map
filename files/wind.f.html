Explanation

Purpose. WIND removes angular momentum from the surface convection zone by a magnetized wind, "a Weber–Davis magnetic wind model" (its comment), over one time step. It is the older loss law; MWIND calls it when LMWIND is off, and otherwise uses its own law.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: $\log L$ (BL), the time step DELTS (s), the convection zone's mass range (HSBOT, HSTOP) and shells (JSTART, JEND), the switch LJDOT, the surface rate OMEGAS, the mass SMASS (in solar masses, probably), $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$, the zone's moment of inertia HICZ. Through COMMON blocks: the law's constant and exponents (CWIND: CONSTFACTOR, EXMD, EXW, EXR, EXM, the saturation WMAX, set by PARMIN); the overturn time TAUCZ (OVRTRN, from GETTAU); LINSTB (ROT).
  • Who calls it: only MWIND.
  • Output: HJM, each shell's specific angular momentum in the zone, reduced.
  • COMMON blocks: CWIND, OVRTRN, ROT, the constants; DEUTER is declared but not used.

The method.

  1. Saturation. $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ = WMAX; but with angular momentum transport (LINSTB) and WMAX > 1, WMAX is read as $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ for the Sun, and $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = $ WMAX$/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ (MHP 3/09); the run stops if $\tau_\mathrm{cz} \le 1$.
  2. The loss law. With $R = \sqrt{L/(4\pi\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4)}$ and the fixed mass-loss rate $\dot M = 2\times10^{-14}\,M_\odot$/yr, $$I\,\frac{d\Omega}{dt} = -K\left(\frac{\dot M}{10^{-14}}\right)^{a}\left(\frac{R}{R_\odot}\right)^{c} M^{d}\;\Omega\,\min(\Omega, \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{\,b-1},$$ with $K$ = CONSTFACTOR, $a$ = EXMD, $b$ = EXW, $c$ = EXR, $d$ = EXM.
  3. Integration. If one explicit step would remove more than $\Omega$, the step is split into NSTEP substeps. In each, $\Omega_\mathrm{new}$ is found by iteration: the loss is evaluated at the mean of the start and the previous estimate, until the relative change is below $10^{-6}$ (at most 21 evaluations).
  4. Removal. $\Delta j = (\Omega_\mathrm{s} - \Omega_\mathrm{new})\,I_\mathrm{cz}/\Delta M_\mathrm{cz}$, with $\Delta M_\mathrm{cz}$ = HSTOP - HSBOT, is subtracted from every shell of the zone.

KC's changes. The unused argument SJTOT was removed (2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. WNEW overwrites a COMMON variable. WNEW, the iterated new rate, is also the first element of COMMON ROT, where it is the rate for converting a model to solid-body rotation (used by STARIN with LWNEW). Each call of WIND (and of MWIND, which does the same) changes it. A later run of the same job that sets up rotation from WNEW would get the last wind rate. A bug?
  2. A fixed mass-loss rate. DMDOT = 2.0D-14 is hard-coded, so the factor $(\dot M/10^{-14})^{a}$ is a constant $2^{a}$. Is this meant?
  3. No convergence check. After 21 evaluations without convergence, the last estimate is used without a message.
  4. The saturation's units. WMAX > 1 switches the meaning of WMAX (rad/s or rad/s × s). A rate above 1 rad/s is unphysical, so the test works, but a separate switch would be clearer.

The older wind braking law: $I\,d\Omega/dt \propto \Omega\,\min(\Omega,\Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{b-1}$ with fixed exponents, integrated over the step and taken from the surface convection zone's specific angular momentum.
