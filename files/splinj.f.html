Explanation

Purpose. SPLINJ computes the second derivatives of a natural cubic spline through $(x_i, y_i)$, $i = 1, \dots, N$ ($y''_1 = y''_N = 0$), for interpolation with SPLINT. The algorithm, with its equations, is that of splinc.f; only the arrays differ: here they are sized N (adjustable arrays), not 5000, and the routine has no SAVE.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: BOOLE and CALCAD, which then call SPLINT.
  • COMMON blocks: none.

Questions for the experts

  1. The work array U. U(N) is a local array with an adjustable size, which standard Fortran 77 does not allow (only dummy arguments may have adjustable bounds). It compiles as an automatic array under modern compilers. Is that the intended reliance on an extension?

2      SUBROUTINE SPLINJ(X,Y,Y2,N)
3      IMPLICIT REAL*8 (A-H,O-Z)
4      IMPLICIT LOGICAL*4(L)
5C     PARAMETER (JSON=5000)
6      REAL*8 X(N),Y(N),Y2(N),U(N)
7C     SAVE
8C NATURAL SPLINE
9      Y2(1) = 0.0D0
10      U(1) = 0.0D0
11      DO I = 2,N-1
12         SIG = (X(I)-X(I-1))/(X(I+1)-X(I-1))
13         P = SIG*Y2(I-1)+2.0D0
14         Y2(I) = (SIG-1.0D0)/P
15         U(I) = (6.0D0*((Y(I+1)-Y(I))/(X(I+1)-X(I))-(Y(I)-Y(I-1))
16     *          /(X(I)-X(I-1)))/(X(I+1)-X(I-1))-SIG*U(I-1))/P
17      END DO
18      QN = 0.0D0
19      UN = 0.0D0
20      Y2(N) = (UN-QN*U(N-1))/(QN*Y2(N-1)+1.0D0)
21      DO K = N-1,1,-1
22         Y2(K) = Y2(K)*Y2(K+1)+U(K)
23      END DO
24      RETURN
25      END
