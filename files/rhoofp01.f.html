Explanation

Purpose. Inverts the OPAL 2001 equation of state: the density for a given $X$, $T_6$ and pressure. It is the 2001 version of RHOOFP and follows it line by line (method and equations there); the 2006 version is RHOOFP06.

How it fits. Called by OEQOS01; calls ESAC01 for each trial density. COMMON: /AEOS/ (tables and grids from READCOEOS01), /BEOS/, /EEOS/ (EOS(1), the pressure), /LREADCO/, /LUOUT/. It returns the density, or $-999$.

Differences from RHOOFP. The grid sizes (191 temperatures, 169 densities) and its own DATA list NRA of the last density at each temperature.

KC's changes. The unused argument ZTAB was removed from the function and from its calls to ESAC01 (KC 2025-05-31); the members of /BEOS/ were reordered (KC 2025-05-30).

Questions for the experts

  1. Two edges that disagree. NRA here and KRA in READCOEOS01 (the edge for the ramp in EQBOUND01) are meant to describe the same table, but they differ: NRA has …,8*116,6*115,8*113,… and KRA has …,8*116,9*115,5*113,…. At three temperatures the two disagree on the last density (115 against 113). Which is right? One list, computed from NTA or ICYCUSE, would avoid this.
  2. The tolerance. $5\times10^{-8}$ here; RHOOFP06 was relaxed to $10^{-5}$ in 2025. Same change here?
