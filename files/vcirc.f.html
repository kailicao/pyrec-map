Explanation

Purpose. VCIRC estimates the velocities of the rotational mixing processes at each interface between shells of a radiative region: meridional (Eddington–Sweet) circulation, the Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke (GSF) instability, and the secular and dynamical shear instabilities. Its header comment: "skipping convective regions, check for instability against each secular mechanism; if unstable, compute a circulation velocity". The sum of the velocities, HV, sets the diffusion coefficient for angular momentum and mixing.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: arguments from SECULR: $\log r$ and $r$ (HR, HRU), the range IMIN–IMAX, the iteration IT, LCZ, OMEGA, the shear QWLNR (in SECULR, $\tfrac14(\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1})$ times a radial factor), its limit for dynamical shear QWRMAX (from SETUPV), the total luminosity CLTOT, the time step DT and $\log P$. Through COMMON blocks, mostly filled by SETUPV: the interpolation weights FACI (INTFAC), the velocity factors FES1–FES3, FGSFK (TAUKH), FACT1–FACT8 (VFACT), the shell-midpoint quantities (INTVAR: RM, HGM, ALM, …), the physics at each shell (MDPHY: $\mu$ AMUM, the viscosity VISCM, the thermal diffusivity THDIFM, …), the quadrupole moment and gravity (QUADRU: QUAD, GG), the $\theta$ terms (OLDROT2), the scale factors FES, FGSF, FSS, RCRIT (VMULT) and the switches IGSF (VMULT2), LNOJ (NOTRAN), LDIFAD (VARFC).
  • Who calls it: only SECULR, before and inside its iterations; SECULR may then call GETFC with HV.
  • Outputs: HV (total velocity per interface) and LDO (true if any is positive); in COMMON TEMP2, VES, VGSF, VSS and their previous values, read by CHECKJ, CODIFF, GETW, STITCH, WRTOUT and WRTMOD; with LDIFAD, the advective and diffusive parts VESA, VESD (DIFAD2, shared with CHECKJ and ROTGRID) and VESD2, VESD3, DSS, DGSF, FV0 (DIFAD3, shared with DADCOEFT and SETUPV); RAT (QUADD), read by CHECKJ, FPFT and WRTMOD; and FESTIME, FV0 rescaled in place.

The method. At the interface between shells $i-1$ and $i$:

  1. Midpoint values. The viscosity $\nu$ and thermal diffusivity $K$ by 4-point interpolation in their logarithms (weights FACI); $\mu$ and $\bar\Omega$ (WM) as the means of the two shells.
  2. Meridional circulation (Zahn 1991, by the comments): $v_\mathrm{ES} = \bar\Omega^2 (F_1 + \bar\Omega^2 F_2)$, multiplied by the quadrupole correction of Zahn (1992), $$R = \frac{\Phi_s + \Phi_2}{\Phi_\mathrm{std}},\quad \Phi_\mathrm{std} = \frac{4}{3}\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2 r}{\bar g},\quad \Phi_s = \frac13\,\bar\Omega^2\,\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{r^2}{g}\right),\quad \Phi_2 = -\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{Q}{g}\right),$$ with a warning printed if $R < 0$. Without LDIFAD, $v_\mathrm{ES} = |R\,v_\mathrm{ES} - F_3\,\bar\Omega\,d\Omega/dr|$. With LDIFAD (MHP 05/02, 06/02), it is split into an advective part, $v_A = \tfrac15(R\,v_\mathrm{ES} + \dot\theta)$ with $\dot\theta$ = THN, and diffusive parts of first to third order.
  3. Inhibition by $\mu$ gradients (Zahn & Maeder 1998): every velocity is multiplied by $$f_\mu = \frac{\max(\Delta\nabla, 10^{-3})}{\max(\Delta\nabla + \nabla_\mu, 10^{-3})},\qquad \nabla_\mu = \frac{\Delta\ln\mu}{\Delta\ln P},$$ with $\Delta\nabla$ = DDELM (in SETUPV, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla + \nabla_\mu$, at least a minimum).
  4. GSF (Kippenhahn 1980): if specific angular momentum decreases outward ($\Omega_i r_i^2 < \Omega_{i-1} r_{i-1}^2$), $$v_\mathrm{GSF} = F_K\,K\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2}{r}\,\max\!\left(2\left|\frac{d\ln j}{dr}\right|, \tfrac14\left|\frac{d\ln\Omega}{dr}\right|\right),$$ otherwise $v_\mathrm{GSF} = \tfrac14 F_K K\,\bar\Omega^2\,|d\ln\Omega/dr|/r$. With IGSF = 0 or 2 it is damped by viscosity: zero if $|q| < q_\nu = 2\sqrt{\nu/K}\,q_\mathrm{max}$, else multiplied by $\sqrt{(|q| - q_\nu)/q_\nu}$; with IGSF = 0 also by the $\mu$ gradient, likewise.
  5. Shear (Endal & Sofia, paper II; Zahn 1991). If $|q| > q_\mathrm{max}$ (dynamical shear), $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K (|q|/q_\mathrm{max})^2/r$, and $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ is rescaled to the gradient at the limit (a message is written to unit 6). Otherwise the secular shear is unstable if $$|q| > q_\mathrm{crit} = \sqrt{\frac{\nu}{K}\,\frac{R_\mathrm{crit}}{8}}\;q_\mathrm{max},$$ and then $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K\,\left[(|q| - q_\mathrm{crit})/q_\mathrm{max}\right]^2/r$, with $q_\mathrm{crit}$ raised by a $\mu$ gradient if that gives the larger limit.
  6. Scale and average. The velocities are multiplied by the user's factors FES, FGSF, FSS; with LDIFAD, the GSF and shear velocities times $r$ join the diffusive part. After the first iteration (IT > 1) each is averaged with the previous one. HV = $v_\mathrm{ES} + v_\mathrm{GSF} + v_\mathrm{SS}$ (zero below $10^{-20}$).
  7. No transport in the core (LNOJ, 9/93): from IMIN outward, the velocities are zeroed while $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, "to avoid numerical problems in the H-burning shell".

Design. Many older prescriptions are kept as comments (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974 circulation, James & Kahn 1971 GSF, older $\mu$ treatments); the flags IES and IMU no longer select anything.

KC's changes. The unused argument HJM was removed (2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. The $\mu$ loop does not skip convective interfaces. The meridional, GSF and shear loops skip interfaces inside convection zones; the Zahn & Maeder loop (step 3) does not, and rescales FESTIME and FV0 there too. Harmless, or should it skip them?
  2. Possible division by zero. In the dynamical-shear branch, $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ is rescaled by DLNWDR/DLNWDR0 or FX/FX2, where DLNWDR0 is zero if $\Omega_i = \Omega_{i-1}$; and $\ln\Omega$ requires $\Omega > 0$ everywhere.
  3. Sign of the meridional velocity. With LDIFAD, the circulation is signed, but VES is made positive by ABS(FES*VES) for every interface before HV is formed, so HV never sees a negative VES; only VESA (the advective part) keeps its sign. So HV takes $|v_\mathrm{ES}|$ of a signed circulation: intended?
  4. Unused values. V2 and WMID are computed but never used; VMU is zeroed and copied to VMUP (COMMON PREVMU, used by no other routine) but no longer computed, since the old $\mu$ treatments are commented out. Can they go?
  5. Output to unit 6. The dynamical-shear message goes to unit 6 (the screen) for every unstable interface, at every iteration.
  6. LNOJ's threshold. The core is defined as $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, hard-coded. Should it be a parameter?
  7. The $\mu$ factor compounds. The $\mu$ loop multiplies FESTIME and FV0 (COMMON, set once by SETUPV before SECULR's iterations) by $f_\mu$ at every call of VCIRC, so the factor compounds over SECULR's iterations ($f_\mu^{\,n}$ after $n$ calls). Should it be applied to a copy?
