Explanation

Purpose. INTRP2 returns the weights of 4-point Lagrange interpolation at a point $x$, without the derivative weights: the same as INTERP minus its C output.

How it fits. Called by the opacity-table routines ALEX06TAB, ALX8TH, ALXZTAB, GETOPAL95, OP95XTAB, OP95ZTAB and YALO3D, by the diffusion regridding EQUAL_TO_MODEL and MICRODIFF_ETM, and by PHYSIC. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

The method. $B_i(x) = \prod_{j\ne i}(x-a_j)/(a_i-a_j)$ for $i = 1..4$.

6      SUBROUTINE INTRP2(A,B,X)
7C INTERP WITHOUT DERIVATIVES
8      IMPLICIT REAL*8 (A-H,O-Z)
9      DIMENSION A(4),B(4)
10      SAVE
11C INTERP IS THE INTERPOLATION ROUTINE FOR THE VANDENBERG
12C OPACITY TABLES(CAPPA), AND IT USES A 4-POINT LAGRANGIAN
13C INTERPOLATION SCHEME.
14      A43 = A(4) - A(3)
15      A42 = A(4) - A(2)
16      A41 = A(4) - A(1)
17      A32 = A(3) - A(2)
18      A31 = A(3) - A(1)
19      A21 = A(2) - A(1)
20      D1 = -A21*A31*A41
21      D2 = A21*A32*A42
22      D3 = -A31*A32*A43
23      D4 = A41*A42*A43
24      XA1 = X - A(1)
25      XA2 = X - A(2)
26      XA3 = X - A(3)
27      XA4 = X - A(4)
28      B(1) = (XA2*XA3*XA4)/D1
29      B(2) = (XA1*XA3*XA4)/D2
30      B(3) = (XA1*XA2*XA4)/D3
31      B(4) = (XA1*XA2*XA3)/D4
32      RETURN
33      END
