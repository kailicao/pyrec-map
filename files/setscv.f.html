Explanation

Purpose. Builds the equation-of-state table for the envelope's mixture from the SCV (Saumon–Chabrier–Van Horn, by the common block's name SCVEOS, "MHP 5/97") tables of pure hydrogen, pure helium and metals, which SETUPS has read. It adds the entropy of mixing, and computes by finite differences the derivatives the structure equations need. (The header comment, "evaluate the EOS with the new tables and call eqstat … and compare", describes an earlier purpose; the routine calls neither.)

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: STARIN, once the starting model's envelope composition is known.
  • Inputs, through COMMON: /SCVEOS/ (the tables TABLEX (hydrogen), TABLEY (helium), TABLEZ (metals) on a grid of temperatures TLOGX(63) and up to 76 pressures NPTSX(I)), and /COMP/ (XENV, ZENV).
  • What it calls: INTER3, three-point Lagrange interpolation weights (FT) and their derivatives (FTD).
  • Outputs, through COMMON /SCVEOS/: column 12 of TABLEX and TABLEY, SMIX (the entropy of mixing), and TABLENV(63, 76, 12), the mixture's table. It is read by EQSCVE and EQSCVG, through EQSTAT, when LSCV is true.

The method. The table columns are used, in the code's notation, as $\log P$ (1), the ionization fractions (2, 3), $\log\rho$ (4), $\log S$ (5), $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ (7), $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$ (8) and $\partial\ln S/\partial\ln T$ (9); we read this from the formulas.

  1. $dU/dT$ of each pure table (column 12), from the two expressions for $c_P$ in the comments, $$c_P = S\left(\frac{\partial\ln S}{\partial\ln T}\right)_P = \frac{dU}{dT} + \frac{P}{\rho T}\,\frac{(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T)^2}{\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P}.$$
  2. Entropy of mixing. With $Y = 1 - X$ (XENV), the mixture's density $\rho_\mathrm{mix} = 1/((1-Y)/\rho_X + Y/\rho_Y)$, the number densities of hydrogen and helium particles and of their electrons from the ionization fractions, and $\beta = (m_H/m_{He})\,Y/(1-Y)$, $\gamma$ the ratio of particles per nucleus, the code computes $$S_\mathrm{mix} = \frac{k}{m_H}\,\frac{2(1-Y)}{1+x_2+3x_1}\left[\ln(1+\beta\gamma) - \frac{n_{e,H}}{n}\ln(1+\delta) + \beta\gamma\ln\Bigl(1+\frac{1}{\beta\gamma}\Bigr) - \frac{n_{e,He}}{n}\ln\Bigl(1+\frac1\delta\Bigr)\right],$$ with $n$ = TNUM the sum of the hydrogen and helium number densities (HNUM + HENUM), $n_{e,H}$, $n_{e,He}$ their electron number densities (HNUME, HENUME, each floored at 1), $\delta$ (DE) the ratio of electrons from helium to those from hydrogen, scaled by $\beta\gamma$ (the last term uses DEI, which is $1/\delta$ when both are positive); $x_1$, $x_2$ are TABLEX(I,J,2) and (I,J,3). Constants: $m_H$ = CMH, $m_{He}$ = CMHE (g), $k$ = CBOLTZ (erg K$^{-1}$). This is the code's formula; its source is not cited.
  3. The mixture at each $(T, P)$, with $X$, $Y = 1 - X - Z$, $Z$ from /COMP/: densities add as volumes, $$\frac1\rho = \frac{X}{\rho_X} + \frac{Y}{\rho_Y} + \frac{Z}{\rho_Z},\qquad \frac{\partial\ln\rho}{\partial\ln P} = \sum_k X_k\frac{\rho}{\rho_k}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\rho}{\partial\ln P}\right)_k,$$ and the same for $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$; the entropy is $S = X S_X + Y S_Y + S_\mathrm{mix}$, and $c_P$ follows, with the metals' term added. Stored: TABLENV(·,·,1..6) = $\log P$, $\log\rho$, $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$, $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$, $c_P$, $dU/dT$.
  4. Derivatives, by three-point Lagrange differentiation in $\ln T$ and $\ln P$: of $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ in $\ln P$ and $\ln T$, of $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln\rho$ in $\ln\rho$, of $\ln c_P$ in $\ln T$, and of $dU/dT$ in $\ln P$ and $\ln T$; stored in columns 7–12.

KC's changes. The unused array ATOMWT and its DATA statement were removed (KC 2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. A fix that is undone. In the derivatives of $dU/dT$ in temperature, the loop sets QR(K) = TABLENV(ii,jj,6) with jj = min(NPTSX(ii),j) (protection against neighbor rows with fewer pressures), and the next line overwrites it with QR(K) = TABLENV(IDT+K-1,J,6). The second line should probably be removed.
  2. The same protection is missing elsewhere. QDTT (from TABLENV(IDT..IDT+2, J, 3)) and QSMIXT (from SMIX(IDT..IDT+2, J)) also read neighbor temperature rows at index J, which may exceed their NPTSX. Use min(NPTSX(ii),j) there too?
  3. Radiation pressure computed, then not used. PRAD $= a T^4/3$ is added to P and PL, and the comments describe the corrections for radiation, but TABLENV(I,J,1) stores the gas pressure from TABLEX, and PL, QPT0 are never used. Where does the code add radiation for the SCV tables (perhaps in EQSCVE)?
  4. $Y$ in the entropy of mixing ignores $Z$. In step 2, $Y = 1 - $ XENV, while step 3 uses $Y = 1 - X - Z$. Intended (mixing of hydrogen and helium only)?
  5. Comment versus code. The list before the derivative loop names "D LN DEL(AD)/D LN P" and "…/D LN T", but the code computes derivatives of $dU/dT$ (QAP, QAT). The comment "READ IN EQUATION OF STATE TABLES FOR HYDROGEN AND HELIUM" also stands over a loop that reads nothing (the reading is in SETUPS). And QCPP ($\partial\ln c_P/\partial\ln P$, in the comment's list) is computed but not stored; is it needed downstream?
