Explanation

Purpose. KSPLINE computes the second derivatives of a natural cubic spline through exactly 4 points (the size NM = 4 is fixed), as in Numerical Recipes' SPLINE. KSPLINT evaluates the spline.

How it fits. Called by the surface-pressure routines KCSURFP and SURFP, the SCV equation of state EQSCVE, and TAUINTNEW. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

The method. The tridiagonal system for the second derivatives $y''_i$ of a natural spline ($y''_1 = y''_4 = 0$), solved by forward elimination and back-substitution.

2      SUBROUTINE KSPLINE(X,Y,Y2)
3      PARAMETER (NM=4)
4      REAL*8 X(NM),Y(NM),Y2(NM),U(NM),SIG,QN,UN,P
5      SAVE
6      N = NM
7C NATURAL SPLINE
8      Y2(1) = 0.0D0
9      U(1) = 0.0D0
10      DO I = 2,N-1
11         SIG = (X(I)-X(I-1))/(X(I+1)-X(I-1))
12         P = SIG*Y2(I-1)+2.0D0
13         Y2(I) = (SIG-1.0D0)/P
14         U(I) = (6.0D0*((Y(I+1)-Y(I))/(X(I+1)-X(I))-(Y(I)-Y(I-1))
15     *          /(X(I)-X(I-1)))/(X(I+1)-X(I-1))-SIG*U(I-1))/P
16      END DO
17      QN = 0.0D0
18      UN = 0.0D0
19      Y2(N) = (UN-QN*U(N-1))/(QN*Y2(N-1)+1.0D0)
20      DO K = N-1,1,-1
21         Y2(K) = Y2(K)*Y2(K+1)+U(K)
22      END DO
23      RETURN
24      END
