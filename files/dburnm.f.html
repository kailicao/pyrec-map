Explanation

Purpose. The same deuterium burning as DBURN, for the substeps of a model in the middle of a time step: it is called by MIDMOD with rates interpolated to the substep and with the accreted mass scaled to the substep's share ("unlike the routine called by MIX, the timestep here is only a portion of the total model DT"). Its header still says "DBURN".

How it fits.

  • Inputs: as DBURN, plus DRATEM, DRATEM0 (the rates at the end and start of the substep, interpolated in MIDMOD from DRATE0, DRATE with the fraction FAC2) and FAC2 itself. DT is in seconds (MIDMOD's FAC2 = DT/DELTS), converted to Gyr with DTT = DT*1.0D-9/CSECYR.
  • Who calls it: MIDMOD.
  • Output: HCOMP (H, $^3$He, D) over the shell or zone.

The method. As in DBURN: mass-weighted means over the zone, substeps with $X_D \leftarrow X_D e^{-2r_k\Delta t/n}$, then $\Delta X = \tfrac12\Delta X_D$ and $\Delta X_3 = -\tfrac32\Delta X_D$. The differences from DBURN:

  • the threshold for zeroing deuterium is $10^{-14}$ (DBURN: $10^{-11}$) and does not include the accreted D;
  • with accretion, the substep's share of the accreted matter is $f_a$ = FAC2*FMASSACC, and the new mean is $$X_D = \frac{X_{D,\mathrm{old}} + f_a X_{D,\mathrm{acc}}}{1 + f_a},$$ written as a weighting by fractions. But FMASSACC is the accreted mass in grams (MDOT sets it to DELM), so $f_a$ is in grams too (question 2).

Questions for the experts

  1. Two copies. DBURN and DBURNM share almost all their code but differ in the time unit of DT, the threshold ($10^{-11}$ against $10^{-14}$) and the accretion weighting; neither tests DMDT0 > 0 in its accretion branch. Which behavior is right, and can the two become one routine in the overhaul? See also the questions on DBURN.
  2. The accretion weighting. FMASSA = FAC2*FMASSACC is a mass in grams, yet the means divide by 1 + FMASSA, as if it were a fraction of the zone's mass. The accreted matter then gets nearly all the weight, so $X_D$, $X$ and $^3$He of the surface zone jump towards the accreted values. DBURN weights by the zone's mass SUMM (grams) instead. This looks like a bug: should it be (XH2NO*SUMM + XH2NA*FMASSA)/(SUMM + FMASSA)?

Review (round 1)
  1. Wrong. "the accreted fraction is $f_a$ = FAC2*FMASSACC … a weighting by fractions": FMASSACC is the accreted mass in grams (MDOT sets it to DELM), so $f_a$ is a mass in grams, and $(X_{D,\mathrm{old}} + f_aX_{D,\mathrm{acc}})/(1+f_a)$ gives the accreted matter nearly all the weight; DBURN weights by the zone's mass SUMM instead. This looks like a bug. Fix: correct the method and make it a question of its own.
  2. Settled. "DTT = DT*1.0D-9/CSECYR: from seconds to Gyr, probably": MIDMOD's DT is in seconds (FAC2 = DT/DELTS). Fix: drop "probably".
  3. Missing. As in DBURN, the accretion branch does not test DMDT0 > 0. Fix: mention in question 1 (two copies).

Revised: the accretion weighting corrected in the method and made question 2 (an apparent bug); DT in seconds stated; the missing DMDT0 test added to question 1.
