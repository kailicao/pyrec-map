Explanation

Purpose. YLLO3D2 is yllo3d.f for the second OPAL92 table at a different $Z$ ("DBG 5/94 opacity at different Z"): the same method and equations, with the COMMON blocks GLLOT2, LLOT2, LLOT42 and KIPMLL2 (used by no other routine), calling YLLO2D2.

How it fits. Called by GETOPAC after YLLO3D when L2Z is true (with LOPAL92): GETOPAC then interpolates $\log\kappa$ and both derivatives linearly in $Z$ between the two tables (ZOPAL1, ZOPAL2); calls FINDEX and YLLO2D2.

Questions for the experts

  1. No initial indices. Unlike YLLO3D, the COMMON KIPMLL2 has no DATA; The indices M12, M22, M32 are starting guesses for FINDEX (M32 through YLLO2D2), so they are read before they are first set. FINDEX resets a guess outside the grid to 1, so this is harmless, but the two routines should agree.
