Explanation

Purpose. Reads the Los Alamos opacity tables of 1989 (LAOL89) for the stellar interior, and, when two metallicities are needed, a second LAOL89 table.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: SETUPOPAC, when LLAOL89 is true, right before SULAOL, which sets up the interpolation in what is read here.
  • Inputs: the formatted table files FLAOL and, when L2Z is true, FLAOL2 (names passed from the namelist through SETUPS), on units IOLAOL and IOLAOL2.
  • Outputs, through COMMON:
  • /NWLAOL/: the first table, OLAOL(X, rho, T) with its grids OXA (hydrogen mass fractions), ORHO, OT and sizes NUMOFXYZ, NUMRHO, NUMT. Read by SULAOL, GTLAOL, GTLAOL2, GETOPAC and others.
  • /NWLAOL2/: the second table (OLAOL2, OXA2, ORHO2, OT2), read by SULAOL and GTLAOL2.
  • /DEBHU/: the relative abundances ZDH(18) of 18 metals, for the Debye–Hückel correction in EQRELV; the first three (C, N, O) are later replaced by the model's own abundances (question 3).
  • the argument V(12): the relative abundances of the Cox–Stewart metal mix (C, N, O, Ne, Na, Mg, Si, Ar, Fe), which SETUPOPAC passes back to SETUPS.
  • Other COMMON: /NEWOPAC/ (the opacity switches, here L2Z), /ZRAMP/ (the unit IOLAOL2), /LUOUT/ (the unit ISHORT for messages).

The method. A formatted read, in this order: the array sizes; ZLOT and the Cox–Stewart mix; ZHIT and the 18 Debye–Hückel abundances; the grids in $X$, $\rho$ and $T$; then the opacities, one record of NUMT values per $(X,\rho)$ pair. The second table is read the same way.

Design. The fixed sizes are 12 × 104 × 52. The table's own metallicities (ZLOT, ZHIT) are read into local variables and not kept, and the second table's mix (V2, ZDH2, ZLOT2, ZHIT2) is read and discarded.

Questions for the experts

  1. The size check. OLAOL and OXA have 12 entries in $X$, but the check stops when NUMOFXYZ exceeds 11. Is 11 intended?
  2. Unused metallicities. ZLOT and ZHIT (and ZLOT2, ZHIT2) are read and thrown away. Should the code check them against the metallicities the run expects (ZLAOL1, ZLAOL2 in /NEWOPAC/)?
  3. ZDH: partly the table's, partly the model's. Of the 18 abundances in ZDH read here (scaled to sum to ZHIT, by the comment), only the first three (C, N, O) are later replaced by the model's mass fractions, in COEFFT, HSUBP, CRRECT, STARIN and others; the other 15 keep the table's values and enter the Debye–Hückel sum in EQRELV. They are not rescaled to the model's $Z$. Is this mix of the model's C, N, O and the table's heavier metals intended?
  4. "WEIGTH". A typo in the comments ("BY WEIGTH"); harmless.

Review (round 1)
  • Wrong question. Question 3 says ZDH is overwritten later in COEFFT and HSUBP and asks whether the table's mix is used. Only ZDH(1..3) (C, N, O) are overwritten (also in CRRECT, GETNEWENV, PHYSIC, SCONVEC, STARIN, WRTOUT, WRTMOD); ZDH(4..18) keep the table's values and are used by EQRELV. Fix: rewrite the question (the mixed source of ZDH, not rescaled to the model's Z) and say in Outputs who uses ZDH.

Revised: applied.
