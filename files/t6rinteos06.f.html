Explanation

Purpose. T6RINTEOS06 interpolates one equation-of-state quantity in temperature T6 and density for the OPAL 2006 EOS (its comment's words). It is t6rinteos01.f line for line (method and equations in t6rinterp.f), with NT = 197 temperatures and COMMON blocks ending in 06.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only ESAC06.
  • What it calls: QUADEOS06.
  • Output: ESACT in COMMON EEOS06, shared with ESAC06, OEQOS06, RADSUB06, READCOEOS06, RHOOFP06 and CALCAD.

Questions for the experts

  1. RECURSIVE. The routine is declared recursive, but does not call itself and has SAVE; T6RINTEOS01 is not recursive. Why?
  2. One routine for both releases. The 2001 and 2006 versions differ only in NT and COMMON names; could one routine with the table as an argument replace them?
