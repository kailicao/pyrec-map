Explanation

Purpose. This file gives the change of the surface convection zone's angular velocity over a time step due to magnetized-wind angular-momentum loss, with the older "Kawaler-type" wind law and a saturation threshold. It is used when the newer Matt et al. law (LMWIND) is off.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments BL ($\log L/L_\odot$), TEFFL, SMASS ($M/M_\odot$), DT (the time step), HICZ (the moment of inertia of the surface convection zone), OMEGAS (the surface $\Omega$ after the previous iteration), WOLD (at the start of the step), IT (the iteration); WMAX, EXMD, EXW, EXR, EXM, CONSTFACTOR in /CWIND/ (set in PARMIN from the input index "ALFA", per the comment); TAUCZ (the convective overturn time) in /OVRTRN/.
  • Who calls it: MCOWIND, when LMWIND is false ("run old windlaw").
  • Outputs: WIND1, WIND2, the change of $\Omega$ over the step computed with $\Omega$ at its start and at its end. The comment says they enter the diffusion equations and are averaged: SECULR, which calls MCOWIND, passes them to DCOEFT or DADCOEFT, where the surface boundary takes $-\tfrac12(\mathtt{WIND1}+\mathtt{WIND2})$ times the moment of inertia.

The method.

  1. Saturation. If WMAX $> 1$, it is read as $\Omega_\mathrm{sat,\odot}\,\tau_\odot$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = \mathtt{WMAX}/\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ (MHP 3/09; the run stops if $\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ is not defined); otherwise $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = \mathtt{WMAX}$.
  2. The radius from $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$: $\log R = \tfrac12(\log(L/L_\odot) + \log L_\odot - \log 4\pi - \log\sigma - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff})$, in cm.

  3. The comment's law, $dJ/dt \propto K\,(\dot M/10^{-14})^{a}\,\Omega^{n}\,(M/M_\odot)^{c}(R/R_\odot)^{b}$, gives $$C = \frac{\Delta t}{I_\mathrm{CZ}}\,K\left(\frac{\dot M}{10^{-14}\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}}\right)^{a}\left(\frac{R}{R_\odot}\right)^{b}\left(\frac{M}{M_\odot}\right)^{c},$$ $$\Delta\Omega = C\,\min(\Omega, \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{\,n-1}\,\Omega,$$ with $K$ = CONSTFACTOR, $a$ = EXMD, $b$ = EXR, $c$ = EXM, $n$ = EXW. So $\Delta\Omega \propto \Omega^n$ below saturation and $\propto \Omega\,\Omega_\mathrm{sat}^{n-1}$ above.

  4. WIND1 uses WOLD. WIND2 uses OMEGAS; from the second iteration on, it is the mean of its previous value and the new one, which damps the iteration.

Design. The mass-loss rate is fixed: DMDOT = 2.0D-14 ($M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$), so the factor $(\dot M/10^{-14})^a$ is the constant $2^a$.

Questions for the experts

  1. A fixed mass-loss rate. $\dot M$ is hard-coded to $2\times10^{-14}\,M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$, not taken from the model's mass loss (/MASSCHG/). Intended as a calibration constant?
  2. The damping of WIND2. Averaging the previous WIND2 with the new value at each iteration means the converged WIND2 mixes values from several iterations. Is this only for stability, and does it converge to the right value?
  3. Unused inputs. /DEUTER/ is included (with the comment "added option to scale the saturation rate by the overturn timescale", which in fact uses /OVRTRN/), and EXTAU, EXL, EXPR, EXCEN, C_2 of /CWIND/ are not used here. Can /DEUTER/ go?
  4. The test WMAX > 1. A real $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ is far below 1 rad s$^{-1}$, so the test works as a switch; is that documented for users?
