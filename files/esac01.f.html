Explanation

Purpose. ESAC01 is ESAC for the OPAL 2001 equation-of-state tables: it interpolates them in $X$, $T_6$ and $\rho$ and returns the pressure, energy, entropy, specific heat and adiabatic exponents. The file carries long comments describing the table's layout.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: OEQOS01 (called from EQSTAT2 when LOPALE01 is set) and RHOOFP01.
  • Inputs, outputs, calls: as ESAC, with the 2001 routines READCOEOS01, QUADEOS01, T6RINTEOS01, GMASS01 and RADSUB01. The output is in /EEOS/, with nine quantities (the 2001 table has no $\partial E/\partial\rho$): EOS(1) $P$, (2) $E$, (3) $S$, (4) $c_V$, (5) $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln\rho$, (6) $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln T$, (7) $\Gamma_1$, (8) $\Gamma_2/(\Gamma_2-1)$, (9) $\Gamma_3-1$.
  • COMMON blocks: the 2001 counterparts of ESAC's blocks: /AEOS/ (the tables), shared with READCOEOS01, T6RINTEOS01, RHOOFP01 and EQBOUND01; /AAEOS/ and /EEEOS/ (workspace), with READCOEOS01 and T6RINTEOS01; /BEOS/, with READCOEOS01, RADSUB01 and RHOOFP01; /BBEOS/ (the indices), with T6RINTEOS01 only; /EEOS/ (the output), with OEQOS01, RADSUB01, READCOEOS01, RHOOFP01 and T6RINTEOS01. /LREADCO/ (ITIME) is shared with ESAC and ESAC06.

The table, per the comments. Five $X$ values (0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8), 169 densities from $10^{-14}$ to $10^{7}$ g cm$^{-3}$, and up to 191 temperatures from $T_6 = 100$ down to 0.002. About 10% of the table, at high density and low temperature, is empty; NTA(l) gives, for each density, the index of the lowest temperature present (set by a DATA statement here).

The method. As ESAC (see esac.f), with two guards added: the stencil is shrunk to 3 points (IPU, IQU) when it would pass the last temperature or density, and the empty-region sums loop over MF..MF2 only. Without radiation, $c_V$ (here EOS(4)) is scaled by $nR/m$ with $R = 83.14511$.

KC's changes. The unused argument ZTAB was removed (2025-05-31), and the elements of /BEOS/ were reordered (2025-05-30).

Questions for the experts

  1. Two values of $R$. APROP is 83.14511 here and 83.1446304 in ESAC. Which is meant (the second is the current CODATA value)?
  2. The format with no values. write (ISHORT,'(A, " ihi,ilo,imd",3i5)') ID prints only ID; ESAC06 adds the three indices.
  3. The questions on ESAC about the shared ITIME flag, the ESK/ESK2 slip (line 437) and the swapped IP,IQ in message 66 apply here too; IORDER above the limit again only writes a message. The table-end and $X < 10^{-6}$ problems of ESAC are fixed here.
