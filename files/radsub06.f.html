Explanation

Purpose. Adds radiation, optionally, to the gas equation of state interpolated in the OPAL 2006 tables. The older versions are RADSUB and RADSUB01.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: ESAC06, only when its argument irad is 1, and it passes irad on.
  • COMMON: /EEOS06/ (ESACT, EOS(MV); shared with ESAC06, OEQOS06, READCOEOS06, RHOOFP06, T6RINTEOS06 and CALCAD) and /BEOS06/ (ZZ, IRI, INDEX, NTA, NRA; shared with ESAC06, READCOEOS06, RHOOFP06 and EQBOUND06).
  • Output: EOS(IRI(1..9)) overwritten.

The method. As in RADSUB01 (equations in RADSUB), with the 2006 order of quantities: IRI(4) is $\partial E/\partial\rho$, IRI(5) is $c_V$, IRI(6) is $\chi_\rho$, IRI(7) is $\chi_T$, IRI(8..10) the $\Gamma$s. With irad $\ne 0$, the radiation terms are added, $\partial E/\partial\rho$ becomes $\partial E_g/\partial\rho - E_r/\rho$, and the differences (with minus without radiation) are added to $\Gamma_1$ and to GAM2PT. With irad $= 0$, the routine only rewrites the gas values, with $c_V$ multiplied by $Nk/M$ (molenak/tmass).

KC's changes. The members of /BEOS06/ were reordered so that ZZ comes first (KC 2025-05-30); unused constants were removed from the declarations and DATA statement (KC 2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. $\Gamma_3$ not corrected. The line that would add the radiation's difference to eos(iri(10)) ($\Gamma_3$) is commented out, while RADSUB01 corrects all three $\Gamma$s. Is this deliberate?
  2. The irad = 0 branch. ESAC06 calls this routine only when irad is 1, so the branch for irad = 0 never runs. Is it kept for another caller, or can it be removed?
