Explanation

Purpose. ESAC06 is ESAC for the OPAL 2006 equation-of-state tables: it interpolates them in $X$, $T_6$ and $\rho$ and returns ten thermodynamic quantities. It is a copy of ESAC01 adapted to the larger 2006 table.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: OEQOS06 (called from EQSTAT2 when LOPALE06 is set), RHOOFP06, and CALCAD, which calls it directly for every point of the model and envelope (with IORDER = 9, IRAD = 1) when LOPALE06 is set; its call for the atmosphere points is commented out.
  • What it calls: READCOEOS06, QUADEOS06, T6RINTEOS06, GMASS06 and RADSUB06.
  • Outputs: in /EEOS06/: EOS(1) $P$, (2) $E$, (3) $S$, (4) $\partial E/\partial\rho|_T$, (5) $c_V$, (6) $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln\rho$, (7) $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln T$, (8) $\Gamma_1$, (9) $\Gamma_2/(\Gamma_2-1)$, (10) $\Gamma_3-1$.
  • COMMON blocks: /AEOS06/ (the tables), shared with READCOEOS06, T6RINTEOS06, RHOOFP06 and EQBOUND06; /AAEOS06/ and /EEEOS06/ (workspace), with READCOEOS06 and T6RINTEOS06; /BEOS06/, with READCOEOS06, RADSUB06, RHOOFP06 and EQBOUND06, which reads NTA and NRA from it; /BBEOS06/ (the indices), with T6RINTEOS06 and EQBOUND06, which starts its search from K3, L2; /EEOS06/ (the output), with OEQOS06, RADSUB06, READCOEOS06, RHOOFP06, T6RINTEOS06 and CALCAD. /LREADCO/ (ITIME) is shared with ESAC and ESAC01.

The table. 169 densities and 197 temperatures. Two DATA statements describe its irregular edge: NTA(l), the lowest temperature index for each density, and NRA(k), the highest density index for each temperature.

The method. As ESAC01 (see esac.f for the steps). Differences: RADSUB06 takes IRAD as an argument, the error messages print their indices, and without radiation $c_V$ is EOS(5).

KC's changes. The unused argument ZTAB was removed (2025-05-31), and the elements of /BEOS06/ were reordered (2025-05-30).

Questions for the experts

  1. An argument that is always 1. The code reads if (irad .eq. 1) then call radsub06(irad,…) else eos(iri(5)) = …. So RADSUB06 is called only when IRAD = 1, and its IRAD argument is always 1. Is the argument needed?
  2. Edge tables in two places. NTA and NRA are hard-coded here; if the table file changes, they must change too. Could READCOEOS06 derive them from the file?
  3. The questions on ESAC about the shared ITIME flag, the ESK/ESK2 slip and the swapped IP,IQ in message 66 apply here too. Also, the check copied from ESAC01 writes "iorder cannot exceed 9", though this table has ten quantities; the routine continues anyway.
