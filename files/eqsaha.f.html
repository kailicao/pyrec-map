Explanation

Purpose. EQSAHA is the Yale equation of state for partially ionized gas: it solves the Saha equations for hydrogen, helium (two stages) and ten metals, and returns the density, the ionization fractions and the thermodynamic derivatives. The name "Prather EOS" in EQSTAT2's comments refers to it.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only EQSTAT2, below the temperature TSCUT. It is called even when the SCV tables are used, since "this EOS replaces the call to EQSAHA, except for derivative purposes"; EQSCVE then overwrites what it can.
  • Inputs: FX, the number fractions of the twelve species (set by EQSTAT2 from FXENV in /COMP/); TL, PL; BETA and its relatives; AMU; LATMO (skip the derivatives, for atmospheres); LDERIV (also compute second derivatives).
  • Outputs: D, DL, EMU, RMU = $R(\mu_\mathrm{ion}^{-1}+\mu_e^{-1})$, FXION (H$^+$, He$^+$, He$^{++}$ fractions), QDT, QDP, QCP, DELA and, with LDERIV, their derivatives. KSAHA is the caller's iteration counter, to which this call adds its iterations.
  • COMMON blocks: only constants (/CONST1/, /CONST2/, with CMKH the constant term of the Saha equation) and /LUOUT/.

The method.

  1. Saha constants. For each species $i$, with ionization temperature $\chi_i/k$ (SAHAT, in K: 59 630 for Na up to 250 200 for Ne; SAHET0 = 285 270 and SAHET1 = 631 370 for He and He$^+$) and a constant $u_i$ (SAHAU), $$K_i = \frac{1}{\beta}\exp\!\left[\ln 10\,(u_i + 2.5\log T - \log P + C) - \frac{\chi_i}{kT}\right].$$ The species order is that of ATOMW2 in EQSTAT2: Na, Al, Mg, Fe, Si, C, H, O, N, Ar, Ne, He. The list is in order of increasing ionization temperature. The loop stops at the first species whose exponent is below $-40$: that species and all later ones are treated as neutral (so hydrogen, seventh, counts as ionized only if NZ0 ≥ 7, and FXION(1) is 0 otherwise). A species whose exponent is above $+40$ gets $K_i = 10^{16}$ (fully ionized) and is left out of the derivative sums.

  2. Electron number. With $E$ the electrons per atom (E) and $\epsilon = E/(1+E)$, the ionized fraction of species $i$ (the ten metals and hydrogen) is $x_i = K_i/(K_i + \epsilon)$, and charge balance reads $$E = \sum_i f_i x_i + f_\mathrm{He}\,(x_1 + 2x_2),$$ with the helium fractions $x_1, x_2$ from their own Saha equations. The routine solves this coupled system by Newton iteration (at most 20 steps, tolerance $10^{-9}$), reducing the 3×3 Jacobian by hand; E and the helium fractions keep their values between calls as the next starting guess.

  3. Density: $\mu_e^{-1} = E\,\mu_\mathrm{ion}^{-1}$ and $\rho = \beta P / [R(\mu_\mathrm{ion}^{-1} + \mu_e^{-1})T]$.

  4. Derivatives. Differentiating the Saha system gives $\partial\ln E/\partial\ln T$ and $\partial\ln E/\partial\ln P$, then $Q_{\rho T}$, $Q_{\rho P}$, the energy derivative $\partial U/\partial T$ (including ionization energy), $c_P = \partial U/\partial T - (P/\rho T)Q_{\rho T}$ and $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} = -(P/\rho T)Q_{\rho T}/c_P$.

KC's changes. Three DO loops that ended on an assignment were rewritten to end on CONTINUE (2025-05-30); the arithmetic is unchanged.

Questions for the experts

  1. No-convergence path. After 20 iterations the routine writes "SAHA FAILURE" and continues with the unconverged $E$. Is that acceptable, or should the caller be told?
  2. State between calls. E, SAHEX1 and SAHEX2 persist between calls (SAVE and DATA). The result can then depend slightly on the order of calls (for example during the numerical derivatives in EQSTAT). Is that a concern at the tolerance used?
  3. The constants $u_i$. SAHAU has no comment; they are probably the logarithms of the partition-function ratios. Could their source be cited?

Review (round 1)
  1. Wrong. "Species with $K_i$ below $e^{-40}$ are treated as neutral; above $e^{40}$, as fully ionized." The test is on the exponent, and the loop stops at the first species below the limit (GO TO 12): that species and all later ones in the list are treated as neutral (the list is in order of increasing SAHAT). A species above the limit gets $K_i = 10^{16}$ and is left out of the derivative sums (NZ1). Fix: state both.
  2. Unclear. "the ionized fraction of a metal is $x_i = K_i/(K_i+\epsilon)$": the same holds for hydrogen, which is one of the eleven species in the loop. Fix: "of species $i$ (the ten metals and hydrogen)".
  3. Wrong premise (question 4). "FXION(1) … is 0 even if hydrogen is slightly ionized." Since the loop stops at the first species below $e^{-40}$, and the species before hydrogen have lower ionization temperatures, NZ0 < 7 means hydrogen's own $K$ is below the limit too (up to the small differences in SAHAU). Fix: drop the question and explain in step 1.

  4. Missing. KSAHA is not reset here; it adds this call's iterations to the caller's counter (callers such as CRRECT, GETNEWENV, GETTAU, HSUBP set it to 0). Fix: reword the output line.

  5. Missing (a clear bug, not yet asked). For $X < 10^{-6}$, ESAC sets MH=1 twice (lines 134–135) where ESAC01 and ESAC06 set MF=1 and MH=1. So MF keeps the value from the bisection, IHI-2 = 0, and the code reads XZ(0,…), EPL(0,…), ZZ(0) and copies ESK = EPL(MF,…) from outside the arrays. Fix: add a question.
