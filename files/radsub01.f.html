Explanation

Purpose. Adds radiation to the gas equation of state interpolated in the OPAL 2001 tables. The version for the older tables is RADSUB, and for the 2006 tables RADSUB06.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: ESAC01, after the table interpolation, and only when its argument irad is 1. Otherwise ESAC01 itself multiplies $c_V$ (eos(iri(4))) by moles*aprop/tmass.
  • COMMON: /EEOS/ (ESACT, EOS(MV); shared with ESAC01, OEQOS01, READCOEOS01, RHOOFP01, T6RINTEOS01) and /BEOS/ (ZZ, the index map IRI, INDEX, NTA; shared with ESAC01, READCOEOS01, RHOOFP01).
  • Output: EOS(IRI(1..9)) overwritten with the totals.

The method. The radiation terms $P_r$, $E_r$, $S_r$ and the totals $P$, $E$, $S$, $\chi_\rho$, $\chi_T$, $c_V$ are those of RADSUB (equations there). Note that the 2001 tables order the quantities differently: here EOS(IRI(4)) is $c_V$, IRI(5) is $\chi_\rho$, IRI(6) is $\chi_T$, and IRI(7..9) are the three $\Gamma$s. The difference is in the $\Gamma$s: the code computes them twice from the same formulas, once without radiation and once with it, and adds only the difference to the tabulated values:

$$\Gamma_1 \leftarrow \Gamma_{1,\mathrm{tab}} + \bigl[\Gamma_1^{\mathrm{rad}} - \Gamma_1^{\mathrm{no\,rad}}\bigr],$$

and the same for GAM2PT and GAM3PT. The comment explains why: the computed $c_V$ "is not accurate enough" to give accurate $\Gamma$s directly (eq. 16.16 of Landau and Lifshitz). There is no UNITF/UNITFOLD rescaling here, unlike RADSUB.

KC's changes. The members of /BEOS/ were reordered so that ZZ comes first (KC 2025-05-30); unused constants were removed from the declarations and DATA statement (KC 2025-05-31).

Questions for the experts

  1. Different constants from RADSUB. APROP is 83.14510 here and 83.1446304 in RADSUB, and the UNITF/UNITFOLD rescaling of RADSUB is absent. Are the 2001 tables already in the new units?
  2. Single-precision constants. The DATA statement sets sigmacc and aprop with single-precision literals (1.8914785e-3, 83.14510, no D exponent), although both are REAL*8 by the IMPLICIT statement, so they keep only about seven significant digits. RADSUB and RADSUB06 write them with D. Should these be D0 constants?
