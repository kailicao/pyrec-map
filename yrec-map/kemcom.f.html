Explanation

Purpose. KEMCOM solves the nuclear burning network for one shell, or one convection zone, over a time step: it finds the new mass fractions of H, ³He, ⁴He, ¹²C, ¹³C, ¹⁴N and ¹⁶O simultaneously and implicitly, by Newton iteration, from the reaction rates computed in ENGEB (its comment: "Don VandenBerg's notes explain what the SR is doing"). ¹⁸O is updated explicitly afterwards.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: MIX and ROTMIX, for each radiative shell (IBEGIN = IEND) and each convection zone (IBEGIN < IEND).
  • Inputs: HT ($\log T$), the rates HR1–HR8, HR10–HR12 and the branching ratio HF1 (per Gyr per amu, without the composition factors, as its comment says), HS2 (shell masses), DDAGE (the step in years); the abundances at the start of the step HCOMPP from the module OLDMOD_MOD.
  • Output: the new HCOMP (elements 1–7, 9 and 11; element 3, $Z$, is either reset or set to its zone mean, see step 4) for shells IBEGIN..IEND.
  • Calls: SIMEQC, the linear solver for the 7×8 Newton system.
  • Modules (COMMON blocks until rp_1): NMLPAR_MOD for the settings CMIN, ABSTOL, RELTOL, KEMMAX (formerly /BURTOL/, set by PARMIN) and TCUT(1), the lowest burning temperature (formerly /CTLIM/); OLDMOD_MOD (the previous model: HCOMPP; written by HPOINT and others); IOUNITS_MOD (the output unit).

The method.

  1. Mixing. For a convection zone, the starting abundances are the mass-weighted means $\bar x_j = \sum_i x_{j,i}\,\Delta m_i / \sum_i \Delta m_i$ over the zone (elements 1–11), and so are the rates. If the zone's (or shell's) first point is below TCUT(1), it only homogenizes and returns.

  2. Equations. With $\Delta t$ in Gyr and $A_k$ the species' mass numbers (1, 3, 4, 12, 13, 14, 16), the residuals are $$G_k(w) = w_k - w_k^0 - A_k\,\Delta t\,R_k(w),$$ where $w = (X, {}^3\mathrm{He}, Y, {}^{12}\mathrm{C}, {}^{13}\mathrm{C}, {}^{14}\mathrm{N}, {}^{16}\mathrm{O})$ are the end-of-step abundances (so the scheme is implicit) and, with $r_n$ the rate GRn and $f_3$ = HF1, $f_4 = 1-f_3$ (the branching of ¹⁵N + p to ¹²C + α or to ¹⁶O): $$R_X = -3r_1X^2 + 2r_2\,{}^3\mathrm{He}^2 - r_3\,{}^3\mathrm{He}\,Y - r_4X\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} - r_5X\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C} - 2X\,(r_6\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N} + r_7\,{}^{16}\mathrm{O}),$$ $$R_{^3\mathrm{He}} = r_1X^2 - 2r_2\,{}^3\mathrm{He}^2 - r_3\,{}^3\mathrm{He}\,Y,$$ $$R_Y = r_2\,{}^3\mathrm{He}^2 + r_3\,{}^3\mathrm{He}\,Y + f_3r_6X\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N} + r_7X\,{}^{16}\mathrm{O} - r_{10}Y\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} - r_8Y\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C} - r_{11}Y\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N} - 3r_{12}Y^3,$$ $$R_{^{12}\mathrm{C}} = r_{12}Y^3 - r_4X\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} - r_{10}Y\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} + f_3r_6X\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N},\qquad R_{^{13}\mathrm{C}} = r_4X\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} - r_5X\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C} - r_8Y\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C},$$ $$R_{^{14}\mathrm{N}} = r_5X\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C} + r_7X\,{}^{16}\mathrm{O} - r_6X\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N} - r_{11}Y\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N},\qquad R_{^{16}\mathrm{O}} = f_4r_6X\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N} + r_{10}Y\,{}^{12}\mathrm{C} - r_7X\,{}^{16}\mathrm{O} + r_8Y\,{}^{13}\mathrm{C}.$$ The reactions by the comments: $r_1$ pp, $r_2$ ³He+³He, $r_3$ ³He+⁴He, $r_4$ ¹²C+p, $r_5$ ¹³C+p, $r_6$ ¹⁴N+p (with ¹⁵N+p), $r_7$ ¹⁶O+p (with ¹⁷O+p), $r_8$ ¹³C+α, $r_{10}$ ¹²C+α, $r_{11}$ ¹⁴N+α, $r_{12}$ triple α; $r_9$ (¹⁶O+α) and $r_{13}$ (¹²C+¹²C) are zeroed.

  3. Newton iteration. The Jacobian $\partial G_k/\partial w_l$ fills A(1..49) column by column and the residuals $G_k$ fill A(50..56) (the array B of rp_1, by EQUIVALENCE, now addressed as A(49+I)); entries below $10^{-8}$ off the diagonal are zeroed, and SIMEQC (which solves $J\,\delta = G$, leaving $\delta$ in A(50..56)) is skipped when the matrix is diagonal. Then $w \leftarrow w - \delta$; abundances below CMIN are set to 0. It stops when $\max|\delta_k| <$ ABSTOL and $\max|\delta_k/w_k| <$ RELTOL, or stops the run after KEMMAX iterations. The first guess is the result for the shell below (or the shell's current HCOMP for the first shell).

  4. After convergence. ¹⁸O explicitly: $x_{18} = x_{18}^0 + 18\,\Delta t\,r_{11}\,Y\,{}^{14}\mathrm{N}$. If the previous model's $X$ at the zone's top (HCOMPP(1,IEND)) is below $5\times10^{-7}$, $Z$ is reset to $1 - X - {}^3\mathrm{He} - Y$ from the new values; otherwise $Z$ is the zone's mean at the start of the step. All shells of the zone get the same new values.

Questions for the experts

  1. ¹⁸O is only produced. The explicit update adds ¹⁴N(α,γ) production but no destruction. Intended?
  2. ¹⁵N and ¹⁷O are not mixed in burning zones. When a convection zone burns, elements 8 and 10 of HCOMP are left unchanged (only the non-burning branch homogenizes all 11). Do they matter anywhere?
  3. Comments that no longer hold. The comment on the first guess speaks of calls from CRRECT with ITLVL > 1, an argument now removed; the error message says "run stopped after 50 attempts", while the limit is KEMMAX.
  4. Hard-coded thresholds. $10^{-8}$ for zeroing matrix entries and $5\times10^{-7}$ for resetting $Z$.
  5. The diagonal shortcut. When all off-diagonal entries are below $10^{-8}$, SIMEQC is skipped and the correction is the residual $G_k$ itself, not $G_k/J_{kk}$. The diagonal entries are $1 + A_k\Delta t\,(\dots)$, close to 1 only for small rates; is the undivided step intended?
