Purpose

LAX_WENDROF1 is the first (half) step of the two-step Lax–Wendroff scheme that GRSETT uses for the explicit, gravitational-settling term of the diffusion equation. It returns the change in the hydrogen abundance at the midpoints of an equally spaced radial grid over half a time step, and the same for the metals when metal diffusion is on.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: GRSETT (helium settling) and MICRODIFF_RUN (the microscopic-diffusion driver), each followed by LAX_WENDROF2.
  • Arguments: DT (time step, s), ECOD1 (on the equal grid: the first term's coefficient, which SETUP_GRSETT builds as $d\ln P/dr$ times a function of $X$, so it acts as a flux), EM (the mass at each grid point), NPT, STOT (total mass), LDOLI; output EX_H.
  • Modules: CONSTANTS_MOD ($4\pi$); NMLPAR_MOD (the switch LDIFZ for metal diffusion); GRAVEZ_MOD, the metals' arrays on the equal grid (ECOD1Z in, EZ_H out), shared with GRSETT, LAX_WENDROF2, MODEL_TO_EQUAL and EQUAL_TO_MODEL.

The method

With $F_i$ = ECOD1(I), $m_i$ = EM(I), $M$ = STOT:

$$\Delta X_{i+1/2} = \frac{\Delta t}{2}\,4\pi\,\frac{F_{i+1}-F_i}{m_{i+1}-m_i}\quad (i = 1..N-1),$$

stored in EX_H(I), where at the centre ($i = 1$) the code takes $F_1 = 0$, $m_1 = 0$, and at the surface ($i = N-1$) $F_N = 0$, $m_N = M$: so the flux is taken as zero at the centre and at the surface. The same formulas, with ECOD1Z and EZ_H, are applied to the metals when LDIFZ is true and LDOLI is false.

Only the increment is computed, not the textbook half step's average of the neighbouring values, $X_{j+1/2}^{n+1/2} = \tfrac12(X_j + X_{j+1}) + \Delta X_{j+1/2}$: The average enters through the midpoint coefficients, which MODEL_TO_EQUAL interpolates from the current abundances, and GRSETT adds the increment by linearizing them, $F_{i+1/2} + \Delta X_{i+1/2}\,\partial F/\partial X$, which is $F$ at the half step's abundance to first order (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: grsett.f, lines 173–185↗; model_to_equal.f, lines 157–170↗). The two boundary cells are not those of the midpoint grid: The central one spans the mass from 0 to EM(2), and the surface one from EM(NPT-1) to STOT, so that each holds the reservoir beyond the grid's end (a convective core or envelope), to which EQUAL_TO_MODEL applies the change at the grid's end point (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 45–47↗, lines 55–57↗; equal_to_model.f, lines 42–44↗, equal_to_model.f, lines 113–116↗).
