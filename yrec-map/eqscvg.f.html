Explanation

Purpose. EQSCVG evaluates the Saumon–Chabrier–Van Horn (SCV) equation of state for a general composition, by combining its pure-hydrogen, pure-helium and metal tables. The header calls them "the general abundance tables".

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only EQSCVE, when $X$ or $Z$ differs from the envelope values by more than $10^{-5}$.
  • Inputs and outputs: as for EQSCVE: TL, T, P, BETA, X, Z in; DL, D, QDT, QDP, QCP, DELA, FXION, LCALC out.
  • What it calls: INTERP, for four-point Lagrange weights and their derivative weights.
  • Modules: SCVEOS_MOD (the tables TABLEX, TABLEY, TABLEZ, SMIX, the grid TLOGX, TABLENV's pressure column, and the indices IDTSCV, IDP), shared with EQSCVE, EQSTAT2 and the setup routines (see eqscve.f); CONSTANTS_MOD for CLN.

The method. All interpolation is four-point Lagrange in $\log P_\mathrm{gas}$ and $\log T$ (weights FP, FT; FTD are the weights of the $\log T$ derivative).

  1. Density by additive volumes, with $\rho_X$, $\rho_Y$, $\rho_Z$ from the three tables (column 4 of TABLEX, TABLEY, TABLEZ): $$\frac{1}{\rho} = \frac{X}{\rho_X} + \frac{Y}{\rho_Y} + \frac{Z}{\rho_Z}.$$

  2. Derivatives as volume-weighted means, with weights $w_k = X_k\rho/\rho_k$: $$Q_{\rho P,0} = \sum_k w_k Q_{\rho P,k}, \qquad Q_{\rho T,0} = \sum_k w_k Q_{\rho T,k}.$$

  3. Specific heat from the entropy: with $S = XS_X + YS_Y + S_\mathrm{mix}$ and the tabulated $\partial\ln S_k/\partial\ln T$, $$c_{P,0} = XS_X\frac{\partial\ln S_X}{\partial\ln T} + YS_Y\frac{\partial\ln S_Y}{\partial\ln T} + S_\mathrm{mix}\frac{\partial\ln S_\mathrm{mix}}{\partial\ln T} + Z\left(\frac{\partial U_Z}{\partial T} + \frac{P_\mathrm{gas}Q_{\rho T,Z}^2}{Q_{\rho P,Z}\,\rho_Z T}\right),$$ then $\partial U/\partial T = c_{P,0} - P_\mathrm{gas}Q_{\rho T,0}^2/(Q_{\rho P,0}\,\rho T)$.

  4. Radiation is added as in EQSCVE: $Q_{\rho P} = Q_{\rho P,0}/\beta$, $\chi_T = -\beta Q_{\rho T,0}/Q_{\rho P,0} + 4(1-\beta)$, $Q_{\rho T} = -\chi_T Q_{\rho P}$, $\partial U/\partial T \mathrel{+}= 12P_\mathrm{rad}/(\rho T)$, and $$c_P = \frac{\partial U}{\partial T} + \frac{P\,Q_{\rho T}^2}{Q_{\rho P}\,\rho T},\qquad \nabla_\mathrm{ad} = -\frac{P\,Q_{\rho T}}{\rho T\,c_P}.$$

  5. Ionization fractions as in EQSCVE.

Questions for the experts

  1. H$_2$ counted once in the hydrogen ionization fraction, as in EQSCVE (question 1 there).
  2. No smoothing. Unlike EQSCVE, there is no blending when the stencil changes, so the outputs can jump between stencils. Is that acceptable for non-envelope compositions?
  3. One pressure grid. The searches start from the indices IDTSCV, IDP left by the last call (of this routine or EQSCVE), which only costs time. But the pressure stencil and its abscissae are taken from TABLENV's row IDTSCV and used for all four rows of TABLEX, TABLEY, TABLEZ and SMIX. Do all these tables share one pressure grid? (Question 3 in EQSCVE.)
  4. A stray string. A comment line before the $Q_{\rho T}$ block ends with zdiffOPEOSfinalewkop95zscv1.full, probably a file name pasted by mistake.
  5. Logarithm of $S_\mathrm{mix}$. $\partial\ln S_\mathrm{mix}/\partial\ln T$ is taken from $\log_{10}$ of the interpolated SMIX values; it fails if SMIX is zero or negative anywhere in the stencil. Can it be?
