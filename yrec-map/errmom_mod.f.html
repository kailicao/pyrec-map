Explanation

Purpose. ERRMOM_MOD holds TOLERI, the relative tolerance of the iteration for the moment of inertia and the surface rotation rate, shared by CHECKJ and SOLID. It replaces COMMON/ERRMOM/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26).

What its variable holds, who sets it, who reads it.

  • TOLERI (REAL*8): nothing in the code gives it a first value (no namelist, DATA statement or assignment), so it starts at zero in practice. CHECKJ relaxes it to at least DJOK/100 around its call to GETROT in the early iterations and then restores it. SOLID iterates the surface $\Omega$ until the angular momentum of the convection zone matches within TOLERI (at most 20 tries).

Design. A blanket USE ERRMOM_MOD. The tolerance reaches GETROT's callees through the module rather than as an argument.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/ERRMOM/ as a module.

Questions for the experts

  1. A tolerance with no value. TOLERI is set nowhere except CHECKJ's temporary relaxation, so outside it SOLID compares with zero and runs its 20 tries (so also at rp_1). Where should its value come from?
