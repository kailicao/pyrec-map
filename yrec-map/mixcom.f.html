Purpose

Solves the diffusion equation for composition across one unstable region, on the equally spaced grid in the coordinate $\chi$ that MIXGRID and GETGRID have set up, and applies the change to the model.

How it fits

  • Inputs: DT (s); ECOD2 and EM on the equal grid, from MIXGRID; the shell masses HS2; the region IBEG..IEND; the convection flags LCZ; LOK; the species range JBEG..JEND; and the grid itself in EGRID_MOD (CHI at the model points, ECHI at the grid points, DCHI, NTOT).
  • Who calls it: only NDIFCOM.
  • What it calls: OSPLIN (interpolation, both ways), CCOEFT (the tridiagonal coefficients, in TRIDI_MOD) and CTRIDI (the solve, result U in TRIDI_MOD).
  • Output: HCOMP, in place.
  • Modules: EGRID_MOD (shared with GETGRID, MIXGRID, ROTGRID, EQ2MOD, CHECKJ, SECULR), SPLIN_MOD (scratch arrays shared with MIXGRID, ROTGRID, EQ2MOD), TRIDI_MOD (with CCOEFT, CTRIDI, DCOEFT, GETQUA, TRIDIA), and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

For each species $k$ in JBEG..JEND (except ⁴He, row 2, which follows from closure) that varies by more than $10^{-14}$ over the region:

  1. Interpolate $X_k$ from the model points ($\chi$ = CHI) to the grid points (ECHI) with OSPLIN.

  2. Solve, implicitly, with $\Delta\chi$ = DCHI, $\Delta M_i$ = EM(I) and $\mathcal{D}$ = ECOD2 (COD2 times $\rho r^2\, d\chi/dr$, see MIXGRID): $$X_i^{n+1} - \frac{\Delta t}{\Delta\chi\,\Delta M_i}\left[\mathcal{D}_{i+1}\,(X^{n+1}_{i+1} - X^{n+1}_i) - \mathcal{D}_i\,(X^{n+1}_i - X^{n+1}_{i-1})\right] = X_i^n,$$ with no flux through the outer edges (CCOEFT, CTRIDI).

  3. Interpolate the change $X^{n+1}-X^n$ back to the model points and add it (the comment explains: extrapolating the new abundances past the last grid points "can lead to serious errors").

  4. A convection zone adjoining the region below or above (whether or not the region's edge point is itself convective, the test MIXGRID's sums use) takes the abundance of the region's edge point if that point is convective, since it was mixed with its neighbor; if the edge point is radiative, the zone gets the end cell's change added instead.

Finally, before the last iteration (LOK = F), $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$ over the region and the adjoining convection zones (lines 196–200↗). On the last iteration the closure needs no update when SECULR calls (rows 5 and up, so $X$, $Z$ and ³He do not change, lines 466–475↗); BURSMIX, which mixes rows 4 and up with ³He among them, sets $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$ itself after its extrapolation (lines 132–136↗) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

The sums DCOMP, DCOMP2, SUMSPEC and SUMSPEC2 (lines 99–104↗, lines 144–182↗) feed only a conservation check that is commented out, which would rescale the species to keep its mass (lines 184–193↗): unused?
