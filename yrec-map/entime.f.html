Purpose

This file limits the time step so that the surface changes from one model to the next stay within the envelope "triangle": the changes of $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ and $\log L$ must not exceed the triangle's size, so that the surface boundary condition is not extrapolated far outside the envelope integrations.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments DELTS (the previous step), HL (luminosity), TEFFL, M; the previous model's surface luminosity HLSURO and TEFFLO (OLDMOD_MOD, saved by HPOINT before the rezoning, and by main.f and STARIN); the settings TRIDT, TRIDL (the triangle's sizes, formerly /CENV/) and ATIME(13) (the limit, formerly /CTLIM/), from NMLPAR_MOD.
  • Who calls it: HTIMER, which sets the next time step.
  • Output: EDELT, the time step this criterion allows.

The method

$$f = \max\left(\frac{|\Delta\log T_\mathrm{eff}|}{\Delta T_\mathrm{tri}}, \frac{|\Delta\log L|}{\Delta L_\mathrm{tri}}\right), \qquad f \leftarrow \min\!\left(\max\left(f, \frac{1}{a}\right), a\right), \qquad \mathtt{EDELT} = \frac{\Delta t}{f},$$ with $a$ = ATIME(13), the largest factor by which the step may change; $f = 1$ if nothing changed. A previous step of 0 thus gives EDELT = 0, which HTIMER does not catch (suspected bug 1). The triangle's sizes are also copied into HMAX(1:2), which nothing reads (unused?). The header's output line, "time step for requested change in P,T,R,L", is copied from PTIME: Only $T_\mathrm{eff}$ and $L$ are tested here (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: line 68↗; lines 73–95↗).
