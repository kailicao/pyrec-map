Explanation

Purpose. This file computes, at one point, the rate of deuterium burning by $^2$H(p,$\gamma$)$^3$He out of equilibrium ("MHP 5/02"), for the burning done in DBURN and DBURNM. Screening is left out, "reasonable given the low temperatures involved".

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments DL ($\log\rho$), TL ($\log T$), X (hydrogen), I (the point), ITLVL (the level of iteration); JCZ (the base of the surface convection zone) from DEUTER_MOD; TAUCZ (convective overturn time) from OVRTRN_MOD; CLN, CC13 and CAVOG (Avogadro's number, $N_A = 1/m_u$) from CONSTANTS_MOD; the deuteron mass in amu, AMDEUT $= 2.013553$, set here (the same value as ANUC(3) in ENGEB; the comment asks to keep the two in step).
  • Who calls it: MIX, for every point from the centre out until $T$ falls below TCUT(1), when the surface deuterium (HCOMP(12,M), or the accreted COMPACC(12) if larger, during accretion) exceeds $10^{-11}$.
  • Outputs: DRATE(I) in DEUTER_MOD, and at the first level of iteration also DRATE0(I) (the rate at the start of the step). DBURN reads both; MIDMOD interpolates between them for DBURNM.

The method. With $T_9 = T/10^9$ K, the reaction rate per mole (the fit of Caughlan and Fowler type, by its form): $$N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle = 2.240\times10^{3}\;T_9^{-2/3}\;e^{-3.72\,T_9^{-1/3}}\left(1 + 0.112\,T_9^{1/3} + 3.38\,T_9^{2/3} + 2.65\,T_9\right),$$ then, with $A_D$ = AMDEUT $= 2.013553$, $$\mathtt{DRATE} = X\,\rho\,N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle \cdot \frac{N_A}{A_D}\cdot C_{21}, \qquad C_{21} = 5.240358\times10^{-8} \approx \frac{3.156\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{s\,Gyr^{-1}}}{N_A},$$ so DRATE $\approx X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle/A_D$ per Gyr (the value of $C_{21}$ is read as seconds per Gyr over $N_A$; this is an inference). The burning routines then use $X_D \propto e^{-2\,\mathtt{DRATE}\,t}$, so the destruction rate per deuteron is $\lambda = 2\,\mathtt{DRATE} = (2/A_D)\,X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle \approx 0.993\,X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle$ per Gyr. The comment calls CAVOG/AMDEUT "Avogadro's number divided by the mass of the deuteron in amu".

The convection-zone cap. In the surface convection zone ($I \ge$ JCZ, with $\tau_\mathrm{cz} > 1$), $X\cdot$RDEUT is capped at $(N_A/A_D)/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ (CAVOG/AMDEUT/TAUCZ), so that deuterium burns "on a time scale no shorter than the convective overturn timescale".

Questions for the experts

  1. The cap's units. The cap makes $2\,\mathtt{DRATE} = 1/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ in units of Gyr$^{-1}$ only if $\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ is in seconds. TAUCAL forms TAUCZ as a length over a convective velocity (CZWIDE/CVEAVG or PSCA/CVEL), so it is in seconds, probably, and the cap is consistent; the test TAUCZ > 1 (one second) then excludes almost nothing.
