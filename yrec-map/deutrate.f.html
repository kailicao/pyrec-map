Explanation

Purpose. This file computes, at one point, the rate of deuterium burning by $^2$H(p,$\gamma$)$^3$He out of equilibrium ("MHP 5/02"), for the burning done in DBURN and DBURNM. Screening is left out, "reasonable given the low temperatures involved".

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments DL ($\log\rho$), TL ($\log T$), X (hydrogen), I (the point), ITLVL (the level of iteration); JCZ (the base of the surface convection zone) from DEUTER_MOD; TAUCZ (convective overturn time) from OVRTRN_MOD; CLN, CC13 from CONSTANTS_MOD.
  • Who calls it: MIX, for every point from the centre out until $T$ falls below TCUT(1), when the surface deuterium (HCOMP(12,M), or the accreted COMPACC(12) if larger, during accretion) exceeds $10^{-11}$.
  • Outputs: DRATE(I) in DEUTER_MOD, and at the first level of iteration also DRATE0(I) (the rate at the start of the step). DBURN reads both; MIDMOD interpolates between them for DBURNM.

The method. With $T_9 = T/10^9$ K, the reaction rate per mole (the fit of Caughlan and Fowler type, by its form): $$N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle = 2.240\times10^{3}\;T_9^{\,2/3}\;e^{-3.72\,T_9^{-1/3}}\left(1 + 0.112\,T_9^{1/3} + 3.38\,T_9^{2/3} + 2.65\,T_9\right)\ \text{(as coded)},$$ then $$\mathtt{DRATE} = X\,\rho\,N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle \cdot \frac{N_A}{2}\cdot C_{21}, \qquad C_{21} = 5.240358\times10^{-8} \approx \frac{3.156\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{s\,Gyr^{-1}}}{N_A},$$ so DRATE $= \tfrac12 X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle$ per Gyr (the value of $C_{21}$ is read as seconds per Gyr over $N_A$; this is an inference). The burning routines then use $X_D \propto e^{-2\,\mathtt{DRATE}\,t}$, so the destruction rate per deuteron is $\lambda = X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle$. The comment calls $3.0115\times10^{23}$ "Avogadro's number divided by the mass of the deuteron in amu".

The convection-zone cap. In the surface convection zone ($I \ge$ JCZ, with $\tau_\mathrm{CZ} > 1$), $X\cdot$RDEUT is capped at $3.0115\times10^{23}/\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$, so that deuterium burns "on a time scale no shorter than the convective overturn timescale".

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused argument XH2 removed.

Changed since rp_1. C21 is now set in double precision (5.240358D-8), which settles the former question on its single-precision constant; COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. A wrong power of $T_9$? The usual fit for $^2$H(p,$\gamma$)$^3$He (Caughlan and Fowler 1988) is $2.24\times10^{3}\,T_9^{-2/3}\exp(-3.720\,T_9^{-1/3})(1 + \dots)$. The code multiplies by T9P23 ($T_9^{+2/3}$), while T9M23 ($T_9^{-2/3}$) is computed and never used. If this is a typo, the rate is too low by $T_9^{4/3}$: about $10^{-4}$ at $10^6$ K. Is it? ENGEB has the same power (its question 4).
  2. The cap's units. The cap makes $2\,\mathtt{DRATE} = 1/\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ in units of Gyr$^{-1}$ only if $\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ is in seconds. TAUCAL forms TAUCZ as a length over a convective velocity (CZWIDE/CVEAVG or PSCA/CVEL), so it is in seconds, probably, and the cap is consistent; the test TAUCZ > 1 (one second) then excludes almost nothing. Can the experts confirm?
