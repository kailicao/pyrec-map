Purpose

This file computes, at one point, the rate of deuterium burning by $^2$H(p,$\gamma$)$^3$He out of equilibrium ("MHP 5/02"), for the burning done in DBURN and DBURNM. Screening is left out, "reasonable given the low temperatures involved."

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments DL ($\log\rho$), TL ($\log T$), X (hydrogen), I (the point), ITLVL (the level of iteration); JCZ (the base of the surface convection zone) from DEUTER_MOD; TAUCZ (convective overturn time) from OVRTRN_MOD; CLN, CC13 and CAVOG (Avogadro's number, $N_A = 1/m_u$) from CONSTANTS_MOD; the deuteron mass in amu, AMDEUT $= 2.013553$, set here (the same value as ANUC(3) in ENGEB; the comment asks to keep the two in step).
  • Who calls it: MIX, for every point from the centre out until $T$ falls below TCUT(1), when the surface deuterium (HCOMP(12,M), or the accreted COMPACC(12) if larger, during accretion) exceeds $10^{-11}$.
  • Outputs: DRATE(I) in DEUTER_MOD, and at the first level of iteration also DRATE0(I) (the rate at the start of the step). DBURN reads both; MIDMOD interpolates between them for DBURNM.

The method

With $T_9 = T/10^9$ K, the reaction rate per mole (the fit of Caughlan and Fowler type, by its form): $$N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle = 2.240\times10^{3}\;T_9^{-2/3}\;e^{-3.72\,T_9^{-1/3}}\left(1 + 0.112\,T_9^{1/3} + 3.38\,T_9^{2/3} + 2.65\,T_9\right),$$ then, with $A_D$ = AMDEUT $= 2.013553$, $$\mathtt{DRATE} = X\,\rho\,N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle \cdot \frac{N_A}{A_D}\cdot C_{21}, \qquad C_{21} = 5.240358\times10^{-8} \approx \frac{3.156\times10^{16}\ \mathrm{s\,Gyr^{-1}}}{N_A},$$ so DRATE $\approx X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle/A_D$ per Gyr (the value of $C_{21}$ is read as seconds per Gyr over $N_A$; this is an inference). The burning routines then use $X_D \propto e^{-2\,\mathtt{DRATE}\,t}$, so the destruction rate per deuteron is $\lambda = 2\,\mathtt{DRATE} = (2/A_D)\,X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle \approx 0.993\,X\rho N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle$ per Gyr. The comment calls CAVOG/AMDEUT "Avogadro's number divided by the mass of the deuteron in amu."

The convection-zone cap. In the surface convection zone ($I \ge$ JCZ, with $\tau_\mathrm{cz} > 1$), $X\cdot$RDEUT is capped at $(N_A/A_D)/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ (CAVOG/AMDEUT/TAUCZ), so that deuterium burns "on a time scale no shorter than the convective overturn timescale." TAUCAL forms TAUCZ as a length in cm over a convective velocity (CZWIDE/CVEAVG or PSCA/CVEL, taucal.f, lines 159–165↗, taucal.f, line 179↗), so in seconds, with YREC's cgs velocities; then DRATE $= C_{21}\,(N_A/A_D)/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ is $1/(A_D\,\tau_\mathrm{cz})$ in Gyr$^{-1}$, and the destruction rate per deuteron, $2\,$DRATE, is $0.993/\tau_\mathrm{cz}$: the cap is consistent with the comment (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 65–66↗, line 81↗).

Question 1 (line 65↗). The test TAUCZ > 1. With TAUCZ in seconds, the test excludes only overturn times below one second, which a convection zone does not have. Is it meant as a guard against an unset or zero TAUCZ (no surface convection zone), and would TAUCZ > 0 say so more plainly?

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. C21 is now set in double precision (5.240358D-8), which settles the former question on its single-precision constant; COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE.

Changed since 09c3b9b. The rate's leading power of $T_9$ is now $T_9^{-2/3}$ (T9M23), as in the usual fit, not $T_9^{+2/3}$, which settles former question 1 (the rate rises by $T_9^{-4/3}$, about $10^{4}$ at $10^6$ K); the factor $3.0115\times10^{23}$ ($6.023\times10^{23}/2$) is now CAVOG/AMDEUT, Avogadro's number from CONSTANTS_MOD over the deuteron's mass in amu (a new DATA value), in the rate and in the cap (both 0.7% smaller); former question 2 is now 1.
