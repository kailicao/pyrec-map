Explanation

Purpose. EGRID_MOD holds the evenly spaced grid on which angular momentum transport and rotational mixing are solved: the grid variable $\chi$ at the model's shells and at the even points, the even points' masses, the spacing and their number. Shared by 7 routines. It replaces COMMON/EGRID/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All are set by GETGRID for the region IBEG…IEND:

  • CHI(JSON): $\chi = \log m/\delta_m + L/\delta_L - \log P/\delta_P$ at each shell of the region, with the spacings from the mesh tolerances HPTTOL.
  • NTOT (INTEGER*4): the number of even points, the number of shells in the region but at least 3.
  • DCHI: the spacing, $(\chi_\mathrm{last} - \chi_1)/(\mathtt{NTOT}-1)$.
  • ECHI(JSON): $\chi$ at the even points, from CHI(1) in steps of DCHI.
  • ES1(JSON): the mass (g) at the even points, interpolated in $\log m$ by OSPLIN.
  • Read by ROTGRID and MIXGRID, which interpolate quantities between the two grids, MIXCOM, EQ2MOD, SECULR (which passes NTOT to the solvers) and CHECKJ (printing).

Design. A blanket USE EGRID_MOD. At rp_1, SECULR named the third member EQS1, harmless with COMMON's storage association.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/EGRID/ as a module.
