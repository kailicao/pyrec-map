Purpose

YTIME gives the time step allowed by helium burning. Its comment: stars with a helium luminosity have a time step limited by the time to burn ATIME(4) of $Y$ at the temperature maximum, or the fraction ATIME(5) of $Y$ there; a high central density is taken as the sign of a giant in the helium flash rather than a horizontal-branch star.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the model (arguments: $\log\rho$ HD, HCOMP, $L$, $m$, $\log T$, JCORE, M, JXBEG), work arrays for the rates, and from modules the limits ATIME, the temperature cuts TCUT and the extended-composition switch LEXCOM (NMLPAR_MOD), and $L_\odot$ CLSUN and $M_\odot$ CMSUN (CONSTANTS_MOD).
  • Who calls it: only HTIMER.
  • What it calls: ENGEB, for the energy rates at the temperature maximum (and, below ATIME(1), at each helium-burning point), which also returns EALPCA and ENU (and a $^7$Be mass fraction this routine does not use).
  • Outputs: DELTSY, the step (in seconds, by its use in HTIMER), and EG(1..6), the energy rates at the temperature maximum.

The method

  • Helium flash ($\log\rho_c \ge 5$): find the shell of maximum temperature, compute its energy rates with ENGEB, and take $$\Delta t = \frac{10^{15}}{\epsilon_5},$$ with $\epsilon_5$ = EG(5), ENGEB's fifth argument E3AL, the triple-α rate (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: line 104↗; line 115↗; line 4↗), at least $10^{-22}$.

    Question 1 (line 69↗; lines 118–119↗). Hard-coded values. The flash criterion $\log\rho_c \ge 5$, the constant $10^{15}$, and the floor $10^{-22}$ are fixed in the code. With $\epsilon_5$ in erg g$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$, $10^{15}$ is in erg g$^{-1}$: the step lets the hottest point release $10^{15}$ erg g$^{-1}$, which burns $\Delta Y \approx 10^{15}/5.85\times10^{17} \approx 0.0017$ there. Is that the intent, and should it follow ATIME(4) or ATIME(5), as the other branches do?

  • Otherwise: with $Y_\mathrm{core}$ at JCORE and $q = 5.85\times10^{17}$ erg/g (the commented-out Q3A), if $Y_\mathrm{core} \ge$ ATIME(1), $$\Delta Y = \min(\texttt{ATIME(4)},\ \texttt{ATIME(5)}\,Y_\mathrm{core}),\qquad \Delta t = \frac{q}{L_\odot}\,\frac{m}{L/L_\odot}\,\Delta Y$$ at JCORE. ATIME(1) is one threshold for both elements by design: XTIME compares the central $X$ with it, this routine $Y_\mathrm{core}$; REMAP's comment calls it the "minimum central abundance to use core burning dt criterion", and the documentation of TIME_CORE_MIN, the "minimum X,Y for using core vs shell burning criteria" (settled from the code and the documentation, Nov 9, 2026: line 123↗; lines 260–261↗).

    Question 2 (lines 122–127↗; lines 171–173↗). $Y$ or $1 - Z$? YCORE = 1D0 - HCOMP(3,JCORE) is $X + Y$, not $Y$ (HCOMP(2,·)). In a helium core $X \approx 0$ and the two agree, but during hydrogen burning they do not. With the defaults (ATIME(4) = 0.02, ATIME(5) = 0.3) $\Delta Y$ is the same for both unless $Y_\mathrm{core} < 0.067$, so the choice matters little in practice. Is $1 - Z$ intended?

Otherwise (the shell branch) the step is the smallest of three limits, starting from $10^{20}$ s: - burning ATIME(14) $M_\odot$ of helium at the luminosity just below the hydrogen shell, $\Delta t = (q/L_\odot)\,\texttt{ATIME(14)}\,M_\odot/(L/L_\odot)$ at JXBEG-1 (when JXBEG > 1 and that $L > 0$); - burning the fraction ATIME(12) of $Y$ at any point with $Y \ge$ ATIME(1) and $\log T \ge$ TCUT(4), $\Delta t = \texttt{ATIME(12)}\,Y/\dot Y$, with $\dot Y = \epsilon_{3\alpha}/q + \epsilon_{\alpha\mathrm{cap}}/(1.7276\times10^{18}\ \mathrm{erg/g})$ from ENGEB (EGG55 and EALPCA, negative values taken as zero); - while $Y_\mathrm{core}$ > YFLOOR ($10^{-5}$, below every END_YCEN in use) and $L > 0$ at JCORE, the core criterion without its cap: $\Delta t = (q/L_\odot)\,\texttt{ATIME(5)}\,Y_\mathrm{core}\,m/(L/L_\odot)$ at JCORE, which resolves central helium exhaustion down to the END_YCEN stop.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. EALPCA and ENU come back from ENGEB as arguments (/NEWEPS/ retired), with the new BE7MFLOC for ENGEB's $^7$Be mass fraction (formerly /BE7/); COMMON blocks to modules, JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Changed since a8a5950. The shell branch (below ATIME(1)) has real limits: It used to rescale DELTSY, which held HTIMER's helium step in seconds, as if it were an amount of $Y$, so the step grew through core helium exhaustion; it now takes the smallest of the ATIME(14), ATIME(12), and core limits above, so ATIME(12) and ATIME(14), read but never used before, now count (this settles former question 2).
