Explanation

Purpose. DISK_MOD holds SAGE, the star's age, which the rotation routines compare with the disk-locking time, shared by 5 routines. It replaces what is left of COMMON/DISK/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26): the disk-locking switch and parameters LDISK, TDISK, PDISK are namelist variables now in NMLPAR_MOD.

What its variable holds, who sets it, who reads it.

  • SAGE (REAL*8): the total age DAGE (Gyr), stored by the main program after each model ("MHP 9/94 store total age in SAGE"). Read by GETW, SECULR and SOLID, which hold the surface rotation locked while LDISK and SAGE ≤ TDISK, and by MDOT, which with LDISK and accretion shortens the step so that it ends at TDISK.

Design. A blanket USE DISK_MOD. Since SAGE is set after the model is computed, the routines of the next step see the age at the start of that step.

Changed since rp_1. New file: SAGE of COMMON/DISK/ as a module; LDISK, TDISK, PDISK moved to NMLPAR_MOD.
