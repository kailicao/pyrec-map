Purpose

Computes the gas's mean molecular weight terms for the equation-of-state routines: the ions' contribution AMU, the free electrons' contribution EMU, and RMU $= \mathcal{R}\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU})$.

How it fits

  • Inputs: $T$, $P$, $\rho$ (D), $X$, $Z$, and BETA (probably the ratio of gas to total pressure, as the name suggests); the envelope composition and its AMUENV from COMP_MOD; the gas constant CGAS from CONSTANTS_MOD.
  • Who calls it: the equation-of-state routine OEQOS06 (at rp_1 also MEQOS, OEQOS and OEQOS01, removed since rp_1).
  • Outputs: AMU, EMU, RMU.
  • Modules: COMP_MOD, CONSTANTS_MOD. The unused /CTLIM/, /CONST1/ and /CCOUT2/ of rp_1 are no longer declared.

The method

  1. Ions. If $X$ and $Z$ differ from the envelope's by less than $10^{-5}$ in total, AMU = AMUENV. Otherwise the change is added species by species, with "excess Z (metals) … in the form of carbon(12)": $$\mathrm{AMU} = \mathrm{AMU_{env}} + \frac{\Delta X}{1.008} + \frac{\Delta Y}{4.004} + \frac{\Delta Z}{12.016},\qquad \Delta Y = -(\Delta X + \Delta Z),$$ (the weights ATOMWT are 0.9921, 0.24975 and 0.08322, i.e. $1/A$; a fourth, ATOMWT(4) $= 0.4995$ ($2/4.004$), is set but never read: unused? line 29↗). So AMU is the number of ions per unit mass in units of $1/m_u$, the inverse of the ions' mean molecular weight.

    Question 1 (lines 40–43↗). Carbon for the metals. The comment says excess metals are carbon, which suits helium-burning regions. Is this still the intended approximation for metal changes from settling, where the excess metals are a mixture with a larger mean $A$?

  2. Electrons. From the gas pressure, $\beta P = \rho\,\mathcal{R}\,T\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU})$, so $$\mathrm{EMU} = \mathrm{AMU}\left(\frac{\beta P}{\rho\,T\,\mathcal{R}\,\mathrm{AMU}} - 1\right),\qquad \mathrm{RMU} = \mathcal{R}\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU}) = \frac{\beta P}{\rho T}.$$

13       beta: float) -> tuple[float, float, float]:

15    Z, excess metals as carbon), the electrons' term emu from the gas
16    pressure beta*p, and rmu = CGAS (amu + emu).
17
18    Translates: mu.f#MU (7be4133)
19
20    Arguments: t, p, d (rho), x, z and beta; rmu, amu and emu are passed in
21    with any value.
22
23    Returns (rmu, amu, emu).
24
25    """
26
27    cgas = constants_mod.cgas
28
29    # The ions' term (Fortran lines 33–44).
30    dfx1 = x - comp_mod.xenv
31    dfx12 = z - comp_mod.zenv
32    if abs(dfx1) + abs(dfx12) < 1.0e-5:
33        # The envelope's abundances.
34        amu = comp_mod.amuenv
35    else:
36        dfx1 = dfx1 * ATOMWT[1]
37        dfx12 = dfx12 * ATOMWT[3]
38        dfx4 = (comp_mod.xenv + comp_mod.zenv - x - z) * ATOMWT[2]
39        # Question (see the page): excess Z (metals) taken as carbon 12.
40        amu = comp_mod.amuenv + dfx1 + dfx4 + dfx12
41
42    # The electrons' term from the gas pressure (Fortran lines 45–47).
43    ee = ((beta * p) / (d * t * cgas * amu)) - 1.0
44    emu = ee * amu
45    rmu = cgas * (amu + emu)
46    return rmu, amu, emu
