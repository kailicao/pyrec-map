Explanation

Purpose. Computes the gas's mean molecular weight terms for the equation-of-state routines: the ions' contribution AMU, the free electrons' contribution EMU, and RMU $= \mathcal{R}\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU})$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: $T$, $P$, $\rho$ (D), $X$, $Z$, and BETA (probably the ratio of gas to total pressure, as the name suggests); the envelope composition and its AMUENV from COMP_MOD; the gas constant CGAS from CONSTANTS_MOD.
  • Who calls it: the equation-of-state routine OEQOS06 (at rp_1 also MEQOS, OEQOS and OEQOS01, removed since rp_1).
  • Outputs: AMU, EMU, RMU.
  • Modules: COMP_MOD, CONSTANTS_MOD. The unused /CTLIM/, /CONST1/ and /CCOUT2/ of rp_1 are no longer declared.

The method.

  1. Ions. If $X$ and $Z$ differ from the envelope's by less than $10^{-5}$ in total, AMU = AMUENV. Otherwise the change is added species by species, with "excess Z (metals) … in the form of carbon(12)": $$\mathrm{AMU} = \mathrm{AMU_{env}} + \frac{\Delta X}{1.008} + \frac{\Delta Y}{4.004} + \frac{\Delta Z}{12.016},\qquad \Delta Y = -(\Delta X + \Delta Z),$$ (the weights ATOMWT are 0.9921, 0.24975 and 0.08322, i.e. $1/A$). So AMU is the number of ions per unit mass in units of $1/m_u$, the inverse of the ions' mean molecular weight.

  2. Electrons. From the gas pressure, $\beta P = \rho\,\mathcal{R}\,T\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU})$, so $$\mathrm{EMU} = \mathrm{AMU}\left(\frac{\beta P}{\rho\,T\,\mathcal{R}\,\mathrm{AMU}} - 1\right),\qquad \mathrm{RMU} = \mathcal{R}\,(\mathrm{AMU}+\mathrm{EMU}) = \frac{\beta P}{\rho T}.$$

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; its callers other than OEQOS06 are removed.

Questions for the experts

  1. An unused weight. ATOMWT(4) $= 0.4995$ ($2/4.004$, probably for ³He or for He's electrons) is never used. A leftover?
  2. Carbon for the metals. The comment says excess metals are carbon, which suits helium-burning regions. Is this still the intended approximation for metal changes from settling?

20    Arguments: t, p, d (rho), x, z and beta; rmu, amu and emu are passed in
21    with any value.
22
23    Returns (rmu, amu, emu).
24    """
25    cgas = constants_mod.cgas
26    dfx1 = x - comp_mod.xenv
27    dfx12 = z - comp_mod.zenv
28    if abs(dfx1) + abs(dfx12) < 1.0e-5:
29        # The envelope's abundances.
30        amu = comp_mod.amuenv
31    else:
32        dfx1 = dfx1 * ATOMWT[1]
33        dfx12 = dfx12 * ATOMWT[3]
34        dfx4 = (comp_mod.xenv + comp_mod.zenv - x - z) * ATOMWT[2]
35        # Question (see the page): excess Z (metals) taken as carbon 12.
36        amu = comp_mod.amuenv + dfx1 + dfx4 + dfx12
37    ee = ((beta * p) / (d * t * cgas * amu)) - 1.0
38    emu = ee * amu

40    return rmu, amu, emu
