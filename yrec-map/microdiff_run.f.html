Purpose

Diffuses one species over one time step on the equally spaced grid, for MICRODIFF, and returns the change in its abundance. The comments describe the diffusion equation as two terms: one with $d\ln P/dr$ and the abundance gradient (solved explicitly, by the two-step Lax–Wendroff scheme of Numerical Recipes), and one with the second derivative of the abundance (solved implicitly).

How it fits

  • Inputs: the equal grid and midpoint variables from MICRODIFF_MTE: mass EM, radius ER, density, temperature, $d\ln P/dr$, $\nabla$ and the three-species abundances ESPEC(3,·); the spacing DR; DT and STOT in Bahcall–Loeb units; and the species' weight THEA, charge THEZ and column J (1 for hydrogen, 3 for metals or a light element).
  • Who calls it: only MICRODIFF, once per diffused species.
  • What it calls: MICRODIFF_COD (the coefficients, twice), LAX_WENDROF1 and LAX_WENDROF2 (the explicit term), GET_IMP_DIFFCO (the matrix) and TRIDIAG_GS (its solution).
  • Output: ESPEC(J,·) is overwritten with the change $X^\mathrm{new}_i - X^\mathrm{old}_i$; EDXDR (the hydrogen concentration gradient, filled on the hydrogen call) is kept for later calls. A line per iteration goes to ISHORT, and a warning to IOWR and ISHORT if the iteration does not converge.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (GRTOL, NITER_GS), CONSTANTS_MOD ($4\pi$, C4PI), IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT, IOWR) and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON). The unused /THEAGE/ of rp_1 is no longer declared.

The method

Let $X_i$ be the abundance at grid point $i$ (radius $r_i$, mass $m_i$), and $X_{i+1/2}$ at the midpoints.

  1. Coefficients at the points. MICRODIFF_COD gives the advective coefficient $C_{1,i}$ (ECOD1) and the diffusion coefficient $C_{2,i}$ (ECOD2).

  2. Lax–Wendroff, first half step (LAX_WENDROF1), the change at the midpoints: $$\Delta X_{i+1/2} = \frac{4\pi\,\Delta t}{2}\;\frac{C_{1,i+1} - C_{1,i}}{m_{i+1} - m_i},$$ where the first cell takes $C_{1,1} = 0$ and $m_1 = 0$, and the last cell $C_{1,N} = 0$ and $m_N =$ STOT, so the first and last cells reach to the centre and the surface.

  3. Coefficients at the midpoints, from the half-step abundances $X_{i+1/2} + \Delta X_{i+1/2}$.

  4. Lax–Wendroff, full step (LAX_WENDROF2), at the points: $$X'_i = X_i + 4\pi\,\Delta t\;\frac{C_{1,i+1/2} - C_{1,i-1/2}}{m_{i+1/2} - m_{i-1/2}},$$ with $C_{1,1/2} = m_{1/2} = 0$ at the first point, and $C_{1,N+1/2} = 0$, $m_{N+1/2} =$ STOT at the last.

  5. The implicit term. With $\alpha_i = 4\pi\,\Delta t / (\Delta r\, \Delta m_i)$ (ALPHA, where $\Delta m_i = m_{i+1/2} - m_{i-1/2}$, from the centre for $i=1$ and to STOT for $i=N$), GET_IMP_DIFFCO builds and TRIDIAG_GS solves $$X_i - \alpha_i\left[C_{2,i+1/2}\,(X_{i+1}-X_i) - C_{2,i-1/2}\,(X_i - X_{i-1})\right] = X'_i .$$ The loop repeats until the largest change between iterations is below GRTOL, at most NITER_GS times.

    Question 1 (lines 160–174↗; get_imp_diffco.f, line 20↗). An iteration that cannot change anything. Inside the loop, GET_IMP_DIFFCO corrects ECOD2_H by EQCOD2X_H * ED_H, but EQCOD2X_H is set to zero just before ("only used in GRSETT … retained for legacy"). So the matrix and the right-hand side ED_PRIME are the same in every iteration, and the second iteration always reproduces the first (its change is zero, below GRTOL). Is the loop (and the convergence test on GRTOL) still meaningful, or should the coefficients be recomputed from the new abundances?

    The comments name the elements of an older use. They speak of "the original run of light element mass fractions," "the new run of LI," and "the corrections to the hydrogen abundance" (line 82↗; line 172↗; lines 176–177↗), but ED is loaded from ESPEC(J,·) (line 80↗) for whichever species J the caller passes, so they could say "the diffused species."

  6. Result: $\Delta X_i = X^\mathrm{new}_i - X_i$ in ESPEC(J,·).

Design

The change rather than the new abundance is returned, as the comments explain, so that the interpolation back to the model grid adds less error. The first and last cells hold the masses of the convective core and envelope, whose changes MICRODIFF_ETM then applies to every point inside them.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: GRTOL (default 1.0D-8) is the numerical tolerance on the abundance changes from diffusion, and NITER_GS (10) the largest number of iterations of the solver.

1. (never occurred) With NITER_GS = 0, the implicit term is skipped and the warning prints old values.

(line 152↗; lines 178–196↗) With NITER_GS = 0 the loop never runs: The implicit (diffusive) term is not applied at all, and the warning "MICRODIFF FAILED TO CONVERGE" prints DXMAX and IMAX left from an earlier call (all locals are SAVEd). Only with NITER_GS = 0 (the default is 10).

A possible fix: reject NITER_GS < 1 where the namelist is read, or set DXMAX = 0.0D0 and IMAX = 0 before the loop so that the message is at least true.

38                  em_h: np.ndarray, er_h: np.ndarray, eden_h: np.ndarray, etem_h: np.ndarray, ehqpr_h: np.ndarray,
39                  edelr_h: np.ndarray, espec_h: np.ndarray, edxdr_h: np.ndarray, thea: float, thez: float,
40                  j: int) -> None:
41    """Diffuse species column j over one step on the equal grid: the
42    advective term by the two-step Lax-Wendroff scheme, then the diffusive
43    term implicitly (a tridiagonal solve, iterated up to NITER_GS times to
44    the tolerance GRTOL). Replaces espec[j, 1..npt] by the change in the
45    abundance.
46
47    Translates: microdiff_run.f#MICRODIFF_RUN (7be4133)
48
49    Arguments: the spacing dr; dt and stot in Bahcall and Loeb's units;
50    npt grid points; the grid variables em, er, eden, etem, ehqpr, edelr
51    and their midpoint versions (_h, npt - 1 of them); the abundances
52    espec[k, i] and espec_h[k, i] (k = 1..3); the hydrogen gradient edxdr,
53    edxdr_h (set by MICRODIFF_COD on the hydrogen call); the species'
54    weight thea, charge thez and column j. Arrays have index 0 unused.
55
56    Sets in place: espec[j, i] (the change), espec_h[j, i] (the half-step
57    abundances), edxdr, edxdr_h. Returns nothing.
58
59    """
60
61    # Dummy vectors for the diffusing element (Fortran lines 73–80).
62    for i in range(1, npt):
63        ed[i] = espec[j, i]
64        ed_h[i] = espec_h[j, i]
65    ed[npt] = espec[j, npt]
66
67    # Its original run (Fortran lines 82–91).
68    # Question (see the page): the comments name light elements.
69    for i in range(1, npt):
70        ed_orig[i] = ed[i]
71        ed_orig_h[i] = ed_h[i]
72    ed_orig[npt] = ed[npt]
73
74    # LDOLI: the Lax-Wendroff routines do not use the old metal vectors
75    # (Fortran lines 93–95).
76    ldoli = True
77
78    # Coefficients at the grid points (Fortran lines 97–102).
79    microdiff_cod(npt, espec, er, eden, etem, ehqpr, edelr, ecod1, ecod2,
80                  edxdr, thea, thez, j)
81
82    # Lax-Wendroff, first half step: the change at the midpoints, and the
83    # half-step abundances (Fortran lines 104–117).
84    lax_wendrof1(dt, ecod1, em, npt, stot, ed_h, ldoli)
85    for i in range(1, npt):
86        espec_h[j, i] = ed_orig_h[i] + ed_h[i]
87
88    # Coefficients at the npt - 1 midpoints, from the provisional solution
89    # (Fortran lines 119–125).
90    npt2 = npt - 1
91    microdiff_cod(npt2, espec_h, er_h, eden_h, etem_h, ehqpr_h, edelr_h,
92                  ecod1_h, ecod2_h, edxdr_h, thea, thez, j)
93
94    # Lax-Wendroff, full step at the points (Fortran lines 127–133).
95    lax_wendrof2(dt, ecod1_h, em_h, ed, npt, stot, ldoli)
96
97    # The implicit term: ed_p is the abundance at the start of the step,
98    # ed_prime the abundance without the second term (Fortran lines
99    # 135–143).
100    for i in range(1, npt + 1):
101        ed_p[i] = ed_orig[i]
102        ed_prime[i] = ed[i]
103
104    # ALPHA, the factor in front of the diffusion coefficients (Fortran
105    # lines 144–150).
106    fac = constants_mod.c4pi * dt / dr
107    alpha[1] = fac / em_h[1]
108    for i in range(2, npt):
109        alpha[i] = fac / (em_h[i] - em_h[i - 1])
110    alpha[npt] = fac / (stot - em_h[npt - 1])
111
112    # The iteration for the new run of abundances (Fortran lines 151–197).
113    # Question (see the page): every iteration solves the same system.
114    # Suspected bug (see the page): with NITER_GS = 0 the Fortran prints
115    # DXMAX and IMAX left from an earlier call; here they are 0.
116    ishort = iounits_mod.ishort
117    converged = False
118    dxmax = 0.0
119    imax = 0
120    for it in range(1, nmlpar_mod.niter_gs + 1):        # loop 100
121        # The change at the midpoints (Fortran lines 153–157).
122        # Suspected bug (see the page): midpoint i is taken between points
123        # i-1 and i here, between i and i+1 in MICRODIFF_MTE.
124        for i in range(2, npt + 1):
125            ed_h[i] = 0.5 * (ed[i] + ed[i - 1] - ed_p[i] - ed_p[i - 1])
126
127        # Store the current run in ed_p, and zero eqcod2x_h, used only by
128        # GRSETT (Fortran lines 158–166).
129        for i in range(1, npt):
130            ed_p[i] = ed[i]
131            eqcod2x_h[i] = 0.0
132        ed_p[npt] = ed[npt]
133
134        # The tridiagonal system and its solution (Fortran lines 167–174).
135        get_imp_diffco(alpha, ecod2_h, ed_h, eqcod2x_h, a, b, c, npt)
136        tridiag_gs(a, b, c, ed_prime, npt, ed)
137
138        # The convergence test (Fortran lines 176–190).
139        # Suspected bug (see the page): the largest signed change, not
140        # |change|.
141        dxmax = 0.0
142        imax = 0
143        for i in range(1, npt + 1):
144            dx = ed[i] - ed_p[i]
145            if dx > dxmax:
146                dxmax = dx
147                imax = i
148        iounits_mod.write(ishort, f" ITERATION {it:3d} DXMAX "
149                          f"{dxmax:10.2E} IMAX {imax:4d}")     # format 90
150        if dxmax < nmlpar_mod.grtol:
151            converged = True
152            break                                           # GOTO 120
153
154    # Not converged (Fortran lines 192–196).
155    if not converged:
156        text = (f" MICRODIFF FAILED TO CONVERGE TO WITHIN "
157                f"{nmlpar_mod.grtol:9.3E} IN {nmlpar_mod.niter_gs:3d}"
158                f"ITERATIONS\n LAST ITERATION CHANGE IN D {dxmax:9.3E}"
159                f" IN EQUALLY SPACED SHELL {imax:5d}")     # format 110
160        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.iowr, text)
161        iounits_mod.write(ishort, text)
162
163    # Label 120: the change in the diffused element (Fortran lines
164    # 199–202).
165    for i in range(1, npt + 1):
166        espec[j, i] = ed[i] - ed_orig[i]
