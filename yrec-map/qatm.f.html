Purpose

The right-hand side of the atmosphere integration: with $\log\tau$ as the independent variable and $\log P$ as the dependent one, it returns $d\log P/d\log\tau$ from a $T$–$\tau$ relation. Its comment: "QATM calculates the derivative d(P)/d(tau), using the Eddington approximation for a T-tau relation."

Question 1 (lines 36–39↗; lines 77–83↗). The comment. It says the routine computes $dP/d\tau$ "using the Eddington approximation"; it computes $d\log P/d\log\tau$ with any of three relations (KTTAU = 0, 1, 2). Should the comment be corrected?

How it fits

  • Inputs: X0 $=\log\tau$, Y(1) $=\log P$; $\log g$ (GL), the distortion factor FPL, TEFFL, $X$, $Z$; the choice of relation KTTAU from NMLPAR_MOD and HRAS from ATMOS_MOD; LPUMOD (pulsation output) from PULSE1_MOD.
  • Who calls it: ENVINT, directly and as the EXTERNAL derivative routine passed to BSSTEP (and so to MMID); this is why it keeps arguments it does not use.
  • What it calls: EQSTAT, GETOPAC, and the function HRA (the Harvard–Smithsonian reference atmosphere).
  • Outputs: DYDX(1); the counter KATM; the point's $\log P$, $\log T$ (and, when saving or for pulsation output, $\log\rho$, $\kappa$, ionization fractions) in ATMPRT_MOD; the thermodynamic quantities for pulsation output in PULSE2_MOD.

The method

The temperature from the $T$–$\tau$ relation:

  • Eddington (KTTAU = 0): $T^4 = \tfrac34\,T_\mathrm{eff}^4\,(\tau + \tfrac23)$, coded as $\log T = \log T_\mathrm{eff} - 0.031235 + \tfrac14\log(\tau + \tfrac23)$ (with $\tfrac14\log\tfrac34 = -0.031235$);
  • Krishna Swamy (1966, ApJ 145, 176; KTTAU = 1): the same with $q(\tau) = 1.39 - 0.815\,e^{-2.54\tau} - 0.025\,e^{-30\tau}$ in place of $\tfrac23$;
  • Harvard–Smithsonian (KTTAU = 2): $\log T = \log T_\mathrm{eff} + \mathrm{HRA}(\tau) - \mathrm{HRA}(\tfrac23)$ (HRAS $=\mathrm{HRA}(\tfrac23)$, set once by SETUPS).

For the tabulated atmospheres (KTTAU = 3, 4, 5) no branch sets TL (lines 77–83↗), but QATM is not called with them: ENVINT takes their surface pressure from SURFP, KCSURFP, or ALSURFP and goes past the atmosphere integration, and after a failed Allard lookup it sets KTTAU = 0 before integrating (lines 228–257↗; settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

Then $\rho$ and $\kappa$ at $(P, T)$, and hydrostatic equilibrium in the form $dP/d\tau = g/\kappa$: $$\frac{d\log P}{d\log\tau} = \frac{g\,\tau}{\kappa\,P},\qquad g = 10^{\log g}\,f_P .$$ Here $P$ is the total pressure, gas plus radiation (EQSTAT is called with it, and returns $\rho$ from $P - aT^4/3$), so the radiative force enters through the radiation pressure $aT^4/3$ set by the $T$–$\tau$ relation, and $g$ is not reduced by a radiative acceleration.
