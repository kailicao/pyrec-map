Explanation

Purpose. EQSTAT is the equation of state that the rest of YREC calls. Its header says it was added ("LLP 10-22-06") as a "new front end" so that the derivatives of the equation of state can be computed numerically, by calling the older routine, now EQSTAT2, at neighbouring points. All the physics is in EQSTAT2.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: ten routines across the code: COEFFT, ENVINT, HSUBP, MASSLOSS, PHYSIC, QATM, QENV, SCONVEC, STARIN and WRTOUT.
  • Inputs: TL, PL ($\log_{10}$ of $T$ in K and $P$ in dyn cm$^{-2}$), X, Z, and the flags LDERIV (second derivatives wanted) and LATMO (atmosphere). DL serves as a starting guess.
  • What it calls: only EQSTAT2.
  • Outputs: those of EQSTAT2: T, P, D, DL, BETA, FXION, RMU, AMU, EMU, ETA, QDT $=\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T|_P$, QDP $=\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P|_T$, QCP ($c_P$), DELA ($\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$), and the derivatives QDTT, QDTP, QAT, QAP, QCPT, QCPP.
  • Modules: it reads only CLN ($\ln 10$, from CONSTANTS_MOD) and LNumDeriv (from NMLPAR_MOD, set in the PHYSICS namelist read by PARMIN).

The method. If LDERIV and LNumDeriv are both set, EQSTAT2 is called four times without derivatives, at $\log T \pm h_T$ and $\log P \pm h_P$, with $h_T = 3\times10^{-4}$ and $h_P = 1.5\times10^{-3}$ (the "approximate table intervals", 0.03 and 0.15, scaled down by 100: "empirically, scaling the intervals down by 100 seemed to work best"). Central differences give the second derivatives, for example $$\frac{\partial Q_{\rho T}}{\partial\ln T} = \frac{Q_{\rho T}^{+} - Q_{\rho T}^{-}}{2h_T\ln 10},\qquad \frac{\partial\log c_P}{\partial\log T} = \frac{\log c_P^{+} - \log c_P^{-}}{2h_T},\qquad \frac{\partial\log\nabla_\mathrm{ad}}{\partial\log T} = \frac{\log\nabla_\mathrm{ad}^{+} - \log\nabla_\mathrm{ad}^{-}}{2h_T},$$ and the same in $\log P$. A last call at the point itself, without derivatives, gives the values; its derivative outputs go to dummy variables so they cannot overwrite the numerical ones. Otherwise EQSTAT2 is called once, with analytic derivatives if LDERIV is set.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; the unused COMMON blocks copied from EQSTAT2 are gone, which settles the former question 3.

Questions for the experts

  1. A typo that leaves a variable undefined. The line KSSAHAx = KSAHA assigns a misspelled variable (declared explicitly as KSSAHAX at 09c3b9b, so IMPLICIT NONE does not flag it); the calls then pass KSAHAx, which is never set. KSAHAx is a saved local that only the four derivative calls increment and nothing reads, so no result changes; the caller's counter KSAHA just misses those iterations. Should it read KSAHAx = KSAHA?
  2. The step size. The steps are 1/100 of an assumed table spacing of 0.15 in $\log P$ and 0.03 in $\log T$. EQSTAT2's own comment gives the SCV tables' spacing as 0.20 and 0.08. Was the step tested for each table EQSTAT2 can use?
