Purpose

MIX updates the composition of a model over one time step: nuclear burning, mixing of convection zones, semiconvection, gravitational settling and deuterium burning. Its header gives the outline: (1) find the convection zones and the mixed regions with overshoot; (2) compute the burning rates for the given composition, density and temperature; (3) solve implicitly for the abundances in radiative regions; (4) solve for burning in each convection zone; (5) semiconvection if desired; (6) microscopic diffusion if desired. The header also says that MIX is called "prior to rezoning and on the third level of iteration in the Henyey portion." So it runs as a predictor and a corrector: the main program saves the start-of-step composition in HCOMPP and calls MIX with ITLVL = 1 on the old structure, before rezoning, to predict the composition (with the explicit equilibrium burning of step 4); then, in every Henyey iteration from level 3 on, CRRECT restores HCOMPP and calls MIX again with the current $T$ and $\rho$. The level-1 result is only a starting guess.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the model (HD, HL, HP, HR, HS = $\log m$, HS1 = $m$, HS2 = shell masses, HSTOT, HT, LC, M), the composition HCOMP(15,M), the step DELTS ($\mathrm{s}$), the iteration level ITLVL, TEFFL and the mixing length CMIXL, passed on to SCONVEC. Parameters come from NMLPAR_MOD: DPENV, LSEMIC, the settling switches and limits (LDIFY, XMIN, YMIN, DT_GS, LNEWDIF), TCUT, the accretion settings (LMDOT, DMDT0, COMPACC) and LEXCOM, all set by PARMIN.
  • Who calls it: CRRECT and the main program in main.f.
  • What it calls: CONVEC (zones), RATES and DEUTRATE (rates), KEMCOM (implicit burning), EQBURN (explicit burning at ITLVL = 1), SCONVEC (semiconvection), MICRODIFF or GRSETT (settling), and DBURN (deuterium).
  • Outputs: HCOMP in place; the time step in years DDAGE; the zone edges JCORE, JENV, MXZONE, MXZON0. The deuterium rate goes to DRATE in DEUTER_MOD. The mixed zones are printed to ISHORT.
  • Modules: besides those above, CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN, CGL, CSECYR), IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT) and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

  1. Deep mixing. If DPENV < 1 (and ITLVL > 1), every shell with $m \le \mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast$ is flagged convective in a copy of the flags (LCC) before CONVEC finds the zones; a commented-out variant used a fraction of the peak luminosity instead (MHP 1/95). Otherwise LCC = LC.

    DPENV mixes from the center. The first comment says "DPENV MIXES THE STAR FROM THE SURFACE TO MASS FRACTION DPENV, E.G. DPENV = 0.7 MEANS THE OUTER .3 OF THE STAR IS MIXED" (lines 127–128↗), but the code flags the shells inside $\mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast$ (lines 129–141↗), as the next comment ("MIX FROM CENTER TO A FIXED MASS FRACTION," line 130↗) and the documentation ("Full mixing from center to fractional mass depth," docs/source/namelist_physics.md, line 296) say, so the first comment is stale. 2. Rates. For every shell hotter than TCUT(1), RATES gives the rates HR1–HR13, HF1, HF2, and DEUTRATE the deuterium rate if the surface (or accreted) deuterium exceeds $10^{-11}$. Cooler shells get zero rates. 3. Implicit burning. KEMCOM solves shell by shell in radiative zones (down to the TCUT(1) cutoff) and zone by zone in convective ones, which also homogenizes them. 4. Explicit burning (only ITLVL = 1): EQBURN gives hydrogen and helium burning rates assuming equilibrium $^3\mathrm{He}$ and CN abundances, which overwrite $X$, $Z$, $^{12}\mathrm{C}$ and $^{16}\mathrm{O}$: $X \to X + \dot X\,\Delta t$, $Z \to Z - \dot Y\,\Delta t$, with $\Delta t$ = DDAGE$\times10^{-9}$ ($\mathrm{Gyr}$). Here $\dot Y < 0$ (DYDT = -4.0D0*(R412 + 3.0D0*R3A), from the $^{12}\mathrm{C} + \alpha$ and triple-$\alpha$ rates), so $Z$ rises: the helium burnt becomes carbon and oxygen, counted in $Z$. EQBURN starts from the start-of-step composition HCOMPP, not from KEMCOM's result, and overwrites only those four (and only where its rates are nonzero); what KEMCOM gave for $^3\mathrm{He}$, the other CNO isotopes ($^{13}\mathrm{C}$, $^{14}\mathrm{N}$, $^{15}\mathrm{N}$, $^{17}\mathrm{O}$, $^{18}\mathrm{O}$) and the light elements is kept.

    Question 1 (lines 279–304↗). The equilibrium predictor. At ITLVL = 1 the burning of $X$, $Z$, $^{12}\mathrm{C}$ and $^{16}\mathrm{O}$ comes from EQBURN, which assumes equilibrium $^3\mathrm{He}$ and CN abundances, although the network (KEMCOM) has just been run. Before and after the main sequence $^3\mathrm{He}$ and CN can be far from equilibrium, so the predictor may start the Henyey iteration further from the answer. Is the EQBURN predictor kept for speed, and is it known to cost iterations where $^3\mathrm{He}$ or CN are out of equilibrium? 5. Semiconvection (LSEMIC, ITLVL > 1): SCONVEC. Then, if LSEMIC or ITLVL = 1, each convection zone is mixed to its mass-weighted mean, $\bar X_k = \sum_j X_{k,j}\,\Delta m_j / \sum_j \Delta m_j$. 6. Settling (LDIFY): with $d\ln P/dr$ from hydrostatic equilibrium, $$\frac{d\ln P}{dr} = -\frac{G\,m\,\rho}{r^2\,P}$$ (HQPR, from the logs and $\log G$ = CGL), and the helium settling time at the outer boundary (the first shell with $Y >$ YMIN, searching inward from JENV, the base of the surface convection zone), from Michaud et al. (1984) as the comment cites, $$\tau = 4.348\times10^{21}\ \mathrm{yr}\;\frac{\Delta M/M_\ast}{T^{3/2}},$$ where $\Delta M/M_\ast$ is the mass fraction above that shell, the step is split into NSTEP substeps of at most DT_GS$\times\tau$ (NSTEP = INT(DELTS/DTMAX), plus one if MOD(DELTS,DTMAX) is not zero or NSTEP is 0), each running MICRODIFF (if LNEWDIF) or GRSETT. Both diffuse only between the convective core (if any) and the base of the surface convection zone, and apply the change found at that base to every shell of the zone (EQUAL_TO_MODEL, MICRODIFF_ETM): the zone stays homogeneous, and the flux through its base sets the surface $Y$ and $Z$.

    Question 2 (lines 459–461↗; rotmix.f, lines 228–230↗). Convection flags for settling. MIX passes LCC (the flags before overshoot, or the deep-mixing flags) to MICRODIFF, while ROTMIX passes flags built from the mixed zones, overshoot included. Should the two agree?

    Question 3 (lines 434–438↗). YMIN. The documentation says diffusion is disabled "once surface helium Y < YMIN"; here YMIN only locates the outer boundary used for the settling time (the first shell inward from the convection zone's base with $Y >$ YMIN), and settling is skipped only when $Y <$ YMIN in every shell below that base. Which is intended?

    Question 4 (lines 444–454↗). The settling substep bound. The substeps are bounded by DT_GS times the helium settling time at the base of the surface convection zone only, $\tau \propto (\Delta M/M_\ast)/T^{3/2}$. Metals and light elements, or deeper layers with steep gradients (near a burning core), may need shorter substeps, and the explicit Lax–Wendroff step of GRSETT on its equally spaced grid has its own stability (Courant) limit. Is DT_GS$\times\tau$ known to satisfy that limit? 7. Clean-up. Every abundance is clipped to $[0,1]$, $Z \le 1 - X - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$, and $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$. 8. Deep mixing again: CONVEC is called with the true flags LC to reset JCORE and JENV. 9. Deuterium (LEXCOM): DBURN, shell by shell in radiative zones and per convection zone.

    DBURN's rate is per $\mathrm{Gyr}$, like DT. The explicit and deuterium burning use DT = DDAGE*1.0D-9, in $\mathrm{Gyr}$ (line 293↗; lines 485–503↗), which matches EQBURN's rates, "per gigayear per amu" (KEMCOM's comment, kemcom.f, lines 85–88↗). DBURN's rate DRATE = X*RDEUT*C21 (deutrate.f, line 81↗) states no units, but RDEUT is per second, with a factor $N_A$/AMDEUT (deutrate.f, line 62↗), and C21 = 5.240358D-8 (deutrate.f, line 30↗) is $3.1558 \times 10^{16}\,\mathrm{s/Gyr}$ divided by $N_A$, so DRATE is per $\mathrm{Gyr}$ per $\mathrm{amu}$ too.

Design

MIX switches settling off for the rest of the run (it sets LDIFY = .FALSE. in NMLPAR_MOD) once the central hydrogen falls below XMIN. A model without a surface convection zone gets only a warning ("diffusion not meaningful") and no settling.

Question 5 (lines 412–416↗). Settling switched off for good. LDIFY = .FALSE. in NMLPAR_MOD when $X_c <$ XMIN stops all settling, including that of metals and light elements, for the rest of the run, and MICRODIFF is reached only when LDIFY is true. Is metal or lithium settling without helium settling meant to be possible?

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: LSEMIC turns on semiconvection; DPENV (experimental) mixes fully "from center to fractional mass depth"; for settling, DT_GS (default 0.1) splits the step into several when the global step exceeds DT_GS times the settling time at the base of the convection zone, XMIN (1.0D-3) turns diffusion off once the central $X$ is below it, and YMIN (1.0D-3), in the documentation's words, "once surface helium Y < YMIN"; LNEWDIF (legacy) turns on the updated settling routines.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. CMIXL is a new last argument (formerly /CONST3/), passed on to SCONVEC; COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; the unused /OLDMOD/, /COMP/, /THEAGE/, /NEWENG/ (and /CTLIM/, whose TCUT now comes from NMLPAR_MOD) are gone, which settles the former question 6.

Changed since a8a5950. The burning rates are no longer copied into HCOMPM, which nothing had read since ROTMIX computes its own rates, and BURN_MOD (removed since a8a5950) is no longer used; the settling substeps are counted with MOD(DELTS,DTMAX) instead of the swapped MOD(DTMAX,DELTS), and a count of 0 is raised to 1 (settles former question 1).

100def mix_zones_mean(hcomp: np.ndarray, hs2: np.ndarray, mxzone: np.ndarray, nzone: int, nspec: int,
101                   xsum: np.ndarray) -> None:
102    """Mix each convection zone (shells I1..I2) to its mass-weighted mean (DO 100, Fortran lines 369–397).
103
104    Parameters
105    ----------
106    hcomp : np.ndarray, shape: `(16, JSON + 1)`, dtype: ``float64``
107        The abundances, species 1..nspec set in place over each zone.
108    hs2 : np.ndarray, shape: `(JSON + 1,)`, dtype: ``float64``
109        The shell masses.
110    mxzone : np.ndarray, shape: `(13, 3)`, dtype: ``int``
111        The zones' first and last shells.
112    nzone, nspec : int
113        The numbers of zones and of species.
114    xsum : np.ndarray, shape: `(16,)`, dtype: ``float64``
115        Work array (mix's SAVE'd XSUM), set in place.
116
117    """
118    for j in range(1, nzone + 1):  # DO 100
119        i1 = mxzone[j, 1]
120        i2 = mxzone[j, 2]
121        if i1 != 1 and i1 >= i2:
122            continue
123        wtsum = 0.0
124        for i in range(1, nspec + 1):
125            xsum[i] = 0.0
126        for jj in range(i1, i2 + 1):
127            wtsum = wtsum + hs2[jj]
128            for i in range(1, nspec + 1):
129                xsum[i] = xsum[i] + hcomp[i, jj] * hs2[jj]
130        for i in range(1, nspec + 1):
131            xsum[i] = xsum[i] / wtsum
132        for jj in range(i1, i2 + 1):
133            for i in range(1, nspec + 1):
134                hcomp[i, jj] = xsum[i]

267def _settle(delts: float, hcomp: np.ndarray, hd: np.ndarray, hp: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hs: np.ndarray,
268            hs1: np.ndarray, hstot: float, ht: np.ndarray, lc: np.ndarray, m: int, jenv: int) -> None:
269    # The IF(LDIFY) block, labels 130-170: gravitational settling in
270    # NSTEP substeps limited by the helium settling time at the outer
271    # boundary. Reads the module-level LCC.
272    c = constants_mod
273    nml = nmlpar_mod
274
275    # Settling off below XMIN (Fortran lines 413–416).
276    # Question (see the page): settling stops for good below XMIN.
277    if hcomp[1, 1] < nml.xmin:
278        nml.ldify = False
279        return  # GOTO 170
280
281    # Fortran lines 417–420. DO 130 is vectorized: EXP is libmvec's vector
282    # exp but on an odd last iteration (Fortran line 419).
283    stot = math.exp(c.cln * hstot)
284    for i in range(1, m + 1):  # DO 130: d ln P/dr = -G m rho / (r^2 P)
285        vexp = libmvec.exp if libmvec.vector_loop(1, m, i) else math.exp
286        hqpr[i] = -vexp(c.cln * (hd[i] + c.cgl + hs[i] - 2.0 * hr[i]
287                                 - hp[i]))
288
289    # The outer boundary: the surface convection zone, or the first point
290    # (inward from JENV) where Y exceeds YMIN (Fortran lines 421–438).
291    # Question (see the page): YMIN as the documentation describes it.
292    if not lc[m]:
293        # A warning only (DBG 2/92).
294        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
295                          " NO SURFACE CZ - DIFFUSION NOT MEANINGFUL")
296        return
297    for j in range(jenv, 0, -1):  # DO 140
298        if hcomp[2, j] > nml.ymin:
299            break  # GOTO 150
300    else:
301        return  # Y < YMIN for the whole star
302
303    # FM: the mass fraction above the outer point; TSCALE: the helium
304    # settling time there (Michaud et al. 1984) (Fortran lines 440–454).
305    # Question (see the page): the substep bound.
306    fm = (stot - hs1[j]) / stot
307    tscale = 4.348e21 * c.csecyr * fm / math.exp(c.cln * 1.5 * ht[j])
308    dtmax = nml.dt_gs * tscale
309    nstep = int(delts / dtmax)
310    if math.fmod(delts, dtmax) != 0.0 or nstep == 0:
311        nstep = nstep + 1
312    dt = delts / float(nstep)
313
314    # The substeps (Fortran lines 455–463).
315    # Question (see the page): LCC, not the mixed-zone flags.
316    for ns in range(1, nstep + 1):  # DO 160
317        if nml.lnewdif:
318            microdiff(dt, hcomp, hqpr, hr, hd, hs1, ht, lcc, m, stot)
319        else:
320            grsett(dt, hcomp, hqpr, hr, hd, hs1, ht, lcc, m, stot)
