Explanation

Purpose. MIX updates the composition of a model over one time step: nuclear burning, mixing of convection zones, semiconvection, gravitational settling and deuterium burning. Its header gives the outline: (1) find the convection zones and the mixed regions with overshoot; (2) compute the burning rates for the given composition, density and temperature; (3) solve implicitly for the abundances in radiative regions; (4) solve for burning in each convection zone; (5) semiconvection if desired; (6) microscopic diffusion if desired. The header also says that MIX is called "prior to rezoning and on the third level of iteration in the Henyey portion". So it runs as a predictor and a corrector: the main program saves the start-of-step composition in HCOMPP and calls MIX with ITLVL = 1 on the old structure, before rezoning, to predict the composition (with the explicit equilibrium burning of step 4); then, in every Henyey iteration from level 3 on, CRRECT restores HCOMPP and calls MIX again with the current $T$ and $\rho$. The level-1 result is only a starting guess.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the model (HD, HL, HP, HR, HS = $\log m$, HS1 = $m$, HS2 = shell masses, HSTOT, HT, LC, M), the composition HCOMP(15,M), the step DELTS (s), the iteration level ITLVL, TEFFL and the mixing length CMIXL, passed on to SCONVEC. Parameters come from NMLPAR_MOD: DPENV, LSEMIC, the settling switches and limits (LDIFY, XMIN, YMIN, DT_GS, LNEWDIF), TCUT, the accretion settings (LMDOT, DMDT0, COMPACC) and LEXCOM, all set by PARMIN.
  • Who calls it: CRRECT and the main program in main.f.
  • What it calls: CONVEC (zones), RATES and DEUTRATE (rates), KEMCOM (implicit burning), EQBURN (explicit burning at ITLVL = 1), SCONVEC (semiconvection), MICRODIFF or GRSETT (settling), and DBURN (deuterium).
  • Outputs: HCOMP in place; the time step in years DDAGE; the zone edges JCORE, JENV, MXZONE, MXZON0. The burning rates go to HCOMPM in BURN_MOD, which HPOINT and ROTMIX read. The deuterium rate goes to DRATE in DEUTER_MOD. The mixed zones are printed to ISHORT.
  • Modules: besides those above, CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN, CGL, CSECYR), IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT) and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method.

  1. Deep mixing. If DPENV < 1 (and ITLVL > 1), every shell with $m \le \mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast$ is flagged convective in a copy of the flags (LCC) before CONVEC finds the zones; a commented-out variant used a fraction of the peak luminosity instead (MHP 1/95). Otherwise LCC = LC.
  2. Rates. For every shell hotter than TCUT(1), RATES gives the rates HR1–HR13, HF1, HF2, and DEUTRATE the deuterium rate if the surface (or accreted) deuterium exceeds $10^{-11}$. Cooler shells get zero rates.
  3. Implicit burning. KEMCOM solves shell by shell in radiative zones (down to the TCUT(1) cutoff) and zone by zone in convective ones, which also homogenizes them.
  4. Explicit burning (only ITLVL = 1): EQBURN gives hydrogen and helium burning rates assuming equilibrium ³He and CN abundances, which overwrite $X$, $Z$, ¹²C and ¹⁶O: $X \to X + \dot X\,\Delta t$, $Z \to Z - \dot Y\,\Delta t$, with $\Delta t$ = DDAGE$\times10^{-9}$ (Gyr). Here $\dot Y < 0$ (DYDT = -4.0D0*(R412 + 3.0D0*R3A), from the ¹²C + α and triple-α rates), so $Z$ rises: the helium burnt becomes carbon and oxygen, counted in $Z$. EQBURN starts from the start-of-step composition HCOMPP, not from KEMCOM's result, and overwrites only those four (and only where its rates are nonzero); what KEMCOM gave for ³He, the other CNO isotopes (¹³C, ¹⁴N, ¹⁵N, ¹⁷O, ¹⁸O) and the light elements is kept.
  5. Semiconvection (LSEMIC, ITLVL > 1): SCONVEC. Then, if LSEMIC or ITLVL = 1, each convection zone is mixed to its mass-weighted mean, $\bar X_k = \sum_j X_{k,j}\,\Delta m_j / \sum_j \Delta m_j$.

  6. Settling (LDIFY): with $d\ln P/dr$ from hydrostatic equilibrium, $$\frac{d\ln P}{dr} = -\frac{G\,m\,\rho}{r^2\,P}$$ (HQPR, from the logs and $\log G$ = CGL), and the helium settling time at the outer boundary (the first shell with $Y >$ YMIN, searching inward from JENV, the base of the surface convection zone), from Michaud et al. (1984) as the comment cites, $$\tau = 4.348\times10^{21}\ \mathrm{yr}\;\frac{\Delta M/M_\ast}{T^{3/2}},$$ where $\Delta M/M_\ast$ is the mass fraction above that shell, the step is split into NSTEP substeps of at most DT_GS$\times\tau$, each running MICRODIFF (if LNEWDIF) or GRSETT. Both diffuse only between the convective core (if any) and the base of the surface convection zone, and apply the change found at that base to every shell of the zone (EQUAL_TO_MODEL, MICRODIFF_ETM): the zone stays homogeneous, and the flux through its base sets the surface $Y$ and $Z$.

  7. Clean-up. Every abundance is clipped to $[0,1]$, $Z \le 1 - X - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$, and $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}$.

  8. Deep mixing again: CONVEC is called with the true flags LC to reset JCORE and JENV.
  9. Deuterium (LEXCOM): DBURN, shell by shell in radiative zones and per convection zone.

Design. MIX switches settling off for the rest of the run (it sets LDIFY = .FALSE. in NMLPAR_MOD) once the central hydrogen falls below XMIN. A model without a surface convection zone gets only a warning ("diffusion not meaningful") and no settling.

Questions for the experts

  1. The number of settling substeps. NSTEP = INT(DELTS/DTMAX), then IF(MOD(DTMAX,DELTS).NE.0) NSTEP=NSTEP+1. The arguments of MOD look swapped (the ceiling would test MOD(DELTS,DTMAX)), and when DTMAX is an exact multiple (two or more) of DELTS, NSTEP stays 0 and DT = DELTS/0. ROTMIX has the same line with .OR.NSTEP.EQ.0 added. Should MIX get the same fix?
  2. DPENV: centre or surface? The comment says "DPENV mixes the star from the surface to mass fraction DPENV, e.g. DPENV = 0.7 means the outer .3 of the star is mixed", but the code (and the next comment, "mix from center to a fixed mass fraction") flags the shells inside $\mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast$. Which is meant?
  3. Settling switched off for good. LDIFY = .FALSE. in NMLPAR_MOD when $X_c <$ XMIN stops all settling, including that of metals and light elements, for the rest of the run, and MICRODIFF is reached only when LDIFY is true. Is metal or lithium settling without helium settling meant to be possible?
  4. Convection flags for settling. MIX passes LCC (the flags before overshoot, or the deep-mixing flags) to MICRODIFF, while ROTMIX passes flags built from the mixed zones, overshoot included. Should the two agree?
  5. Units of DT in the burning steps. The explicit and deuterium burning use DT = DDAGE*1.0D-9 (Gyr). Are EQBURN's rates and DBURN per Gyr? (The units are not stated here.)
  6. YMIN. The documentation says diffusion is disabled "once surface helium Y < YMIN"; here YMIN only locates the outer boundary used for the settling time (the first shell inward from the convection zone's base with $Y >$ YMIN), and settling is skipped only when $Y <$ YMIN in every shell below that base. Which is intended?
  7. The equilibrium predictor. At ITLVL = 1 the burning of $X$, $Z$, ¹²C and ¹⁶O comes from EQBURN, which assumes equilibrium ³He and CN abundances, although the network (KEMCOM) has just been run. Before and after the main sequence ³He and CN can be far from equilibrium, so the predictor may start the Henyey iteration further from the answer. Is the EQBURN predictor kept for speed, and is it known to cost iterations where ³He or CN are out of equilibrium?
  8. The settling substep bound. The substeps are bounded by DT_GS times the helium settling time at the base of the surface convection zone only, $\tau \propto (\Delta M/M_\ast)/T^{3/2}$. Metals and light elements, or deeper layers with steep gradients (near a burning core), may need shorter substeps, and the explicit Lax–Wendroff step of GRSETT on its equally spaced grid has its own stability (Courant) limit. Is DT_GS$\times\tau$ known to satisfy that limit?
