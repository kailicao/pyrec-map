Purpose

TEMP_MOD holds physics at each shell for rotational mixing: specific heat, mean molecular weight, a density derivative, and the radiative diffusivity and viscosity. Shared by 5 routines. It replaces COMMON/TEMP/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • CP, SQDT (JSON each): $c_P$ and $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ (QCP, QDT), and HAMU(JSON): the mean molecular weight of a fully ionized gas, set by PHYSIC. CP is read by VISCOS, HAMU by GETW.
  • VISC(JSON): the viscosity, radiative plus molecular, and THDIF(JSON): the radiative diffusivity $16\sigma T^3/(3\kappa\rho^2 c_P)$, set by VISCOS.
  • All five are read by HPOINT and MIDMOD, HPOINT saves them as the values at the start of the step, and MIDMOD interpolates between those and the current ones.

A blanket USE TEMP_MOD in each routine.
