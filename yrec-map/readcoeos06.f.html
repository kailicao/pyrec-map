Purpose

Reads the OPAL 2006 equation-of-state tables (five hydrogen abundances) into memory. It follows READCOEOS01 (removed since rp_1, with the OPAL 2001 equation of state) closely; see also READCO (removed since rp_1, with the OPAL 1995 equation of state).

How it fits

  • Who calls it: SETEOS06, once at set-up (from SETUPS).
  • Input: the file on unit IOPALE (from IOUNITS_MOD), opened by PARMIN; closed here. The close (2) before the reading (line 95↗), as in READCOEOS01, closes a unit that no part of YREC opens (no unit number in IOUNITS_MOD or PARMIN is 2), so it does nothing (unused?).
  • Outputs, through modules: AEOS06_MOD (the tables XZ(5, 10, 197, 169), grids, inverse spacings; shared with ESAC06, RHOOFP06, T6RINTEOS06, EQBOUND06; its MIDX, formerly M, is the loop index over the compositions here) and EOS06MISC_MOD (ZZ read here; INDEX, NTA used; EOS(1..10) set to 1 on the first call). The per-composition headers and the extra columns AMU_M, ALOGNE, formerly /EEEEOS06/, are saved local arrays; AMU_M and ALOGNE are read nowhere (unused?), nor is the array ALOGR (line 64↗, unused?).
  • Other modules: EEEOS06_MOD (XX, for the inverse spacings DFSX); ISHORT from IOUNITS_MOD.

The method

As in READCOEOS01, with 197 temperatures, and rows that hold $T_6$, a mean molecular weight (AMU_M), $\log n_e$ (ALOGNE, by its name) and nine quantities. Unlike the 2001 version, it does not set up the ramp's edge RMX; EQBOUND06 uses NRA in /BEOS06/ (now in EOS06MISC_MOD) instead.

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

30def _fields(line: str, widths: list, start: int = 0, ints: tuple = ()) -> list:
31    """Split a fixed-format record into numbers (a blank field reads as 0,
32    as in Fortran); the fields numbered in ints are integers.
33
34    """
35
36    out, pos = [], start
37    for n, w in enumerate(widths):
38        text = line[pos:pos + w].strip()
39        if n in ints:
40            out.append(int(text) if text else 0)
41        else:
42            out.append(float(text.replace('D', 'E')) if text else 0.0)
43        pos += w
44    return out
