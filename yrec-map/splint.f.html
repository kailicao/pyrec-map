Purpose

SPLINT evaluates a cubic spline at one point $x$, from the table $(x_i, y_i)$ and the second derivatives $y''_i$ computed beforehand: by SPLINJ, or, for the opacity routine GTPURZ, by CSPLINE (computed there, or tabulated by ZSULAOL). The comments say it is taken from Numerical Recipes (Press et al., p. 89) and was changed from REAL*4, for the opacities, to REAL*8 (MHP, 10/02).

Question 1 (lines 7–10↗). The comment's reference. The header's "SPLINE ABOVE" is Numerical Recipes' text: its spline (with end slopes) is CSPLINE here, while spline.f is an unrelated quadratic spline. A comment to update?

How it fits

  • Who calls it: BOOLE, CALCAD, and the low-temperature opacity routine GTPURZ (GTLAOL and GTLAOL2, and the table routine SULAOL, removed since rp_1, were the others).
  • Outputs: the value Y, and the bracketing indices KLO, KHI.
  • Modules: IOUNITS_MOD, for the unit ISHORT of its error message.

The method

A bisection finds $x_\mathrm{lo} \le x < x_\mathrm{hi}$ (KLO, KHI). With $h = x_\mathrm{hi} - x_\mathrm{lo}$, $A = (x_\mathrm{hi} - x)/h$ and $B = 1 - A$:

$$y = A\,y_\mathrm{lo} + B\,y_\mathrm{hi} + \frac{h^2}{6}\left[(A^3 - A)\,y''_\mathrm{lo} + (B^3 - B)\,y''_\mathrm{hi}\right].$$

If two abscissas coincide ($h = 0$) it writes an error, with the abscissa and $x$, to the terminal and to ISHORT, and stops the run (STOP 1). Outside the table it extrapolates the end interval's cubic, without a warning.

Design

KLO and KHI are arguments but are reset on entry, so a caller's previous bracket is not used as a starting guess.
