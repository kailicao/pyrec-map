Explanation

Purpose. HPOINT rezones the model: at the start of each step it chooses a new set of mass points so that the jumps between neighbours in mass, luminosity, pressure, $X$ and $Z$ stay below user limits, keeps real discontinuities (convection-zone edges, composition jumps) as fixed points, and interpolates every model variable to the new points. It also adds or removes outer points when the outermost point drifts out of the allowed envelope temperature range, and recomputes the shell masses and, for rotating models, the rotation quantities.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: the main program, main.f, once per evolutionary step.
  • Inputs (arguments, changed in place): the model (HS = $\log m$, HP, HT, HR, HL, HD, HCOMP, LC, M), HSTOT ($\log M_*$), the envelope-fitting values PS, RS, ESTORE, ISTORE, the indices JCORE, JENV (edges of the convective core and envelope), JXBEG (H-shell base), LSHELL, the model number MODEL, the rotation arrays (OMEGA, ETA2, R0, HI, HJM, HKEROT, FP, FT, HG, QIW), the totals SJTOT, SKEROT, TEFFL, and CMIXL (the run's mixing-length parameter, which main.f sets from CMIXLA(NK); passed on to PHYSIC).
  • Outputs: the rezoned model; HS1 (mass at each point) and HS2 (mass of each shell); LRESET (true when outer points were added or removed); and in modules, the previous model on the new grid for the next step.
  • Calls: SPLINC and SPLINTD2 (splines to place the points), OSPLIN (an "oscillatory" spline for interpolating the variables); for rotation OVROT, MOMI, GETROT, FPFT; PHYSIC (the physics at the new points); SURFOPAC (a new surface opacity table when surface $X$ changed).
  • Modules, the main ones: NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist parameters: HPTTOL(1..12), the zoning limits, from PARMIN; TENV0, TENV1; LEXCOM, whether light elements 12–15 are followed; LROT; the overshoot settings LOVSTE, ALPHAE; the debug output LDEBUG, NPOINT); OLDMOD_MOD (after the rezoning, HSS, HPO, HTO, HRO, HLO, HDO and HCOMPP hold the start-of-step model on the new grid, and TEFFLO, MO the start-of-step $T_\mathrm{eff}$ and number of points; read by KEMCOM, LIBURN, LIRATE88, CRRECT and others); BURN_MOD (HCOMPM, the burning changes, with MIX, ROTMIX, MIDMOD); ENTROP_MOD (the previous step's rates of change of $\log T$, $\log P$, $\ln L$, $\log r$, which main.f uses for its first guess of the new structure; shared with main.f only); DEUTER_MOD (DRATE0); OLDROT_MOD, OLDROT2_MOD, OLDPHY_MOD, DWMAX_MOD (rotation and the physics at the start of the step, for the angular-momentum transport in GETW and MIDMOD); SCRTCH_MOD, TEMP_MOD (the physics just computed by PHYSIC); COMP_MOD (XNEW, ZNEW, SENV); CONSTANTS_MOD; IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT, IOWR, IDEBUG); JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD; 15 modules in all.

The method.

  1. Outer boundary. If $\log T$ at the outermost point is below TENV0, drop the outer points down to the first one hotter than TENV0 (and put the dropped mass into the envelope, SENV). If it is above TENV1, add one point from the stored envelope integration: $\Delta\log P$ from ESTORE, $\Delta\log T = 0.25\,\Delta\log P$, $\Delta\log r$ from ESTORE, the same $L$ and composition, and $\log\rho = \log P - \log T - 8$.
  2. Fixed points. Flag the edge of the convective core, the base of the convective envelope, the base of the H shell, every point where $|\Delta X|$ > HPTTOL(3), $|\Delta Z|$ > HPTTOL(4), or (rotating models) $|\Delta\log\Omega|$ > HPTTOL(12), the base of envelope overshoot and the base of a finely zoned region around the envelope's base (HPTTOL(7) in $\log P$). At most 100 flags; sort them and remove duplicates.

  3. New mass points. Build a cumulative "distance" along the star, $$\chi_j = \chi_{j-1} + \frac{\Delta\log m}{\delta_m} + \frac{\Delta L}{\delta_L} - \frac{\Delta\log P}{\delta_P},$$ with $\delta_m$ = HPTTOL(2), $\delta_L$ = HPTTOL(9) times the largest $L$, and $\delta_P$ = HPTTOL(10) in the finely zoned region, HPTTOL(11) below it, HPTTOL(8) in the convective envelope (the $L$ term only where $L$ increases outward). Between consecutive flagged points, place points evenly in $\chi$, one per unit, and find their masses from a spline of $\log m$ against $\chi$.

  4. Refinement in X and Z. Where $X$ increases outward between new points by more than HPTTOL(5), or $Z$ decreases outward by more than HPTTOL(6), insert evenly spaced points. Then drop points closer than HPTTOL(1) in $\log m$.

  5. Convection flags. Relocate JCORE and JENV on the new grid and reset LC.
  6. Interpolation. With OSPLIN, interpolate to the new points: the entropy-term variables (ENTROP), the composition HCOMP and the starting composition HCOMPP, the burning changes HCOMPM (7 species), the deuterium burning rate (if followed), then $\log P$, $\log r$, $L$, $\log T$, $\log\rho$, and for rotating models $\Omega$, $j$, $f_P$, $f_T$, $r_0$, $\eta_2$; and the energy generation at the start of the step when needed.

  7. Masses. With $m_i = 10^{\log m_i}$: $$\texttt{HS1}_i = m_i,\qquad \texttt{HS2}_i = \tfrac12\,(m_{i+1} - m_{i-1}),\qquad \texttt{HS2}_1 = \tfrac12(m_2 + m_1),\qquad \texttt{HS2}_M = M_* - \tfrac12(m_{M-1}+m_M).$$

  8. Rotation. First guess of the moment of inertia $I_i = \tfrac23\,\Delta m_i\,r_i^2$; overshoot zones (OVROT); moment of inertia (MOMI); $\Omega$ from the conserved specific angular momentum (GETROT); the distortion terms (FPFT); MOMI again; then the totals $J = \sum j_i\Delta m_i$ and $K = \sum \tfrac12\Omega_i j_i \Delta m_i$, printed against the previous ones; and the old rotation state saved in OLDROT.

  9. Physics at the start of the step (rotating models, or light elements with envelope overshoot): PHYSIC at the new points; the results stored in OLDPHY, DWMAX, and the shear term $$\theta_i = \frac{\bar r^{\,4}}{G\,\bar m}\,\bar\Omega\,\frac{\Delta\Omega}{\Delta r}$$ (THO, with geometric means of $r$ and $m$ over the two points) and $d\Omega/dr$ (QWRST) in OLDROT2.

  10. A new surface opacity table (SURFOPAC) if surface $X$ changed by more than $10^{-8}$.

Design. It prints the flag points and the old and new numbers of points to the short output every step, and a full table of old and new points to the debug unit every NPOINT models when LDEBUG is on. Several module arrays of the old model (HPO, HTO, HDO) serve as scratch space before receiving their final contents.

Questions for the experts

  1. No size check in the X and Z refinement. Step 3 stops if the new points exceed JSON (5000), but the insertions of step 4 can add points without any check.
  2. Flags beyond 100. The convection-zone edges and the H-shell base are flagged first, but when the composition and $\Omega$ tests reach 100 flags, the code jumps (GOTO 130) past the flags for the base of envelope overshoot and the base of the finely zoned region. Should those two be flagged before the gradient tests?
  3. Unused values. PMAX1–PMAX5, HPMAX(1), OMEGAL and the variables JOS, JFINE beyond their flags are computed but not used for the zoning (step 3 uses HPTTOL directly). Leftovers of an older scheme?
  4. The added outer point. $\Delta\log T = 0.25\,\Delta\log P$ and $\log\rho = \log P - \log T - 8$ are rough, hard-coded guesses. Are they only starting values for the next Henyey iteration?
  5. The debug table. Under the heading "old points" it prints HKO and HFO, which by then hold $X$ and $Z$ at the new points, not the old ones. The comments also say these values go into HIO and HGO.
  6. MO is the old number of points. MO = M is set before the rezoning, while HLO (and HPO, …) end on the new grid with MNEW points. ENTIME (called by HTIMER when LTRIST) reads HLO(MO) as the previous model's surface luminosity, which is the surface only if the number of points did not change. Should MO = MNEW be set after the rezoning?
  7. ALPHAE as a depth in $\log P$. The base of envelope overshoot is the first point where $\log_{10}P - \log_{10}P(\texttt{JENV}) >$ ALPHAE, so ALPHAE acts as a depth in $\log_{10}P$. main.f uses it as a fraction of $H_P$ (PSCAL0 = ALPHAE $\cdot H_P$); an overshoot of $\alpha H_P$ is about $\Delta\ln P = \alpha$, i.e. $\Delta\log_{10}P \approx 0.43\,\alpha$. Which is meant here?
  8. The JNT comment. "WALPCZ does run in this version but I don't think it can hurt": is the extra MOMI call before GETROT still needed?
