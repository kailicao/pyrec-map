Purpose

The thermal neutrino energy-loss rate of stellar matter, from the analytic fits of Itoh et al. (1996, ApJS 102, 411), with its derivatives with respect to temperature, density, mean atomic weight and mean charge. Five processes are summed: pair annihilation, plasmon decay, the photoneutrino process, bremsstrahlung and recombination. The routine is written in a different style from the rest of YREC (lower case, implicit none, c.. comments); it is probably an import of a widely used public routine, our inference.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: NULOSSES, which first computes $\bar A$ and $\bar Z$ from the mass fractions (AZBAR); NULOSSES is called by NEUTRINO, which ENGEB calls only when LNULOS1 is set (lines 1240–1244↗). LNULOS1 is off by default (line 246↗; a legacy option in the documentation, "Use Itoh et al. (1996) neutrino loss rates"), but 710 of the 965 namelists in the repository that set it turn it on, with the comment "recommended to be set to TRUE" (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).
  • What it calls: IFERMI12 (the inverse Fermi–Dirac integral of order 1/2, to get the electron chemical potential) and ZFERMIM12 (the integral of order −1/2, for its derivative), in the recombination part.
  • Inputs: temp ($T$, K), den ($\rho$, g cm$^{-3}$), abar ($\bar A$), zbar ($\bar Z$).
  • Outputs: snu, the total loss in erg g$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$, and its derivatives dsnudt, dsnudd, dsnuda, dsnudz. No COMMON blocks.

The method

Below $T = 10^7$ K everything is zero. Otherwise, with $Y_e = \bar Z/\bar A$, $T_9 = T/10^9$ K, $\lambda = T_9/5.9302$ (that is, $kT/m_ec^2$, xl) and $\xi = (\rho Y_e/10^9)^{1/3}/\lambda$ (zeta), each process follows the equations of Itoh et al. that the comments cite:

  • Pair (eqs. 2.5–2.8): $Q_\mathrm{pair} = \tfrac12\bigl[C_+ + C_-\,q_\mathrm{pair}\bigr]\,g(\lambda)\,e^{-2/\lambda}\,f_\mathrm{pair}$, where, with $n$ = xnufam neutrino flavors, $$C_\pm = C_V^2 \pm C_A^2 + (n-1)\bigl(C_V'^2 \pm C_A'^2\bigr),\qquad C_V = \tfrac12 + 2\sin^2\theta_W,\ C_A = \tfrac12,\ C_V' = 1 - C_V,\ C_A' = 1 - C_A,$$ ($C_+$ is tfac1, $C_-$ is tfac2, tfac3 $= C_-/C_+$, tfac4 $= C_+/2$, tfac5 $= C_-/2$), $g(\lambda)$ a polynomial in $\lambda^2$ and $f_\mathrm{pair}$ a rational function of $\xi$ and $\lambda$.

    Question 1 (line 109↗; line 117↗). Number of flavors. The comment gives "xnufam … = 3.02 plus/minus 0.005 (1998)", the code uses 3.0. Which value is meant, and should the comment say so?

  • Plasma (eqs. 4.1–4.11): transverse and longitudinal plasmon terms with a correction factor $f_{xy}$.

  • Photoneutrino (eqs. 3.2–3.8): a Fourier-type fit in $\tau = \log_{10}(T/10^7)$, with coefficient sets for $10^7$–$10^8$, $10^8$–$10^9$ and above $10^9$ K; negative results are set to zero.
  • Bremsstrahlung (eqs. 5.1–5.26): the weakly degenerate form when $T > 0.01\,T_F$, with $T_F = 5.9302\times10^9\bigl(\sqrt{1 + 1.018(\rho Y_e/10^6)^{2/3}} - 1\bigr)$ K; otherwise the liquid-metal form "with C12 parameters (not too different for other elements)".

  • Recombination (eqs. 6.5–6.14): with the chemical potential $\nu$ from $\mathcal F_{1/2}^{-1}$, only for $-20 \le \nu \le 10$.

    Question 2 (lines 1212–1232↗; line 1237↗). Recombination limited to $-20 \le \nu \le 10$. Outside this range the recombination loss is zero. Is that a property of Itoh's fit, or a cut that could matter in degenerate cores?

The sum is converted from erg cm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$ to erg g$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$ by dividing by $\rho$ ("comment these out to duplicate the Itoh et al. plots"), and every derivative is carried along by the chain rule.

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) A typo in tfac2. (lines 124–125↗) The parameter is tfac2 = cv*cv - ca*ca + (xnufam-1.0d0) * (cvp*cvp - cap-cap). By the pattern of tfac1 and the definition of $C_-$, the last term should be cvp*cvp - cap*cap. As written it is $C_V'^2 - 2C_A' = C_V'^2 - 1$, so $C_-$ = −1.318 instead of 0.182, and tfac3 $= C_-/C_+$ = −0.784 instead of 0.108. tfac3 enters the pair rate as $1 + $ tfac3$\,q_\mathrm{pair}$ (lines 356–357↗) and the photoneutrino rate as $1 - $ tfac3$\,q_\mathrm{phot}$ (lines 834–835↗), and tfac5 enters both bremsstrahlung forms (line 1015↗, line 1171↗). Taken whenever LNULOS1 is on (off by default, on in most of the repository's namelists) and $\log T$ is above TCUT(5). Read from the code, not run; the effect on the total loss was not computed.
