Explanation

Purpose. CENT_MOD holds the central conditions and the base of the surface convection zone of the last model written. It replaces COMMON/CENT/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26).

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All are set by WRTOUT: TCENTER, PCENTER, DCENTER, the central $\log T$, $\log P$, $\log\rho$; CZsurf_M, the mass of the surface convection zone ($M_\odot$; the star's mass when it is fully convective, 0 without one); CZsurf_R, the radius of its base ($R_\odot$). The main program reads them for the neutrino-flux output.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/CENT/ as a module.
