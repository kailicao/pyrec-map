Explanation

Purpose. DEBHU_MOD holds the inputs of the Debye–Hückel correction to the equation of state: the constant CDH, the charges of the 18 heavy elements and the local composition, shared by 11 routines. It replaces COMMON/DEBHU/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), less the switch LDH and ETADH0, ETADH1 (now in NMLPAR_MOD).

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • CDH: $-\sqrt{\pi/(k\,m_H^3)}\,e^3/3$, set in SETUPS.
  • DHNUE(18): the charges of C, N, O, Ne, Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ar, Ca, Ti, Cr, Mn, Fe, Ni (SETUPS's comment), set in SETUPS.
  • XXDH, YYDH, ZZDH, ZDH(18): the local $X$, $Y$ (He3 + He4), $Z$ and the heavy-element abundances (C12 + C13, N14 + N15, O16 + O18 from HCOMP), copied from the shell's composition just before the equation of state is called, by nine routines: COEFFT, CRRECT, GETNEWENV, HSUBP, PHYSIC, SCONVEC, STARIN, WRTMOD and WRTOUT.
  • All are read only by EQRELV, which, with LDH, forms $\sum_i$ DHNUE(i) ZDH(i) and the Debye–Hückel pressure $P_\mathrm{DH} = $ CDH × (charge factor) × $\sqrt{\rho/T}\,\rho$.

Design. A blanket USE DEBHU_MOD. The composition reaches EQRELV through the module rather than as arguments, so each caller of the equation of state must copy it first; the copies are the same few lines in nine files. At rp_1, two files (EQSTAT, EQSTAT2) named the second member XXDY, harmless with COMMON's storage association.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/DEBHU/ as a module, with LDH, ETADH0, ETADH1 moved to NMLPAR_MOD.
