Explanation

Purpose. Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the surface composition (MHP 7/98: "get OPAL95 opacity from table at surface X,Z"): a two-dimensional interpolation in $\log T$ and $\log R$, where $R = \rho/T_6^3$. It is the fastest of three routines; OP953D adds $X$ and OP954D adds $X$ and $Z$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the surface table CAPPAE(70,19) of $\log\kappa$ in LLOT95E_MOD, built by OP95XTAB; the indices and the Lagrange weights prepared by GETOPAL95 in OP95INTERP_MOD (formerly /OP95INDX/, /OP95FACT/ and /OP95EXT/), where $\log R$ is now named RHO_T63L (formerly RL) and its lowest table value RHO_T63LL (formerly RLL).
  • Who calls it: GETOPAL95, when the point's $Z$ matches the table's ZTAB (to a relative $10^{-4}$) and its $X$ the surface table's XTAB (to $10^{-4}$), and the acoustic depth is not being computed (LADON and LACOUT, which force OP954D).
  • Outputs: $\kappa$ (O), $\log\kappa$ (OL), QOD $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log\rho$ and QOT $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$.

The method. At each of the four nearest temperatures $T_i$, four-point Lagrange interpolation in $\log R$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its slope: $$C_i = \sum_k b^{(D)}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k},\qquad C'_i = \sum_k b^{(D')}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k}.$$ If $\log R$ lies below the table (LEXTL) or above it at some $T_i$ (LEXH(I)), the value at the edge is extrapolated linearly, $C_i \to C_i + (\log R - \log R_\mathrm{edge})\,C'_i$. Then, in $\log T$, $\log\kappa = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C_i$, $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T|_R = \sum_i b^{(T')}_i C_i$ and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log R|_T = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C'_i$. Since $\log R = \log\rho - 3\log T_6$, $$\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T,\qquad \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_\rho = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_R - 3\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T .$$

Design. The table dimensions are fixed (70 temperatures, 19 values of $\log R$), now as private parameters of LLOT95E_MOD; the routine itself no longer declares them.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the three interpolation blocks merged into OP95INTERP_MOD, with RL, RLL renamed RHO_T63L, RHO_T63LL so as not to clash with the radius RL), the dimension parameters moved into LLOT95E_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

15    """Get the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the
16    surface composition (MHP 7/98): four-point Lagrange interpolation in
17    log R (R = rho/T6**3), with linear extrapolation beyond the table, then
18    in log T, with the indices and weights GETOPAL95 left in
19    op95interp_mod.
20
21    Translates: op952d.f#OP952D (09c3b9b)
22
23    Arguments: o, ol, qod, qot, passed in with any value.
24
25    Returns (o, ol, qod, qot): kappa, log kappa, dlog kappa/dlog rho and
26    dlog kappa/dlog T (at constant rho).
27    """
28    im = op95interp_mod
29    cappae = llot95e_mod.cappae
30    # Log kappa at the desired rho, and its slope in log R, at each of the
31    # four nearby values of T.
32    for i in range(1, 5):
33        it = im.jt + i - 1
34        id_ = im.jd[i]
35        c[i] = (im.bd[i, 1] * cappae[it, id_] + im.bd[i, 2] * cappae[it, id_ + 1]
36                + im.bd[i, 3] * cappae[it, id_ + 2]
37                + im.bd[i, 4] * cappae[it, id_ + 3])
38        qc[i] = (im.bdd[i, 1] * cappae[it, id_]
39                 + im.bdd[i, 2] * cappae[it, id_ + 1]
40                 + im.bdd[i, 3] * cappae[it, id_ + 2]
41                 + im.bdd[i, 4] * cappae[it, id_ + 3])
42    # Outside the table in rho: extrapolate with the slope at the edge.
43    if im.lextl:
44        deltar = im.rho_t63l - im.rho_t63ll
45        for i in range(1, 5):
46            c[i] = c[i] + deltar * qc[i]
47    elif im.lexth:
48        for i in range(1, 5):
49            if im.lexh[i]:
50                deltar = im.rho_t63l - im.rlh[i]
51                c[i] = c[i] + deltar * qc[i]
52    # Interpolate in T: log kappa, dlog kappa/dlog T and dlog kappa/dlog R.
53    ol = im.bt[1] * c[1] + im.bt[2] * c[2] + im.bt[3] * c[3] + im.bt[4] * c[4]
54    qot = (im.btt[1] * c[1] + im.btt[2] * c[2] + im.btt[3] * c[3]
55           + im.btt[4] * c[4])
56    qod = (im.bt[1] * qc[1] + im.bt[2] * qc[2] + im.bt[3] * qc[3]
57           + im.bt[4] * qc[4])
58    # From the derivative at fixed R to the derivative at fixed rho.
59    qot = qot - 3.0 * qod
60    o = 10.0**ol
61    return o, ol, qod, qot
