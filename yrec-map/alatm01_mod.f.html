Purpose

ALATM01_MOD holds the Allard model-atmosphere table, shared by the four Allard routines. It replaces COMMON/ALATM01/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); the sizes NGA = 25 and NTA = 250 are private, since each user declares its own.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All are set by ALFILEIN, which reads the table file:

  • TEFFLs(NTA), GLs(NGA): the grid in $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ and $\log g$, with their sizes nTEFF, nGL.
  • FeHs(NGA), ALPHAs(NGA), with nFeH, nALPHA: the metallicities and $\alpha$-enhancements found in the file, used only within ALFILEIN to choose the table.
  • PLs, P100Ls, T100Ls (NTA × NGA): $\log P$ where $T = T_\mathrm{eff}$, and $\log P$ and $\log T$ at $\tau = 100$ (for the option LALTPTau100).
  • LOldNextGen: the file's format, set and not read elsewhere.

Read by ALTABINIT (the table's limits), ALPRINT and ALSURFP (the interpolation).
