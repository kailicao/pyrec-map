Explanation

Purpose. This file integrates the outer layers of the star, which the interior model does not contain: first the atmosphere, from small optical depth to the photosphere, then the envelope, from the photosphere down to the interior's outermost mass point. The result is the pressure, temperature and radius at that fitting point, which SURFBC turns into the surface boundary condition of the interior; the run of the envelope is stored for output, the acoustic depth and the convective overturn time.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments B ($L$), TEFFL, GL ($\log g$), RL ($\log R$), HSTOT ($\log M$), X, Z, FPL, FTL (rotational corrections), PLIM (the expected end pressure), IE (which point of the envelope triangle), LSBC, LPRT, LPULPT, and CMIXL (the mixing-length constant, passed on to QENV through QENVMIX_MOD, since QENV is called through BSSTEP with a fixed argument list). From NMLPAR_MOD: the atmosphere choice KTTAU (0 Eddington gray, 1 Krishna Swamy, 2 the HRA $T$–$\tau$ relation, 3 Kurucz, 4 Allard, 5 Castelli–Kurucz 2004); the integration controls (ATMERR, ATMD0, ATMBEG, … and ENVERR, ENVBEG, …); TENV0, TENV1, from which TENV = (TENV0 + TENV1)/2 is computed here; LCALCENV, LNEWTCZ; the output switches (LSTATM, LSTENV, LSTCH, IPVER). Also STOLR0 (INTPAR_MOD), SENV ($\log$ of the fitting point's mass fraction, COMP_MOD), HRAS (ATMOS_MOD), LCLCD (ACDPTH_MOD), the constants (CONSTANTS_MOD) and the unit numbers (IOUNITS_MOD).
  • Who calls it: SURFBC (the boundary condition), CALCAD, GETNEWENV, GETTAU, STARIN, STITCH and WRTMOD.
  • What it calls: for tabulated atmospheres SURFP, KCSURFP or ALSURFP; for gray ones HRA, EQSTAT and GETOPAC; for both integrations BSSTEP with the derivative routines QATM and QENV; at the end TAUCAL.
  • Outputs:
  • arguments: PS(IE), TS(IE), RS(IE) (the fitting point's $\log P$, $\log T$, $\log r$), ESTORE, ISTORE (the state where $T$ first reaches TENV), all set only when LSBC; counters KATM, KENV, KSAHA;
  • ENVSTRUCT_MOD: the envelope profile ($\log P$, $\log T$, $\log m$, $\log\rho$, $\log r$, $X$, $Z$, convective flag, gradients, velocities, $\beta$, $\Gamma_1$, $c_P$, ionization, opacity, $L$, $\rho_T$), reordered from the centre outward; read by CALCAD, GETNEWENV, GETTAU, STARIN, STITCH, TAUINT;
  • ATMSTRUCT_MOD (the atmosphere profile, for STITCH); PPHOT (OVRTRN_MOD, the photospheric pressure, for AMCALC); LPUMOD (PULSE1_MOD, whether QATM and QENV compute the pulsation values) and QQED, QQET, QFS (PULSE2_MOD);
  • text: the atmosphere and envelope tables to ISTOR, summaries to ISHORT, pulsation files IOPATM, IOPENV.

The atmosphere.

  • Tables (KTTAU = 3, 4, 5): the routine reads $\log P$ (and $\log T$) at the photosphere from the table; if the Allard table fails, it switches the run to the gray atmosphere (it sets KTTAU = 0 for good).
  • Gray (KTTAU = 0, 1, 2): start at a small optical depth, with the temperature from the $T$–$\tau$ relation at $\tau \to 0$ (Eddington: $\log T = \log T_\mathrm{eff} - \tfrac14\log\tfrac43 + \tfrac14\log\tfrac23$, that is $T^4 = T_\mathrm{eff}^4/2$; Krishna Swamy: $T^4 = \tfrac34 T_\mathrm{eff}^4 \times 0.550$; HRA: its own value at $\tau = 2/3$ shifted by HRAS) and the pressure $P = \rho\mathcal{R}T + aT^4/3$ at a chosen density ATMD0 (the gas term with $\mu$ taken as 1, as the code writes CGAS*ATMDX*T; it only sets the starting point: "The integration starts at an arbitrary low density", YREC paper §2.3.6). The starting optical depth is $\tau_0 = \kappa P/g$, where $\kappa$ is the opacity at that $P$ and $T$ (X0 = PL - GL + DLOG10(O); whether this is meant as the optical depth of the column above the starting layer is question 11). Then, with $\log\tau$ as the variable, $$\frac{d\log P}{d\log\tau} = \frac{g\,\tau}{\kappa P},$$ up to $\tau = 2/3$ (Eddington and HRA; $\tau = 0.312$ for Krishna Swamy, the depth where its $T = T_\mathrm{eff}$). The tables, too, give $P$ at $\tau = 2/3$ (YREC paper, §2.3.6), so every option ends where $T = T_\mathrm{eff}$ and the envelope below starts at $r = R$ from $L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$; switching KTTAU changes the photospheric pressure, not the radius. If the starting $\tau$ lies beyond the end, the density is divided by 10 and the start retried.

The envelope. With $\log P$ as the variable and $y = (\log(m/M), \log T, \log r)$, QENV gives $$\frac{d\log m}{d\log P} = -\frac{4\pi r^4 P}{G m^2 f_P}, \qquad \frac{d\log T}{d\log P} = \nabla, \qquad \frac{d\log r}{d\log P} = -\frac{P r f_P}{G m \rho},$$ where $f_P$ is the rotational distortion factor (FPL); note that it multiplies $d\log r/d\log P$ where consistency with the interior equations may ask for $1/f_P$ (QENV, question 1). The integration starts at the photosphere, with $\log(m/M) = 0$, $r = R$ (RL), and the photospheric $T$ and $P$ from the atmosphere or the table, and runs with BSSTEP until $\log(m/M)$ reaches SENV; the step is shortened or reversed to land on it, and, when LSBC is false, the first step past PLIM is cut to end on it. The end point is interpolated linearly to SENV. On the way down, when LSBC, the state where $\log T$ first reaches TENV (6.0 with the defaults TENV0 = 3, TENV1 = 9) is kept in ESTORE ($\log P$, $\log T$, $\log r$, $\log(m/M)$) and ISTORE (the vertex), for HPOINT. TENV0 and TENV1 bound the fitting point's temperature: HPOINT deletes the outer interior points cooler than TENV0 (moving SENV inward), and, using ESTORE, adds a new outermost interior point, at SENV = ESTORE(4), when the outermost point has grown hotter than TENV1. The defaults are 3 and 9; all 51 test-suite runs use 2 and 9, and their input files' comment calls the bounds a failsafe, while the documentation says "Code stops below this" and "above this" (whether they are only safeguards is question 12). If the envelope mass is negligible (SENV > -1e-12), the photosphere is the fitting point.

After the integration. The profile is reversed; for TAUCAL the masses are passed in grams (ENVS1) and as differences in units of the total mass (ENVS2, used only as weights in an average, so the unit cancels); the base of the convection zone is found by linear interpolation of $\nabla_\mathrm{rad} - \nabla$ (its radius is no longer kept), and TAUCAL computes the overturn time (unless LNEWTCZ). With LCALCENV false, the routine stops after the atmosphere, having set PPHOT.

Design. The routine mixes physics with output for several purposes (the .store tables, pulsation files, acoustic-depth arrays), and each output path keeps its own copies of $\Gamma_1$ and related values. Failure to finish in 2000 steps stops the run. LDERIV is set by the pulsation test and then forced to .TRUE. on the next line.

Questions for the experts

  1. A broken swap. In the loop that reverses the envelope profile, ENVL is swapped without the line ENVL(I2) = DUM1 (added with "08/25 JVS"). The first half then gets the second half's values and the second half keeps its own, Since every entry of ENVL is B (the luminosity is constant in the envelope), nothing goes wrong now; the swap would fail only if ENVL ever varied.
  2. ENVG never set. The local array ENVG is passed to TAUCAL as HG (gravity) but never filled here. TAUCAL computes HG itself at the two points it needs when LROT is false; with rotation it uses HG as passed, so the overturn time of a rotating model would use an unset $g$. Should ENVINT fill ENVG?
  3. A computed overturn time not kept. TAUCZENV ($\sum \Delta r/v$ in the convection zone) is computed in the loop but never stored or returned. Dead code, or meant to feed TAUCZ?
  4. Wrong $c_P$ in a print. At the first atmosphere point, the printed $\Gamma_1$ uses QCPP ($\partial c_P/\partial\ln P$) in place of QCP. It affects only the .store output.
  5. Pulsation variables on other paths. The block run when LCLCD or LSTCH (not only for pulsation output) uses TAUP, OP, DP, which are set at the start only when LPULPT.AND.LPRT; otherwise they keep values from an earlier call.
  6. Two ways of storing the atmosphere. ATMOP, ATMOT, … are stored when LPRT.AND.LSTATM, but ATMOR, ADELS and NUMATM when LCLCD, LSTCH or pulsation output is on. Can the two be out of step for STITCH?
  7. No limit on retries. The retry with ATMDX/10 has no limit.

  8. A global switch. On an Allard failure, KTTAU is set to 0 in NMLPAR_MOD, so the rest of the run uses gray atmospheres, even if a later model would fit the table.

  9. ENVCZL now unused. With ENVRCZ gone, ENVCZL (the convection zone's base in $\log(r/R_\odot)$) is computed but no longer used, although the new comment says "ENVCZL above is still used below". Remove the computation, or keep the radius somewhere?

  10. ATMD0 a logarithm? The documentation calls ATMD0 the "log10 of starting density at τ = 0", but ENVINT uses it as the density itself (PL = log10((CGAS*ATMDX + CA3*T**3)*T), and divides it by 10 to retry), and the default, 1.0D-10, is a density in g cm⁻³. The documentation looks wrong; confirm?
  11. The starting optical depth. The integration starts at $\tau_0 = \kappa P/g$. If the pressure there is the weight of the column above, $P = g\Sigma$, this is $\kappa\Sigma$, the optical depth of that column with $\kappa$ taken as constant: is that the reasoning, and does the start's error matter once the integration reaches $\tau = 2/3$?
  12. TENV0 and TENV1. HPOINT deletes outer interior points cooler than TENV0 and adds one when the outermost point is hotter than TENV1; the documentation describes them as limits at which the "code stops". Are they meant only as safeguards that a normal run never reaches, and is the test suite's 2 (against the default 3) a deliberate choice?

SUBROUTINE ENVINT(B,FPL,FTL,GL,HSTOT,IE,LPRT,LSBC,PLIM,RL,TEFFL,

Integrates the atmosphere (gray $T$–$\tau$ or tabulated) and the envelope (in $\log P$, with BSSTEP and QENV) down to the fitting mass, returns the fitting point's $P$, $T$, $r$, stores the envelope profile in /ENVSTRUCT/, and finds the convection zone's base and overturn time.
