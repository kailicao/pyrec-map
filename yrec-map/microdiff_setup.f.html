Explanation

Purpose. The first step of MICRODIFF: it decides which part of the star takes part in settling, and converts the model's variables to the units of Bahcall and Loeb (1989), as its comments call them.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments of MICRODIFF (see there); XMIN and YMIN from NMLPAR_MOD (namelist variables read by PARMIN); the solar constants in CONSTANTS_MOD (not used: the routine has its own constants).
  • Who calls it: only MICRODIFF.
  • Outputs: IBEGIN, IEND, LALLCZ; the unlogged radius HRU and temperature HTU in the new units; the unlogged density ODEN and temperature OTEM in cgs units; and, in place, HS1, HQPR, DT and STOT, converted to the new units. MICRODIFF_ETM converts them back at the end.
  • Modules: it writes the conversion factors to CONFAC_MOD, which MICRODIFF_ETM reads (as do SETUP_GRSETT and EQUAL_TO_MODEL in the older settling path). It reads NMLPAR_MOD (XMIN, YMIN), CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN) and IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT, for the messages), and takes JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD. The unused COMMON blocks of rp_1 (/GRAVST/, /GRAVS3/, /SCRTCH/, /GSCOF/, /CMIXING/) are no longer declared.

The method.

  1. The diffusion region. If the centre is convective (LC(1)), IBEGIN is the last point of the convective core; if every point is convective, settling is skipped (LALLCZ = .TRUE.). IBEGIN then moves out to the first point with $X >$ XMIN. If the surface is convective, IEND is the innermost point of the surface convection zone, otherwise the last point M; it then moves inward to the outermost point with $Y >$ YMIN. A model with $X \le$ XMIN or $Y \le$ YMIN everywhere is skipped.
  2. Units. With $R_\odot = 6.9598\times10^{10}$ cm (CRSUN_BAH) and the year $3.1558\times10^{7}$ s (CSECYR_BAH): $$r' = \frac{r}{R_\odot},\quad T' = \frac{T}{10^7\ \mathrm{K}},\quad m' = \frac{10^{-2}\,m}{R_\odot^{3}},\quad \left(\frac{d\ln P}{dr}\right)' = R_\odot\,\frac{d\ln P}{dr},\quad \Delta t' = \frac{\Delta t}{2.7\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{yr}}.$$ That is, the mass unit is $100\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\times R_\odot^3$. The comment says the time unit "includes factor of 2.2 from ln Lambda", the Coulomb logarithm that MICRODIFF_COD divides by.

Design. HS1, HQPR, DT and STOT belong to the caller and are converted in place; MICRODIFF_ETM converts them back. When settling is skipped, the routine returns before converting, so the two stay consistent.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the unused ones dropped), IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. A copied comment. The second exit (no helium above YMIN) is commented "HYDROGEN-FREE MODEL", copied from the first.
  2. Overshoot. Two comments say "COMPUTE OVERSHOOT (TO BE ADDED)". Settling ignores overshoot at the convective boundaries. Is this still planned?
  3. Constants of its own. The routine uses its own $R_\odot$ and year rather than CRSUN and CSECYR from CONSTANTS_MOD, which it uses. Intended, to keep Bahcall and Loeb's values?
  4. No check that IEND > IBEGIN. After the two searches, nothing checks that the region between IBEGIN and IEND is not empty. If it is, MICRODIFF_MTE divides the total width (zero or negative) by the minimum spacing, which is the same number, and builds a grid with $\Delta r \le 0$ (or 0/0 when the width is zero). Should settling be skipped then, as for a fully convective model?

convert the model to Bahcall and Loeb's units; skip settling
(lallcz = True) for a fully convective model, or one with X <= XMIN or

  2. Unclear. "IEND is the first convective point of the envelope; it then moves in to the last point with $Y >$ YMIN": the code sets IEND to the innermost point of the surface convection zone, then searches inward for the outermost point with $Y >$ YMIN. Fix: say "innermost" and "outermost".
  3. Missing question. Nothing checks that IEND > IBEGIN after the two searches. If the radiative region shrinks to nothing, MICRODIFF_MTE divides DRTOT (zero or negative) by itself and builds a grid with $\Delta r \le 0$. Fix: add a question.
