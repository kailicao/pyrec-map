Purpose

The first step of MICRODIFF: it decides which part of the star takes part in settling, and converts the model's variables to the units of Bahcall and Loeb (1989), as its comments call them.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments of MICRODIFF (see there); XMIN and YMIN from NMLPAR_MOD (namelist variables read by PARMIN); the solar constants in CONSTANTS_MOD (not used: the routine has its own constants).
  • Who calls it: only MICRODIFF.
  • Outputs: IBEGIN, IEND, LALLCZ; the unlogged radius HRU and temperature HTU in the new units; the unlogged density ODEN and temperature OTEM in cgs units; and, in place, HS1, HQPR, DT and STOT, converted to the new units. MICRODIFF_ETM converts them back at the end.
  • Modules: it writes the conversion factors to CONFAC_MOD, which MICRODIFF_ETM reads (as do SETUP_GRSETT and EQUAL_TO_MODEL in the older settling path). It reads NMLPAR_MOD (XMIN, YMIN), CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN) and IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT, for the messages), and takes JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD. The unused COMMON blocks of rp_1 (/GRAVST/, /GRAVS3/, /SCRTCH/, /GSCOF/, /CMIXING/) are no longer declared.

The method

  1. The diffusion region. If the centre is convective (LC(1)), IBEGIN is the last point of the convective core; if every point is convective, settling is skipped (LALLCZ = .TRUE.). IBEGIN then moves out to the first point with $X >$ XMIN. If the surface is convective, IEND is the innermost point of the surface convection zone, otherwise the last point M; it then moves inward to the outermost point with $Y >$ YMIN. A model with $X \le$ XMIN or $Y \le$ YMIN everywhere is skipped.

    Question 1 (line 101↗; line 123↗). Overshoot. Two comments say "COMPUTE OVERSHOOT (TO BE ADDED)," so settling ignores overshoot at the convective boundaries: IBEGIN and IEND are the convective boundaries themselves. Is this still planned?

    The second exit's comment is copied from the first. The exit taken when no helium is above YMIN (lines 133–137↗) is commented "HYDROGEN-FREE MODEL," as the hydrogen exit is (line 110↗); its message says "Y BELOW," so the comment should read "HELIUM-FREE MODEL."

  2. Units. With $R_\odot = 6.9598\times10^{10}$ cm (CRSUN_BAH) and the year $3.1558\times10^{7}$ s (CSECYR_BAH): $$r' = \frac{r}{R_\odot},\quad T' = \frac{T}{10^7\ \mathrm{K}},\quad m' = \frac{10^{-2}\,m}{R_\odot^{3}},\quad \left(\frac{d\ln P}{dr}\right)' = R_\odot\,\frac{d\ln P}{dr},\quad \Delta t' = \frac{\Delta t}{2.7\times10^{13}\ \mathrm{yr}}.$$ That is, the mass unit is $100\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\times R_\odot^3$. The comment says the time unit "includes factor of 2.2 from ln Lambda," the Coulomb logarithm that MICRODIFF_COD divides by.

    Question 2 (lines 86–87↗; lines 144–148↗). Constants of its own. The routine uses its own $R_\odot$ and year (CRSUN_BAH, CSECYR_BAH) rather than CRSUN and CSECYR from CONSTANTS_MOD, which it uses (for CLN). Is this intended, to keep Bahcall and Loeb's values?

Design

HS1, HQPR, DT and STOT are converted in place, and MICRODIFF_ETM converts them back; since MICRODIFF passes copies of them (microdiff.f, lines 112–117↗), the caller's own values are not touched. When settling is skipped, the routine returns before converting, so the two stay consistent.

1. (cannot be reached) No check that IEND > IBEGIN.

(lines 107–116↗; lines 130–139↗) After the two searches, nothing checks that the region from IBEGIN to IEND is not empty. The search for $X >$ XMIN runs outward to M, past IEND, so if the hydrogen-exhausted centre reached the base of the convective envelope, IBEGIN would end inside the envelope, beyond IEND (but settling stops before the centre is exhausted: below). What follows in MICRODIFF_MTE: the total width DRTOT is zero or negative and is divided by the minimum spacing, which is the same number (microdiff_mte.f, lines 54–63↗); with IEND = IBEGIN this is 0/0 and DR = 0, and MICRODIFF_RUN divides by DR (microdiff_run.f, line 145↗); with IEND < IBEGIN the grid runs inward, and MICRODIFF_ETM applies both the central and the envelope change to the shells between them. Only with LNEWDIF and LDIFY, LDIFZ or LDIFLI (all off by default; the test suite's settling decks set them).
