Explanation

Purpose. GETGRID builds the equally spaced grid on which the diffusion equations for rotational mixing and angular momentum transport are solved. The grid variable is the combination of $\log m$, $L$ and $\log P$ that HPOINT uses to place the model's points, with one difference: HPOINT scales $\Delta_L$ by the largest $L$ in the model, GETGRID by the surface $L$.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: MIXGRID and ROTGRID, for one region (IBEG..IEND).
  • Inputs: HL ($L$ in solar units), HP ($\log P$), HS ($\log m$).
  • What it calls: OSPLIN, a shape-preserving osculatory quadratic spline.
  • Outputs: in EGRID_MOD: CHI (the grid variable at the model points), ECHI (equally spaced values), ES1 (their masses, in g), DCHI, NTOT. EQ2MOD, SECULR, MIXCOM and CHECKJ read them.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD gives the point-spacing tolerances HPTTOL (formerly in /CTOL/, set in PARMIN); EGRID_MOD (output); $\ln 10$ from CONSTANTS_MOD.

The method. With the model's spacing criteria $\Delta_m$ = HPTTOL(2), $\Delta_L$ = HPTTOL(9)$\cdot L_\mathrm{surf}$ (with $L_\mathrm{surf}$ = HL(M)), $\Delta_P$ = HPTTOL(11), $$\chi_i = \frac{\log m_i}{\Delta_m} + \frac{L_i}{\Delta_L} - \frac{\log P_i}{\Delta_P}.$$ The equally spaced grid runs from $\chi_1$ to $\chi_N$ with $N$ = NTOT points, $\Delta\chi = (\chi_N - \chi_1)/(N-1)$, and $\log m$ is interpolated onto it with OSPLIN, then unlogged.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. Number of points. NTOT is set to the number of model points in the region (at least 3); the formula that set it from the range of $\chi$ ("modulus of final chi plus one") is commented out. So the grid is equally spaced in $\chi$ but has as many points as the model. Is that the intended resolution?
  2. Monotonic $\chi$. OSPLIN needs increasing abscissae. $\chi$ increases outward when $\log P$ decreases and $L$ does not drop sharply; can $L$ decrease enough (for example in a region with neutrino losses) to break this?
