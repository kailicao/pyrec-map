Explanation

Purpose. PULSE1_MOD holds the thermodynamic and opacity derivatives at every shell of the interior, stored for the pulsation output, and the switch that makes the envelope routines keep their derivatives. Shared by 6 routines. It replaces COMMON/PULSE1/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • PQDP, PQDT, PQED, PQET, PQOD, PQOT, PQCP, PRMU, PEMU (JSON each): at each shell, the equation of state's $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$ and $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ (QDP, QDT), the energy generation's and the opacity's logarithmic derivatives in $\rho$ and $T$ (QED, QET, QOD, QOT), $c_P$ (QCP), and RMU, EMU. Stored by COEFFT when LPULSE is set; PQDP and PQDT always, since GAM1 (SOUND_MOD) and the storage file need them ("JVS 01/11"). WRTMOD writes them in the pulsation model; STITCH writes PQDT to the stored model.
  • LPUMOD: set by ENVINT to LPULPT .AND. LPRT (pulsation output of the envelope); QENV and QATM then save their point's values in PULSE2_MOD.

Design. A blanket USE PULSE1_MOD in each routine.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/PULSE1/ as a module.
