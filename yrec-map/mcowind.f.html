Explanation

Purpose. MCOWIND returns the change in the angular velocity of the surface convection zone caused by magnetized-wind angular-momentum loss over a (diffusion) time step. It implements the newer wind law (G. Somers 3/17, "PMM-style", by its comment after van Saders and Pinsonneault, probably) with saturation, an optional Rossby scaling and the centrifugal correction of Matt et al. (2012, ApJ 754, L26). When the new law is off (LMWIND false) it hands over to the older COWIND.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only SECULR, in the angular-momentum transport, once per iteration IT of the diffusion solution.
  • Inputs: BL ($\log L/L_\odot$), DT (the diffusion step, s), HICZ (moment of inertia of the surface convection zone), IT, OMEGAS (surface $\Omega$ at the end of the previous iteration), WOLD (surface $\Omega$ at the start of the step), SMASS ($M/M_\odot$), TEFFL.
  • Outputs: WIND1 and WIND2, the changes in $\Omega$ over the step computed with $\Omega$ at the start and at the end of the step; the caller averages them (its comment).
  • Calls: COWIND (old law) or AMCALC, which returns STRUCTFACTOR (an output argument, formerly in CWIND) from $M$, $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (the run's parameters, from PARMIN: the wind law's WMAX, EXW, CONSTFACTOR, EXCEN, formerly in CWIND, and LMWIND, LROSSBY, LBSCALE, the solar values PMMSOLW, PMMSOLTAU, formerly in PMMWIND), OVRTRN_MOD (TAUCZ, TAUCZ0, FRACSTEP: the convective turnover time at the start and end of the step and the fraction of the step reached), and CONSTANTS_MOD (the constants, with C_2, set once in PARMIN).

The method.

  1. Saturation. With Rossby scaling, the turnover time is interpolated in the step, $\tau = \tau_0 + f\,(\tau_1 - \tau_0)$, and the saturation rate is $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = \Omega_\mathrm{max}\,\tau_\odot/\tau$. With the extra field scaling as well (LBSCALE, which acts only with Rossby scaling) the rates $\Omega$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ are multiplied by $\tau/\tau_\odot$. Otherwise $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ = WMAX.
  2. Coefficient. $C = (\Delta t/I_\mathrm{cz})\,K_\mathrm{const}\,K_\mathrm{struct}$.

  3. Centrifugal term. With $f = \Omega^2R^3/(2GM)$, for the star (at the start and end values of $\Omega$) and for the Sun ($f_\odot$), $$F_\mathrm{cen} = \left(\frac{c_2^2 + f_\odot}{c_2^2 + f}\right)^{n},$$ $c_2$ = C_2, $n$ = EXCEN; $R$ from $L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$.

  4. Torque. With $\Omega_p = \min(\Omega,\Omega_\mathrm{sat})$ and the exponent $a$ = EXW, $$\Delta\Omega_1 = C\,\Omega_{p,\mathrm{start}}^{\,a-1}\,\Omega_\mathrm{start}\,F_{\mathrm{cen},1},\qquad \Delta\Omega_2 = C\,\Omega_{p,\mathrm{now}}^{\,a-1}\,\Omega_\mathrm{now}\,F_{\mathrm{cen},2},$$ and WIND2 is the running mean of $\Delta\Omega_2$ over the iterations (set at IT = 1, then averaged with the previous value).

KC's changes. 2025-05-30: the variables of COMMON PMMWIND reordered (the character variable AWIND and the logicals moved to the end), the same in all units that share it. The block is gone since: its members are in NMLPAR_MOD, and AWIND is a local of PARMIN.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; CWIND and PMMWIND retired (C_2 to CONSTANTS_MOD, the rest to NMLPAR_MOD), so STRUCTFACTOR is now returned by AMCALC as an argument; the unused DEUTER dropped. No question settled: GL is still a local that is never set.

Questions for the experts

  1. GL is never set. The solar term is FSUN = 0.5*PMMSOLW**2*CRSUN**3/EXP(CLN*GL)/CMSUN; GL is a local variable that is never assigned (the stellar terms use CGL, $\log G$). So $f_\odot$ is computed with an undefined $G$ (probably $10^0 = 1$ if the compiler zeroes it). Was CGL meant?
  2. WIND2 averaging. For IT > 1, WIND2 is $\tfrac12$(previous WIND2 + new value), which weights the latest iterations most, not an average over iterations. Intended (as a damping)?
  3. Rates scaled for LBSCALE. With LBSCALE, WFIRST and WNOW are scaled by $\tau/\tau_\odot$ but the final formula multiplies by the unscaled WOLD and OMEGAS. So the torque goes as $(\Omega\tau)^{a-1}\Omega$. Is that the intended law?
  4. Single-precision literals. 0.5, 3.0 in the centrifugal terms are single precision; harmless here, but inconsistent with the rest.

43    wnow = omegas

51            wfirst = wfirst * tcz / n.pmmsoltau

57    structfactor = amcalc(smass, bl, teffl, 0.0)
58    # The new wind coefficient.

60    # Centrifugal correction (Matt et al. 2012).
61    # Question (see the page): GL is never set; CGL was probably meant.
62    fsun = 0.5 * n.pmmsolw**2 * c.crsun**3 / math.exp(c.cln * GL) / c.cmsun

67    fcen2 = ((c.c_2**2 + fsun) / (c.c_2**2 + fcorr2))**n.excen

72    if it == 1:
73        wind2 = temp
74    else:
75        # Question (see the page): a running half-and-half mean.
76        wind2 = 0.5 * (wind2 + temp)
