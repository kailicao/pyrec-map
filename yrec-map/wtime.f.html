Explanation

Purpose. WTIME limits the time step of a rotating model by the change of the angular velocity since the previous model: if $\Omega$ changed by more than the user's fraction DTWIND somewhere (the comment gives a default of 0.08; NMLPAR_MOD initializes it to 10), the step shrinks; if less, it grows. Which value a run uses: DTWIND is a namelist variable of PARMIN, initialized to 10; REMAP replaces it with TIME_DW_GLOBAL (initialized to 0.08 in NMLBYPASS_MOD) only when LNEWVARS is set, and LNEWVARS is .FALSE. by default. A run that sets neither thus has DTWIND = 10, a tenfold change of $\Omega$ in one step, so that rotation almost never limits the step; with LNEWVARS it is 0.08, and it often does.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the previous step DELTS, M, the current OMEGA; through modules, the previous model's rates WOLD (OLDROT_MOD, saved by STARIN and the rotation routines), DTWIND (NMLPAR_MOD, read from the namelist by PARMIN; REMAP may reset it) and the step-change limit ATIME(13) (NMLPAR_MOD).
  • Who calls it: only HTIMER, which takes the smallest of its candidate steps.
  • Outputs: DELTSW, the rotation-based step, and DWMAX, the largest relative change.

The method. The largest relative change over all shells, $$\delta_\mathrm{max} = \max_i \frac{2\,|\Omega_i - \Omega_i^\mathrm{old}|}{\Omega_i + \Omega_i^\mathrm{old}},$$ gives $f = \delta_\mathrm{max}/$DTWIND, limited to $1/a \le f \le a$ with $a$ = ATIME(13) (MHP 10/14), and $\Delta t_\Omega = \Delta t/f$. If nothing changed ($f = 0$), $\Delta t_\Omega = 10^{20}$, so that rotation does not limit the step.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON/OLDROT/ to OLDROT_MOD, /CT2/ and /CTLIM/ to NMLPAR_MOD (where DTWIND now has the initial value 10), JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. A non-rotating shell. If $\Omega_i = \Omega_i^\mathrm{old} = 0$ in any shell, the ratio is 0/0.
  2. The comment and the limiter. The comment says the change is restricted to "a factor of ATIME(14)%", while the code uses ATIME(13) as a factor (MHP 10/14).
  3. IWMAX (the shell of the largest change) is found but not returned or used.

Limit the step of a rotating model by the largest relative change
of Omega since the previous model: the step is divided by that change
over dtwind, the factor kept within [1/atime(13), atime(13)]; with no
change at all, the step is 1e20 (no limit).
