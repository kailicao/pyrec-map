Purpose

GRSETT computes the gravitational settling of helium (and, with LDIFZ, of the metals) over one time step, "using the formalism of Bahcall and Loeb 1989" (its comment). The code solves for the divergence of a flux, in mass (as LAX_WENDROF1 differences it), $$\frac{\partial X}{\partial t} = 4\pi\,\frac{\partial}{\partial m}\Bigl[D_1 + D_2\,\frac{\partial X}{\partial r}\Bigr],\qquad D_1 = f\,\frac{r^2T^{5/2}}{\ln\Lambda}\,X\,(A_P + A_T\nabla)\,\frac{d\ln P}{dr},\qquad D_2 = f\,\frac{r^2T^{5/2}}{\ln\Lambda}\,A_C,$$ where $r$ is in $R_\odot$ and $T$ in $10^7$ K (Bahcall and Loeb's units), $A_P$, $A_T$, $A_C$ are the coefficients of the pressure, temperature and concentration gradients (AP, AT/$\nabla$, AC, from Thoul et al. with LTHOUL; without it, Bahcall and Loeb's fits in $X$), $f$ the user's factor (below), and $D_1$, $D_2$ are COD1, COD2 from SETUP_GRSETT. The first term is advective (settling at a velocity proportional to $d\ln P/dr$), the second diffusive; the code's comment, a first term "that depends on DLNP/DR*DX/DR" and a second "on D^2X/DR^2", describes them only loosely. The factor $f$ is FGRY for helium and FGRZ for the metals (namelist settings, default 1), and with LTHOUL also CSTMIXING (and CSTDIFFMIX on the metals' settling term), factors added in 10/09 to "mimic mixing to reduce settling" (their $X$-derivatives are not scaled: suspected bug 1). GRSETT treats the first term explicitly with a two-step Lax–Wendroff scheme and the second implicitly, iterating on the coefficients.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: MIX and ROTMIX, the mixing drivers of the evolution step.
  • Inputs: DT0 (time step, s), HCOMP (mass fractions; HCOMP(1) = $X$, HCOMP(2) = $Y$, HCOMP(3) = $Z$), HQPR0 = $d\ln P/dr$, HR = $\log r$, HD = $\log\rho$ (passed on to SETUP_GRSETT, which needs it for the Coulomb logarithm), HS10 = $m$ (g, not logged), HT = $\log T$, LC (convective or not), M, STOT0. GRSETT copies DT0, STOT0, HS10 and HQPR0 into its own DT, STOT, HS1, and HQPR, which SETUP_GRSETT converts to Bahcall and Loeb's units in place and EQUAL_TO_MODEL converts back, so the caller's values are not changed.
  • Output: the new HCOMP(1) and HCOMP(2), and with LDIFZ HCOMP(3) ($Z$, the CNO rows scaled with it) (via EQUAL_TO_MODEL, which uses $X+Y+Z=1$).
  • Calls, in order: SETUP_GRSETT (the coefficients COD1, COD2 and their $X$-derivatives, the convection-zone edges, and whether the model is fully convective), MODEL_TO_EQUAL (to an equally spaced grid in radius), LAX_WENDROF1 and LAX_WENDROF2, then in each iteration GET_IMP_DIFFCO and TRIDIAG_GS, and at the end EQUAL_TO_MODEL.
  • Modules (COMMON blocks until rp_1): NMLPAR_MOD for the settings GRTOL, NITER_GS (formerly /GRAVST/, set by PARMIN) and LDIFZ (formerly /GRAVS3/); GRAVEZ_MOD (the metals on the equal grid, with the Lax–Wendroff pair and the regridding routines); CONSTANTS_MOD; IOUNITS_MOD (the output units). The metals' coefficients COD1Z, COD2Z, QCOD1Z, QCOD2Z (formerly /GRAVSZ/) are now GRSETT's own arrays, filled by SETUP_GRSETT and passed to MODEL_TO_EQUAL.

The method

  1. Set up the coefficients; return at once for a fully convective model. Settling is computed only between the edge of a convective core (or the centre, or the first shell with $X >$ XMIN) and the base of the surface convection zone (or, if helium is too scarce there, the first shell below it with $Y >$ YMIN): IBEGIN and IEND. What lies beyond them is not on the grid; each side acts as one reservoir at the grid's end (the boundary cells of LAX_WENDROF1 hold all the mass beyond the grid), and every shell below IBEGIN (a convective core, or a hydrogen-exhausted region, which may be radiative) and above IEND gets the change found at the grid's end point (step 5); for a convective zone this keeps it homogeneous.
  2. Move to $N$ (NPT) equally spaced radii $r_i$ (step $\Delta r$), with values at the points (E…) and at the midpoints (E…_H). Keep the starting $X_i^0$ (EX_ORIG).

  3. Explicit term. LAX_WENDROF1 gives the half-step changes $\Delta X_{i+1/2}$; the midpoint coefficients are linearized in them, $$F_{i+1/2} \leftarrow F_{i+1/2} + \Delta X_{i+1/2}\,\frac{\partial F}{\partial X}\Big|_{i+1/2},$$ and LAX_WENDROF2 applies the full step, giving $X'_i$ (EX_PRIME).

  4. Implicit term. With $\alpha_i = 4\pi\Delta t/(\Delta r\,\Delta m_i)$ (ALPHA; $\Delta m_i$ the mass between midpoints, $m_{1/2}$ at the centre and $M - m_{N-1/2}$ at the surface), iterate up to NITER_GS times: take the change at the midpoints $\tfrac12(X_i + X_{i-1} - X^p_i - X^p_{i-1})$ since the previous iterate $X^p$; let GET_IMP_DIFFCO build the tridiagonal coefficients $a_i, b_i, c_i$ from ECOD2_H corrected by that change; solve $$a_i X_{i-1} + b_i X_i + c_i X_{i+1} = X'_i$$ with TRIDIAG_GS. With $D_{i+1/2}$ = ECOD2_H(I) (the coefficient between points $i$ and $i+1$), the rows are those of an implicit (backward Euler) step: $$\bigl(1+\alpha_i(D_{i-1/2}+D_{i+1/2})\bigr)X_i - \alpha_i D_{i-1/2}X_{i-1} - \alpha_i D_{i+1/2}X_{i+1} = X'_i,$$ with $D_{1/2} = D_{N+1/2} = 0$ (no flux through the centre or the surface). Stop when $\max_i (X_i - X^p_i) <$ GRTOL, the largest signed change (suspected bug 2); print each iteration's largest change. The change at the midpoints is stored one index off from the coefficients it corrects (suspected bug 1).

    Question 1 (lines 247–253↗). Failure is only reported. When the iteration does not converge in NITER_GS steps, GRSETT writes a message and carries on with the last iterate. Should it stop the run, or shorten the settling step and retry? How often the message appears in the .short files of settling runs would show whether it matters. 5. Return the change $X_i - X_i^0$ (and $Z_i - Z_i^0$), which EQUAL_TO_MODEL interpolates back to the model's points (the change rather than the abundance, "to minimize errors from the interpolation"). ZZ1 and ZZ2, the metals' changes at the two ends (lines 260–261↗), are computed and never read (unused?).

Design

Each iteration writes a line to the short output (ISHORT). The metals get the explicit term only; the implicit iteration is for $X$ alone.

Question 2 (lines 80–82↗; lines 178–185↗; lines 180–181↗). The metals' diffusion term. The header still says "settling of metals will be added later", though metal settling came in 3/94 (LDIFZ), for the explicit term only: SETUP_GRSETT computes the metals' $D_2$ (COD2Z) and MODEL_TO_EQUAL interpolates it to the midpoints (ECOD2Z_H), but nothing reads it. Should the metals also get the implicit term? Comparing the surface $Z$ of a solar model with and without it would show whether it matters.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: GRTOL (default 1.0D-8) is the numerical tolerance on the abundance changes from diffusion, and NITER_GS (10) the largest number of iterations of the solver.

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) The coefficient correction is one midpoint off. (lines 213–215↗; lines 19–21↗) In the iteration, loop 30 sets EX_H(I), for I = 2..NPT, to the change at the midpoint between points I-1 and I; but GET_IMP_DIFFCO adds EQCOD2X_H(I)*EX_H(I) to ECOD2_H(I), the coefficient between points I and I+1 (its matrix uses ECOD2_H(I-1) on the left of point I and ECOD2_H(I) on the right, and MODEL_TO_EQUAL puts ECOD2_H(1) at ER(1) + $\Delta r/2$: line 76↗). ECOD2_H(1) also gets EX_H(1), which loop 30 never sets: it keeps the half-step change from LAX_WENDROF1 (lines 45–47↗), and is added again at every iteration. What follows: Each diffusion coefficient is corrected with its neighbour's change in $X$, and the first one drifts by the same amount per iteration; the size of the effect was not estimated. Only with LDIFY (off by default; set by LDIFZ and LDIFLI) and LNEWDIF false (the default, but LDIFLI sets it true). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

264                  END DO

58def grsett(dt0: float, hcomp: np.ndarray, hqpr0: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hd: np.ndarray, hs10: np.ndarray,
59           ht: np.ndarray, lc: np.ndarray, m: int, stot0: float) -> None:
60    """Settling of helium (and, with LDIFZ, of the metals) in one step.
61
62    Regrids to equally spaced radii, treats the settling term explicitly
63    (two-step Lax-Wendroff) and the diffusion term implicitly (an iterated
64    tridiagonal solve, for X only), and regrids the change back.
65
66    Translates: grsett.f#GRSETT (7be4133)
67
68    Parameters
69    ----------
70    dt0 : float
71        Time step (s). Copied to the local dt, which SETUP_GRSETT rescales
72        to the Bahcall-Loeb units and EQUAL_TO_MODEL restores.
73    hcomp : array
74        Mass fractions; X and Y updated.
75    hqpr0, hr, hd, hs10, ht, lc : arrays
76        d ln P / dr, log r, log rho, m (g), log T, convective flags. HQPR0
77        and HS10 are copied (to hqpr and hs1) and not changed.
78    m : int
79    stot0 : float
80        Total mass; copied to the local stot like ``dt0``.
81
82    Returns nothing.
83
84    """
85
86    gz = gravez_mod
87    nml = nmlpar_mod
88
89    # Work on copies of DT, STOT, HS1 and HQPR (Fortran lines 73–78).
90    dt = dt0
91    stot = stot0
92    for i in range(1, m + 1):
93        hs1[i] = hs10[i]
94        hqpr[i] = hqpr0[i]
95
96    # Coefficients, convection-zone edges, and the fully convective case
97    # (Fortran lines 124–128).
98    dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz = setup_grsett(
99        dt, hqpr, hr, hd, hs1, ht, lc, m, stot, cod1, cod2,
100        hcomp, hru, htu, 0, 0, False, qcod1x, qcod2x, cod1z, cod2z,
101        qcod1z, qcod2z)
102    if lallcz:
103        return
104
105    # To an equally spaced grid in radius (Fortran lines 135–138).
106    dr, npt = model_to_equal(cod1, cod2, hcomp, hru, hs1,
107                             ibegin, iend, m, qcod1x, qcod2x, 0.0,
108                             ecod1, ecod1_h, ecod2_h, em, em_h,
109                             eqcod1x_h, eqcod2x_h, er, ex, ex_h, 0,
110                             cod1z, cod2z, qcod1z, qcod2z)
111
112    # The starting X (and Z) (Fortran lines 145–153).
113    for i in range(1, npt + 1):                          # label 5
114        ex_orig[i] = ex[i]
115    if nml.ldifz:
116        for i in range(1, npt + 1):
117            ez_orig[i] = gz.ez[i]
118
119    # Explicit term, half step (Fortran lines 163–164).
120    ldoli = False
121    lax_wendrof1(dt, ecod1, em, npt, stot, ex_h, ldoli)
122
123    # Midpoint coefficients linearized in the half-step change
124    # (Fortran lines 173–185).
125    for i in range(1, npt):                              # label 10
126        ecod1_h[i] = ecod1_h[i] + ex_h[i] * eqcod1x_h[i]
127        ex_p[i] = ex_orig[i]
128    ex_p[npt] = ex_orig[npt]
129    if nml.ldifz:
130        for i in range(1, npt):
131            gz.ecod1z_h[i] = gz.ecod1z_h[i] + gz.ez_h[i] * gz.eqcod1z_h[i]
132            ez_p[i] = ez_orig[i]
133        ez_p[npt] = ez_orig[npt]
134
135    # Explicit term, full step (Fortran line 193).
136    lax_wendrof2(dt, ecod1_h, em_h, ex, npt, stot, ldoli)
137
138    # Implicit term: the abundance without it (Fortran lines 199–201).
139    for i in range(1, npt + 1):                          # label 15
140        ex_prime[i] = ex[i]
141
142    # ALPHA, the factor in front of the coefficients (Fortran lines
143    # 203–208).
144    fac = constants_mod.c4pi * dt / dr
145    alpha[1] = fac / em_h[1]
146    for i in range(2, npt):                              # label 20
147        alpha[i] = fac / (em_h[i] - em_h[i - 1])
148    alpha[npt] = fac / (stot - em_h[npt - 1])
149
150    # The iteration (Fortran lines 210–247).
151    converged = False
152    for iter_ in range(1, nml.niter_gs + 1):             # label 100
153        # Change at the midpoints since the last iterate (Fortran lines
154        # 213–221). Suspected bug (see the page): EX_H(I) is the change
155        # between points I-1 and I, but GET_IMP_DIFFCO applies it to
156        # ECOD2_H(I), between I and I+1; EX_H(1) is left from
157        # LAX_WENDROF1.
158        for i in range(2, npt + 1):                      # label 30
159            ex_h[i] = 0.5 * (ex[i] + ex[i - 1] - ex_p[i] - ex_p[i - 1])
160        for i in range(1, npt + 1):                      # label 40
161            ex_p[i] = ex[i]
162
163        # New coefficients and the tridiagonal solve (Fortran lines
164        # 225–229).
165        get_imp_diffco(alpha, ecod2_h, ex_h, eqcod2x_h, a, b, c, npt)
166        tridiag_gs(a, b, c, ex_prime, npt, ex)
167
168        # Convergence test (Fortran lines 233–246). Suspected bug (see the
169        # page): the largest signed change, not ABS(DX).
170        dxmax = 0.0
171        imax = 0
172        for i in range(1, npt + 1):                      # label 50
173            dx = ex[i] - ex_p[i]
174            if dx > dxmax:
175                dxmax = dx
176                imax = i
177        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
178                          f" ITERATION {iter_:3d} DXMAX {dxmax:10.2E} "
179                          f"IMAX {imax:4d}")             # format 90
180        if dxmax < nml.grtol:
181            converged = True
182            break                                        # GOTO 120
183
184    # No convergence (Fortran lines 248–252). Question (see the page):
185    # failure is only reported.
186    if not converged:
187        text = (f" GRSETT FAILED TO CONVERGE TO WITHIN {nml.grtol:9.3E} IN "
188                f"{nml.niter_gs:3d}ITERATIONS\n LAST ITERATION CHANGE IN X "
189                f"{dxmax:9.3E} IN EQUALLY SPACED SHELL {imax:5d}")  # format 110
190        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.iowr, text)
191        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort, text)
192
193    # label 120: return the change, not the abundance (Fortran lines
194    # 255–265).
195    for i in range(1, npt + 1):                          # label 130
196        ex[i] = ex[i] - ex_orig[i]
197    if nml.ldifz:
198        zz1 = gz.ez[1] - ez_orig[1]  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
199        zz2 = gz.ez[npt] - ez_orig[npt]  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
200        for i in range(1, npt + 1):
201            gz.ez[i] = gz.ez[i] - ez_orig[i]
202
203    # Back to the model's points, and the units restored (Fortran lines
204    # 269–270).
205    dt, stot = equal_to_model(dt, er, ex, ibegin, iend, npt, hcomp, hqpr,
206                              hru, hs1, htu, m, stot)
