Purpose

GRAVEZ_MOD holds the metals ($Z$) on the equally spaced radial grid of the older settling routine GRSETT, with their diffusion coefficients at the grid's points and midpoints, used with metal diffusion (LDIFZ). Shared by 5 routines. It replaces COMMON/GRAVEZ/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • EZ(JSON): $Z$ at the grid points, interpolated from the model by MODEL_TO_EQUAL, advanced by LAX_WENDROF2, and turned into the change over the step by GRSETT (EZ - EZ_ORIG), which EQUAL_TO_MODEL applies to the model's points.
  • EZ_H(JSON): $Z$ at the midpoints, set by MODEL_TO_EQUAL and by LAX_WENDROF1 (the half step); read by GRSETT.
  • ECOD1Z(JSON): the settling coefficient at the grid points, set by MODEL_TO_EQUAL, read by LAX_WENDROF1.
  • ECOD1Z_H(JSON): the settling coefficient at the midpoints, set by MODEL_TO_EQUAL and updated by GRSETT with the half-step abundances (ECOD1Z_H + EZ_H*EQCOD1Z_H); read by LAX_WENDROF2.
  • EQCOD1Z_H(JSON): the abundance-dependent part of that coefficient, set by MODEL_TO_EQUAL, read by GRSETT.
  • ECOD2Z_H, EQCOD2Z_H (JSON each): the diffusion coefficient and its abundance-dependent part at the midpoints, set by MODEL_TO_EQUAL (lines 105–107↗; lines 180–187↗); read nowhere (unused?), since the metals get only the explicit settling term (see GRSETT's notes).

Design

A blanket USE GRAVEZ_MOD. The hydrogen counterparts (EX, ECOD1, …) are arguments of the same routines; only the metals go through the module.
