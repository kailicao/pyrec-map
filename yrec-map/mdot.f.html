Purpose

MDOT applies a mass change (loss or accretion) to the model over a step: it limits the step so that not too much mass changes, removes mass from the surface convection zone or adds it at the surface, rescales the mass coordinates, the total mass and the shell masses, removes angular momentum with lost mass, and for accretion mixes the accreted composition into the convection zone. Sign convention: DMDT > 0 is a mass gain.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: only MASSLOSS, when LMDOT is true.
  • Inputs and outputs (arguments): DELTS (s, may be shortened), the model (HCOMP, HD, HJM, HP, HR, HS, HS1, HS2, HT, OMEGA, M, JENV), HSTOT and SMASS (updated), DMDT ($M_\odot$/yr), RTOT, SUMDM (the convection zone's mass, from MASSLOSS); EACC, ETHAV and RMU have no effect (unused?; see step 4); outputs LNEW (= true: recompute the surface boundary condition) and SENVOLD (the old HS(M) − HSTOT, for GETNEWENV).
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD, the namelist variables (FCZDMDT, FTOTDMDT, COMPACC, LMDOT, TDISK, LDISK, LROT, WALPCZ, LSOLWIND); MASSCHG2_MOD (SCEN, SMASS0, DLOGP, DLOGT; with MASSLOSS and COEFFT, which adds DLOGT, DLOGP to $\log T$, $\log P$ at and above JENV in its entropy terms when LMDOT and DMDT0 > 0); DISK_MOD (the age SAGE, for the disk lifetime); COMP_MOD (STOTAL, SENV); DEUTER_MOD (FMASSACC, read by LIBURN, DBURN and DBURNM); CONSTANTS_MOD; JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD. The solar-wind parameters DMSUN, DMWSUN, DMWMAX (formerly COMMON MASSCHG3) are now locals set to zero by DATA.

The method

Two masses of the convection zone appear: $M_\mathrm{cz}$ = DMCZ = HS1(M) − HS1(JENV) (or HS1(M) if JENV = 1), and $M'_\mathrm{cz}$ = DMCZT = SUMDM.

  1. Optionally (LSOLWIND with rotation, and only if DMWSUN > 0) $\dot M = \dot M_\odot\,\min\big((\Omega/\Omega_\odot)^2, (\Omega_\mathrm{max}/\Omega_\odot)^2\big)$, with $\dot M_\odot$ = DMSUN, $\Omega_\odot$ = DMWSUN, $\Omega_\mathrm{max}$ = DMWMAX. Since these three are zero, the branch is inert; the guard on DMWSUN keeps it from dividing by zero now that LSOLWIND reaches this routine from the namelist.
  2. Rate in g/s: $|\dot M|\,M_\odot/\mathrm{yr}$. Step limits: $\Delta t \le f_\mathrm{tot}M/|\dot M|$, $\Delta t \le f_\mathrm{cz}\,M_\mathrm{cz}/|\dot M|$ ($f_\mathrm{tot}$ = FTOTDMDT, $f_\mathrm{cz}$ = FCZDMDT; a zero rate sets neither), and at most half the mass outside the fitting point, $\tfrac12(10^{\texttt{HSTOT}} - 10^{\texttt{HS(M)}})/|\dot M|$. The comment speaks of adding mass, but this limit applies to loss as well. With a disk (LDISK) and accretion, the step ends at the disk's lifetime TDISK (Gyr), and LMDOT is then turned off (bug 1). A model without a surface convection zone (JENV = M) stops the run (bug 2).
  3. $\Delta M = \dot M\,\Delta t$ (g).
  4. Accretion ($\Delta M > 0$):
  5. the new mean thermal energy $\bar e = (e_\mathrm{acc}\Delta M + \bar e_\mathrm{th}M'_\mathrm{cz})/(\Delta M + M'_\mathrm{cz})$ and FAC = $\log_{10}(\bar e/\bar e_\mathrm{th})$ are computed but not used (their use is commented out);
  6. RMU = $P\beta/(\rho T)$ (the gas constant over $\mu$) is recomputed at JENV, and a scale factor $\Delta\ln E = (\Delta M/M_\mathrm{cz})\,s_\mathrm{cen}/(\mathcal{R}/\mu)$, with $s_\mathrm{cen}$ = SCEN, gives $\Delta\log r = \tfrac23\Delta\ln E/\ln 10$, $\Delta\log T = -\Delta\log r$, $\Delta\log P = -4\Delta\log r$ (homologous expansion of the envelope: $P \propto r^{-4}$, $T \propto r^{-1}$ at fixed $m$; the mass term DLNM is set to zero);
  7. if JENV = 1 (fully convective), $\log r$, $\log P$, $\log T$ of every point change by these amounts; otherwise only $P$ and $T$ at JENV, and the radii above it are stretched about $r_\mathrm{b} = r_\mathrm{JENV}$: $r \leftarrow r_\mathrm{b} + 10^{\Delta\log r}(r - r_\mathrm{b})$.

MASSLOSS sets SCEN = 0, so these changes are zero in practice: The code is kept for later work, as part of the experimental mass-loss section, "not finished", not "dead" (settled from KNOWN_ISSUES.md, issue 12, Nov 9, 2026).

> **Question** ([lines 173–174](#L173-L174)<a class='gh' href='https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mdot.f#L173-L174' title='these lines on GitHub'>↗</a>). **The form of the expansion.** A commented-out line scaled the expansion by `SACC - SCEN`, the entropy of the accreted matter relative to the envelope's; the active one by `SCEN` alone. An expansion scaled by `SCEN` alone depends on the units, since the entropy is defined only up to an additive constant (it contains $\ln T$ and $\ln\rho$ in cgs). When the code is revived, is `SACC - SCEN` the intended form?

  1. Angular momentum (rotation, loss): the lost matter carries the surface's specific angular momentum, $\Delta J = \tfrac23 R^2\,\Omega_s\,\Delta M$ (solid-body zone, WALPCZ ≥ 0), or $j_s\Delta M$ (WALPCZ ≤ −2), or the same with $\Omega_s = \Omega_M (R/r_M)^{\texttt{WALPCZ}}$, the law $\Omega \propto r^{\texttt{WALPCZ}}$ that WCZIMP imposes; the zone's new angular momentum is $J_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta J$, with $J_\mathrm{cz} = \sum$ HJM·HS2 from JENV to M. Its specific angular momentum HJM is scaled after step 7, once the new shell masses are known, by $(J_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta J)/\sum$ HJM·HS2, so that the zone holds exactly $J_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta J$.

    Question 1 (line 206↗; line 280↗). Angular momentum of accreted matter. For accretion HJM is not changed while HS2(M) grows, so the accreted matter implicitly takes the outer point's specific angular momentum. Is that intended?

  2. Mass coordinates (loss): points in the zone are compressed toward its base, $m_i \leftarrow m_\mathrm{b} + \frac{M_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta M}{M_\mathrm{cz}}(m_i - m_\mathrm{b})$; in a fully convective star (JENV = 1), toward the center, $m_i \leftarrow \frac{M_\mathrm{cz}+\Delta M}{M_\mathrm{cz}}\,m_i$ for every point, consistent with $M_\mathrm{cz}$ = HS1(M) there. For accretion HS1 is not changed: the gain goes to the outermost shell.

    Question 2 (lines 240–258↗; line 280↗). All accreted mass in the last shell. For accretion HS1 is unchanged and the gain goes to HS2(M), which over many steps grows unless rezoning redistributes it. The main program calls HPOINT after every step (except in helium-flash runs, LKUTHE). Does its criterion split a growing outermost shell, so that the surface resolution survives long accretion?

  3. New total mass (SMASS, SMASS0, HSTOT, STOTAL), shell masses HS2 from JENV to M − 1 as half the distance between neighbours (for JENV = 1, HS2(1) = (HS1(1) + HS1(2))/2, the innermost shell running from the center), HS2(M) = $10^{\texttt{HSTOT}}$ − (HS1(M) + HS1(M−1))/2, and SENV.

  4. Accretion: elements 1–11 of the zone are mixed with the accreted composition COMPACC, $\bar x = \big(\sum_j x_j\,\Delta m_j + x_\mathrm{acc}\Delta M\big)/\big(\sum_j \Delta m_j+\Delta M\big)$, summed over the zone's shells JENV to M with their masses before the accretion ($\Delta m_j$ = HS2(J), less $\Delta M$ for HS2(M)), so that every species is conserved; FMASSACC = $\Delta M$.

Design

Several unconditional WRITE(*,…) lines report the step reduction, the angular momentum and the mass change.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options lists LMDOT, "experimental mass-loss module", and LSOLWIND, mass loss scaled to the solar wind, "not implemented", among the experimental options.

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) Accretion past the disk's lifetime gives a negative step. (lines 146–157↗; line 981↗) With LDISK and accretion, TEST = SAGE + 1.0d-9*DELTS/CSECYR is the age at the end of the step, and when it exceeds TDISK the step is cut to DELTS = (TDISK-SAGE)*1.0D9*CSECYR. If SAGE already exceeds TDISK, this is negative: DELM = DMDT*CMSUN*DELTS/CSECYR is then negative, so the accretion is applied as a loss, and the main program goes on with a negative step (what it then does was not traced). Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; MASSCHG3 dropped (its three reals now zeroed locals, LSOLWIND from the namelist); the solar-wind branch guarded by DMWSUN > 0 (a bug fix: it would have given $\dot M$ = NaN). The call from MASSLOSS now matches the interface (fixed in massloss.f), which settles former question 1; former question 2 (MASSCHG3 set nowhere) is settled by the authors' fix, which confirms it and keeps the option inert until the three parameters are given a source.

Changed since a8a5950. Five fixes in the experimental mass-change path: a zero rate no longer divides by zero in the step limits; the wind's surface rate now follows $\Omega \propto r^{\texttt{WALPCZ}}$, the law WCZIMP imposes (the old form had the ratio inverted), which settles former question 5; HJM is now scaled after the new shell masses are known, so that the zone loses exactly $\Delta J$, which settles former question 6; in a fully convective star the mass loss compresses the points about the center, and HS2(1) is (HS1(1) + HS1(2))/2 instead of reading HS1(0), which settles former question 4; accretion mixes the zone's mass-weighted abundances instead of the base shell's. Former question 7 is now 4.

25    """Apply a mass change (loss, or accretion if dmdt > 0) to the model

62    # lines 118–123).

72    # star (FTOTDMDT) (Fortran lines 128–145).

200        for j in range(jenv, m + 1):
201            cjsum = cjsum + hjm[j] * hs2[j]
202        ratio = cjnew / cjsum
203        for j in range(jenv, m + 1):
