Purpose

"Equal to model": the last step of MICRODIFF. It interpolates the changes in abundance from the equally spaced grid back to the model points, applies them to HCOMP, recomputes helium, and converts the variables changed by MICRODIFF_SETUP back to cgs units.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the equal grid radii ER, the changes EDELX, EDELZ, EDELLI from MICRODIFF_RUN, IBEGIN, IEND, NPT, and the conversion factors in CONFAC_MOD (written by MICRODIFF_SETUP).
  • Who calls it: only MICRODIFF.
  • What it calls: INTRP2, for four-point Lagrange weights.
  • Outputs: HCOMP updated in place; HRU, HTU, HS1, HQPR, DT and STOT converted back (lines 165–172↗), though MICRODIFF passes its local HRU and HTU and copies of the other four, and returns right after the call (lines 112–117↗; lines 242–244↗): unused?
  • Modules: CONFAC_MOD, NMLPAR_MOD (LDIFZ, LDIFLI), DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

  1. Central region (points 1..IBEGIN, the convective core or the hydrogen-exhausted centre): the change at the first grid point is added to each point: $X \to \max(X + \Delta X_1, 0)$, the light elements likewise floored at zero, and $Z \to \min(Z + \Delta Z_1,\ 1 - X - X_3)$.
  2. Radiative interior (points IBEGIN+1..IEND-1): the change is interpolated with four-point Lagrange weights $w_k$ over the grid points around the model radius, $\Delta X(r) = \sum_k w_k\,\Delta X_{k_0+k-1}$, and $X \to \min(X + \Delta X, 1 - Z - X_3)$.

  3. Envelope (points IEND..M): the change at the last grid point is added, with $X$ capped as above and $Z$ floored at zero.

    Question 1 (lines 47–48↗; lines 104–111↗; lines 135–142↗). The guard comment. The comment says the failsafes guard "against negative X and Z>1 respectively", and so they do in the centre ($X \ge 0$, $Z \le 1 - X - X_3$); but in the interior $X$ and $Z$ are both capped from above, and in the envelope $X$ is capped from above and $Z$ floored at zero. Which guards are intended where?

  4. Metals are scaled as a whole: with $Z' = Z + \Delta Z$ (capped at $1 - X - X_3$ in the centre and the interior), the individual metal rows 5–11 of HCOMP (¹²C, ¹³C, ¹⁴N, ¹⁵N, ¹⁶O, ¹⁷O, ¹⁸O, as STARIN names them) are multiplied by $Z'/Z$.

  5. Helium follows from closure: $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_3$, where $X_3$ is ³He, HCOMP(4,·) (STARIN fills it from XHE3_INI).

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) Light elements in the interior and the envelope are not floored at zero. (lines 66–74↗; lines 122–131↗; lines 156–163↗) The light elements (HCOMP(13..15,·)) are floored at zero only in the centre (MAX(…,XMINIM)); in the interior (HCOMP(II,I) + DXMOD, with DXMOD from four-point Lagrange interpolation, which can overshoot) and in the envelope the change is added with no floor. What follows: where an element is nearly depleted, its abundance can become negative and stays in the model. Only with LNEWDIF and LDIFLI (both off by default; the test suite's settling decks set them); whether a run produces a negative value was not traced. Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

C code changed to floor the light elements at zero in the interior

...
               HCOMP(II,I)=MAX(HCOMP(II,I) + EDELLI(KK,NPT),0.0D0)

  2. (b) Division by zero where $Z = 0$. (line 53↗; line 112↗; line 143↗) ZZ2 = ZZ/HCOMP(3,I) divides by zero at a point with $Z = 0$; with IEEE arithmetic it gives an infinity or NaN rather than a stop, and the metal rows 5–11 (zero there) become NaN. Only with LNEWDIF and LDIFZ and a model with $Z = 0$ somewhere (a metal-free model). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix (the same at the three places; where $Z = 0$ the rows 5–11 stay zero):
