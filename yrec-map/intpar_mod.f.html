Purpose

INTPAR_MOD holds the settings of the Bulirsch–Stoer integration of the atmosphere and envelope, shared by PARMIN, ENVINT and BSSTEP. It replaces COMMON/INTPAR/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), the first of the conversion's DATA migrations: the three defaults come from PARMIN's DATA STOLR0,IMAX,NUSE/1.0D-3,11,7/.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All three are in the PHYSICS namelist read by PARMIN, so a run can change them; no routine assigns them.

  • STOLR0 (REAL*8, 1.0D-3): the integration tolerance. ENVINT uses it as such for the atmosphere and scales it by $|$SENV$|$ for the envelope.
  • IMAX (INTEGER*4, 11): the largest number of step subdivisions BSSTEP tries.
  • NUSE (INTEGER*4, 7): the number of points used in BSSTEP's extrapolation to zero step (passed to RATEXT), and the subdivision count that decides the next step.

Design
