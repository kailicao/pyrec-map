Explanation

Purpose. XTIME gives the time step allowed by hydrogen burning (its comment). While the central hydrogen abundance is at least ATIME(1), the step is the time to burn a given amount of the core's hydrogen; afterwards, in shell burning, the smaller of the time to burn a fraction of the star's hydrogen and the time to deplete a given amount of $X$ at the shell's midpoint.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the model (arguments: $\rho$, HCOMP, $L$, $m$, shell masses, $T$), the hydrogen-burning luminosity HYDLUM, the convective core's edge JCORE, the shell's midpoint JXMID; through modules the limits ATIME (NMLPAR_MOD), the constants (CONSTANTS_MOD).
  • Who calls it: only HTIMER.
  • What it calls: RATES and EQBURN, for $dX/dt$ at the shell's midpoint.
  • Output: DELTSH, in seconds (by its use in HTIMER).
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (formerly COMMON/CTLIM/), CONSTANTS_MOD, DIMENSIONS_MOD.

The method. With $q = 6.0\times10^{18}$ erg released per gram of hydrogen burned (the code's constant):

  • Core burning ($X_c \ge$ ATIME(1)): $\Delta X = \min(\texttt{ATIME(2)},\ \texttt{ATIME(3)}\,X_\mathrm{core})$ and $$\Delta t = \frac{q}{L_\odot}\,\frac{m_\mathrm{core}}{L(m_\mathrm{core})/L_\odot}\,\Delta X,$$ with $m$ and $L$ at JCORE, so that a convective core counts as a whole (the comment).
  • Shell burning: $\Delta t = q\,\texttt{ATIME(6)}\,X_s M_\odot/L_\odot\,/\,L_H$, with $X_s$ the surface $X$ and $L_H$ = HYDLUM; and, if $dX/dt < 0$ at the shell's midpoint and ATIME(7) > 0, $$\Delta t = \min\!\left(\Delta t,\ \left|\frac{\texttt{ATIME(7)}}{dX/dt}\right|\,\mathrm{CSECYR}\times10^9\right).$$

KC's changes. Removed the unused argument JXBEG, and shortened the calls to RATES and EQBURN to their new argument lists (2025-05-31).

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: /CTLIM/ to NMLPAR_MOD, the constants to CONSTANTS_MOD; the unused /CT3/ and /FLAG/ dropped; IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. The star's mass or the Sun's? In shell burning, the hydrogen mass is ATIME(6)*HCOMP(1,M)*CMSUN, one solar mass, whatever the star's mass. The comment says "the mass fraction ATIME(6) of X in the entire star". Should it use the star's mass?
  2. Units of $dX/dt$. The second limit multiplies by CSECYR*1.0D9, as if EQBURN returned $dX/dt$ per Gyr. Is that its unit?
  3. The core's hydrogen. The comment says the core criterion burns "the fraction ATIME(3) of the central X"; the code uses $X$ at JCORE (the core's edge), which equals the centre's only for a mixed core.

Python version: xtime
The hydrogen-burning time step: while the central X is at least
atime(1), the time to burn a given amount of the core's hydrogen;
afterwards the time to burn a fraction atime(6) of the hydrogen, and,
if X falls at the shell's midpoint jxmid, at most the time to deplete
atime(7) of X there.

(index 0 unused); the hydrogen-burning luminosity hydlum; jcore, the
convective core's edge; jxmid, the shell's midpoint; m, the number of
shells; deltsh is passed in with any value; hr1..hr13, hf1, hf2, the
rate arrays that rates fills (at element jxmid).
