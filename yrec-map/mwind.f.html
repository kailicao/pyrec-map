Purpose

Removes angular momentum from the surface convection zone by a magnetized stellar wind over one time step. If LMWIND is false it hands over to the older wind law in WIND; otherwise it uses the wind law of Matt et al. (2012), as G. Somers' comments (3/17) call it, with a centrifugal correction from Matt et al. (2012, ApJ 754, L26; MHP 8/17) and optional Rossby scaling.

How it fits

  • Inputs: $\log L/L_\odot$ (BL), the step DELTS (s), the wind switch LJDOT (no angular momentum is removed when it is false), the masses at the top and bottom of the convection zone (HSTOP, HSBOT), its shells JSTART..JEND, the surface angular velocity OMEGAS, the mass in solar units SMASS (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: $f$ divides by SMASS and CMSUN, line 140↗, and mdot.f, line 262↗ updates it in solar masses), $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ (TEFFL), the zone's moment of inertia HICZ, and the specific angular momentum HJM. Wind parameters (LMWIND, LROSSBY, LBSCALE, WMAX, EXW, CONSTFACTOR, EXCEN, PMMSOLW, PMMSOLTAU) are namelist inputs in NMLPAR_MOD, read by PARMIN; the constant C_2 is in CONSTANTS_MOD (set in PARMIN); the convective turnover times at the start and end of the step and the fraction of the step (TAUCZ0, TAUCZ, FRACSTEP) come from OVRTRN_MOD (AMCALC also reads the photospheric pressures there).
  • Who calls it: GETW and SECULR.
  • What it calls: WIND (old law) or AMCALC, which returns the structure factor STRUCTFACTOR as an output argument.
  • Output: HJM of the convection zone's shells, reduced.
  • Modules: DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON), OVRTRN_MOD, NMLPAR_MOD and CONSTANTS_MOD. NMLPAR_MOD holds WNEW, the name the routine uses for the new angular velocity, so MWIND overwrites the input WNEW (bug 1).

The method

  1. The switch. With LJDOT false, nothing is removed.

  2. Rossby scaling (LROSSBY): the turnover time is interpolated in the step, $\tau = \tau_0 + f\,(\tau_1 - \tau_0)$ (FRACSTEP). With LBSCALE, the rotation rate used in the saturation term is scaled, $\Omega' = \Omega\,\tau/\tau_\odot$; otherwise the saturation threshold is scaled, $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}' = \Omega_\mathrm{sat}\,\tau_\odot/\tau$. LBSCALE acts only with LROSSBY.

    Question 1 (lines 90–98↗). $\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ = 0. With LROSSBY, WSAT = WMAX*PMMSOLTAU/TCZ, and with LROSSBY and LBSCALE, WNOW = OMEGAS*TCZ/PMMSOLTAU. GETTAU sets TAUCZ = 0 first (gettau.f, line 79↗), and TAUINTNEW (tauintnew.f, line 279↗) and MASSLOSS (massloss.f, line 120↗) set it to 0 when they find no surface convection zone. MWIND's callers call it only with a surface convection zone (LCZSUR), but GETTAU and the callers judge the zone differently. Can MWIND be reached with TAUCZ and TAUCZ0 both 0, for example as a surface convection zone appears or vanishes, giving an infinite saturation threshold (a division by zero) or $\Omega' = 0$ (no torque)? Should it guard against it?

  3. The structure factor from AMCALC: $S = (M/M_\odot)^{a_M}\,(R/R_\odot)^{a_R}\,(L/L_\odot)^{a_L}\,(P_\mathrm{phot}/P_{\mathrm{phot},\odot})^{a_P}\,(\tau/\tau_\odot)^{a_\tau}$.

  4. The centrifugal factor, relative to the Sun, with $f = \Omega^2 R^3/(2GM)$ and $R$ from $L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$: $$F_\mathrm{cen} = \left(\frac{c_2^2 + f_\odot}{c_2^2 + f}\right)^{e}\quad(c_2 = \texttt{C\_2},\ e = \texttt{EXCEN}),$$ where $f_\odot = \Omega_\odot^2R_\odot^3/(2GM_\odot)$ with $\Omega_\odot$ = PMMSOLW. The constant $c_2$ = 0.0506 (set in PARMIN) and $f$, which is $\tfrac12 f_\mathrm{M}^2$ for Matt et al.'s $f_\mathrm{M} = \Omega R^{3/2}/(GM)^{1/2}$, follow the centrifugal correction of Matt et al. (2012), written in PARMIN's comment as $\{K_2^2/(K_2^2 + 0.5\,\Omega^2R^3/GM)\}$ raised to a power.

  5. The step. The torque law is $dJ/dt = -K\,S\,\Omega\,\min(\Omega', \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{a_\Omega-1}F_\mathrm{cen}$: $\propto\Omega^{a_\Omega}$ unsaturated ($a_\Omega$ = 3; the YREC paper, §2.6.4, says of the Kawaler law that "the torque for slow rotators therefore scales as $\Omega^3$"; the spin-down this gives is asked below) and $\propto\Omega$ saturated; with LBSCALE, $\propto \Omega\,(\Omega\tau/\tau_\odot)^{a_\Omega-1}$, a dependence on $\mathrm{Ro}^{-1}$. PARMIN sets EXW $= 1 + a - 2am + 4mb$ from the law's exponents (PMMA, PMMM, PMMB), which is 3 for both V13 and K97 (with $a = 2$, $b = 1$ the terms in $m$ cancel); only CUS can give another value. The loop's comment, "THE CONSTANT AND EXPONENTS ARE SET IN PARMIN BASED ON THE INPUT INDEX ALFA" (lines 158–159↗), describes the old law: PARMIN uses ALFA only when LMWIND is off (parmin.f, lines 1024–1040↗), and sets EXW for this law from PMMA, PMMM, PMMB (parmin.f, line 1092↗) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). The convection zone spins down as $$\Omega_\mathrm{new} = \Omega_s - \frac{\Delta t}{I_\mathrm{cz}}\;K\,S\;\Omega\;\min(\Omega', \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{\,a_\Omega - 1}\;F_\mathrm{cen},$$ with $K$ = CONSTFACTOR, $a_\Omega$ = EXW. If the first guess of the change exceeds $\Omega_s$, the step is split into $\lfloor \Delta\Omega/\Omega_s \rfloor + 1$ substeps, so $\Omega$ stays positive. In each, $\Omega$ in the loss term is iterated as the mean of the start and the previous end value (the mean of the explicit and implicit solutions), until the relative change is below $10^{-6}$ or 20 iterations. The new rate is stored in WNEW, which is also a namelist variable (bug 1).

    Question 2 (lines 164–200↗). Silent non-convergence. After 21 evaluations without convergence (lines 194–197↗), the substep continues with the last value, without a warning. Should the routine warn, or cut the substep?

    Question 3 (lines 142–143↗; lines 186–187↗; parmin.f, line 1092↗). The spin-down law. With $a_\Omega$ = 3, $dJ/dt \propto \Omega^3$ unsaturated; at fixed moment of inertia that integrates to $\Omega \propto t^{-1/2}$, the Skumanich law. Is matching the Skumanich spin-down the reason both presets give EXW = 3, and does a changing moment of inertia on the pre-main sequence or the subgiant branch spoil the match?

  6. The loss. $\Delta j = (\Omega_s - \Omega_\mathrm{new})\,I_\mathrm{cz}/\Delta M_\mathrm{cz}$ is subtracted from every shell of the zone.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Angular momentum loss & winds): LJDOT0 turns on angular-momentum loss and LMWIND the wind model of Matt et al. (2012); AWIND chooses the law: V13 (van Saders & Pinsonneault 2013), K97 (the legacy Krishnamurthi et al. 1997) or CUS, custom, which alone reads PMMA…PMMM. FK scales the loss, usually to reproduce the Sun's rotation at its age. LROSSBY (default true) scales the loss with the Rossby number, and should go with a saturation threshold: WMAX (rad/s), replaced by WMAX_SUN $\times\,\Omega_\odot$ when WMAX_SUN is below 1000. PMMSOLTAU is the Sun's convective overturn time, and PMMSOLP $\log P$ at $T_\mathrm{eff}$ in a solar model, for the field's calibration.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only, apart from one call: COMMON blocks to modules (/CWIND/ retired, its namelist members and those of /PMMWIND/ to NMLPAR_MOD, C_2 to CONSTANTS_MOD; /CONST/…/CONST3/ merged into CONSTANTS_MOD; /DEUTER/ and the character AWIND dropped), IMPLICIT NONE; AMCALC now returns STRUCTFACTOR as a fourth argument instead of setting it in /CWIND/. /ROT/ is no longer declared, but WNEW now comes from NMLPAR_MOD, so question 5 (now bug 1) stands.

Changed since a8a5950. With the Matt et al. law, the routine now returns without removing angular momentum when LJDOT is false, as WIND does (SECULR calls it whatever LJDOT0 is, so before, a run without the wind still lost angular momentum here); the extra field scaling by TCZ in the iteration now acts only with LROSSBY, as in the scaling above it (before, LBSCALE without LROSSBY used TCZ, which is set only under LROSSBY, and gave no torque), which settles former question 1; former questions 2–7 are now 1–6.

12# Fortran SAVEs its locals. TCZ is set only when LROSSBY is true, and is
13# read only then.
14_tcz = 0.0

17def mwind(bl: float, delts: float, hsbot: float, hstop: float, jstart: int, jend: int, ljdot: bool, omegas: float,
18          smass: float, teffl: float, hicz: float, hjm: np.ndarray) -> None:
19    """Remove angular momentum from the surface convection zone by a wind.
20
21    Uses the magnetized-wind law of Matt et al. (2012) with their
22    centrifugal correction and optional Rossby scaling, over one time step
23    (split into substeps if needed); hands over to WIND (the older law)
24    if LMWIND is false. The loss of specific angular momentum is
25    subtracted from every shell of the zone.
26
27    Translates: mwind.f#MWIND (7be4133)
28
29    Parameters
30    ----------
31    bl : float
32        log10 L/Lsun.
33    delts : float
34        Time step (s).
35    hsbot, hstop : float
36        Masses at the bottom and top of the convection zone.
37    jstart, jend : int
38        First and last shells of the zone.
39    ljdot : bool
40        The wind switch: no angular momentum is removed when it is false.
41    omegas : float
42        Surface angular velocity at the start of the step.
43    smass : float
44        Mass in solar units.
45    teffl : float
46        log10 Teff.
47    hicz : float
48        Moment of inertia of the zone.
49    hjm : ndarray, shape (JSON + 1,)
50        Specific angular momentum, changed in place for shells
51        ``jstart..jend``.
52
53    Notes
54    -----
55    Sets ``nmlpar_mod.wnew`` (the new surface angular velocity), as the
56    Fortran does through the module.
57
58    """
59
60    global _tcz
61    c = constants_mod
62    nml = nmlpar_mod
63
64    # If desired, remove angular momentum using a Weber-Davis wind model;
65    # otherwise use the old law (Fortran lines 76–81).
66    if not nml.lmwind:
67        wind(bl, delts, hsbot, hstop, jstart, jend, ljdot, omegas,
68             smass, teffl, hicz, hjm)
69        return  # label 9999
70
71    # Honour the wind switch, as WIND does (Fortran line 85).
72    if not ljdot:
73        return  # label 9999
74
75    # Rossby scaling, if desired; tau_cz interpolated within the step
76    # (Fortran lines 88–103).
77    # Question (see the page): tcz may be 0.
78    if nml.lrossby:
79        _tcz = (ovrtrn_mod.taucz0
80                + ovrtrn_mod.fracstep * (ovrtrn_mod.taucz - ovrtrn_mod.taucz0))
81        if nml.lbscale:
82            wnow = omegas * _tcz / nml.pmmsoltau
83            wsat = nml.wmax
84        else:
85            wnow = omegas
86            wsat = nml.wmax * nml.pmmsoltau / _tcz
87    else:
88        wnow = omegas
89        wsat = nml.wmax
90
91    # The structure factor of the Matt et al. (2012) law (Fortran line
92    # 120).
93    structfactor = amcalc(smass, bl, teffl, 0.0)
94
95    # Centrifugal reduction term (Matt et al. 2012, ApJ 754, L26),
96    # relative to the Sun. C_2 = 0.0506 (Fortran lines 137–141).
97    fsun = (0.5 * nml.pmmsolw ** 2 * c.crsun ** 3
98            / math.exp(c.cln * c.cgl) / c.cmsun)
99    # Radius:
100    rl = 0.5 * (bl + c.clsunl - c.c4pil - c.csigl - 4.0 * teffl)
101    fcorr = (0.5 * omegas ** 2 * math.exp(c.cln * (3.0 * rl - c.cgl))
102             / smass / c.cmsun)
103    fcen = ((c.c_2 ** 2 + fsun) / (c.c_2 ** 2 + fcorr)) ** nml.excen
104
105    # First guess of the change in Omega; if it exceeds Omega, use
106    # several smaller steps so that Omega stays positive (Fortran lines
107    # 142–153).
108    dwtest = ((delts / hicz) * nml.constfactor * structfactor * omegas
109              * min(wnow, wsat) ** (nml.exw - 1.0) * fcen)
110    if dwtest > omegas:
111        nstep = int(dwtest / omegas) + 1
112        dt = delts / float(nstep)
113    else:
114        nstep = 1
115        dt = delts
116
117    # Iterative solution in each substep: first guess uses the starting
118    # Omega; then the average of the starting and the previous ending
119    # Omega (Fortran lines 156–200).
120    ws = omegas
121    for n in range(1, nstep + 1):  # label 100
122        k = 0
123        w = ws
124        wnew0 = ws
125        while True:  # label 5
126            if nml.lrossby and nml.lbscale:
127                wnow = w * _tcz / nml.pmmsoltau
128            else:
129                wnow = w
130            k = k + 1
131            fcorr = (0.5 * w ** 2 * math.exp(c.cln * (3.0 * rl - c.cgl))
132                     / smass / c.cmsun)
133            fcen = ((c.c_2 ** 2 + fsun) / (c.c_2 ** 2 + fcorr)) ** nml.excen
134            # Suspected bug (see the page): WNEW is the namelist input in
135            # nmlpar_mod, overwritten here.
136            nml.wnew = (ws - (dt / hicz) * nml.constfactor * structfactor
137                        * w * min(wnow, wsat) ** (nml.exw - 1.0) * fcen)
138            dw = 2.0 * abs((wnew0 - nml.wnew) / (wnew0 + nml.wnew))
139            if dw > 1.0e-6:
140                w = 0.5 * (ws + nml.wnew)
141                wnew0 = nml.wnew
142                # Question (see the page): after 21 passes the loop ends
143                # without a warning.
144                if k <= 20:
145                    continue
146            break
147        ws = nml.wnew
148
149    # DM is the total mass in the convection zone; subtract the change in
150    # angular momentum per unit mass from every shell of the zone (Fortran
151    # lines 205–215).
152    dm = hstop - hsbot
153    fjdom = (omegas - nml.wnew) * hicz / dm
154    for ii in range(jstart, jend + 1):  # label 10
155        hjm[ii] = hjm[ii] - fjdom
