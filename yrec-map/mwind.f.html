Explanation

Purpose. Removes angular momentum from the surface convection zone by a magnetized stellar wind over one time step. If LMWIND is false it hands over to the older wind law in WIND; otherwise it uses the wind law of Matt et al. (2012), as G. Somers' comments (3/17) call it, with a centrifugal correction from Matt et al. (2012, ApJ 754, L26; MHP 8/17) and optional Rossby scaling.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: $\log L/L_\odot$ (BL), the step DELTS (s), the masses at the top and bottom of the convection zone (HSTOP, HSBOT), its shells JSTART..JEND, the surface angular velocity OMEGAS, the mass in solar units SMASS, $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ (TEFFL), the zone's moment of inertia HICZ, and the specific angular momentum HJM. Wind parameters (LMWIND, LROSSBY, LBSCALE, WMAX, EXW, CONSTFACTOR, EXCEN, PMMSOLW, PMMSOLTAU) are namelist inputs in NMLPAR_MOD, read by PARMIN; the constant C_2 is in CONSTANTS_MOD (set in PARMIN); the convective turnover times at the start and end of the step and the fraction of the step (TAUCZ0, TAUCZ, FRACSTEP) come from OVRTRN_MOD (AMCALC also reads the photospheric pressures there).
  • Who calls it: GETW and SECULR.
  • What it calls: WIND (old law) or AMCALC, which returns the structure factor STRUCTFACTOR as an output argument.
  • Output: HJM of the convection zone's shells, reduced.
  • Modules: DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON), OVRTRN_MOD, NMLPAR_MOD and CONSTANTS_MOD. NMLPAR_MOD holds WNEW, the name the routine uses for the new angular velocity, so MWIND overwrites the input WNEW (question 5).

The method.

  1. Rossby scaling (LROSSBY): the turnover time is interpolated in the step, $\tau = \tau_0 + f\,(\tau_1 - \tau_0)$ (FRACSTEP). With LBSCALE, the rotation rate used in the saturation term is scaled, $\Omega' = \Omega\,\tau/\tau_\odot$; otherwise the saturation threshold is scaled, $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}' = \Omega_\mathrm{sat}\,\tau_\odot/\tau$.
  2. The structure factor from AMCALC: $S = (M/M_\odot)^{a_M}\,(R/R_\odot)^{a_R}\,(L/L_\odot)^{a_L}\,(P_\mathrm{phot}/P_{\mathrm{phot},\odot})^{a_P}\,(\tau/\tau_\odot)^{a_\tau}$.

  3. The centrifugal factor, relative to the Sun, with $f = \Omega^2 R^3/(2GM)$ and $R$ from $L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$: $$F_\mathrm{cen} = \left(\frac{c_2^2 + f_\odot}{c_2^2 + f}\right)^{e}\quad(c_2 = \texttt{C\_2},\ e = \texttt{EXCEN}),$$ where $f_\odot = \Omega_\odot^2R_\odot^3/(2GM_\odot)$ with $\Omega_\odot$ = PMMSOLW. The constant $c_2$ = 0.0506 (set in PARMIN) and $f$, which is $\tfrac12 f_\mathrm{M}^2$ for Matt et al.'s $f_\mathrm{M} = \Omega R^{3/2}/(GM)^{1/2}$, follow the centrifugal correction of Matt et al. (2012), written in PARMIN's comment as $\{K_2^2/(K_2^2 + 0.5\,\Omega^2R^3/GM)\}$ raised to a power.

  4. The step. The torque law is $dJ/dt = -K\,S\,\Omega\,\min(\Omega', \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{a_\Omega-1}F_\mathrm{cen}$: $\propto\Omega^{a_\Omega}$ unsaturated ($a_\Omega$ = 3; the YREC paper, §2.6.4, says of the Kawaler law that "the torque for slow rotators therefore scales as $\Omega^3$"; the spin-down this gives is question 7) and $\propto\Omega$ saturated; with LBSCALE, $\propto \Omega\,(\Omega\tau/\tau_\odot)^{a_\Omega-1}$, a dependence on $\mathrm{Ro}^{-1}$. PARMIN sets EXW $= 1 + a - 2am + 4mb$ from the law's exponents (PMMA, PMMM, PMMB), which is 3 for both V13 and K97 (with $a = 2$, $b = 1$ the terms in $m$ cancel); only CUS can give another value. The convection zone spins down as $$\Omega_\mathrm{new} = \Omega_s - \frac{\Delta t}{I_\mathrm{cz}}\;K\,S\;\Omega\;\min(\Omega', \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{\,a_\Omega - 1}\;F_\mathrm{cen},$$ with $K$ = CONSTFACTOR, $a_\Omega$ = EXW. If the first guess of the change exceeds $\Omega_s$, the step is split into $\lfloor \Delta\Omega/\Omega_s \rfloor + 1$ substeps, so $\Omega$ stays positive. In each, $\Omega$ in the loss term is iterated as the mean of the start and the previous end value (the mean of the explicit and implicit solutions), until the relative change is below $10^{-6}$ or 20 iterations.

  5. The loss. $\Delta j = (\Omega_s - \Omega_\mathrm{new})\,I_\mathrm{cz}/\Delta M_\mathrm{cz}$ is subtracted from every shell of the zone.

KC's changes. 2025-05-30: the elements of /PMMWIND/ reordered (the character variable AWIND moved to the end); 2025-05-31: the unused argument SJTOT removed from the routine and from the call to WIND.

From YREC's documentation. Physics Options (Angular momentum loss & winds): LJDOT0 turns on angular-momentum loss and LMWIND the wind model of Matt et al. (2012); AWIND chooses the law: V13 (van Saders & Pinsonneault 2013), K97 (the legacy Krishnamurthi et al. 1997) or CUS, custom, which alone reads PMMA…PMMM. FK scales the loss, usually to reproduce the Sun's rotation at its age. LROSSBY (default true) scales the loss with the Rossby number, and should go with a saturation threshold: WMAX (rad/s), replaced by WMAX_SUN $\times\,\Omega_\odot$ when WMAX_SUN is below 1000. PMMSOLTAU is the Sun's convective overturn time, and PMMSOLP $\log P$ at $T_\mathrm{eff}$ in a solar model, for the field's calibration.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only, apart from one call: COMMON blocks to modules (/CWIND/ retired, its namelist members and those of /PMMWIND/ to NMLPAR_MOD, C_2 to CONSTANTS_MOD; /CONST/…/CONST3/ merged into CONSTANTS_MOD; /DEUTER/ and the character AWIND dropped), IMPLICIT NONE; AMCALC now returns STRUCTFACTOR as a fourth argument instead of setting it in /CWIND/. /ROT/ is no longer declared, but WNEW now comes from NMLPAR_MOD, so question 5 stands.

Questions for the experts

  1. TCZ undefined. With LBSCALE true but LROSSBY false, the loop uses TCZ, which is set only in the LROSSBY branch (it then keeps a value from an earlier call, or none). Should LBSCALE require LROSSBY?
  2. Silent non-convergence. After 20 iterations the loop continues with the last value without a warning.
  3. An outdated comment. "The constant and exponents are set in PARMIN based on the input index ALFA" describes the old law. Does PARMIN still use ALFA for the Matt et al. law?
  4. Two masses. SMASS is in solar masses (it is multiplied by CMSUN in $f$, and raised to EXM in AMCALC). The argument list does not say so; worth a comment.
  5. WNEW overwritten in NMLPAR_MOD. The local name WNEW is also the namelist input in NMLPAR_MOD (formerly the first element of /ROT/) that STARIN uses (with LWNEW) to start a model in solid-body rotation at $\Omega =$ WNEW. Every call of MWIND (and of WIND, which does the same) overwrites it with the latest surface angular velocity. STARIN is called again for each run in the main program's DO 200 NK = 1, NUMRUN loop, so a later run with LWNEW true would start from the wind's last value, not the input. Should the local variable be renamed?
  6. $\tau_\mathrm{cz}$ = 0. With LROSSBY, WSAT = WMAX*PMMSOLTAU/TCZ, and with LBSCALE, WNOW = OMEGAS*TCZ/PMMSOLTAU. GETTAU (through TAUINTNEW) and MASSLOSS set TAUCZ = 0 when there is no surface convection zone. Can MWIND be reached with TAUCZ or TAUCZ0 = 0, for example as a surface convection zone appears or vanishes, giving an infinite saturation threshold (a division by zero) or $\Omega' = 0$? Should it guard against it?
  7. The spin-down law. With $a_\Omega$ = 3, $dJ/dt \propto \Omega^3$ unsaturated; at fixed moment of inertia that integrates to $\Omega \propto t^{-1/2}$, the Skumanich law. Is matching the Skumanich spin-down the reason both presets give EXW = 3, and does a changing moment of inertia on the pre-main sequence or the subgiant branch spoil the match?
