Purpose

The same deuterium burning as DBURN, for the substeps of a model in the middle of a time step: it is called by MIDMOD with rates interpolated to the substep and with the accreted mass scaled to the substep's share ("unlike the routine called by MIX, the timestep here is only a portion of the total model DT"). Its header still says "DBURN."

How it fits

  • Inputs: as DBURN, plus DRATEM, DRATEM0 (the rates at the end and start of the substep, interpolated in MIDMOD from DRATE0, DRATE with the fraction FAC2) and FAC2 itself. DT is in seconds (MIDMOD's FAC2 = DT/DELTS), converted to Gyr with DTT = DT*1.0D-9/CSECYR (CSECYR from CONSTANTS_MOD, formerly /CONST3/). The other module data are DBURN's: HCOMPP from OLDMOD_MOD, FMASSACC from DEUTER_MOD, LMDOT and COMPACC from NMLPAR_MOD.
  • Who calls it: MIDMOD.
  • Output: HCOMP (H, $^3$He, D) over the shell or zone.

The method

As in DBURN: mass-weighted means over the zone, substeps with $X_D \leftarrow X_D e^{-2r_k\Delta t/n}$ (the factor 2 as in DBURN, whose question 2 asks what it is for, dburn.f, question 2), then $\Delta X = \tfrac12\Delta X_D$ and $\Delta X_3 = -\tfrac32\Delta X_D$. The differences from DBURN:

  • the threshold for zeroing deuterium is $10^{-14}$ (DBURN: $10^{-11}$) and does not include the accreted D;
  • with accretion, the substep's share of the accreted matter is $f_a$ = FAC2*FMASSACC, in grams, since FMASSACC is the accreted mass (MDOT sets it to DELM); with $M$ = SUMM the region's mass (for a single shell, its own mass), the new mean is weighted by mass, as in DBURN: $$X_D = \frac{X_{D,\mathrm{old}}\,M + f_a X_{D,\mathrm{acc}}}{M + f_a}.$$

Question 1 (line 83↗; dburn.f, lines 75–80↗; line 50↗). Two copies. DBURN and DBURNM share almost all their code but differ in the time unit of DT and in the zeroing test: $10^{-11}$ in DBURN, with the accreted D included at the surface, against $10^{-14}$ here, without it. Which test is right, and can the two become one routine in the overhaul? See also the questions on DBURN.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE, and the unused /CONST1/ gone.

Changed since a8a5950. The accretion blend is weighted by mass, $(X M + X_\mathrm{acc} f_a)/(M + f_a)$, as in DBURN; before, it divided by $1 + f_a$ with $f_a$ in grams, so any accretion replaced the region's D, H, and $^3$He by the accreted values. For a single shell, the region mass SUMM is now set to the shell's own mass (before, a value left from an earlier call). This settles former question 2.
