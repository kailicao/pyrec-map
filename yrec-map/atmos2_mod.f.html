Purpose

ATMOS2_MOD holds the surface-pressure table read when KTTAU is 3 or 4 (used only with 3; see below): $\log P$ on a grid of 57 values of $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ and 11 of $\log g$. It replaces COMMON/ATMOS2/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); the sizes NG, NT are private.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. SETUPS reads them all from the file FATM: ATMZ, the table's metallicity; ATMTL(NT), the $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ grid; ATMGL(NG), the $\log g$ grid; ATMPL(NT,NG), $\log P$. SURFP interpolates in them. ATMZ is read by no routine; SETUPS also stores in it the metallicity of the KTTAU = 5 table (ATMOS2C_MOD).

Why KTTAU = 4 loads it. With KTTAU = 4 (Allard atmospheres, ALFILEIN, ALSURFP) SETUPS reads this Kurucz table too, but no routine then uses it: above the Allard table's top $T_\mathrm{eff}$ (AL_TEFFLmax, ALATM05_MOD), or off its gravities, SURFBC and ENVINT switch to the gray atmosphere (KTTAU = 0), not to Kurucz, although ALSURFP's header names Kurucz (KTTAU = 3) as a fallback the caller could choose.

Question 1 (lines 251–268↗; lines 96–108↗; lines 241–254↗; lines 32–34↗). The Kurucz table with Allard atmospheres. SETUPS reads the Kurucz table FATM whenever KTTAU is 3 or 4, but with KTTAU = 4 nothing reads it: when $T_\mathrm{eff}$ leaves the Allard table, SURFBC and ENVINT fall back to the gray atmosphere. A student expected the Kurucz table to take over there, as ALSURFP's header suggests. Is the read a remnant of an older fallback to Kurucz, and should a hot Allard run switch to Kurucz rather than gray? It matters for the boundary condition of stars that heat past the Allard table.
