Purpose

MASSLOSS is the driver for mass loss or accretion in a step (MHP 10/01). It sets the rate $\dot M$ (constant, or from a Reimers formula), computes quantities of the convective envelope and of accreted matter, and calls MDOT to change the model's mass. Sign convention: $\dot M > 0$ is a mass gain (accretion), $\dot M < 0$ a loss. Its header comment: "This is experimental code and valid for Allard atmospheres only" (llp 06/15/2009). KNOWN_ISSUES.md (issue 12) calls this section "experimental, partially implemented": The quantities it computes for physics "not wired up yet" are "not finished", not "dead". The entropies serve accretion: a commented-out line in MDOT used SACC - SCEN, the entropy of the accreted matter relative to the envelope's (whether that difference was meant to decide if accretion expands the envelope is asked on MDOT's page). As the code stands, MDOT (its step 4) scales the expansion or contraction by the envelope's entropy SCEN alone, and SCEN set to 0 at the end of this routine switches the effect off.

Question 1 (lines 6–7↗; lines 180–182↗). Allard atmospheres only. ALSURFP is called whatever atmosphere the run uses. Should the routine check the atmosphere choice, or stop otherwise?

How it fits

  • Who calls it: the main program, main.f, in a step when LAGE is true (main.f:700–705), before the lithium burning of LIBURN; the main program sets LNEWFIT false just before the call.
  • Inputs: BL ($\log L/L_\odot$), DAGE (Gyr), DELTS (s), the model (HCOMP, HD, HP, HR, HS, HS1, HS2, HT, HJM, OMEGA, M), HSTOT (log of the total mass in g), SMASS (mass in $M_\odot$), TEFFL, JENV (the base of the surface convection zone).
  • Outputs: LNEWFIT, set true only for accretion; the main program then calls GETNEWENV to restore the earlier fitting point (main.f:1008). Through MDOT: the changed model, DELTS, SMASS, HSTOT, LNEW, SENVOLD. In MASSCHG2_MOD: SACC and SCEN (MDOT reads SCEN; COEFFT reads only DLOGT and DLOGP, which MDOT sets). In DEUTER_MOD: JCZ, the base of the convective envelope (or M if there is none).
  • Calls: ALSURFP (surface pressure from Allard atmospheres), EQSTAT (twice), MDOT.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (the run's parameters, formerly in COMMON MASSCHG: DMDT0, CREIM, LREIMER, LMDOT, COMPACC; set in PARMIN, defaults DMDT0 = −1e−14 $M_\odot$/yr, CREIM = −4e−13, LMDOT and LREIMER false), MASSCHG2_MOD, ATMPRT_MOD (the surface point from ALSURFP), OVRTRN_MOD (TAUCZ, the convective turnover time; the comment at line 104 says it is computed in MIXCZ, but it is now set by GETTAU, through TAUINTNEW (the new method), TAUINT or TAUCAL), DEUTER_MOD, CONSTANTS_MOD (the constants), and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

  1. If mass change is off (LMDOT false), set LNEWFIT false and return. Otherwise $\dot M$ = DMDT0 (in $M_\odot$/yr, as MDOT converts it).
  2. Radius from $L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$: $\log R = \tfrac12(\log L - \log 4\pi\sigma - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff})$, with $L$ in erg/s.
  3. Reimers (LREIMER): $\dot M = \eta\,L/(g R)$ with $\eta$ = CREIM and $g = GM/R^2$, all in cgs (bug 1 on the units). The age in seconds, AGETOT, is computed and not used (unused?, line 97↗).
  4. If there is a convective envelope (JENV < M), print TAUCZ (in years) and $R/R_\odot$ (labeled MASSLOSS: TAUCZ (YR), R/RSUN), and set JCZ = JENV; otherwise set TAUCZ = 0 (in the shared block) and JCZ = M. The envelope's mass $M_\mathrm{cz}$ = SUMDM $= \sum_{j=\mathrm{JENV}}^{M}$ HS2(j).
  5. If $\dot M > 0$ (accretion), set LNEWFIT true, and with $e_\mathrm{acc} = f\,GM/R$ (EACC0; $f$ = FACC = 1), which the comments describe as the kinetic energy per gram of accreted matter (from the virial theorem):
  6. over the convective envelope, the mass-weighted means of $e_\mathrm{acc}[1 + \tfrac12(R/r - 1)]$ (this mean overwrites EACC), of the thermal energy $P\beta/\rho$ (ETHAV), and of the entropy-like quantity $$s = \frac{P\beta}{\rho T}\left(\tfrac32\ln T - \ln\rho\right),\qquad \beta = 1 - \frac{aT^4}{3P}$$ (SCEN; $a/3$ = CA3; $P\beta/(\rho T)$ = RMU is the gas constant over the mean molecular weight);
  7. at the photosphere from ALSURFP and the EOS: SACC, the same $s$, only if the lookup succeeded (LAlFail false); otherwise a message says that the accreted matter's entropy was not computed, and SACC keeps its earlier value;
  8. an alternative surface state "from Stahler 1988", with $\dot M$ in g/s: $P = \frac{\dot M}{4\pi}\sqrt{2f\,g/R^3}$, $T = \big(\tfrac34\,\dot M\,e_\mathrm{acc}/(4\pi R^2\sigma)\big)^{1/4}$ (with EACC0), its $s$ in SACC2;
  9. finally SCEN is set to 0 in both branches, so the envelope's mean entropy computed above is discarded, and SACC2 is not used (unused?: The lines that used it are commented out, lines 221–224↗); the code is unfinished (issue 12 above; settled from YREC's notes, Nov 9, 2026: lines 152–177↗, lines 209–227↗).
  10. Call MDOT with $\dot M$, the mean $e_\mathrm{acc}$, the mean thermal energy, $M_\mathrm{cz}$, and RMU. MDOT recomputes RMU before using it, and uses EACC and ETHAV only for ETHNEW and FAC, which it does not use (lines 162–174↗): The three arguments have no effect (unused?), so for a loss it does not matter that they are stale values from an earlier call (SAVE), or undefined on a first call.

Design

It writes TAUCZ (in years) and $R/R_\odot$ to the terminal on every call with a convective envelope. Its locals are SAVEd.

Question 2 (lines 116–117↗). A debug line. The unconditional write of TAUCZ/CSECYR and RTOT/CRSUN (labeled MASSLOSS: TAUCZ (YR), R/RSUN since a8a5950) looks like debugging output. Should it go, or move to the .short file?

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) The Reimers rate is in g/s, read as $M_\odot$/yr. (lines 100–103↗; line 111↗; line 159↗) DMDT = CREIM*10.0D0**(BL + CLSUNL)/GSURF/RTOT is $\eta\,L/(gR) = \eta\,LR/(GM)$ with $L$, $R$, $M$ in cgs, a rate in g/s times $\eta$: for the Sun $L_\odot R_\odot/(GM_\odot) \approx 2.0\times10^{18}$ g/s. MDOT reads DMDT as $M_\odot$/yr (it multiplies by CMSUN/CSECYR). With the default CREIM = −4e−13, Reimers' coefficient for $L$, $R$, $M$ in solar units and $\dot M$ in $M_\odot$/yr, the Sun would lose about $8\times10^{5}\,M_\odot$/yr, and MDOT's step limits would cut the step accordingly. Only with LMDOT and LREIMER (both off by default). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix: Reimers' law in solar units:
