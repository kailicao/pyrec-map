Purpose

LIBURN burns the light elements ⁶Li, ⁷Li and ⁹Be (HCOMP(13), HCOMP(14), HCOMP(15)) over one time step: in the radiative interior shell by shell, and in the surface convection zone as one mixed reservoir, with the rates changing during the step. Its accuracy is controlled by Richardson-type extrapolation over ever finer substeps. Its comment: "lithium and beryllium burning in a non-rotating model."

How it fits

  • Who calls it: the main program, main.f, at the end of each step when light elements are followed (LEXCOM) and the model is not rotating with rotational mixing; and GETW for rotating models. The cheaper variant LIBURN2 (one step, no extrapolation) is used by GETW and BURSMIX.

  • Inputs: DELTS (the step; main.f passes it in seconds), HCOMP, HR, HS1, HS2, HT, M; JNENV0 and JNENV, the index of the base of the surface convection zone at the start and at the end of the step. The rates at the start (OLDRAT_MOD) and end (NEWRAT_MOD) of the step, from LIRATE88.

    The step is in seconds. The comments call DELT "the fractional timestep in years" (lines 204–208↗; lines 382–385↗), but DT = FRAC*DELTS, main.f passes DELTS in seconds (main.f, lines 1068–1069↗), and the rates from LIRATE88 are per second (RLI6 = FLI6*RHOX, $\rho X N_A\langle\sigma v\rangle$: lirate88.f, lines 131–133↗), so the comment's "in years" is stale.

  • Output: the new HCOMP(13..15).

  • Calls: RATEXT (rational extrapolation) and SAFEDIVEXP (below).
  • Modules: NEWRAT_MOD, OLDRAT_MOD (the rates; with LIRATE88, LIBURN2, GETW, BURSMIX); SCRTCH_MOD (SDEL, the gradients $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ from PHYSIC); MDPHY_MOD (the same for rotating models); OLDMOD_MOD (HRO, the old radii); LIOV_MOD (PSCAL0, PSCAL1, the overshoot distances at the start and end of the step, set in main.f); PREVCZ_MOD (RBASE0, the end-of-step rates BLI6OLD… with the flag LBLIOLD that says they are valid, and JENVOS, kept for the next step); DEUTER_MOD (FMASSACC, for mass accretion); NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist switches and values: LOVSTE, envelope overshoot; LROT, LINSTB; for accretion LMDOT, DMDT0, COMPACC); CONSTANTS_MOD, IOUNITS_MOD, DIMENSIONS_MOD.

The method

  1. Base of the convection zone. Locate the base between the last convective point and the one below by linear interpolation of $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla_\mathrm{rad}$: an offset $f_x \in [-\tfrac12, \tfrac12]$ of a zone. With envelope overshoot, move the base down by the overshoot distance (PSCAL0, PSCAL1) at the start and end of the step. At the start, the base's radius is the midpoint between HRO(JNENV0) and the point below; if the start-of-step model is fully convective (JNENV0 = 1), the radius below shell 1 is taken as zero. At the end, the offset of the new base is measured in the zone width of the end-of-step model (HR).

  2. Radiative interior (points below both bases, from the centre out, stopping at the first point where the ⁹Be rate, at the start or at the end, is at most $10^{-32}$; points where all three abundances are below $10^{-24}$ are skipped). Above $\log T = 7$ the elements are destroyed outright. Elsewhere, for $n$ = 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, … , 96 substeps of $h = \Delta t/n$: the rate at substep $k$ is interpolated linearly in its log between start and end, $$\ln\lambda_k = \ln\lambda_0 + \tfrac{k - 1/2}{n}\,(\ln\lambda_1 - \ln\lambda_0),$$ and the abundance decays as $x \leftarrow x\,e^{-\lambda_k h}$ (set to 0 when $\lambda_k h \ge 30$). The results for successive $n$ are extrapolated to $h \to 0$ in $h^2$ by RATEXT; it stops when the relative error estimates are below $10^{-6}$. Values below $10^{-24}$ are set to 0.

    Question 1 (line 194↗; liburn2.f, line 158↗). The radiative loop's exit. It leaves the loop at the first point (from the center) where the ⁹Be rate is at most $10^{-32}$, assuming the rates only fall outward; LIBURN2 skips such points instead. The last loop, over the region left by a retreating zone, leaves in the same way here (line 523↗) and in LIBURN2 (liburn2.f, line 334↗), so the choice reaches all three loops. Which is meant?

    Question 2 (lines 93–94↗; line 197↗; lines 232–246↗; lines 280–284↗). Hard-coded values. The destruction temperature $\log T = 7$, the cut $\lambda h \ge 30$, the floor $10^{-24}$, the substep counts and the tolerances $10^{-6}$ are fixed in the code. Are they meant to stay fixed, or should any be a namelist setting (a run with tolerances ten times smaller would show whether the results depend on them)? 3. Convection zone. The zone's starting abundance is the mass-weighted mean over the zone at the start (or at the end, if the zone deepened). Its rate is the mass-weighted mean $\bar\lambda = \sum\lambda_i\Delta m_i/\sum\Delta m_i$ at the start (or the previous step's end value, BLI6OLD…, when the flag LBLIOLD is set) and at the end, the bottom point's mass and rate adjusted for the offset $f_x$. The same substepping and extrapolation as in step 2 follow. With accretion (LMDOT, DMDT0 > 0), accreted material of composition COMPACC is added at each substep, assumed to burn for half a substep on arrival, and mixed in by mass with the factor FMASSACC. All points from the shallower of the two bases (MAXJ) to the surface get the result, and the end rates are stored for the next step with LBLIOLD set. The flag is cleared by main.f at each kind card, by LIBURN when the whole zone is below the burning threshold (the zone is skipped), and by LIBURN2, which always computes its start rates. The adjusted rates of the bottom point stay in NEWRAT_MOD (only its mass is restored). 4. Region left by a retreating zone (points JENV0..JENV−1, between the old and the new base): each is burned once with a rate weighted geometrically between its own end-of-step rate and the zone's start rate, $$x \leftarrow x\,\exp\!\left(-\Delta t\,\lambda_\mathrm{rad}^{\,f}\,\bar\lambda_0^{\,1-f}\right),\qquad f = \frac{m - m_\mathrm{beg}}{m_\mathrm{beg} - m_\mathrm{end}},$$ with $m$ = HS1 (the mass inside the point, in g), and $m_\mathrm{beg}$, $m_\mathrm{end}$ the masses at the old and the new base (HSBEG, HSEND; midpoints between the base point and the one below). The comment says that a point a fraction $f$ of the way (in mass) from the old base to the new one spent the fraction $f$ of the step in the zone, so $f$ should lie between 0 and 1; as written, $f$ is negative (bug 1 below).

1. (harmless in the runs) The sign of FRAD.

(lines 509–528↗) For the points left by a retreating zone (JENV > JENV0), DMASS = HSBEG - HSEND is negative, since HS1 grows outward, while HS1(I) - HSBEG is positive, so FRAD lies between −1 and 0. The rate $\lambda_\mathrm{rad}^{f}\bar\lambda_0^{1-f}$ then extrapolates beyond the zone's rate instead of interpolating between the two rates. Even with the sign corrected, the code gives the radiative rate the weight $f$, while the comment (lines 509–512) says that a point the fraction $f$ of the way from the old base to the new one spent the fraction $f$ of the step in the zone, which calls for the weight $f$ on the zone's rate: A point at the old base ($f$ = 0) left the zone at once. What follows: The light elements in the region a retreating zone leaves are burned at a rate up to $\bar\lambda_0^2/\lambda_\mathrm{rad}$, outside the range of the two rates. Only with LEXCOM (off by default), on steps where the zone retreats.

(lines 191–193↗; lines 373–379↗; lines 496–500↗) When the zone deepens (JENV < JENV0), its starting abundance is the mean over the end-of-step zone, JENV to M, which includes the points JENV to JENV0−1; but the result is written only from MAXJ = JENV0 outward, and the radiative loop stops below JENV. Only with LEXCOM (off by default), on steps where the zone deepens.
