Explanation

Purpose. VCIRC estimates the velocities of the rotational mixing processes at each interface between shells of a radiative region: meridional (Eddington–Sweet) circulation, the Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke (GSF) instability, and the secular and dynamical shear instabilities. Its header comment: "skipping convective regions, check for instability against each secular mechanism; if unstable, compute a circulation velocity". The sum of the velocities, HV, sets the diffusion coefficient for angular momentum and mixing.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: arguments from SECULR: $\log r$ and $r$ (HR, HRU), the range IMIN–IMAX, the iteration IT, LCZ, OMEGA, the shear QWLNR (in SECULR, $\tfrac14(\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1})$ times a radial factor), its limit for dynamical shear QWRMAX (from SETUPV), the total luminosity CLTOT, the time step DT, $\log P$, and the interpolation weights FACI (filled by SETUPV; formerly COMMON INTFAC, now owned by SECULR). Through modules, mostly filled by SETUPV: the velocity factors FES1–FES3, FGSFK, FACT6 (TAUKH_MOD), FACT1–FACT8 (VFACT_MOD), the shell-midpoint quantities (INTVAR_MOD: RM, HGM, ALM, …), the physics at each shell (MDPHY_MOD: $\mu$ AMUM, the viscosity VISCM, the thermal diffusivity THDIFM, …), the quadrupole moment and gravity (QUADRU_MOD: QUAD, GG), the $\theta$ terms (OLDROT2_MOD), and the namelist parameters in NMLPAR_MOD (formerly VMULT, VMULT2, NOTRAN, VARFC): the scale factors FES, FGSF, FSS, FMU, FC, FESC, FGSFC, RCRIT and the switches IES, IGSF, IMU, LNOJ, LDIFAD.
  • Who calls it: only SECULR, before and inside its iterations; SECULR may then call GETFC with HV.
  • Outputs: HV (total velocity per interface) and LDO (true if any is positive); in TEMP2_MOD, VES, VGSF, VSS, VMU and their previous values, read by CHECKJ, CODIFF, PUTSTORE and STITCH; with LDIFAD, the advective and diffusive parts VESA, VESD (DIFAD2_MOD, shared with CHECKJ and ROTGRID) and VESD2, VESD3, DSS, DGSF, FV0 (DIFAD3_MOD, shared with DADCOEFT and SETUPV); RAT (QUADD_MOD), read by CHECKJ and FPFT; and FESTIME, FV0 rescaled in place. The midpoint values VISCMI, THDIFMI, AMUMI, WM and the previous VMU, VMUP, are now local arrays kept between calls (formerly COMMON INTVR2 and PREVMU).

The method. At the interface between shells $i-1$ and $i$:

  1. Midpoint values. The viscosity $\nu$ and thermal diffusivity $K$ by 4-point interpolation in their logarithms (weights FACI); $\mu$ and $\bar\Omega$ (WM) as the means of the two shells.
  2. Meridional circulation (Zahn 1991, by the comments): $v_\mathrm{ES} = \bar\Omega^2 (F_1 + \bar\Omega^2 F_2)$, multiplied by the quadrupole correction of Zahn (1992), $$R = \frac{\Phi_s + \Phi_2}{\Phi_\mathrm{std}},\quad \Phi_\mathrm{std} = \frac{4}{3}\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2 r}{\bar g},\quad \Phi_s = \frac13\,\bar\Omega^2\,\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{r^2}{g}\right),\quad \Phi_2 = -\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{Q}{g}\right),$$ with a warning printed if $R < 0$. Without LDIFAD, $v_\mathrm{ES} = |R\,v_\mathrm{ES} - F_3\,\bar\Omega\,d\Omega/dr|$. With LDIFAD (MHP 05/02, 06/02), it is split into an advective part, $v_A = \tfrac15(R\,v_\mathrm{ES} + \dot\theta)$ with $\dot\theta$ = THN, and diffusive parts of first to third order.

IES = 2 (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974, restored MHP 4/26) replaces this: $v_\mathrm{ES} = |F_1'(F_2' R\,\bar\Omega^2 + F_6\,\Delta(\Omega r)^2/\Delta r)|$, with $F_1'$, $F_2'$, $F_6$ = FACT1, FACT2, FACT6 and $R$ = PHIS2/PHISTD (the quadrupole term is computed, then dropped); QW, THN and the LDIFAD parts are not set. 3. Inhibition by $\mu$ gradients (Zahn & Maeder 1998): every velocity is multiplied by $$f_\mu = \frac{\max(\Delta\nabla, 10^{-3})}{\max(\Delta\nabla + \nabla_\mu, 10^{-3})},\qquad \nabla_\mu = \frac{\Delta\ln\mu}{\Delta\ln P},$$ with $\Delta\nabla$ = DDELM (in SETUPV, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla + \nabla_\mu$, at least a minimum).

IMU = 3 (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974, restored MHP 4/26) replaces this with "fictitious $\mu$ currents" at each radiative interface, $v_\mu = |f_\mu\,F_1' F_4\,\bar\mu\,(d\mu/dr)/\sqrt{\nu c/K}|$ (VMU, with $f_\mu$ = FMU, $F_4$ = FACT4, $c$ = CC) and $v_{\mu2} = f_\mu\,\bar g\,|d\mu/dr|/(\bar\mu\,\bar\Omega^2)$ (VMU2), which are subtracted in step 6. 4. GSF (Kippenhahn 1980): if specific angular momentum decreases outward ($\Omega_i r_i^2 < \Omega_{i-1} r_{i-1}^2$), $$v_\mathrm{GSF} = F_K\,K\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2}{r}\,\max\!\left(2\left|\frac{d\ln j}{dr}\right|, \tfrac14\left|\frac{d\ln\Omega}{dr}\right|\right),$$ otherwise $v_\mathrm{GSF} = \tfrac14 F_K K\,\bar\Omega^2\,|d\ln\Omega/dr|/r$. With IGSF = 0 or 2 it is damped by viscosity: zero if $|q| < q_\nu = 2\sqrt{\nu/K}\,q_\mathrm{max}$, else multiplied by $\sqrt{(|q| - q_\nu)/q_\nu}$; with IGSF = 0 also by the $\mu$ gradient, likewise. 5. Shear (Endal & Sofia, paper II; Zahn 1991). If $|q| > q_\mathrm{max}$ (dynamical shear), $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K (|q|/q_\mathrm{max})^2/r$, and $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ is rescaled to the gradient at the limit (a message is written to unit 6). Otherwise the secular shear is unstable if $$|q| > q_\mathrm{crit} = \sqrt{\frac{\nu}{K}\,\frac{R_\mathrm{crit}}{8}}\;q_\mathrm{max},$$ and then $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K\,\left[(|q| - q_\mathrm{crit})/q_\mathrm{max}\right]^2/r$, with $q_\mathrm{crit}$ raised by a $\mu$ gradient if that gives the larger limit. 6. Scale and average. The velocities are multiplied by the user's factors FES, FGSF, FSS. With IMU = 3, after VMU is averaged with its previous value (IT > 1), the $\mu$ currents are subtracted instead: $v_\mathrm{ES} \to \max(0, F v_\mathrm{ES} - \sqrt{F v_\mathrm{ES}\,r}\,\sqrt{f_c f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}}\,v_\mu)$ with $F$ = FES, $f_c$ = FC, $f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}$ = FESC, or $|F v_\mathrm{ES}|/(1 + v_{\mu2})$ where that is not positive; likewise $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ with FGSF and FGSFC; $v_\mathrm{SS} \to \max(0, $ FSS $v_\mathrm{SS})$. with LDIFAD, the GSF and shear velocities times $r$ join the diffusive part. After the first iteration (IT > 1) each is averaged with the previous one. HV = $v_\mathrm{ES} + v_\mathrm{GSF} + v_\mathrm{SS}$ (zero below $10^{-20}$). 7. No transport in the core (LNOJ, 9/93): from IMIN outward, the velocities are zeroed while $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, "to avoid numerical problems in the H-burning shell".

Design. Many older prescriptions are kept as comments (James & Kahn 1971 GSF, older $\mu$ treatments); IES = 2 and IMU = 3 select the restored Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff (1974) treatments, and any other value (the defaults are 1) the Zahn (1991) circulation and the Zahn & Maeder (1998) $\mu$ factor.

Questions for the experts

  1. The $\mu$ loop does not skip convective interfaces. The meridional, GSF and shear loops skip interfaces inside convection zones; the Zahn & Maeder loop (step 3) does not, and rescales FESTIME and FV0 there too. Harmless, or should it skip them?
  2. Possible division by zero. In the dynamical-shear branch, $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ is rescaled by DLNWDR/DLNWDR0 or FX/FX2, where DLNWDR0 is zero if $\Omega_i = \Omega_{i-1}$; and $\ln\Omega$ requires $\Omega > 0$ everywhere.
  3. Sign of the meridional velocity. With LDIFAD, the circulation is signed, but VES is made positive by ABS(FES*VES) for every interface before HV is formed, so HV never sees a negative VES; only VESA (the advective part) keeps its sign. So HV takes $|v_\mathrm{ES}|$ of a signed circulation: intended?
  4. Unused values. V2 and WMID are computed but never used. VMU is computed only with IMU = 3; otherwise it is zeroed and copied to VMUP for nothing. Can they go?
  5. Output to unit 6. The dynamical-shear message goes to unit 6 (the screen) for every unstable interface, at every iteration.
  6. LNOJ's threshold. The core is defined as $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, hard-coded. Should it be a parameter?
  7. The $\mu$ factor compounds. The $\mu$ loop multiplies FESTIME and FV0 (COMMON, set once by SETUPV before SECULR's iterations) by $f_\mu$ at every call of VCIRC, so the factor compounds over SECULR's iterations ($f_\mu^{\,n}$ after $n$ calls). Should it be applied to a copy?
  8. CC is never set. With IMU = 3, TKHS = SQRT(VISCMI(I)*CC/THDIFMI(I)) uses the local CC, whose DATA statement (the speed of light) is commented out, so CC is undefined (probably zero with SAVE, which makes TKHS zero and VMU infinite). Should the DATA statement be restored?
  9. RAT with IES = 2. RAT(I) = (PHIS2+PHI2)/PHISTD is overwritten at once by RAT(I) = PHIS2/PHISTD, so the quadrupole term is dropped, unlike the default branch. Intended? With LDIFAD, this branch also leaves VESA, VESD, THN from the previous call, which are then scaled by FES again and averaged.
  10. FES for the GSF velocity. With IMU = 3, when the reduced GSF velocity is not positive, VGSF(I) = ABS(FES*VGSF(I)/FM) uses FES, not FGSF. A typo?
  11. VMU2 at convective interfaces. With IMU = 3, VMU and VMU2 are skipped at convective interfaces, but the scaling loop uses FM = 1+VMU2(I) at every interface, so VMU2 there keeps a value from an earlier call (it is a local array kept between calls). VMU2 also divides by $\bar\Omega^2$, which fails without rotation.
