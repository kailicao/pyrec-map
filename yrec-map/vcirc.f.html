Purpose

VCIRC estimates the velocities of the rotational mixing processes at each interface between shells of a radiative region: meridional (Eddington–Sweet) circulation, the Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke (GSF) instability, and the secular and dynamical shear instabilities. Its header comment: "skipping convective regions, check for instability against each secular mechanism; if unstable, compute a circulation velocity". The sum of the velocities, HV, sets the diffusion coefficient for angular momentum and mixing: each "velocity" becomes a diffusion coefficient over a length, $D = 4\pi f_w\,v\,r$ (CODIFF), so that, for example, $v_\mathrm{SS} \propto K/r$ gives $D_\mathrm{SS} \propto K$.

How it fits

  • Inputs: arguments from SECULR: $\log r$ and $r$ (HR, HRU), the range IMIN–IMAX, the iteration IT, LCZ, OMEGA, the shear QWLNR (in SECULR, $\tfrac14(\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1})$ times a radial factor), its limit for dynamical shear QWRMAX (from SETUPV), the total luminosity CLTOT, the time step DT, $\log P$, and the interpolation weights FACI (filled by SETUPV; formerly COMMON INTFAC, now owned by SECULR). Through modules, mostly filled by SETUPV: the velocity factors FES1–FES3, FGSFK, FACT6 (TAUKH_MOD), FACT1–FACT8 (VFACT_MOD), the shell-midpoint quantities (INTVAR_MOD: RM, HGM, ALM, …), the physics at each shell (MDPHY_MOD: $\mu$ AMUM, the viscosity VISCM, the thermal diffusivity THDIFM, …), the quadrupole moment and gravity (QUADRU_MOD: QUAD, GG), the $\theta$ terms (OLDROT2_MOD), and the namelist parameters in NMLPAR_MOD (formerly VMULT, VMULT2, NOTRAN, VARFC): the scale factors FES, FGSF, FSS, FMU, FC, FESC, FGSFC, RCRIT and the switches IES, IGSF, IMU, LNOJ, LDIFAD.
  • Who calls it: only SECULR, before and inside its iterations; SECULR may then call GETFC with HV.
  • Outputs: HV (total velocity per interface) and LDO (true if any is positive); in TEMP2_MOD, VES, VGSF, VSS, VMU and their previous values, read by CHECKJ, CODIFF, PUTSTORE and STITCH; with LDIFAD, the advective and diffusive parts VESA, VESD (DIFAD2_MOD, shared with CHECKJ and ROTGRID) and VESD2, VESD3, DSS, DGSF, FV0 (DIFAD3_MOD, shared with DADCOEFT and SETUPV); RAT (QUADD_MOD), read by CHECKJ and FPFT; and FESTIME, FV0 rescaled in place. The midpoint values VISCMI, THDIFMI, AMUMI, WM and the previous VMU, VMUP, are now local arrays kept between calls (formerly COMMON INTVR2 and PREVMU).

The method

At the interface between shells $i-1$ and $i$, with $q = d\Omega/d\ln r$ there (QWLNR, from SECULR); $q_\mathrm{max}$ (QWRMAX, from SETUPV) the largest shear stable against the dynamical shear instability, from the Richardson criterion $\mathrm{Ri} = N^2/q^2 \ge \tfrac14$; $F_1$, $F_2$, $F_3$ = FES1–FES3 and $F_K$ = FGSFK, from SETUPV:

  1. Midpoint values. The viscosity $\nu$ and thermal diffusivity $K$ by 4-point interpolation in their logarithms (weights FACI); $\mu$ and $\bar\Omega$ (WM) as the means of the two shells.
  2. Meridional circulation (Zahn 1991, by the comments): $v_\mathrm{ES} = \bar\Omega^2 (F_1 + \bar\Omega^2 F_2)$, multiplied by the quadrupole correction of Zahn (1992), $$R = \frac{\Phi_s + \Phi_2}{\Phi_\mathrm{std}},\quad \Phi_\mathrm{std} = \frac{4}{3}\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2 r}{\bar g},\quad \Phi_s = \frac13\,\bar\Omega^2\,\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{r^2}{g}\right),\quad \Phi_2 = -\frac{d}{dr}\!\left(\frac{Q}{g}\right),$$ with a warning printed if $R < 0$. Without LDIFAD, $v_\mathrm{ES} = |R\,v_\mathrm{ES} - F_3\,\bar\Omega\,d\Omega/dr|$. With LDIFAD (MHP 05/02, 06/02), it is split into an advective part, $v_A = \tfrac15(R\,v_\mathrm{ES} + \dot\theta)$ with $\dot\theta$ = THN, and diffusive parts of first to third order.

IES = 2 (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974, restored MHP 4/26) replaces this: $v_\mathrm{ES} = |F_1'(F_2' R\,\bar\Omega^2 + F_6\,\Delta(\Omega r)^2/\Delta r)|$, with $F_1'$, $F_2'$, $F_6$ = FACT1, FACT2, FACT6 and $R$ = PHIS2/PHISTD (the quadrupole term is computed, then dropped); QW, THN and the LDIFAD parts are not set (bug 4).

IMU = 3 (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974, restored MHP 4/26) replaces this with "fictitious $\mu$ currents" at each radiative interface, $v_\mu = |f_\mu\,F_1' F_4\,\bar\mu\,(d\mu/dr)/\sqrt{\nu c/K}|$ (VMU, with $f_\mu$ = FMU, $F_4$ = FACT4, $c$ = CC) and $v_{\mu2} = f_\mu\,\bar g\,|d\mu/dr|/(\bar\mu\,\bar\Omega^2)$ (VMU2), which are subtracted in step 6. CC, the speed of light by the comment, is never set (bug 2). At a convective interface VMU2 keeps the value of an earlier call, but it only divides velocities that are zero there (lines 152–153↗, line 230↗, line 352↗), and its $\bar\Omega^2$ needs $\Omega > 0$, which the logarithms of step 4 already need (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). 4. GSF (Kippenhahn 1980): if specific angular momentum decreases outward ($\Omega_i r_i^2 < \Omega_{i-1} r_{i-1}^2$), $$v_\mathrm{GSF} = F_K\,K\,\frac{\bar\Omega^2}{r}\,\max\!\left(2\left|\frac{d\ln j}{dr}\right|, \tfrac14\left|\frac{d\ln\Omega}{dr}\right|\right),$$ otherwise $v_\mathrm{GSF} = \tfrac14 F_K K\,\bar\Omega^2\,|d\ln\Omega/dr|/r$ (lines 396–405↗). With IGSF = 0 or 2 it is damped by viscosity: zero if $|q| < q_\nu = 2\sqrt{\nu/K}\,q_\mathrm{max}$, else multiplied by $\sqrt{(|q| - q_\nu)/q_\nu}$; with IGSF = 0 also by the $\mu$ gradient, likewise.

Should this case be handled as dynamical (immediate redistribution), as for the dynamical shear, rather than at the GSF's thermal rate?

If $|q| > q_\mathrm{max}$ (dynamical shear), $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K (|q|/q_\mathrm{max})^2/r$, and $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ is rescaled to the gradient at the limit (a message is written to unit 6, the screen, for every such interface at every call, lines 494–496↗): With $\Omega_\mathrm{min} = \Omega_i + (\Omega_{i-1} - \Omega_i)\,q_\mathrm{max}/|q|$ (WMIN), the value of $\Omega_{i-1}$ at which $|q|$ would equal $q_\mathrm{max}$, the gradients of $\ln\Omega$ and $\ln j$ from $\Omega_i$ to $\Omega_\mathrm{min}$ replace those from $\Omega_i$ to $\Omega_{i-1}$ in the formulae of step 4, the branch being chosen from the actual $\Omega_i r_i^2$ and $\Omega_{i-1} r_{i-1}^2$ (lines 445–497↗). The rescaling divides by no zero: The branch runs only when $|q| > q_\mathrm{max}$, and $q$ is proportional to $\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1}$ (line 177↗), so the two differ and DLNWDR0 and FX2 are positive (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026); the logarithms need $\Omega > 0$. WMID, computed at lines 451–455↗, is never used: unused?. Otherwise the secular shear is unstable if $$|q| > q_\mathrm{crit} = \sqrt{\frac{\nu}{K}\,\frac{R_\mathrm{crit}}{8}}\;q_\mathrm{max},$$ and then $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45} K\,\left[(|q| - q_\mathrm{crit})/q_\mathrm{max}\right]^2/r$, with $q_\mathrm{crit}$ raised by a $\mu$ gradient if that gives the larger limit.

    Question 2 (lines 445–447↗). Dynamical shear at a thermal rate. For $|q| > q_\mathrm{max}$, $v_\mathrm{SS} = \tfrac{8}{45}K(|q|/q_\mathrm{max})^2/r$ is scaled by the thermal diffusivity $K$, although the dynamical shear instability acts on a dynamical timescale and would mix far faster. Why is its rate tied to $K$; is the intent only to make it large enough to remove the excess shear within a step?

  2. Scale and average. The velocities are multiplied by the user's factors FES, FGSF, FSS. With IMU = 3, after VMU is averaged with its previous value (IT > 1), the $\mu$ currents are subtracted instead: $v_\mathrm{ES} \to \max(0, F v_\mathrm{ES} - \sqrt{F v_\mathrm{ES}\,r}\,\sqrt{f_c f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}}\,v_\mu)$ with $F$ = FES, $f_c$ = FC, $f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}$ = FESC, or $|F v_\mathrm{ES}|/(1 + v_{\mu2})$ where that is not positive; likewise $v_\mathrm{GSF}$ with FGSF and FGSFC (but with FES where the result is not positive: bug 3); $v_\mathrm{SS} \to \max(0, $ FSS $v_\mathrm{SS})$. with LDIFAD, the GSF and shear velocities times $r$ join the diffusive part. After the first iteration (IT > 1) each is averaged with the previous one. HV = $v_\mathrm{ES} + v_\mathrm{GSF} + v_\mathrm{SS}$ (zero below $10^{-20}$). Without IMU = 3, VMU is only zeroed (line 155↗) and copied to VMUP (line 138↗): unused? there.

    Question 3 (line 595↗; lines 269–271↗; line 605↗). Sign of the meridional velocity. With LDIFAD, the circulation is signed, but VES is made positive by ABS(FES*VES) for every interface before HV is formed, so HV never sees a negative VES; only VESA (the advective part) keeps its sign. So HV takes $|v_\mathrm{ES}|$ of a signed circulation: intended?

  3. No transport in the core (LNOJ, 9/93): from IMIN outward, the velocities are zeroed while $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, "to avoid numerical problems in the H-burning shell".

    Question 4 (lines 671–687↗). LNOJ's threshold. The core is defined as $L(r)/L_\mathrm{tot} < 0.99$, hard-coded. Should it be a parameter?

Design

Many older prescriptions are kept as comments (James & Kahn 1971 GSF, older $\mu$ treatments); IES = 2 and IMU = 3 select the restored Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff (1974) treatments, and any other value (the defaults are 1) the Zahn (1991) circulation and the Zahn & Maeder (1998) $\mu$ factor.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: IGSF chooses the stability condition for the GSF and ABCD instabilities; 1 is recommended. In the code, 1 applies none, 2 the condition on the angular-velocity gradient against viscosity and thermal diffusion, and 0 adds a mean-molecular-weight condition. LDIFAD, experimental, treats meridional circulation as diffusion plus advection; FC scales the mixing coefficients relative to angular momentum.

Velocities of meridional circulation (Zahn 1991, with Zahn 1992's quadrupole term, or Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff 1974), the GSF instability (Kippenhahn 1980) and secular and dynamical shear at each radiative interface, with $\mu$-gradient inhibition (Zahn & Maeder 1998, or Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff's $\mu$ currents), the user's scale factors and averaging between iterations.

Uses modules CONSTANTS_MOD, DIFAD2_MOD, DIFAD3_MOD, DIMENSIONS_MOD, EGRIDDER_MOD, INTVAR_MOD, MDPHY_MOD, NMLPAR_MOD, OLDROT2_MOD, QUADD_MOD, QUADRU_MOD, TAUKH_MOD, TEMP2_MOD, VFACT_MOD

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. The options IES = 2 (Kippenhahn & Möllenhoff circulation) and IMU = 3 (their $\mu$ currents, VMU and the restored VMU2, with the scale factors applied in the same step) are restored (MHP 4/26); FACI is now an argument; the midpoint arrays and VMUP are local; declarations: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE. VMU is computed again with IMU = 3.

Changed since a8a5950. In the dynamical-shear branch, WMIN is taken from the centred gradient QWLNR that triggers the branch, so that it lies between $\Omega_i$ and $\Omega_{i-1}$ and is positive; the plain difference quotient used before could put it beyond $\Omega_{i-1}$, or at or below zero (a NaN in its logarithm). The GSF branch is chosen from the actual profile, as its normalization FX2 is, not from the limited one.

43def _interp4(f: np.ndarray, faci: np.ndarray, i: int, j0: int) -> float:
44    """4-point Lagrangian interpolation in the logarithm of f, from shells
45    j0..j0+3 with the weights FACI(1..4, I)."""
46
47    return math.exp(math.log(f[j0]) * faci[1, i]
48                    + math.log(f[j0 + 1]) * faci[2, i]
49                    + math.log(f[j0 + 2]) * faci[3, i]
50                    + math.log(f[j0 + 3]) * faci[4, i])

53def vcirc(hr: np.ndarray, hru: np.ndarray, imin: int, imax: int, it: int, lcz: np.ndarray, m: int, omega: np.ndarray,

252                continue

254        else:
255            fxx = 1.0

257        if n.igsf == 0:

261                vgsf[i] = 0.0
262                continue

264            qq = 1.0e-2 * qwlnr[i]
265            if abs(qwrmx) < abs(qq):
266                fxx2 = 1.0
267            elif abs(qwrmx) < 1.0e-20:
268                fxx2 = 1.0
269            else:
270                fxx2 = math.sqrt((abs(qwlnr[i]) - qwrmx) / qwrmx)

272        rmid = 0.5 * (hru[i] + hru[i - 1])
273        dlnwdr = abs(math.log(omega[i]) - math.log(omega[i - 1])) / dr
274        # If d ln(j)/dr < 0, use the larger of the two criteria; otherwise
275        # only d omega/dz nonzero. The factor 1/r: all the velocities are
276        # later multiplied by r.
277        if omega[i] * hru[i]**2 < omega[i - 1] * hru[i - 1]**2:
278            dlnjmdr = abs(2.0 / rmid + (math.log(omega[i])
279                                        - math.log(omega[i - 1])) / dr)
280            fx = max(2.0 * dlnjmdr, 0.25 * dlnwdr)
281            vgsf[i] = tk.fgsfk[i] * fx * thdifmi[i] * wm[i]**2 / rmid
282        else:
283            vgsf[i] = 0.25 * tk.fgsfk[i] * thdifmi[i] * dlnwdr * wm[i]**2 \
284                / rmid
285        vgsf[i] = abs(fxx * vgsf[i])
286
287    # Secular and dynamical shear (Endal & Sofia, paper II) (Fortran lines
288    # 441–532).
289    for i in range(imin, imax + 1):  # label 60
290        if abs(qwlnr[i]) > qwrmax[i]:
291            # Dynamical shear: a (large) velocity, and the GSF velocity
292            # rescaled to the gradient at the limit.
293            qwr = abs(qwlnr[i])
294            vss[i] = 8.0 / 4.5e1 * thdifmi[i] * (qwr / qwrmax[i])**2 / rm[i]
295            dr = hru[i] - hru[i - 1]
296            rmid = 0.5 * (hru[i] + hru[i - 1])
297            if omega[i] < omega[i - 1]:
298                wmid = omega[i] + qwrmax[i] * cln * (hr[i] - math.log10(rmid))  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
299            else:
300                wmid = omega[i] - qwrmax[i] * cln * (hr[i] - math.log10(rmid))  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
301            # WMIN: the OMEGA(I-1) at which the centred gradient QWLNR
302            # equals QWRMAX, between OMEGA(I) and OMEGA(I-1) (since 7be4133).
303            wmin = omega[i] + (omega[i - 1] - omega[i]) * qwrmax[i] / qwr
304            # DLNWDR0 > 0: the branch runs only if the two omegas differ.
305            dlnwdr0 = abs(math.log(omega[i]) - math.log(omega[i - 1])) / dr
306            dlnjmdr0 = abs(2.0 / rmid + (math.log(omega[i])
307                                         - math.log(omega[i - 1])) / dr)
308            dlnjmdr = abs(2.0 / rmid + (math.log(omega[i])
309                                        - math.log(wmin)) / dr)
310            dlnwdr = abs(math.log(omega[i]) - math.log(wmin)) / dr
311            # The branch from the actual profile (since 7be4133).
312            if omega[i] * hru[i]**2 < omega[i - 1] * hru[i - 1]**2:
313                fx = max(2.0 * dlnjmdr, 0.25 * dlnwdr)
314                fx2 = max(2.0 * dlnjmdr0, 0.25 * dlnwdr0)
315                vgsf[i] = vgsf[i] * fx / fx2
316            else:
317                vgsf[i] = vgsf[i] * dlnwdr / dlnwdr0
318            # To unit 6 at every such interface, at every call.
319            print(f" DYNAMICAL SHEAR-SHELL{i:5d} WTOP{omega[i]:11.3E}"
320                  f" WBOT{omega[i - 1]:11.3E} LIMIT{wmin:11.3E}")  # 9911
321            continue
322        # Secular shear: the maximum gradient it allows, with the square
323        # root of the Prandtl number; velocity from Zahn 1991 (MHP 3/92).
324        qwrmx = math.sqrt(viscmi[i] / thdifmi[i] * 1.25e-1 * n.rcrit) \
325            * qwrmax[i]
326        if abs(qwlnr[i]) > qwrmx:
327            if abs((amum[i] - amum[i - 1]) / amumi[i]) < 1.0e-10:
328                qwrmx2 = 0.0
329                qwr = abs(qwlnr[i]) - qwrmx
330                vss[i] = 8.0 / 4.5e1 * thdifmi[i] * (qwr / qwrmax[i])**2 \
331                    / rm[i]
332            else:
333                # The limit set by the mu gradient; the larger one is used.
334                qwrmx2 = 2.0 * math.sqrt(max(1.0e-20, vf.fact5[i] * abs(
335                    (amum[i] - amum[i - 1]) / amumi[i])))
336                if abs(qwlnr[i]) > qwrmx2:
337                    qwrmx = max(qwrmx2, qwrmx)
338                    qwr = abs(qwlnr[i]) - qwrmx
339                    vss[i] = 8.0 / 4.5e1 * thdifmi[i] \
340                        * (qwr / qwrmax[i])**2 / rm[i]
341
342    # The user's scale factors (and, with IMU = 3, the mu currents)
343    # (Fortran lines 540–599).
344    if n.imu == 3:
345        if it > 1:
346            for i in range(1, m + 1):
347                vmu[i] = 0.5 * (vmu[i] + vmup[i])
348        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
349            # VMU2 is stale at convective interfaces, where the velocities
350            # it divides are zero.
351            fm = 1.0 + vmu2[i]
352            rmid = 0.5 * (hru[i] + hru[i - 1])
353            fcc = math.sqrt(n.fc * n.fesc)
354            vest = max(0.0, n.fes * ves[i]
355                       - math.sqrt(n.fes * ves[i] * rmid) * fcc * vmu[i])
356            if vest <= 0.0:
357                ves[i] = abs(n.fes * ves[i] / fm)
358            else:
359                ves[i] = vest
360            fcc = math.sqrt(n.fc * n.fgsfc)
361            if vgsf[i] > 0.0:
362                vgsft = max(0.0, n.fgsf * vgsf[i]
363                            - math.sqrt(n.fgsf * vgsf[i] * rmid) * fcc
364                            * vmu[i])
365            else:
366                vgsft = 0.0
367            if vgsft <= 0.0:
368                # Suspected bug (see the page): FES, not FGSF.
369                vgsf[i] = abs(n.fes * vgsf[i] / fm)
370            else:
371                vgsf[i] = vgsft
372            vss[i] = max(0.0, n.fss * vss[i])
373    else:
374        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
375            ves[i] = abs(n.fes * ves[i])
376            vgsf[i] = n.fgsf * vgsf[i]
377            vss[i] = n.fss * vss[i]
378
379    # Repeat for the diffusive and advective parts (MHP 11/94) (Fortran
380    # lines 602–607).
381    # Question (see the page): VES is made positive, VESA keeps its sign.
382    if n.ldifad:
383        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
384            d2.vesd[i] = abs(n.fes * d2.vesd[i])
385            d2.vesa[i] = n.fes * d2.vesa[i]
386
387    # The GSF and shear velocities, times r, join the diffusive part (MHP
388    # 05/02) (Fortran lines 619–647).
389    if n.ldifad:
390        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
391            d2.vesd[i] = d2.vesd[i] + rm[i] * (abs(vgsf[i]) + abs(vss[i]))
392            d3.dss[i] = rm[i] * abs(vss[i])
393            d3.dgsf[i] = rm[i] * abs(vgsf[i])
394
395    # Average the previous and new estimates after the first iteration
396    # (Fortran lines 649–662).
397    if it > 1:
398        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):  # label 70
399            vgsf[i] = 0.5 * (vgsf[i] + t2.vgsf0[i])
400            ves[i] = 0.5 * (ves[i] + t2.ves0[i])
401            vss[i] = 0.5 * (vss[i] + t2.vss0[i])
402        if n.ldifad:
403            for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
404                d2.vesa[i] = 0.5 * (d2.vesa[i] + d2.vesa0[i])
405                d2.vesd[i] = 0.5 * (d2.vesd[i] + d2.vesd0[i])
406
407    # The total velocity (Fortran lines 663–667).
408    for i in range(imin, imax + 1):  # label 80
409        hv[i] = vgsf[i] + ves[i] + vss[i]
410        if hv[i] < 1.0e-20:
411            hv[i] = 0.0
412        if hv[i] > 0.0:
413            ldo = True
414
415    # No transport in the core (L(r)/L < 0.99), to avoid numerical problems in
416    # the H-burning shell (9/93) (Fortran lines 671–687).
417    # Question (see the page): the threshold is hard-coded.
418    if n.lnoj:
419        for i in range(imin, imax + 1):
420            dell = iv.alm[i] / cltot
421            if dell < 9.9e-1:
422                hv[i] = 0.0
423                vgsf[i] = 0.0
424                ves[i] = 0.0
425                vss[i] = 0.0
426                d2.vesa[i] = 0.0
427                d2.vesd[i] = 0.0
428            else:
429                break  # GOTO 81
430
431    return ldo
