Purpose

Quadratic interpolation through three points for the 2006 OPAL equation of state; a copy of QUAD and QUADEOS01 (both removed since rp_1) under its own name, with its own cache.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: ESAC06 and T6RINTEOS06.

The method

As in QUAD: $y(x) = c_1 + c_2 x + c_3 x^2$ through the three points; with IC = 0 the abscissa factors are computed and cached in slot I (up to 30), otherwise reused. It differs from QUADEOS01 only in its name and in redundant parentheses.
