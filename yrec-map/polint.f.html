Purpose

Polynomial interpolation through N points by Neville's algorithm (the routine POLINT of Numerical Recipes, by its name and form): it returns the value Y at X and an error estimate DY, the last correction added.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: ALSURFP (the Allard model atmospheres' surface pressures) and FPFT (the rotational distortion factors).
  • Modules: IOUNITS_MOD, for the unit ISHORT of its error message.
  • Limits: at most 20 points (the arrays are fixed at 20, "for consistency with fpft," MHP 10/02).

The method

Starting from the nearest tabulated point, the tableau of differences $C_{i}$, $D_{i}$ is built column by column, $$D_i \leftarrow \frac{(x_{i+j}-x)\,(C_{i+1}-D_i)}{x_i - x_{i+j}},\qquad C_i \leftarrow \frac{(x_i-x)\,(C_{i+1}-D_i)}{x_i - x_{i+j}},$$ and the path through it that stays closest to the centre adds one correction per column to $y$.

Two equal abscissas ($|x_i - x_{i+j}| < 10^{-20}$) stop the run (STOP 1) with a message to the terminal and to ISHORT giving the two abscissas, $x$ and N.
