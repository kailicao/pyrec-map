Purpose

SCVEOS_MOD holds the tables of the SCV equation of state ("MHP 5/97"): those of pure hydrogen, pure helium and the metals as read, the table built from them for the envelope's mixture, and the search indices of the last interpolation. Shared by 4 routines. It replaces COMMON/SCVEOS/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), less its switch LSCV, now in NMLPAR_MOD, and with IDT renamed IDTSCV (10/9/26), since PARMIN and STARIN had an unrelated IDT of COMMON/OPTAB/ and called this one IDTT. The dimensions NTS = 63 (temperatures) and NPS = 76 (pressures per temperature) are PRIVATE, because the importing files declare their own.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • TLOGX(NTS), NPTSX(NTS): the tables' $\log T$ values and the number of pressure points at each; read by SETUPS from the hydrogen table.
  • TABLEX(NTS,NPS,12), TABLEY(NTS,NPS,12): the hydrogen and helium tables (11 columns as read by SETUPS, the first $\log P$); SETSCV adds column 12, $\mathrm{d}U/\mathrm{d}T$. TABLEZ(NTS,NPS,13): the metals' table, read by SETUPS.
  • SMIX(NTS,NPS): the entropy of mixing of hydrogen and helium, computed by SETSCV; read by SETSCV and EQSCVG.
  • TABLENV(NTS,NPS,12): the table for the envelope's mixture ($\log P$, $\log\rho$, the derivatives, $c_P$, $\mathrm{d}U/\mathrm{d}T$, …), built by SETSCV; read by SETSCV, EQSCVE and EQSCVG.
  • IDTSCV, IDP: the first indices of the four-point stencil in temperature and in pressure, kept between calls as the starting point of the next search; set and read by EQSCVE, EQSCVG and SETSCV.

Design

A blanket USE SCVEOS_MOD in each routine. EQSCVE and EQSCVG share IDTSCV and IDP, so a call of one starts its search where the other left off.
