Explanation

Purpose. Nuclear burning and gravitational settling for one step of the rotational-mixing loop. Its header comment lists the steps: (1) use KEMCOM to solve implicitly for the abundances in radiative regions, shell by shell; (2) solve for the burning in each convection zone as a whole. It then applies the settling of helium and renormalizes the composition.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: MIDMOD (with the temperatures at the current time, HTM, "for thermodynamic consistency", MHP 6/00) and BURSMIX, in their loops of substeps.
  • Inputs: the step DELTS (s), the composition HCOMP(15, ·), shell masses HS2, temperatures HT ($\log T$), and the zone lists MRZONE (radiative) and MXZONE (convective); for the settling, HD, HS, HR, HP, HS1, HSTOT and the convection flags LC.
  • The rates: read from BURN_MOD (HCOMPM(1..15, ·)), where MIX stored the output of RATES: HCOMPM(1..13) are the rates HR1…HR13, HCOMPM(14) and (15) the branchings HF1, HF2.
  • What it calls: KEMCOM, then either MICRODIFF (when LNEWDIF is true) or GRSETT (the older Bahcall–Loeb settling).
  • Output: HCOMP, updated in place.
  • Modules, other: NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist parameters LDIFY, which this routine may switch off, DT_GS, XMIN, YMIN, LNEWDIF, TCUT(1) and LEXCOM: 15 species or 11), MDPHY_MOD (DELM), SCRTCH_MOD (SDEL, temporarily overwritten), CONSTANTS_MOD, IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT), DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method.

  1. Burning. For each radiative shell above the cut $\log T >$ TCUT(1) (the loop stops at the first shell below it, assuming $T$ falls outward), KEMCOM is called for the shell alone; for each convection zone, for the whole zone at once, which also homogenizes it. The step is passed in years, DDAGE = DELTS/CSECYR.
  2. Settling (if LDIFY). It is skipped once the central hydrogen is below XMIN (and LDIFY is then switched off for good), if the star is fully convective, or if $Y <$ YMIN everywhere. It stops the run if there is no surface convection zone. The outer point JMAX is the top of the last radiative zone, moved down to the first shell with $Y >$ YMIN. The settling time there is taken from Michaud et al. (1984): $$t_\mathrm{settle} = 4.348\times10^{21}\;\frac{f_M}{T^{3/2}}\ \text{yr},\qquad f_M = \frac{M_* - m(\texttt{JMAX})}{M_*},$$ with $T$ in K at JMAX (the code multiplies by CSECYR to get TSCALE in seconds, the unit of DELTS), and the step is divided into NSTEP substeps of at most DT_GS $\times\,t_\mathrm{settle}$. The pressure gradient passed to the settling routine is $$\frac{d\ln P}{dr} = -\frac{G m \rho}{P r^2}\quad(\texttt{HQPR}).$$ During the settling, SDEL(2,·) holds DELM and is restored afterwards.
  3. Renormalization. Every abundance is clipped to $[0, 1]$; $Z$ (HCOMP(3)) is capped at $1 - X - X_4$, and $Y$ is set to $1 - X - Z - X_4$, where $X_4$ is the fourth species, He3 (as STARIN fills it).

KC's changes. The unused argument ITLVL was removed (KC 2025-05-31), with the calls in MIDMOD and BURSMIX; the calls to KEMCOM drop HR9, HR13 and ITLVL to match its new signature (KC 2025-05-31).

Changed since rp_1. The two debug writes to unit 69 are commented out (settles former question 1); COMMON blocks to modules (the unused /CTLIM/ dropped), IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. MOD with its arguments reversed. NSTEP = INT(DELTS/DTMAX), then one more substep IF(MOD(DTMAX,DELTS).NE.0). To test whether DELTS is a multiple of DTMAX, it would be MOD(DELTS,DTMAX). As written, whenever DTMAX < DELTS the test is always true, so one extra substep is always added. The same line is in MIX. Intended?
  2. Message names the wrong routine. The stop for "NO SURFACE CZ" says "STOPPED IN SUBROUTINE MIX" (copied from MIX).
  3. HR9, HR13, HF2 are copied but not used. The copies from HCOMPM remain after KC's change to the KEMCOM call. They could go.
  4. The burning loop exits at the first cool shell. GOTO 45 leaves both the shell loop and the zone loop, so any radiative zone listed after the first cool shell is skipped, even if it were hot (for example in a shell-burning star, if the zones were not ordered outward). Are MRZONE entries always ordered from the center out?
