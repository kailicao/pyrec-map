Purpose

Nuclear burning and gravitational settling for one step of the rotational-mixing loop. Its header comment lists the steps: (1) use KEMCOM to solve implicitly for the abundances in radiative regions, shell by shell; (2) solve for the burning in each convection zone as a whole. It then applies the settling of helium and renormalizes the composition.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: MIDMOD (with the temperatures at the current time, HTM, "for thermodynamic consistency", MHP 6/00) and BURSMIX, in their loops of substeps.
  • Inputs: the step DELTS (s), the composition HCOMP(15, ·), shell masses HS2, temperatures HT ($\log T$), densities HD (also used for the rates), and the zone lists MRZONE (radiative) and MXZONE (convective); for the settling, HS, HR, HP, HS1, HSTOT and the convection flags LC. The zone lists come from CONVEC, whose end of the surface radiative zone is misplaced (suspected bug 1).
  • The rates: computed here by RATES on the model passed in (HD, HT, and the substep's own composition), as MIX does: the rates HR1…HR13 and the branchings HF1, HF2, set to zero in shells with $\log T \le$ TCUT(1).
  • What it calls: RATES, KEMCOM, then either MICRODIFF (when LNEWDIF is true) or GRSETT (the older Bahcall–Loeb settling).
  • Output: HCOMP, updated in place.
  • Modules, other: NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist parameters LDIFY, which this routine may switch off, DT_GS, XMIN, YMIN, LNEWDIF, TCUT(1) and LEXCOM: 15 species or 11), MDPHY_MOD (DELM), SCRTCH_MOD (SDEL, temporarily overwritten), CONSTANTS_MOD, IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT), DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

  1. Burning. For each radiative shell above the cut $\log T >$ TCUT(1) (the loop stops at the first shell below it, assuming $T$ falls outward), KEMCOM is called for the shell alone; for each convection zone, for the whole zone at once, which also homogenizes it. The step is passed in years, DDAGE = DELTS/CSECYR.

    Question 1 (lines 118–130↗). The burning loop exits at the first cool shell. GOTO 45 leaves both the shell loop and the zone loop, so any radiative zone listed after the first cool shell is skipped, even if it were hot (for example in a shell-burning star, if the zones were not ordered outward). Are MRZONE entries always ordered from the center out?

  2. Settling (if LDIFY). It is skipped once the central hydrogen is below XMIN (and LDIFY is then switched off for good), if the star is fully convective, or if $Y <$ YMIN everywhere. It stops the run (exit status 1) if there is no surface convection zone. The outer point JMAX is the top of the last radiative zone; the comment says it is moved down to the first shell with $Y >$ YMIN, but the search moves only J, so JMAX stays the top of the last radiative zone (suspected bug 1). The settling time there is taken from Michaud et al. (1984): $$t_\mathrm{settle} = 4.348\times10^{21}\;\frac{f_M}{T^{3/2}}\ \text{yr},\qquad f_M = \frac{M_* - m(\texttt{JMAX})}{M_*},$$ with $T$ in K at JMAX (the code multiplies by CSECYR to get TSCALE in seconds, the unit of DELTS), and the step is divided into NSTEP substeps of at most DT_GS $\times\,t_\mathrm{settle}$ (one more than INT(DELTS/DTMAX) unless DELTS is an exact multiple of DTMAX). The pressure gradient passed to the settling routine is $$\frac{d\ln P}{dr} = -\frac{G m \rho}{P r^2}\quad(\texttt{HQPR}).$$ During the settling, SDEL(2,·) holds DELM and is restored afterwards.

  3. Renormalization. Every abundance is clipped to $[0, 1]$; $Z$ (HCOMP(3)) is capped at $1 - X - X_3$, and $Y$ is set to $1 - X - Z - X_3$, where $X_3$ is ³He, the fourth species HCOMP(4,·) (as STARIN fills it).

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (c) The outer point for the settling step is not moved down. (lines 156–160↗; lines 174–180↗; lines 191–194↗) The comment says the outer boundary is the surface convection zone or "the first point where the helium abundance rises above" YMIN. Loop 140 finds that point as J, but JMAX is never set to it, so FM and TSCALE, and with them the number of settling substeps, are always taken at the top of the last radiative zone. 91    for k in range(1, nrzone + 1):
92        for j in range(mrzone[k, 1], mrzone[k, 2] + 1):
93            if ht[j] <= n.tcut[1]:
94                cool = True
95                break
96            ibegin = j
97            iend = j
98            kemcom(ht, ibegin, iend, hr1, hr2, hr3, hr4, hr5, hr6, hr7,
99                   hr8, hr10, hr11, hr12, hf1, hs2, hcomp, ddage)
100        if cool:
101            break
102
103    # label 45. Convection zones, each as a unit (kemcom homogenizes it) (Fortran lines 135–142).
104    for k in range(1, nzone + 1):
105        ibegin = mxzone[k, 1]
106        iend = mxzone[k, 2]
107        kemcom(ht, ibegin, iend, hr1, hr2, hr3, hr4, hr5, hr6, hr7,
108               hr8, hr10, hr11, hr12, hf1, hs2, hcomp, ddage)
109
110    # Microscopic diffusion of helium (Fortran lines 151–237).
111    if n.ldify:
112        _settle(delts, hcomp, ht, m, mrzone, mxzone, nrzone, nzone,
113                hstot, hd, hs, hr, hp, lc, hs1)
114    # End of gravitational settling (label 170).
115
116    # Renormalize the composition (small negative abundances...) (Fortran lines 242–249).
117    for i in range(1, m + 1):
118        for j in range(1, nspec + 1):
119            hcomp[j, i] = max(hcomp[j, i], 0.0)
120            hcomp[j, i] = min(hcomp[j, i], 1.0)
121        hcomp[3, i] = min(hcomp[3, i], 1.0 - hcomp[1, i] - hcomp[4, i])
122        hcomp[2, i] = 1.0 - hcomp[1, i] - hcomp[3, i] - hcomp[4, i]
123

125def _settle(delts: float, hcomp: np.ndarray, ht: np.ndarray, m: int, mrzone: np.ndarray, mxzone: np.ndarray,
126            nrzone: int, nzone: int, hstot: float, hd: np.ndarray, hs: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hp: np.ndarray,
127            lc: np.ndarray, hs1: np.ndarray) -> None:
128    """The settling block inside IF(LDIFY); each early return is a
129    GOTO 170.
130
131    """
132
133    c = constants_mod
134    n = nmlpar_mod
135    sdel = scrtch_mod.sdel
136
137    # Settling stops for good once the central X is below XMIN (Fortran lines 152–155).
138    if hcomp[1, 1] < n.xmin:
139        n.ldify = False
140        return
141
142    # Locate the outer boundary (Fortran lines 163–180).
143    if not lc[m]:
144        text = ("NO SURFACE CZ - DIFFUSION NOT MEANINGFUL\n"
145                "STOPPED IN SUBROUTINE MIX")   # format 911
146        # Suspected bug (see the page): the message names MIX.
147        print(" " + text)
148        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort, " " + text)
149        raise SystemExit(1)    # STOP 1, the run-time error code
150    # Exit if overshoot makes the star fully convective (MHP 7/92).
151    if mxzone[nzone, 1] <= 2 and mxzone[nzone, 2] == m:
152        return
153    jmax = mrzone[nrzone, 2]
154    for j in range(jmax, 0, -1):
155        if hcomp[2, j] > n.ymin:
156            break                                   # go to 150
157    else:
158        return                  # Y < YMIN for the whole star
159    # label 150. Suspected bug (see the page): the loop above moves J, not
160    # JMAX, so JMAX stays the top of the last radiative zone and the first
161    # shell with Y > YMIN is never used.
162
163    # The pressure gradient; SDEL(2,.) holds DELM meanwhile (Fortran lines 181–186).
164    stot = math.exp(c.cln * hstot)
165    for i in range(1, m + 1):
166        temp[i] = sdel[2, i]
167        sdel[2, i] = mdphy_mod.delm[i]
168        hqpr[i] = -math.exp(c.cln * (hd[i] + c.cgl + hs[i]
169                                     - 2.0 * hr[i] - hp[i]))
170
171    # Only check for timestep cutting if JMAX is large (BC 6/92) (Fortran lines 189–213).
172    if jmax >= 2:
173        # FM is the mass fraction above the outer point.
174        fm = (stot - hs1[jmax]) / stot
175        # Settling time of helium there (Michaud et al. 1984).
176        tscale = 4.348e21 * c.csecyr * fm / math.exp(c.cln * 1.5 * ht[jmax])
177        dtmax = n.dt_gs * tscale
178        nstep = int(delts / dtmax)
179        if math.fmod(delts, dtmax) != 0.0 or nstep == 0:
180            nstep = nstep + 1
181        dt = delts / float(nstep)
182    else:
183        nstep = 1
184        dt = delts
185
186    # The convection flags from the zone lists (Fortran lines 214–223).
187    for j in range(1, nrzone + 1):
188        for i in range(mrzone[j, 1], mrzone[j, 2] + 1):
189            lcz[i] = False
190    for j in range(1, nzone + 1):
191        for i in range(mxzone[j, 1], mxzone[j, 2] + 1):
192            lcz[i] = True
193
194    # The settling substeps (Fortran lines 224–232).
195    for ns in range(1, nstep + 1):
196        # LNEWDIF: the new routines in microdiff; else grsett.
197        if n.lnewdif:
198            # Since 7be4133 microdiff and grsett work on copies of dt
199            # and stot and leave them unchanged.
200            microdiff(dt, hcomp, hqpr, hr, hd, hs1, ht, lcz, m, stot)
201        else:
202            grsett(dt, hcomp, hqpr, hr, hd, hs1, ht, lcz, m, stot)
203
204    # SDEL(2,.) restored (Fortran lines 233–235).
205    for i in range(1, m + 1):
206        sdel[2, i] = temp[i]
