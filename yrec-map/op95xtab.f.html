Purpose

Builds the OPAL95 opacity table at one fixed hydrogen abundance (the surface $X$), from the table at the model's $Z$ (MHP 7/98). The result is what OP952D interpolates, for the outer layers, where $X$ and $Z$ are those of the surface.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the desired $X$ (the envelope's, XENV); the table at the model's $Z$, CAPPA and ZTAB in LLOT95_MOD (from OP95ZTAB); the $X$ grid XXG in LLOT95A_MOD.
  • Who calls it: SETUPOPAC (at start-up) and SURFOPAC (which rebuilds the surface tables for a new envelope $X$, XENV).
  • What it calls: INTRP2, four-point Lagrange weights.
  • Output: CAPPAE(70,19) and XOP95 (the table's $X$, formerly XTAB) in LLOT95E_MOD, read by OP952D and GETOPAL95 (which uses this table when the point's $X$ is within $10^{-4}$ of XOP95).

The method

It picks four $X$ tables around the desired $X$ and interpolates every $(T, R)$ entry with Lagrange weights $b_k$: $$\log\kappa_\mathrm{E}(T, R) = \sum_{k=1}^4 b_k\,\log\kappa(X_k, T, R).$$ For $X \le 0.8$, or $Z \le 0.04$ (where all ten $X$ tables exist), the four nearest tables. For $0.04 < Z < 0.1$, tables 6, 7, 8 and 10 (table 9, $X = 0.95$, is missing). For $Z \ge 0.1$, tables 6–9: This branch is unused? since OP95ZTAB stops the run for any $Z > 0.08$; if it were reached, it would read table 9, which OP95ZTAB does not fill at that $Z$, where GETOPAL95 uses tables 5–8 (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 66–71↗; lines 41–46↗, lines 68–70↗; lines 292–296↗).

Question 1 (line 75↗, lines 85–95↗). The sentinel IDX = 11. The value marks the "6, 7, 8, 10" case for the index selection below; a named flag would be clearer.

Question 2 (lines 82–83↗). A wrong comment. "Find interpolation factors in Z" precedes the interpolation in $X$.

  1. (d) Table 9 at $Z = 0.04$. (lines 53–65↗; lines 68–70↗) This routine treats exactly $Z = 0.04$ as "low Z" (ZTAB.LE.0.04D0, all ten tables), while OP95ZTAB skips table 9 ($X = 0.95$) for Z.GE.0.04D0, and GETOPAL95 avoids it for ZTAB.GE.0.04D0 (lines 283–290↗).
