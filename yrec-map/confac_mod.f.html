Purpose

CONFAC_MOD holds the factors that convert radius, mass, temperature and time between the code's units and those of the diffusion routines (from Bahcall's): CON_RAD, CON_MASS, CON_TEMP, CON_TIME. It replaces COMMON/CONFAC/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26).

Who sets them, who reads them. Set by MICRODIFF_SETUP and SETUP_GRSETT, with the same formulas ($1/R_\odot$, $10^{-2}$ CON_RAD³, $10^{-7}$, $2.7\times10^{13}$ yr in seconds: the units of Bahcall and Loeb (1989), a length unit $R_\odot$, a mass unit $100\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\times R_\odot^3$, a temperature unit $10^7$ K, and a time unit that "includes factor of 2.2 from ln Lambda", the Coulomb logarithm; see MICRODIFF_SETUP); read by them and by EQUAL_TO_MODEL and MICRODIFF_ETM.
