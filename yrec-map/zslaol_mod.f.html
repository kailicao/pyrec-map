Purpose

ZSLAOL_MOD holds the density splines of the pure-metal LAOL89 opacity table (ZLAOL_MOD), one natural cubic spline of $\log\kappa$ against $\log\rho$ per temperature, through the table's nonzero entries. Shared by 2 routines. It replaces COMMON/ZSLAOL/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26).

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All are set by ZSULAOL and read by GTPURZ.

  • NUMRSZ(52): the number of nonzero entries at each temperature, or 0 when there are fewer than four (no spline).
  • ZSLAOLL(104,52), ZSRHOL(104,52): $\log\kappa$ and $\log\rho$ at those entries; ZSDORL(104,52): the spline's second derivatives, from CSPLINE.

Design

A blanket USE ZSLAOL_MOD in each routine.
