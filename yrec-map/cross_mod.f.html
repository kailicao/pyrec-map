Explanation

Purpose. CROSS_MOD holds the factors by which the nuclear reaction rates are adjusted for user-changed cross sections: the S-factor derivative factors and the N15+p branching factors, shared by 3 routines. It replaces COMMON/CROSS/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), less SSTANDARD (now in NMLPAR_MOD) and LNEWNUC (now in NMLBYPASS_MOD).

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • QS0E(8), QQS0EE(8): factors on the first-derivative ($Q_2$, $Q_3$) and second-derivative ($Q_4$, $Q_5$) terms of the eight pp and CNO rates, proportional to $S'/S$ and $S''/S$ relative to the reference values (REMAP's comments). REMAP sets them to 1, or, with LNEWNUC, from the user's S-factors and their derivatives.
  • FC12, FO16: the factors on the N15+p → C12+α and → O16+γ branches, 1 or, with LNEWNUC, the ratio of the user's S-factor to the reference; set in REMAP.
  • All four are read by ENGEB and RATES, which multiply the rate brackets and the branching ratio by them.

Design. A blanket USE CROSS_MOD in each of the three routines.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/CROSS/ as a module, with SSTANDARD and LNEWNUC moved to the namelist modules.
