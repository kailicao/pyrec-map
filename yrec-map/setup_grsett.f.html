Purpose

Prepares the gravitational settling (microscopic diffusion) of helium, and optionally of metals, for the older settling solver: it finds the range of shells where settling is computed, converts the model to the units of Bahcall and Loeb, and computes the diffusion coefficients and their derivatives with respect to $X$.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: GRSETT, the Bahcall–Loeb settling solver (used when LNEWDIF is false; see ROTMIX and MIX). The newer path is MICRODIFF_SETUP.
  • Inputs: the step DT (s), $d\ln P/dr$ (HQPR), $\log r$, $\log\rho$, $\log T$ (HR, HD, HT), the enclosed masses HS1 (g), the total mass STOT, the convection flags LC and HCOMP; from modules, SDEL(2,·) (SCRTCH_MOD, which ROTMIX loads with the gradient DELM) and the namelist parameters in NMLPAR_MOD: ILAMBDA, XMIN, YMIN, LTHOULFIT, FGRY, FGRZ, LTHOUL, LDIFZ, CSTMIXING and CSTDIFFMIX.
  • What it calls: THDIFF, the full diffusion coefficients of Thoul et al., when LTHOUL is true and LTHOULFIT false.
  • Outputs: IBEGIN, IEND (the range), LALLCZ (true when settling is skipped), HRU, HTU (radius and temperature in Bahcall–Loeb units), HQPR, HS1, DT, STOT converted in place, and the coefficients COD1, COD2, QCOD1X, QCOD2X; for metals, COD1Z, COD2Z, QCOD1Z, QCOD2Z, arguments whose storage GRSETT owns and passes on to MODEL_TO_EQUAL. The conversion factors go to CONFAC_MOD (CON_RAD, CON_MASS, CON_TEMP, CON_TIME), shared with EQUAL_TO_MODEL and the MICRODIFF routines.

The method

  1. Range. IBEGIN is the first shell above a convective core with $X >$ XMIN; IEND is the last shell below a convective envelope. The helium check then moves IEND down to the first shell at or below it with $Y >$ YMIN (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: IEND = I after label 47, lines 149–160↗). A fully convective model, or one with no shell above either limit, is skipped (LALLCZ), with a message; the comment before the helium check's exit says "HYDROGEN-FREE MODEL", but the test and the printed message are on helium (lines 154–156↗). The coefficients below are computed for every shell, 1 to M, not only within the range.

  2. Units (Bahcall and Loeb): $r$ in $R_\odot$ (6.9598×10$^{10}$ cm), $T$ in $10^7$ K, masses multiplied by CON_MASS $=10^{-2}/R_\odot^3$ ($R_\odot$ in cm), time in $2.7\times10^{13}$ yr ("includes factor of 2.2 from ln lambda").

    Question 1 (lines 168–169↗; line 206↗). $\ln\Lambda$ counted twice? CON_TIME "includes factor of 2.2 from ln lambda", and FAC also divides by $\ln\Lambda$, which is 2.2 by default. Does the time unit already absorb the 2.2, so that dividing again gives a factor $1/2.2$ too many? Or is the comment about the unit's history only? 3. Coulomb logarithm $\ln\Lambda$, by ILAMBDA: 2.2 (default); Noerdlinger (1977), $\ln\Lambda = -19.7 - \tfrac12\ln\frac{4}{3X+1} - \tfrac12\ln\rho + \tfrac32\ln T$; Loeb (1989), $\ln\Lambda = -19.105747 - \tfrac12\ln\rho + \tfrac32\ln T$; or (4) a separate value for each pair of species from the Debye length or interionic distance, $\ln\Lambda_{ij} = 0.81245\,\ln\bigl(1 + 0.18769\,x_{ij}^{1.2}\bigr)$. Option 4 reaches only THDIFF (helium with LTHOUL true and LTHOULFIT false); FAC below, and the metals' factor, then use the default 2.2.

    Question 2 (lines 193–204↗; lines 228–267↗; lines 1245–1252↗). ILAMBDA. In the code, 2 is Noerdlinger's formula, 3 Loeb's, 4 (in the Thoul solution) a value from the local density and temperature, and any other value the constant 2.2; the default is 1, the constant. The documentation recommends 4 and calls it "a constant, 2.2". Which value is recommended, and should the default follow? (LDIFLI sets ILAMBDA = 4 in PARMIN; LTHOUL, "rarely used" in the documentation, is also forced on by LDIFZ and LDIFLI there.) 4. Helium coefficients. The equation, in the comment's words, is $$\frac{\partial X}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{\rho r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\Bigl(D_1 + D_2\frac{\partial X}{\partial r}\Bigr),$$ with $F = f_Y\,r^2 T^{5/2}/\ln\Lambda$ (FAC, $f_Y$ = FGRY). Without LTHOUL (Bahcall and Loeb): $$D_1 = F\,\frac{d\ln P}{dr}\,X(1-X-Z)\left[\frac54 + \nabla\,\frac{6(X+0.32)}{5.4+6.3X-4.5X^2}\right],\qquad D_2 = F\,\frac{X+3}{(X+1)(5X+3)},$$ with $\nabla$ = SDEL(2,I) and $Z$ = HCOMP(3) + HCOMP(4). With LTHOUL (Thoul et al.): $D_1 = c\,F\,\frac{d\ln P}{dr}\,X\,(A_P + A_T)$ and $D_2 = c\,F\,A_C$, where $A_P$, $A_T$ come from THDIFF or from the quadratic fits in $X$ (LTHOULFIT), $A_C = 1.15 - 1.42X + 0.647X^2$, and $c$ = CSTMIXING ("FD 10/09, mimic mixing to reduce settling"). QCOD1X, QCOD2X are the derivatives with respect to $X$; with ILAMBDA = 2, QCOD1X gets an extra term from the $X$-dependence of $\ln\Lambda$. The variable X3 (line 213↗) is set and never read (unused?).

    Question 3 (lines 186–187↗; lines 214–220↗). $+0.32$ or $-0.32$? The comment writes the thermal term as DEL*6*(X-0.32)/(5.4+6.3X-4.5X**2); the code uses (X+0.32D0), and its derivative QCOD1X (the factor 3.384D0+2.88D0*X+4.5D0*X2) is that of the $+0.32$ form. Which is Bahcall and Loeb's?

    Question 4 (lines 275–285↗). Derivatives from the fits, coefficients from THDIFF. With LTHOUL and not LTHOULFIT, $A_P$ and $A_T$ come from THDIFF, but their $X$-derivatives (QAPX, QATX) and $A_C$ always come from the quadratic fits. Is this intended as an approximation, and how far do the fits' derivatives stray from those of THDIFF's coefficients over the range of $X$ in a solar model? 5. Metals (with LDIFZ and LTHOUL): the same form with Thoul's coefficients for fully ionized iron, $f_Z$ = FGRZ, and the factor CSTDIFFMIX on $D_1$. LDIFZ always comes with LTHOUL, since PARMIN sets LTHOUL (and LDIFY) whenever LDIFZ is on, so the metals' coefficients are always set when they are used (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 1239–1242↗; lines 295–319↗).

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Gravitational settling & microscopic diffusion): LDIFY, LDIFZ, LDIFLI turn on the settling of helium, metals and the light species; FGRY, FGRZ (default 1) scale the diffusion coefficients of helium and metals (for example 0.75 without rotational mixing); XMIN and YMIN (1.0D-3) turn diffusion off at low central $X$ (and, the documentation says, low surface $Y$). ILAMBDA chooses the Coulomb logarithm of the collision integrals: the documentation's line reads "4 (recommended) is a constant, 2.2". Among the rarely used options: LTHOUL, the formalism of Thoul et al., and LTHOULFIT, its published fit instead of the full solution (not recommended).

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) The derivatives miss the mixing factors. (lines 288–291↗; line 311↗; line 316↗) With LTHOUL, COD1 and COD2 are multiplied by CSTMIXING, but their $X$-derivatives QCOD1X, QCOD2X are not; COD1Z is multiplied by CSTDIFFMIX*CSTMIXING, but its derivative QCOD1Z by CSTMIXING only. GRSETT uses the derivatives to correct the coefficients for the change in $X$ (and $Z$) within the step (lines 173–185↗; lines 19–21↗), so with a factor other than 1 the correction is that of the unscaled coefficient. What follows: The second-order correction of the settling coefficients is off by the factor; the size of the effect was not estimated. Only with LTHOUL (off by default; forced on by LDIFZ and LDIFLI) and CSTMIXING or CSTDIFFMIX set to a value other than 1 (both default 1). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

30def setup_grsett(dt: float, hqpr: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hd: np.ndarray, hs1: np.ndarray, ht: np.ndarray,
31                 lc: np.ndarray, m: int, stot: float, cod1: np.ndarray, cod2: np.ndarray, hcomp: np.ndarray,
32                 hru: np.ndarray, htu: np.ndarray, ibegin: int, iend: int, lallcz: bool, qcod1x: np.ndarray,
33                 qcod2x: np.ndarray, cod1z: np.ndarray, cod2z: np.ndarray, qcod1z: np.ndarray,
34                 qcod2z: np.ndarray) -> tuple[float, float, int, int, bool]:
35    """Find the shells where settling is computed (above a convective
36    core with X > XMIN, below a convective envelope), convert the model to
37    Bahcall and Loeb's units (conversion factors to confac_mod), and
38    compute the helium (and, with LDIFZ, metal) diffusion coefficients and
39    their X-derivatives for every shell.
40
41    Translates: setup_grsett.f#SETUP_GRSETT (7be4133)
42
43    Arguments: dt (s), stot (g), hqpr (d ln P/dr), hs1 (g): converted in
44    place or returned; hr, hd, ht (logs); lc (convective flags); m; hcomp;
45    hru, htu (filled: r, T in B&L units); ibegin, iend, lallcz (only
46    set); cod1, cod2, qcod1x, qcod2x, cod1z, cod2z, qcod1z, qcod2z
47    (filled, index 0 unused).
48
49    Returns (dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz).
50
51    """
52
53    c = constants_mod
54    n = nmlpar_mod
55    cf = confac_mod
56    sdel = scrtch_mod.sdel
57
58    # Solar radius (cm) and seconds per year, Bahcall and Loeb's values
59    # (Fortran lines 107–108).
60    crsun_bah = 6.9598e10
61    csecyr_bah = 3.1558e7
62
63    # LALLCZ: fully convective (or no shell qualifies), no settling
64    # (Fortran line 110).
65    lallcz = False
66
67    # Convective core (Fortran lines 112–126).
68    if lc[1]:
69        for i in range(2, m + 1):
70            if not lc[i]:
71                break                                   # go to 20
72        else:
73            lallcz = True
74            iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
75                              "  FULLY CONVECTIVE MODEL - NO SETTLING")
76            return dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz       # go to 9999
77        # Overshoot (to be added).
78        ibegin = i - 1
79    else:
80        ibegin = 1
81
82    # Hydrogen-exhausted core (MHP 6/90) (Fortran lines 128–137).
83    for i in range(ibegin, m + 1):
84        if hcomp[1, i] > n.xmin:
85            break                                       # go to 25
86    else:
87        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
88                          f" X BELOW {n.xmin:9.6f} IN WHOLE MODEL-NO SETTLING")
89        lallcz = True
90        return dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz
91    ibegin = i                                          # label 25
92
93    # Convective envelope (Fortran lines 139–148).
94    if lc[m]:
95        i = m - 1
96        for i in range(m - 1, 1, -1):
97            if not lc[i]:
98                break                                   # go to 40
99        else:
100            i = 1           # the Fortran's DO index after the last pass
101        # Overshoot (to be added).
102        iend = i + 1
103    else:
104        iend = m
105
106    # Helium-exhausted surface: the outer point is wherever Y > YMIN
107    # (Fortran lines 151–160).
108    for i in range(iend, 0, -1):
109        if hcomp[2, i] > n.ymin:
110            break                                       # go to 47
111    else:
112        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
113                          f" Y BELOW {n.ymin:9.6f} IN WHOLE MODEL-NO SETTLING")
114        lallcz = True
115        return dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz
116    iend = i                                            # label 47
117
118    # Conversion factors (Fortran lines 165–169).
119    cf.con_rad = 1.0 / crsun_bah
120    cf.con_mass = 1.0e-2 * cf.con_rad**3
121    cf.con_temp = 1.0e-7
122    # Includes the factor 2.2 from ln Lambda. Question (see the page): ln
123    # Lambda counted twice?
124    cf.con_time = 2.7e13 * csecyr_bah
125
126    # To Bahcall and Loeb's units (Fortran lines 172–181).
127    for i in range(1, m + 1):
128        hru[i] = math.exp(c.cln * hr[i]) * cf.con_rad
129        htu[i] = math.exp(c.cln * ht[i]) * cf.con_temp
130        hs1[i] = hs1[i] * cf.con_mass
131        hqpr[i] = hqpr[i] / cf.con_rad
132    dt = dt / cf.con_time
133    stot = stot * cf.con_mass
134
135    # Diffusion coefficients, for every shell (BC 5/90) (Fortran lines
136    # 189–327).
137    for i in range(1, m + 1):                           # loop 60
138        # The Coulomb logarithm (Fortran lines 191–204). Question (see the
139        # page): which ILAMBDA is recommended?
140        x = hcomp[1, i]
141        if n.ilambda == 2:
142            # Noerdlinger (1977).
143            ln_lambda = (-1.97e1 - 0.5 * math.log(1.0 / (0.75 * x + 0.25))
144                         - 0.5 * hd[i] * c.cln + 1.5 * ht[i] * c.cln)
145        elif n.ilambda == 3:
146            # Loeb (1989).
147            ln_lambda = -1.9105747e1 - 0.50 * hd[i] * c.cln \
148                + 1.5 * ht[i] * c.cln
149        else:
150            ln_lambda = 2.2
151
152        # Fortran lines 206–210.
153        fac = n.fgry * hru[i]**2 * htu[i]**2.5 / ln_lambda
154        z = hcomp[3, i] + hcomp[4, i]
155        zz = hcomp[3, i]
156        x2 = x * x
157
158        if not n.lthoul:
159            # Bahcall and Loeb (Fortran lines 211–222).
160            xz = x * z
161            x3 = x2 * x  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
162            fac2 = 5.4 + 6.3 * x - 4.5 * x2
163            # Question (see the page): +0.32 here, -0.32 in the comment.
164            cod1[i] = fac * hqpr[i] * (x - x2 - xz) * (
165                1.25 + sdel[2, i] * 6.0 * (x + 0.32) / fac2)
166            cod2[i] = fac * (x + 3.0) / (5.0 * x2 + 8.0 * x + 3.0)
167            qcod1x[i] = fac * hqpr[i] * (
168                (1.0 - 2.0 * x - z)
169                * (1.25 + (6.0 * sdel[2, i] * (x + 0.32)) / fac2)
170                + (x - x2 - xz) * 6.0 * sdel[2, i]
171                * (3.384 + 2.88 * x + 4.5 * x2) / fac2**2)
172            qcod2x[i] = (-fac * (5.0 * x2 + 3.0e1 * x + 2.1e1)
173                         / (5.0 * x2 + 8.0 * x + 3.0)**2)
174        else:
175            # Thoul et al.: A_P and A_T from THDIFF or the fits (Fortran
176            # lines 224–281).
177            xfrac[1] = hcomp[1, i]
178            xfrac[2] = hcomp[2, i]
179            xfrac[3] = hcomp[3, i]
180            if not n.lthoulfit:
181                if n.ilambda == 4:
182                    _pair_coulomb_logs(hd[i], ht[i])
183                else:
184                    for ii in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
185                        for jj in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
186                            tcl[ii, jj] = ln_lambda
187                thdiff(NSPEC, ATOMWT, ZSPEC, xfrac, tcl, tap, tat, tac)
188                ap = -tap[1]
189                at = -sdel[2, i] * tat[1]
190            else:
191                ap = 1.58 - 2.42 * x + 0.844 * x2
192                at = sdel[2, i] * (1.90 - 2.69 * x + 0.805 * x2)
193
194            # A_C and the derivatives, from the fits (Fortran lines
195            # 282–285). Question (see the page): derivatives from the fits
196            # always.
197            ac = 1.15 - 1.42 * x + 0.647 * x2
198            qapx = -2.42 + 1.688 * x
199            qatx = sdel[2, i] * (-2.69 + 1.61 * x)
200            qacx = -1.42 + 1.294 * x
201
202            # Mimic mixing, to reduce settling (FD 10/09) (Fortran lines
203            # 288–291). Suspected bug (see the page): the derivatives lack
204            # CSTMIXING.
205            cod1[i] = n.cstmixing * fac * hqpr[i] * x * (ap + at)
206            cod2[i] = n.cstmixing * fac * ac
207            qcod1x[i] = fac * hqpr[i] * (ap + at + x * (qapx + qatx))
208            qcod2x[i] = fac * qacx
209
210        # Metals: Thoul et al.'s coefficients for fully ionized iron
211        # (Fortran lines 295–319); PARMIN sets LTHOUL with LDIFZ.
212        if n.ldifz:
213            fac = n.fgrz * hru[i]**2 * htu[i]**2.5 / ln_lambda
214            if n.lthoul:
215                if n.lthoulfit:
216                    ap = -0.157 - 0.511 * x + 0.389 * x2
217                    at = sdel[2, i] * (-1.36 - 1.42 * x + 0.549 * x2)
218                else:
219                    ap = -tap[3]
220                    at = -sdel[2, i] * tat[3]
221                ah = -0.0375 - 0.193 * x + 0.107 * x2
222                cod1z[i] = (n.cstdiffmix * n.cstmixing * fac * hqpr[i]
223                            * zz * (ap + at))
224                # Positive diffusion coefficients needed.
225                cod2z[i] = n.cstmixing * abs(fac * ah)
226                # Suspected bug (see the page): QCOD1Z lacks CSTDIFFMIX.
227                qcod1z[i] = n.cstmixing * fac * hqpr[i] * (ap + at)
228                qcod2z[i] = 0.0
229
230        # Noerdlinger's ln Lambda adds a term to d(D1)/dX (Fortran lines
231        # 323–326).
232        if n.ilambda == 2:
233            qcod1x[i] = qcod1x[i] + cod1[i] * 1.5 / (
234                ln_lambda * (3.0 * x + 1.0))
235
236    return dt, stot, ibegin, iend, lallcz              # label 9999

239def _pair_coulomb_logs(hdi: float, hti: float) -> None:
240    """ILAMBDA = 4: a Coulomb logarithm for each pair of species, from the
241    larger of the Debye length and the interionic distance (into tcl)
242    (Fortran lines 229–267).
243
244    """
245
246    c = constants_mod
247    rho = math.exp(c.cln * hdi)
248    t = math.exp(c.cln * hti)
249
250    # Concentrations from mass fractions (Fortran lines 232–239).
251    zxa = 0.0
252    for ii in range(1, NSPEC):
253        zxa = zxa + ZSPEC[ii] * xfrac[ii] / ATOMWT[ii]
254    for ii in range(1, NSPEC):
255        cfrac[ii] = xfrac[ii] / (ATOMWT[ii] * zxa)
256    cfrac[NSPEC] = 1.0
257
258    # Electron density from the mass density (Fortran lines 241–245).
259    ac = 0.0
260    for ii in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
261        ac = ac + ATOMWT[ii] * cfrac[ii]
262    ne = rho / (1.6726e-24 * ac)
263
264    # Interionic distance (Fortran lines 247–251).
265    ni = 0.0
266    for ii in range(1, NSPEC):
267        ni = ni + cfrac[ii] * ne
268    ao = (0.23873 / ni)**c.cc13
269
270    # Debye length (Fortran lines 253–257).
271    cz = 0.0
272    for ii in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
273        cz = cz + cfrac[ii] * ZSPEC[ii]**2
274    lambdad = 6.9010 * math.sqrt(t / (ne * cz))
275
276    # The Coulomb logarithms (Fortran lines 259–267).
277    lam = max(lambdad, ao)
278    for ii in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
279        for jj in range(1, NSPEC + 1):
280            xij = 2.3939e3 * t * lam / abs(ZSPEC[ii] * ZSPEC[jj])
281            tcl[ii, jj] = 0.81245 * math.log(1.0 + 0.18769 * xij**1.2)
