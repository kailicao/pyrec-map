Purpose

EQRELV is the equation of state for fully ionized gas with partially degenerate, possibly relativistic electrons. Given $\log T$ and $\log P$, it finds the density whose total pressure (ions, electrons and radiation, plus an optional Debye–Hückel correction) equals $P$, and then the thermodynamic derivatives the structure equations need. The comment points to "Prather's thesis" for details.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: only EQSTAT2: alone where the gas is fully ionized ($\log T \ge$ TSCUT), and blended with the Saha solution (EQSAHA) in a transition band below that.
  • Inputs: TL, PL ($\log_{10}$ of T and P), BETA ($P_\mathrm{gas}/P$), AMU (ions per unit mass, in units of $1/m_\mathrm{H}$), EMU (electrons per unit mass, same units); DL is the starting guess.
  • Outputs: density DL, D; electron degeneracy ETA; and the derivatives: QDT $=\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$, QDP $=\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$, QCP ($c_P$), DELA ($\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$), and their logarithmic derivatives QDTT, QDTP, QCPT, QCPP, QAT, QAP.
  • Modules: CCR_MOD (formerly /CCR/) holds the electron (Fermi) table DTABLE, its abscissae DVAL, the degeneracy ETAT8 and an index table IDEN; it is shared only with SETUPS, which reads it from the Fermi-table file (unit IFERMI) and builds IDEN. DEBHU_MOD (formerly /DEBHU/) holds the Debye–Hückel constant CDH and the composition, and NMLPAR_MOD the settings ETADH0, ETADH1 (the ramp limits) and the flag LDH; the callers of the equation of state (PHYSIC, COEFFT, CRRECT, SCONVEC, HSUBP, GETNEWENV, STARIN, WRTMOD, WRTOUT) set its composition XXDH, YYDH, ZZDH, ZDH from the shell's HCOMP before each call. CONSTANTS_MOD holds the constants ($\ln 10$ = CLN, $R$ = CGAS); IOUNITS_MOD the output unit ISHORT.

The method

  1. Table lookup. The electron pressure is tabulated as a function of $x = \log\rho + \log\mu_e^{-1} - \tfrac32\log T$ and $y = \log T$ (columns of DTABLE: $\log P_e$, $\log U_e$, and derivatives). The code interpolates with three points in each direction: Newton (equal spacing) in $y$, column $j$ at $\log T = 5 + 0.2j$, so the 20 columns span $\log T = 5.2$ to 9.0; Lagrange in $x$ (unequal spacing).

The temperature index is forced into the table (JT1 between 1 and 18) and the density index into 1..261; outside the table the values are extrapolated silently. Should a warning be written? 2. Pressure balance. With $P_\mathrm{rad} = (1-\beta)P$ and $P_\mathrm{ion} = R\mu_\mathrm{ion}^{-1}\rho T$ (PA = CGAS*AMU*T*D), $$P_\mathrm{tot}(\rho) = P_\mathrm{ion} + P_e + P_\mathrm{rad}\;(+\,P_\mathrm{DH}),\qquad P_\mathrm{DH} = C_\mathrm{DH}\,c_\mathrm{mix}\,\rho^{3/2}T^{-1/2}.$$ It solves $\log P_\mathrm{tot} = \log P$ for $\log\rho$ by a Newton step with a second-order correction: with $f = (\log P_\mathrm{tot} - \log P)/\chi_\rho$ (PR8), $\chi_\rho = \partial\log P_\mathrm{tot}/\partial\log\rho$ (PR7) and the curvature term PR9, $$\Delta\log\rho = -f - \tfrac12\,\mathrm{PR9}\,f^2,$$ limited to $\pm 4$ $\mathrm{dex}$, until $|\Delta| < 10^{-8}$, at most 20 iterations.

    Question 2 (lines 210–222↗). Unconverged return. After 20 iterations without convergence the routine writes a message and returns at once (the STOP is commented out). It returns before DL = DL8, so DL and D keep the caller's starting guess, and the derivatives (all arguments) keep whatever the caller's variables held; no flag tells the caller. Is this fallback intended? 3. Debye–Hückel ramp. With $c_0 = 2X + \tfrac32 Y + \tfrac12(Z + \alpha)$ (CMFDH0), $c_1 = X + Y + \tfrac12\alpha$ (CMFDH1) and $\alpha = \sum_i$ DHNUE(i)*ZDH(i) over the metal mixture, the composition factor $c_\mathrm{mix}$ is $c_0^{3/2}$ for non-degenerate electrons ($\eta <$ ETADH0), $c_0\sqrt{c_1}$ for degenerate ones ($\eta >$ ETADH1), and linear in $\eta$ between; the comment calls this "not correct but, hopefully, close enough." 4. Derivatives. From the table's derivative columns it forms $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln T$ and $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln\rho$ and their second derivatives, then inverts them: $Q_{\rho P} = 1/\chi_\rho$, $Q_{\rho T} = -\chi_T/\chi_\rho$. The internal energy is $U = \tfrac32 P_\mathrm{ion}/\rho + U_e + 3P_\mathrm{rad}/\rho\;(+\,3P_\mathrm{DH}/\rho)$, and $$c_P = -\frac{P}{\rho T}Q_{\rho T}\,\chi_T + \frac{U}{T}\,\frac{\partial\ln U}{\partial\ln T}\Big|_\rho, \qquad \nabla_\mathrm{ad} = -\frac{P}{\rho T}\frac{Q_{\rho T}}{c_P}.$$
