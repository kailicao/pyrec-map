Purpose

SURFBC provides the outer boundary condition of the interior solution, by the "envelope triangle". Envelopes are integrated at three points around the model's $(\log T_\mathrm{eff}, \log L)$; each gives $\log P$, $\log T$ and $\log r_\mathrm{f}$, the radius at the fitting point. A plane through the three points then gives $\log r_\mathrm{f}$, $\log L$ and $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ as linear functions of $\log P$ and $\log T$ at the fitting point, which the Henyey solution uses as its outer boundary. It also switches between atmosphere tables and the gray atmosphere as $T_\mathrm{eff}$ changes.

The fitting point. It is the interior's outermost mass point, at $\log(m_\mathrm{f}/M)$ = SENV (SENV = HS(M) - HSTOT, set in CRRECT, HPOINT and STARIN; the documentation's standard is $-10^{-4}$, so about $2\times10^{-4}$ of the mass lies outside it). The layers above it are not on the Henyey grid but are integrated as a static envelope by ENVINT: the YREC paper (§2.2) explains that they hold little mass, typically one part in $10^4$ or less, but pressure and density fall steeply there, so leaving them out of the interior model is much faster, and the structure equations are more stable with a deeper fitting point. The outermost point has four unknowns ($\log P$, $\log T$, $\log r$, $L$); the plane fixes two relations among them, which are the two outer equations of the Henyey system in CRRECT (HSRF): $$\log r = c_1\log P + c_2\log T + c_3, \qquad \log L = c_4\log P + c_5\log T + c_6,$$ where $c_1, \dots, c_6$ are CFENV(1:6) and $P$, $T$, $r$, $L$ are the outermost point's; after the solution, CRRECT sets $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ from the third plane, CFENV(7:9).

How it fits

  • Inputs: arguments from CRRECT: the current $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ (TEFFL), $\log L/L_\odot$ (BL), the triangle (TRIT, TRIL, FTRI) and its envelopes' results (PS, TS, RS) from earlier calls, the surface $X$, $Z$, the rotation factors FPL, FTL, the pressure limit PLIM, and the mixing length CMIXL (passed on to ENVINT). The triangle's size TRIDT, TRIDL, the atmosphere choice (KTTAU, KTTAU0, LTTAU) and the spot parameters (SPOTF, SPOTX) come from NMLPAR_MOD; the Allard table's upper limit AL_TEFFLmax from ALATM05_MOD, set by ALTABINIT.
  • Who calls it: only CRRECT.
  • What it calls: ENVINT for each envelope to be recomputed.
  • Outputs: CFENV(1:9), the plane's coefficients, which CRRECT uses for the boundary equations and to update $T_\mathrm{eff}$; the triangle and PS, TS, RS; LFLP (true if an envelope was recomputed); a table of the triangle on ISHORT.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (the atmosphere flags, shared with ENVINT, PARMIN, SETUPS and others; this routine changes KTTAU and LTTAU), COMP_MOD (the total mass STOTAL), ALATM05_MOD, CONSTANTS_MOD, IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT), JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

The method

  1. Atmosphere switch (MHP 9/01, 06/13). With Kurucz tables (KTTAU = 3) above $\log T_\mathrm{eff} = 3.95$, or Allard tables (KTTAU = 4) above the table's maximum, the code switches to the gray atmosphere (KTTAU = 0), remembers it (LTTAU), and starts a new triangle; it switches back when $T_\mathrm{eff}$ drops below the limit again, and starts a new triangle then too, since the old vertices were integrated with the gray $T(\tau)$.

    Question 1 (lines 83–108↗). The Kurucz–Castelli tables. The switch to the gray atmosphere covers KTTAU = 3 (Kurucz) and 4 (Allard), but not KTTAU = 5 (Kurucz–Castelli, used by ENVINT through KCSURFP), whose lookup extrapolates above the table's last temperature without a message. Does that table need no limit?

    Question 2 (line 84↗; line 110↗). Hard-coded limit. The gray switch for Kurucz tables is at $\log T_\mathrm{eff} = 3.95$, written in the code; should it come from the table's range, as for the Allard tables?

  2. A new triangle (LNEW). With LRESET (from CRRECT) all three envelopes are recomputed without starting a new triangle; with LNEW the triangle is new too, and its vertices, with $T = \log T_\mathrm{eff}$, $L = \log L$, $$(T - \tfrac12\Delta T,\ L - \tfrac12\Delta L),\quad (T + \tfrac12\Delta T,\ L - \tfrac12\Delta L),\quad (T,\ L + \tfrac12\Delta L).$$ Otherwise the code checks that the point lies inside the triangle: for each vertex $i$, with the other two $j$, $k$, the sign of $$f\left[(L_j - L_k)(T - T_j) + (T_k - T_j)(L - L_j)\right]$$ ($f$ = FTRI, the orientation) must not be negative. If it is, vertex $i$ is replaced by $T_i \to T_j + T_k - T_i$, $L_i \to L_j + L_k - L_i$ (the triangle flips across that side, and its orientation changes), and the check repeats. After 1,000 reflections the run stops (STOP 1), with a message on the screen and ISHORT; the walk never ended for a degenerate triangle.

    Question 3 (line 138↗; line 144↗; line 167↗; line 172↗). The tolerance TRIERR. It is always zero, so the point must be strictly inside or on the triangle. Is the variable left from an older tolerance?

  3. New envelopes. For each new vertex, ENVINT integrates at $L_i$ and $T_i$ (the vertex's $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ is kept in the local VTEFFL), with $\log R = \tfrac12(\log L + \log L_\odot - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff} - \log 4\pi - \log\sigma)$ and $\log g = \log G + \log M - 2\log R$; for a spotted star whose outermost point is convective (LC(M)), at the ambient temperature $T_\mathrm{amb}$ of the unspotted surface, $$T_\mathrm{amb}^4 = \frac{T_\mathrm{eff}^4}{f x^4 + 1 - f},$$ where $f$ is the spot filling factor (SPOTF) and $x = T_\mathrm{spot}/T_\mathrm{amb}$ (SPOTX), so that spots and ambient surface together radiate $\sigma T_\mathrm{eff}^4$ (the same formula as in starin.f).

  4. The plane. With $(P_i, T^\mathrm{f}_i)$ the fitting point's $\log P$ and $\log T$ of envelope $i$, each $Q \in \{\log r_\mathrm{f}, \log L, \log T_\mathrm{eff}\}$ is fitted exactly through the three envelopes: $$Q = c_1\,\log P + c_2\,\log T + c_3,$$ with CFENV(1:3) for $\log r_\mathrm{f}$, the radius at the fitting point, CFENV(4:6) for $\log L$, CFENV(7:9) for $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$. A degenerate fit stops the run (STOP 1), with the three fitting points on the screen and ISHORT: two points at the same $\log T$ ($|T^\mathrm{f}_2 - T^\mathrm{f}_1| \le 10^{-10}|T^\mathrm{f}_3 - T^\mathrm{f}_1|$), or a pivot DEN below $10^{-10}$ of the differences it is made of (three points collinear in $(\log P, \log T)$); either gave infinite or NaN coefficients before.

    Question 4 (lines 146–154↗; lines 253–262↗). The accuracy of the plane. The boundary condition is linear in $\log P$ and $\log T$ across a triangle of $\Delta\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ = TRIDT = 0.01 and $\Delta\log L$ = TRIDL = 0.08 by default. What error in $R$ or $T_\mathrm{eff}$ does this linearization introduce, and was the default size tested against a smaller triangle, for instance in the solar calibration? A reader would expect the curvature of $\log r_\mathrm{f}$ with $T_\mathrm{eff}$ to matter most where the envelope's structure changes fast, such as near the onset of surface convection.

Design

NUMENV here is a local counter (DATA NUMENV/0/), not the envelope's point count in ENVSTRUCT_MOD. The comment "IF LNEW0, REDO ALL 3 ENVELOPES EVERY MODEL" refers to the caller; LNEW0 is not used here.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Surface boundary conditions): the surface boundary condition is solved from three envelope models; TRIDT (default 0.01) is the spacing in $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ between two of them, and TRIDL (0.08) the spacing in $\log L$ of the third, at their mean $T_\mathrm{eff}$. KTTAU selects the atmosphere (the documentation lists 0 Eddington, 1 Krishna Swamy, 3 Kurucz, 4 Allard).

16# calls (not the envelope's point count in envstruct_mod) (Fortran line 77).
17numenv = 0

20def surfbc(trit: np.ndarray, tril: np.ndarray, cfenv: np.ndarray, ps: np.ndarray, ts: np.ndarray, rs: np.ndarray,
21           ftri: float, istore: int, estore: np.ndarray, lnew: bool, lreset: bool, ksaha: int, kenv: int, katm: int,
22           hstot: float, bl: float, teffl: float, x: float, z: float, fpl: float, ftl: float, lflp: bool, plim: float,
23           lc: np.ndarray, m: int, cmixl: float) -> tuple[float, int, bool, bool, int, int, int, bool]:
24    """Keep three envelope integrations around (log Teff, log L), flip the
25    triangle when the model leaves it, and fit the plane cfenv that gives
26    log R, log L and log Teff from log P and log T at the fitting point;
27    switch between atmosphere tables and the gray atmosphere as Teff
28    changes.
29
30    Translates: surfbc.f#SURFBC (7be4133)
31
32    Arguments: the triangle trit, tril (log Teff, log L of its vertices,
33    index 1-3) and its envelopes' fitting-point values ps, ts, rs (log P,
34    log T, log R), cfenv (the plane's 9 coefficients), ftri (orientation),
35    istore, estore (passed to envint), lnew (start a new triangle), lreset
36    (recompute all three envelopes), the counters ksaha, kenv, katm, hstot
37    (log M), bl (log L/Lsun), teffl (log Teff), the surface x, z, the
38    rotation factors fpl, ftl, plim, lc (convective flags; lc[m] for the
39    spots), m, and cmixl (the mixing length, passed to envint). Arrays are
40    changed in place; lflp is output only. Stops (SystemExit) when the
41    triangle's reflection walk exceeds 1000 reflections or the plane fit is
42    degenerate.
43
44    Returns (ftri, istore, lnew, lreset, ksaha, kenv, katm, lflp): lflp is
45    True if an envelope was recomputed. teffl is no longer changed (7be4133:
46    each vertex is integrated at a local vteffl).
47
48    """
49
50    global numenv
51    c = constants_mod
52    nml = nmlpar_mod
53    ltri = [None, False, False, False]
54
55    # Above the atmosphere table's range, switch to the gray atmosphere and
56    # start a new triangle (MHP 9/01) (Fortran lines 82–108).
57    # Question (see the page): the limit 3.95 is hard-coded; KTTAU = 5 has
58    # no switch.
59    if nml.kttau == 3:
60        if teffl >= 3.95:
61            print()
62            print(f"LOG TEFF OF {teffl:7.3f} ABOVE 3.95 - SWITCH"
63                  f"TO GRAY ATMOSPHERE BOUNDARY CONDITION")  # format 5
64            print()
65            nml.kttau = 0
66            lnew = True
67            nml.lttau = True  # remember that the flag is switched (MHP 06/13)
68    if nml.kttau == 4:
69        if teffl >= alatm05_mod.al_tefflmax:
70            print()
71            print(f"LOG TEFF OF {teffl:7.3f} ABOVE Allard Table max "
72                  f"{alatm05_mod.al_tefflmax:7.3f}"
73                  f"  - SWITCH TO GRAY ATMOSPHERE BOUNDARY CONDITION")
74            print()
75            nml.kttau = 0
76            lnew = True
77            nml.lttau = True
78
79    # Switch back below the limit, with a new triangle, since the old
80    # vertices were integrated with the gray T(tau) (MHP 10/30/26)
81    # (Fortran lines 109–129).
82    if nml.lttau:
83        if nml.kttau0 == 3 and teffl < 3.95:
84            nml.kttau = nml.kttau0
85            nml.lttau = False
86            lnew = True
87            print(f"LOG TEFF OF {teffl:7.3f} BELOW 3.95 - SWITCH"
88                  f" BACK TO KURUCZ ATMOSPHERE BOUNDARY CONDITION")
89        elif nml.kttau0 == 4 and teffl < alatm05_mod.al_tefflmax:
90            nml.kttau = nml.kttau0
91            nml.lttau = False
92            lnew = True
93            print(f"LOG TEFF OF {teffl:7.3f} below Allard Table max "
94                  f"{alatm05_mod.al_tefflmax:7.3f}"
95                  f"  - SWITCH BACK TO ALLARD ATMOSPHERE BOUNDARY CONDITION")
96
97    # Which envelopes to redo (Fortran lines 130–145). Question (see the
98    # page): trierr is always zero.
99    lflp = False
100    if lnew or lreset:
101        # Redo all three envelopes.
102        ltri[1] = ltri[2] = ltri[3] = False
103        istore = 0
104        trierr = 0.0
105        lreset = False
106    else:
107        ltri[1] = ltri[2] = ltri[3] = True
108        trierr = 0.0
109
110    if lnew:
111        # A new triangle about (teffl, bl) (Fortran lines 146–155).
112        ftri = 1.0
113        trit[3] = teffl
114        trit[1] = trit[3] - 0.5 * nml.tridt
115        trit[2] = trit[1] + nml.tridt
116        tril[3] = bl + 0.5 * nml.tridl
117        tril[1] = tril[3] - nml.tridl
118        tril[2] = tril[1]
119        lnew = False
120    else:
121        # Check that (teffl, bl) is inside the triangle; flip a vertex
122        # across the opposite side until it is, at most 1000 times
123        # (MHP 10/30/26) (Fortran lines 156–185).
124        nrefl = 0
125        flipped = True
126        while flipped:  # label 10
127            flipped = False
128            for i1 in range(1, 4):  # loop 20
129                i2 = i1 % 3 + 1
130                i3 = i2 % 3 + 1
131                temp = ftri * ((tril[i2] - tril[i3]) * (teffl - trit[i2])
132                               + (trit[i3] - trit[i2]) * (bl - tril[i2]))
133                if temp < -trierr:
134                    ftri = -ftri
135                    trit[i1] = trit[i2] + trit[i3] - trit[i1]
136                    tril[i1] = tril[i2] + tril[i3] - tril[i1]
137                    ltri[i1] = False
138                    trierr = 0.0
139                    nrefl = nrefl + 1
140                    if nrefl > 1000:
141                        text = (" SURFBC: ENVELOPE TRIANGLE WALK FAILED AFTER"
142                                f" 1000 REFLECTIONS AT LOG TEFF{teffl:9.5f}"
143                                f" LOG L{bl:9.5f}\n RUN STOPPED")  # format 60
144                        print(text)
145                        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort, text)
146                        raise SystemExit(1)
147                    flipped = True
148                    break  # GOTO 10
149
150    # Compute new envelopes where needed (Fortran lines 186–229).
151    lrenv = False
152    for i in range(1, 4):  # loop 40
153        if not ltri[i]:
154            lflp = True
155            # The vertex's log Teff, kept local (MHP 10/29/26).
156            vteffl = trit[i]
157            b = math.exp(c.cln * tril[i])
158            rl = 0.5 * (tril[i] + c.clsunl - 4.0 * vteffl - c.c4pil
159                        - c.csigl)
160            gl = c.cgl + comp_mod.stotal - rl - rl
161            numenv = numenv + 1
162            lpenv = False  # part of LSTENV (G Somers 11/14)
163            ixx = i
164            lsbc0 = True
165            lpulpt = False  # no pulsation output
166            # For spotted stars, the ambient temperature (G Somers 10/14).
167            if lc[m] and nml.spotf != 0.0 and nml.spotx != 1.0:
168                ateffl = vteffl - 0.25 * math.log10(
169                    nml.spotf * nml.spotx**4.0 + 1.0 - nml.spotf)
170            else:
171                ateffl = vteffl
172            rl, istore, katm, kenv, ksaha = envint(
173                b, fpl, ftl, gl, hstot, ixx, lpenv, lsbc0, plim, rl, ateffl,
174                x, z, estore, istore, katm, kenv, ksaha, ps, rs, ts, lpulpt,
175                cmixl)
176            lrenv = True
177
178    if lrenv:
179        # The plane through the three envelopes, for log R, log L, log Teff;
180        # a degenerate fit stops the run (MHP 10/30/26) (Fortran lines
181        # 230–262).
182        if abs(ts[2] - ts[1]) <= 1.0e-10 * abs(ts[3] - ts[1]):
183            _degenerate_fit(ps, ts)
184        temp = 1.0 / (ts[2] - ts[1])
185        temp1 = (ts[3] - ts[1]) * temp
186        den = ps[3] - ps[1] - temp1 * (ps[2] - ps[1])
187        if abs(den) <= 1.0e-10 * (abs(ps[3] - ps[1])
188                                  + abs(temp1 * (ps[2] - ps[1]))):
189            _degenerate_fit(ps, ts)
190        temp2 = 1.0 / den
191        cfenv[1] = (rs[3] - rs[1] - temp1 * (rs[2] - rs[1])) * temp2
192        cfenv[4] = (tril[3] - tril[1] - temp1 * (tril[2] - tril[1])) * temp2
193        cfenv[7] = (trit[3] - trit[1] - temp1 * (trit[2] - trit[1])) * temp2
194        cfenv[2] = (rs[2] - rs[1] - cfenv[1] * (ps[2] - ps[1])) * temp
195        cfenv[5] = (tril[2] - tril[1] - cfenv[4] * (ps[2] - ps[1])) * temp
196        cfenv[8] = (trit[2] - trit[1] - cfenv[7] * (ps[2] - ps[1])) * temp
197        cfenv[3] = rs[1] - cfenv[1] * ps[1] - cfenv[2] * ts[1]
198        cfenv[6] = tril[1] - cfenv[4] * ps[1] - cfenv[5] * ts[1]
199        cfenv[9] = trit[1] - cfenv[7] * ps[1] - cfenv[8] * ts[1]
200
201        # Format 50 (Fortran lines 263–268).
202        labels = ["", "        LOG(R)", "     LOG(L/L0)", "       LOG(TE)"]
203        text = (" ENVELOPE TRIANGLE  LOG(TE)  LOG(L)   LOG(P)   LOG(T)"
204                "   LOG(R)   COEFFICIENTS")
205        for i in range(1, 4):
206            row = "".join(f"{v:9.5f}"
207                          for v in (trit[i], tril[i], ps[i], ts[i], rs[i]))
208            coef = "".join(f"{cfenv[3 * i - 3 + j]:10.5f}"
209                           for j in range(1, 4))
210            text += (f"\n{i:14d}{'T' if ltri[i] else 'F'}  {row}"
211                     f"{labels[i]}{coef}")
212        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort, text)
213    else:
214        # Fortran lines 270–273.
215        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
216                          " ENVELOPE TRIANGLE NOT CHANGED")  # format 70
217
218    return ftri, istore, lnew, lreset, ksaha, kenv, katm, lflp

221def _degenerate_fit(ps, ts):
222    """Write format 61 to the screen and ISHORT, then STOP 1."""
223
224    # Fortran lines 237–252.
225    text = (" SURFBC: DEGENERATE ENVELOPE TRIANGLE FIT; FITTING POINTS"
226            " (LOG T, LOG P):\n"
227            + "".join(f"{ts[i]:12.7f}{ps[i]:12.7f}\n" for i in range(1, 4))
228            + " RUN STOPPED")
229    print(text)
230    iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort, text)
231    raise SystemExit(1)
