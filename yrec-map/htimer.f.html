Purpose

HTIMER sets the time step of the next model. Several criteria each propose a step, and it takes the most restrictive one (its comment): structure changes (PTIME: the change of $T$, $P$, $r$ and $L$ at any point since the previous model), angular-velocity changes for rotating models (WTIME: the largest fractional change of $\Omega$), hydrogen burning (XTIME: the hydrogen burnt in the core, or in the shell once the core is exhausted), helium burning (YTIME: the helium burnt at the temperature maximum) and, since 04/14, the motion of the model in the envelope-fitting "triangle" (ENTIME: the change of $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ and $\log L$); and the step may not grow by more than a factor ATIME(13) from the previous one (default 1.5, "originally = 1.5" by PARMIN's comment; under its newer name TIME_MAX_DT_FRAC, also 1.5, when REMAP applies the newer names).

How it fits

  • Who calls it: the main program, main.f: at the start of each kind card, and after each converged model to set the next step. main.f always passes $|\Delta t|$ after a model (DELTS = DABS(DELTS)), and makes the step negative itself before a relaxation step.
  • Inputs: the previous step DELTS (s); the model (HD, HL, HS1, HS2, HT, HP, HR, HCOMP, OMEGA, M, TEFFL); JCORE, JXMID, JXBEG (core and burning-shell indices); TLUMX (the luminosities by source, in solar units: elements 1–4 hydrogen burning, 5 helium burning, 7 the gravitational term); the age DAGE (Gyr); the run number NK.
  • Outputs: the new DELTS (s), DELTSH (= DELTS, except on the relaxation branch, where it is left as passed unless the final-age cut applies), DDAGE (the step in years), and DWMAX (from WTIME: the largest change in $\Omega$, used by the rotational-mixing routines to set their number of substeps).
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD holds the settings: ATIME (the step controls, formerly /CTLIM/); per run, SETDT for a fixed step and ENDAGE for a final age, and the stop values for the central abundances END_DCEN, END_XCEN, END_YCEN (since 10/24, which is why LENDAG alone no longer means a stop age); LPTIME (formerly /CT3/; HTIMER can switch it off; default true, set by PARMIN's namelist or by REMAP from LSTRUCT_TIME; main.f restores it at each solar-calibration cycle); LTRIST (the triangle governor, formerly /GOVS/) and LROT (formerly /ROT/). RUNLOOP_MOD holds the per-run flags LSETDT (fixed step) and LENDAG (final age); with SETDT and ENDAGE these were /SETT/, set by PARMIN and shared with main.f, CALCAD, CHKSCAL, SETCAL, SETSCAL. CONSTANTS_MOD gives CSECYR (seconds per year).

The method

  1. A negative DELTS means the model is being relaxed, not evolved: skip to the end.

    Question 1 (lines 85–89↗; lines 211–213↗; main.f, lines 1051–1057↗). The relaxation branch. The comment says a negative DELTS marks a model being relaxed, but main.f passes DABS(DELTS) after every model, so the branch can only be reached at the first call of a kind card (with the step from STARIN); relaxation steps otherwise go through all the criteria. The routine also returns ABS(DELTS), and the ENDAGE truncation applies to every branch. Is the negative branch still needed?

  2. A fixed step for this run (LSETDT(NK)): $\Delta t$ = SETDT(NK) years, skip to the end.

  3. If $\log T_c > 7.1$ and the gravitational luminosity TLUMX(7) is negative, switch the structure criterion off (LPTIME = false) for the rest of the job. Its comment is cut short ("this is done when"); the purpose is in REMAP's comment, "this is used only in the pre-MS and disabled for MS stars automatically," and in the documentation of LSTRUCT_TIME, "disabled once Lgrav<0 (minimum radius, close to ZAMS)" (settled from the code and the documentation, Nov 9, 2026: lines 96–104↗; remap.f, lines 283–285↗).

    Question 2 (lines 98–104↗; main.f, line 383↗; main.f, line 1154↗). The LPTIME switch-off lasts for the job. Once off, LPTIME stays off for all later kind cards of the job; main.f restores it only at each solar-calibration cycle. Should a later kind card that starts again from a pre-main-sequence model turn it back on, and should it stay off through the subgiant branch, where the structure changes fast?

  4. Collect the proposals, each $10^{20}$ s when not applicable or turned off: $\Delta t_P$ from PTIME (if LPTIME), $\Delta t_\Omega$ from WTIME (if rotating), $\Delta t_H$ from XTIME (if the H-burning luminosity exceeds $10^{-34}$), $\Delta t_{He}$ from YTIME (likewise for He), $\Delta t_E$ from ENTIME (if LTRIST and not LPTIME). The structure and rotation proposals ($\Delta t_P$, $\Delta t_\Omega$) below 1 s are replaced by $10^{20}$ ("a zero timestep in the model will fritz out the code"); the others are not checked (for ENTIME, see bug 1).

    Question 3 (lines 134–153↗; xtime.f, lines 65–68↗; ytime.f, lines 122–127↗; ytime.f, lines 171–173↗). Unchecked burning proposals. The core branches of XTIME and YTIME divide by HL(JCORE) without checking its sign (YTIME's shell branch does check it), and HTIMER does not protect $\Delta t_H$ and $\Delta t_{He}$ against values below 1 s, so a zero or negative luminosity at JCORE would give a negative or infinite step, which passes through the minimum (and comes out as its absolute value at the end). Can HL(JCORE) be zero or negative while the central $X$ is at least ATIME(1), or in a helium-burning core?

    Question 4 (line 136↗; line 146↗; line 112↗). Thresholds. The cut $10^{-34}\,L_\odot$ for "no burning" and $10^{20}$ s for "no limit" are hard-coded. Are they meant to stay fixed, or should they be settings?

  5. The new step: $$\Delta t = \min\left(f\,\Delta t_\mathrm{prev},\ \Delta t_H,\ \Delta t_{He},\ \Delta t_\Omega,\ \Delta t_P,\ \Delta t_E\right),\qquad f = \texttt{ATIME(13)},$$ leaving out the first term when $\Delta t_\mathrm{prev} \le 1$ s.

  6. At the end: $\Delta t \leftarrow |\Delta t|$, DDAGE $= \Delta t/$CSECYR; if the run stops at an age (LENDAG(NK) and ENDAGE(NK) > 0), shorten the step so that the age does not pass ENDAGE (in years; the age DAGE is in Gyr).

Design

The switch-off of LPTIME changes a module flag (a namelist setting) permanently, and is announced with a bare WRITE(*,…). A block of commented-out code ("Grant added") once tightened ATIME(5) and ATIME(6) with the helium luminosity.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Timestep criteria): LTRIST "restricts the timestep during the Hertzsprung gap"; in the code it keeps the model from moving more than TRIDT in $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ or TRIDL in $\log L$ in a step (the size of the envelope triangle), at any phase, when the structural criterion (LPTIME, LSTRUCT_TIME) is off. The step limits ATIME are documented under their newer names on REMAP's page; ENDAGE (Control Options) ends a run at an age.

Suspected bugs, checked by running the code

Each bug is marked by what running the code showed: (changes results, large) or (small), the fix moving a run clearly beyond a null change; (stops the run); (wrong output), the fix changing only what is written; (harmless in the runs), within the noise; (never occurred), the code ran but the fault did not; (not reached); (cannot be reached), a guard prevents it; (withdrawn), the suspicion was wrong.

1. (harmless in the runs) A zero previous step passes through the envelope-triangle criterion.

(lines 158–162↗; lines 171–182↗; entime.f, lines 92–95↗) ENTIME returns DELTS/FAC with FAC $\ge 1/$ATIME(13), so a previous step of 0 (or under 1 s) gives an EDELT of at most ATIME(13) times it. Unlike PDELT and DELTSW (line 116↗; line 131↗), it is not replaced by $10^{20}$, and when the previous step is at most 1 s the minimum (line 181↗) takes it: The next step is again under 1.5 s, or 0 for every later model. This needs LTRIST (off by default) with LPTIME off.

37def htimer(delts: float, deltsh: float, m: int, hd: np.ndarray, hl: np.ndarray, hs1: np.ndarray, hs2: np.ndarray,
38           ht: np.ndarray, hcomp: np.ndarray, jcore: int, jxmid: int, tlumx: np.ndarray, dage: float, ddage: float,
39           nk: int, hp: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, omega: np.ndarray, dwmax: float, jxbeg: int,
40           teffl: float) -> tuple[float, float, float, float]:
41    """Set the next time step: the minimum of the steps proposed by
42    PTIME (structure), WTIME (rotation), XTIME (hydrogen burning), YTIME
43    (helium burning) and ENTIME (the envelope triangle), and of
44    atime[13] times the previous step; a fixed step for the run, or a
45    negative step (relaxation), skips the criteria; the step is cut so
46    that the age does not pass the run's final age.
47
48    Translates: htimer.f#HTIMER (7be4133)
49
50    Arguments: delts (the previous step, s), deltsh (left as passed on
51    the relaxation branch), the model (m, hd, hl, hs1, hs2, ht, hcomp,
52    hp, hr, omega, teffl), jcore, jxmid, jxbeg (core and shell indices),
53    tlumx (luminosities by source, solar units), dage (age, Gyr), nk
54    (the run); ddage is only set; dwmax is set by WTIME when rotating.
55
56    Returns (delts, deltsh, ddage, dwmax).
57
58    """
59
60    nml = nmlpar_mod
61    run = runloop_mod
62    c = constants_mod
63
64    # A negative step marks a model being relaxed; a fixed step for this run
65    # (Fortran lines 89–95).
66    relax_or_fixed = False
67    # Question (see the page): is the negative branch still needed?
68    if delts < 0.0:
69        relax_or_fixed = True  # GOTO 310
70    elif run.lsetdt[nk]:
71        deltsh = nml.setdt[nk] * c.csecyr
72        delts = deltsh
73        relax_or_fixed = True  # GOTO 310
74
75    if not relax_or_fixed:
76        # Switch the structure criterion off near the ZAMS (Fortran lines
77        # 96–104).
78        # Question (see the page): it stays off for the rest of the job.
79        if nml.lptime:
80            if ht[1] > 7.1 and tlumx[7] < 0.0:
81                print(f'LPTIME SET FALSE - TC {ht[1]:7.4f}'
82                      f' EGRAV {tlumx[7]:10.2E}')
83                nml.lptime = False
84
85        # The structure criterion; a zero time step in the model will fritz
86        # out the code (Fortran lines 109–116).
87        if nml.lptime:
88            pdelt = ptime(delts, hl, hp, hr, ht, m, 0.0)
89        else:
90            pdelt = 1.0e20
91        if pdelt < 1.0:
92            pdelt = 1.0e20
93
94        # The rotation criterion (Fortran lines 124–131).
95        if nml.lrot:
96            deltsw, dwmax = wtime(delts, m, omega, 0.0, dwmax)
97        else:
98            deltsw = 1.0e20
99        if deltsw < 1.0:
100            deltsw = 1.0e20
101
102        # Hydrogen burning (Fortran lines 134–143).
103        # Question (see the page): the burning proposals are not checked
104        # against values below 1 s; the thresholds are hard-coded.
105        hydlum = tlumx[1] + tlumx[2] + tlumx[3] + tlumx[4]
106        if hydlum > 1.0e-34:
107            deltsh = xtime(hd, hcomp, hl, hs1, hs2, ht, hydlum, jcore, jxmid,
108                           m, deltsh, hr1, hr2, hr3, hr4, hr5, hr6, hr7, hr8,
109                           hr9, hr10, hr11, hr12, hr13, hf1, hf2)
110        else:
111            deltsh = 1.0e20
112
113        # Helium burning (Fortran lines 146–153).
114        if tlumx[5] > 1.0e-34:
115            deltsy = ytime(eg, hcomp, hd, hl, hs1, ht, jcore, m, 0.0, hr1,
116                           hr2, hr3, hr4, hr5, hr6, hr7, hr8, hr9, hr10, hr11,
117                           hr12, hr13, hf1, hf2, jxbeg)
118        else:
119            deltsy = 1.0e20
120
121        # The envelope triangle (Fortran lines 158–162).
122        # Suspected bug (see the page): edelt is not checked against values
123        # below 1 s, as pdelt and deltsw are.
124        if nml.ltrist and not nml.lptime:
125            edelt = entime(delts, hl, teffl, m, 0.0)
126        else:
127            edelt = 1.0e20
128
129        # The minimum; the step may grow by at most atime[13] (Fortran
130        # lines 171–182).
131        if delts > 1.0:
132            delts = nml.atime[13] * delts
133            delts = min(delts, deltsh, deltsy, deltsw, pdelt, edelt)
134        else:
135            delts = min(deltsh, deltsy, deltsw, pdelt, edelt)
136
137        # Fortran line 211.
138        deltsh = delts
139
140    # Label 310: do not pass the run's final age (endage in years, dage in
141    # Gyr) (Fortran lines 212–226).
142    delts = abs(delts)
143    ddage = delts / c.csecyr
144    if run.lendag[nk] and nml.endage[nk] > 0.0:
145        tleft = nml.endage[nk] - dage * 1.0e9
146        if tleft < ddage:
147            ddage = tleft
148            deltsh = ddage * c.csecyr
149            delts = deltsh
150
151    return delts, deltsh, ddage, dwmax
