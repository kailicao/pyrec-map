Purpose

EQUAL_TO_MODEL ends a step of gravitational settling (microscopic diffusion): the diffusion equations are solved on an equally spaced grid in radius, and this routine applies the resulting changes in hydrogen (and, with metal diffusion, in $Z$) to the model's own points. It then converts the model's arrays back from the dimensionless units used by the diffusion solver.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: only GRSETT, at the end of the settling step; MODEL_TO_EQUAL does the opposite transformation at its start. MICRODIFF_ETM has the same unit conversion for the newer diffusion scheme.
  • Inputs: the equally spaced radii ER (NPT points) and the changes EX in $X$ on them; the range IBEGIN..IEND of model points inside the grid; the model arrays HCOMP, HRU (radius), HTU (temperature), HS1 (mass), HQPR, the time step DT and the total mass STOT, all in the solver's units.
  • What it calls: INTRP2, four-point Lagrange weights.
  • Outputs: HCOMP updated, and HRU, HTU, HS1, HQPR, DT, STOT back in physical units.
  • Modules: CONFAC_MOD holds the conversion factors CON_RAD, CON_MASS, CON_TEMP, CON_TIME, set by MICRODIFF_SETUP and SETUP_GRSETT; GRAVEZ_MOD holds the change in $Z$ on the grid, EZ (with GRSETT, MODEL_TO_EQUAL and the Lax–Wendroff routines, which use its other entries; this routine reads only EZ); NMLPAR_MOD gives the flag LDIFZ (formerly in /GRAVS3/); DIMENSIONS_MOD gives the array size JSON.

The method

  1. Inside the grid (IBEGIN < $i$ < IEND): for each model point, find the four grid points around its radius, get Lagrange weights $b_k$, and $$\Delta X_i = \sum_{k=1}^{4} b_k\,\Delta X(r_k), \qquad X_i \leftarrow \min(X_i + \Delta X_i,\; 1 - Z_i - X_{3,i}),$$ where $X_3$ is $^3$He (HCOMP(4,·)). With metal diffusion, $Z$ changes the same way, and every metal species (rows 5–11) is scaled by $Z_\mathrm{new}/Z_\mathrm{old}$. Helium takes the rest: $Y = 1 - X - Z - X_3$. The scaling divides by the old $Z$ (suspected bug 1). The line IF (J .EQ. 0) PRINT*, 'line 47 etm' (line 69↗) cannot fire, since J starts at JMIN ≥ 2 (unused?); nor can IF (K0 .EQ. 0) K0=1 (lines 76–77↗), since NPT is at least 4 (unused?).

  2. Below the grid ($i \le$ IBEGIN) the change at the first grid point is applied; above it ($i \ge$ IEND), the change at the last. The points IBEGIN and IEND are themselves the grid's end points: MODEL_TO_EQUAL puts the first grid point at HRU(IBEGIN) and spaces the grid so that the last falls at HRU(IEND), with the model's values there (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 42–44↗, lines 113–116↗; model_to_equal.f, line 73↗, model_to_equal.f, lines 195–198↗, model_to_equal.f, lines 205–206↗, model_to_equal.f, lines 266–267↗). The comment explains the guards: "because metals sink, and hydrogen rises, the failsafes are opposite" ($X \ge 0$ and $Z \le Z_\mathrm{max}$ below; $X \le X_\mathrm{max}$ and $Z \ge 0$ above).

  3. Units: $r \leftarrow r/c_r$, $T \leftarrow T/c_T$, $m \leftarrow m/c_m$, HQPR $\leftarrow$ HQPR$\cdot c_r$, $\Delta t \leftarrow \Delta t\,c_t$, $M \leftarrow M/c_m$.

1. (not reached) Division by $Z$.

(line 52↗; line 103↗; line 122↗) With metal diffusion, each shell's metal species are scaled by ZZ2 = ZZ/HCOMP(3,I), the new $Z$ over the old; a shell with $Z = 0$ divides by zero, and rows 5–11 become NaN (or infinite). What follows: A metal-free model (Population III) cannot be run with LDIFZ. Only with LDIFZ (off by default) and LNEWDIF false, in a shell with $Z = 0$.
