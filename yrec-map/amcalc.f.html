Explanation

Purpose. This file computes the structure-dependent factor of the magnetic-braking torque, "according to the Matt et al. (2012) formulation" (comment by G. Somers, 6/16). The factor scales the angular-momentum loss of the wind with the star's mass, radius, luminosity, photospheric pressure and, optionally, its convective overturn time.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments SMASS (the star's mass in solar masses: MDOT updates it as SMASS + DELM/CMSUN, and COWIND's header says "stellar mass (solar units)"), BL ($\log L/L_\odot$) and TEFFL ($\log T_\mathrm{eff}$); the exponents EXM, EXR, EXL, EXPR, EXTAU, the solar normalizations PMMSOLP and PMMSOLTAU and the switch LROSSBY, all namelist parameters in NMLPAR_MOD (set in PARMIN); the photospheric pressure PPHOT, PPHOT0, the overturn time TAUCZ, TAUCZ0 and FRACSTEP in OVRTRN_MOD.
  • Who calls it: MWIND and MCOWIND.
  • Output: the argument STRUCTFACTOR, which MWIND and MCOWIND multiply with CONSTFACTOR and the time step to get the spin-down.
  • Modules: CONSTANTS_MOD (CLSUNL, C4PIL, CSIGL, CLN, CRSUN), NMLPAR_MOD and OVRTRN_MOD.

The method.

  1. The radius from the Stefan–Boltzmann law, in cgs units: $$\log R = \tfrac12\left(\log L - \log 4\pi - \log\sigma - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff}\right),$$ with $\log L$ = BL + CLSUNL; then $R/R_\odot$ (RRAD).

  2. $L/L_\odot = 10^{\mathtt{BL}}$.

  3. The photospheric pressure, interpolated within the step between its values at the start (PPHOT0) and end (PPHOT) of the step, both $\log P$, and normalized to the solar value: $$\frac{P_\mathrm{phot}}{P_{\mathrm{phot},\odot}} = \frac{10^{\,p_0 + f\,(p - p_0)}}{10^{\,p_\odot}},$$ with $f$ = FRACSTEP.

  4. If LROSSBY, the overturn time likewise, $\tau/\tau_\odot = [\tau_0 + f(\tau - \tau_0)]/\tau_\odot$; otherwise 1.

  5. The factor: $$\mathtt{STRUCTFACTOR} = M^{a_M}\,\left(\frac{R}{R_\odot}\right)^{a_R}\left(\frac{L}{L_\odot}\right)^{a_L}\left(\frac{P_\mathrm{phot}}{P_{\mathrm{phot},\odot}}\right)^{a_P}\left(\frac{\tau}{\tau_\odot}\right)^{a_\tau}.$$

Design. The radius comes from $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$, not from the model's outer radius. The mass is used as given, with no solar normalization in this routine.

KC's changes. 2025-05-30: the elements of /PMMWIND/ reordered (reals first, then logicals and the character AWIND); the old order is kept as a comment. Since then /PMMWIND/ is gone, its members moved to NMLPAR_MOD.

Changed since rp_1. STRUCTFACTOR is now an output argument (/CWIND/ retired); COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; the unused constants and AWIND are gone, which settles the former question on them.

11def amcalc(smass, bl, teffl, structfactor):
12    """Compute the structure factor of the wind torque: the mass, R/Rsun,
13    L/Lsun, the photospheric pressure over the solar one and, if LROSSBY,
14    the convective overturn time over the solar one, each raised to its
15    exponent from nmlpar_mod.
16
17    Translates: amcalc.f#AMCALC (09c3b9b)
18
19    Arguments: smass (mass, solar units), bl (log L/Lsun), teffl
20    (log Teff); structfactor is output only (the value passed is unused).
21
22    Returns structfactor.
23    """
24    # Mass
25    rmass = smass
26    # Radius, from L and Teff (Stefan-Boltzmann), in solar units
27    rl = 0.5 * (bl + constants_mod.clsunl - constants_mod.c4pil
28                - constants_mod.csigl - 4.0 * teffl)
29    rtot = math.exp(constants_mod.cln * rl)
30    rrad = rtot / constants_mod.crsun
31    # Luminosity
32    rlum = 10.0 ** bl
33    # Photospheric pressure, interpolated within the step
34    rphot = (10.0 ** (ovrtrn_mod.pphot0 + ovrtrn_mod.fracstep
35                      * (ovrtrn_mod.pphot - ovrtrn_mod.pphot0))
36             / 10.0 ** nmlpar_mod.pmmsolp)
37    # Convective overturn timescale
38    if nmlpar_mod.lrossby:
39        rtau = (ovrtrn_mod.taucz0 + ovrtrn_mod.fracstep
40                * (ovrtrn_mod.taucz - ovrtrn_mod.taucz0)) / nmlpar_mod.pmmsoltau
41    else:
42        rtau = 1.0
43    # Combine them all
44    structfactor = (rmass ** nmlpar_mod.exm * rrad ** nmlpar_mod.exr
45                    * rlum ** nmlpar_mod.exl * rphot ** nmlpar_mod.expr
46                    * rtau ** nmlpar_mod.extau)
47    return structfactor
