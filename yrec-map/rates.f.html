Purpose

Computes the nuclear reaction rates at one shell, for the abundance updates. Its header calls it "a stripped-down version of ENGEB": the same reactions, screening and constants as ENGEB, but without the energy generation. The rates are those of Bahcall's Neutrino Astrophysics (1989), with the alpha captures from Caughlan and Fowler (1988), as the comments say.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: MIX (for each shell, before the composition is mixed and burned), ROTMIX (for each shell of its substeps, since 7be4133), and XTIME (at the shell IEND, for the time step).
  • Inputs: $\log_{10}\rho$ (DL), $\log_{10}T$ (TL), the mass fractions X, Y, XHE3, XC12, XC13, XN14, XO16, XO18, and the shell index IU.
  • Outputs: the arrays HR1…HR13 (the rates of the 13 reactions) and HF1, HF2 (two branching fractions) at element IU. KEMCOM reads them (all but HR9, HR13 and HF2) to update the abundances; no routine computes with HF2, the Be7 branching $F_1$ (unused?). ROTMIX calls RATES itself, shell by shell, for its own substeps.
  • Modules: CROSS_MOD (the derivative scalings QS0E, QQS0EE and the N15+p branching factors FO16, FC12, set in REMAP, shared with ENGEB); NMLPAR_MOD (the cross-section factors SSTANDARD(17), set in PARMIN or by REMAP; WEAKSCREENING, 0.03 by default in PARMIN; the temperature cuts TCUT); CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN $=\ln 10$, CC13 $=1/3$, CC23 $=2/3$).

The method

  1. Composition sums. Over the 13 isotopes (n, H1, D, H3, He3, He4, C12, C13, N14, O16, O18, Ne20, Mg24; those of n, D, H3, Ne20, Mg24 set to zero) with mass fractions $X_i$, bare nuclear masses $A_i$ (ANUC) and charges $Z_i$: $$\mu_I^{-1} = \sum_i \frac{X_i}{A_i}\ (\texttt{AMU}),\quad \mu_e^{-1} = \sum_i \frac{Z_iX_i}{A_i}\ (\texttt{EMU}),\quad \texttt{ZET} = \sum_i\frac{Z_i^2X_i}{A_i},\quad \texttt{XTR} = \sum_i\frac{Z_i^{1.58}X_i}{A_i}.$$
  2. Below the temperature cut $\log T \le$ TCUT(1), all rates are zero (with the default 6.5 this stops pp burning and $^3$He production too, not only deuterium burning; see question 2). On that path (lines 346–351↗) F1 and F3 are not set, so HF1(IU), HF2(IU) would get the values kept (by SAVE) from the previous call; no caller takes it: MIX and ROTMIX call RATES only above TCUT(1) and zero the rates and HF1, HF2 of the colder shells themselves (line 181↗, lines 219–237↗; lines 88–103↗), and XTIME's EQBURN does not read HF1 (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).
  3. Electron degeneracy (for Salpeter's screening): with $\rho/\mu_e$ (RWE), $p_F^2/(m_ec)^2 = 1.017677\times10^{-4}(\rho/\mu_e)^{2/3}$ and $E_F/kT = 5.92986\,T_9^{-1}\bigl(\sqrt{1+p_F^2/m_e^2c^2}-1\bigr)$. The factor $f'/f$ (FPRF) is 1 for $E_F/kT \le 0.01$, 0 for $\log_{10}(E_F/kT) \ge 1.5$, and a sixth-order polynomial in $\log_{10}(E_F/kT)$ between.
  4. Screening. $\Lambda_0 = 5.9426\times10^{-6}\,T_9^{-3/2}\sqrt{\rho/\mu_I}$ (XXL), $\tilde\zeta = \sqrt{(\texttt{ZET} + f'/f\cdot\mu_e^{-1})\,\mu_I}$ (ZCURL), $\bar Z = \mu_I/\mu_e$ (ZBAR). For reaction $j$ with charge product $Z_1Z_2$ (ZPRD): $$U_\mathrm{wk} = Z_1Z_2\,\Lambda_0\,\tilde\zeta .$$ If $U_\mathrm{wk} \le$ WEAKSCREENING, weak screening is used, $U = U_\mathrm{wk}$. Otherwise the intermediate form $$U_\mathrm{int} = 0.38\,\Lambda_0^{0.86}\,\frac{\texttt{XTR}\;\zeta_{86,j}}{\mu_I^{-1}\,\tilde\zeta^{0.58}\,\bar Z^{0.28}}$$ (Graboske et al. 1973, with their coefficients Z86), or, for $U_\mathrm{wk} > 2$, the strong form $$U_\mathrm{str} = 0.624\,\bar Z^{1/3}\Lambda_0^{2/3}\Bigl[\zeta_{53,j} + 0.316\,\bar Z^{1/3}\zeta_{43,j} + \frac{0.737\,\zeta_{23,j}}{\bar Z\,\Lambda_0^{2/3}}\Bigr]$$ (USTR, with the coefficients Z53, Z43, Z23) when it is smaller than $U_\mathrm{int}$ or when $U_\mathrm{wk} \ge 5$. The rate is multiplied by $e^{U}$.
  5. Reactions 1–7 (pp, He3+He3, He3+He4, p+C12, p+C13, p+N14, p+O16), with $T_9 = T/10^9$ K: $$r_j = \rho\,\Bigl[T_9^{-2/3} + q_{1}T_9^{-1/3} + s'_j\,(q_{2} + q_{3}T_9^{1/3}) + s''_j\,(q_{4}T_9^{2/3} + q_{5}T_9)\Bigr]\exp\bigl(q_{6}T_9^{-1/3} + q_{7} + (q_{8}T_9)^2 + U_j\bigr)\;S_j,$$ with $q_{k} =$ Qk(j) (the terms of eq. 3.14 of Neutrino Astrophysics, as the comments explain), $s'_j$, $s''_j$ = QS0E(j), QQS0EE(j) (user scalings of the S-factor's first and second derivatives) and $S_j$ = SSTANDARD(j). Rates below $10^{-30}$ are set to zero. All are skipped when $X = 0$.

  6. Be7. Electron capture: $\lambda_e = 1.752\times10^{-10}\,T_9^{-1/2}\,[1 + 0.004(1000\,T_9 - 16)]\,\mu_e^{-1}\,S_{15}$. Proton capture: $\lambda_p = 3.128813\times10^5\,X\,S_{16}\,e^{-10.2625\,T_9^{-1/3}}$ times the bracket of step 5 with Q(8), and its own weak or intermediate screening. Both are multiplied by $\rho\cdot 8.584981\times10^{22}$, and $F_1 = \lambda_e/(\lambda_e+\lambda_p)$ is the fraction of Be7 that captures an electron.

The $(p,\alpha)$ and $(p,\gamma)$ channels have their own brackets, times $67500\,f_{C12}$ and $64\,f_{O16}$ (FC12, FO16); $F_3 = \mathrm{C12ALPHA}/(\mathrm{C12ALPHA}+\mathrm{O16GAMMA})$.     Question 2 (line 516↗; lines 433–434↗; line 331↗). Comments that disagree with the code. "F3 IS THE FRACTION OF THE N14 THAT IS BURNED BY P, ALPHA" should read N15; the comment above the screening loop says WEAKSCREENING is passed "in the FLUX COMMON block", but it comes from NMLPAR_MOD (/WEAK/ at rp_1); "DL AND DT" are DL and TL. Should these comments be corrected? 8. Alpha captures (only when $\log T \ge$ TCUT(4)): C13$(\alpha,n)$O16 (RATE(8)), C12$(\alpha,\gamma)$O16 (RATE(10)), N14$(\alpha,\gamma)$F18 (RATE(11)) and the triple-alpha (RATE(12), $\propto\rho^2 T_9^{-3} e^{-4.4027/T_9}$), as fits of the Caughlan and Fowler form. RATE(9) and RATE(13) are always zero, and every rate at or below $10^{-5}$ is set to zero.

    Question 3 (line 494↗; lines 646–648↗). Two thresholds for zero. Reactions 1–7 are zeroed below $10^{-30}$, but at the end every rate at or below $10^{-5}$ (per second per gram, before C21) is set to zero (ENGEB has the same cuts). At low density or temperature the second cut could switch off slow but relevant rates. Is it intended? 9. Output units. Each rate (per second per gram, mass fractions left out) is multiplied by C21 $= 5.240358\times10^{-8}$, "(10^9 years/1 second)·(1 atomic mass unit/1 gram)", which gives HRj(IU). HF1(IU) $= F_3$ and HF2(IU) $= F_1$. Before C21, $r_jX_aX_b$ is the number of reactions per gram per second (the comment: RATE(I) is "the rate of the different reactions per second per gram, except that the mass fractions are omitted"), and by the comment on Q7 its logarithmic normalization carries $-\ln(A_0A_1)$, $-\ln S_0$ and $-\ln(1+\delta_{01})$, where $\delta_{01}$ = 1 "only when the two reacting nuclei … are identical" (pp, ³He+³He); the full form, with Avogadro's number and the triple-α prefactor, is the question below. Multiplying by the amu in grams turns a number of reactions into a mass, and by the seconds in a Gyr gives the rate per Gyr. KEMCOM then writes $dX_k/dt = A_k\sum_j \pm\,\mathrm{HR}_j X_aX_b$, with $X$ mass fractions and $t$ in Gyr. (A check of the units against the Sun is the second question below.)

    Question 4 (lines 193–200↗; lines 640–641↗). The rates' normalization. From the comment on Q7, $r_jX_aX_b = \rho N_A^2\langle\sigma v\rangle_j (X_a/A_a)(X_b/A_b)/(1+\delta_{ab})$ reactions per gram per second, so that $r_j = \rho N_A^2\langle\sigma v\rangle_j/[A_aA_b(1+\delta_{ab})]$, with $N_A$ inside the constant 70.62860: is that the intended form? Two numbers seem to bear it out, though neither is in the code: $e^{\mathtt{Q7(1)}} \approx 1.19\times10^9$ is about half of $N_A$ times Caughlan & Fowler's pp coefficient, $4.01\times10^{-15}$; and triple α's prefactor, $1.565315\times10^{21}$ (RATE(12), with $Y^3$), equals $N_A/(3!\,A_\alpha^3)$ with the code's CAVOG and $A_\alpha$ = 4.0026 (the atomic weight in gmass06.f, not ANUC(6) = 4.001506). Does that prefactor carry the $1/3!$ for three identical reactants, and which $A_\alpha$ is meant?

    Question 5 (line 658↗). A check against the Sun. Taking the Sun's center at $\rho \approx 150$ g cm$^{-3}$ and $T \approx 1.57\times10^7$ K (values not in the code), HR1 comes out about 0.23 per Gyr, and $3\,\mathrm{HR}_1X^2$ with $X \approx 0.35$ gives $dX/dt \approx -0.08$ per Gyr, close to a mean loss of central hydrogen from about 0.70 to 0.35 in 4.6 Gyr. Does a calibrated solar model from YREC show that rate, so that C21's units (per Gyr, mass fractions) are confirmed?

Design

All constants are DATA statements (double precision since rp_1), with earlier sets kept as comments (BP00, 7/21/03). The routine duplicates much of ENGEB, so a change to one must be copied to the other.

Question 6 (lines 149–280↗; lines 259–461↗). Duplication with ENGEB. Are the constants here guaranteed to match those in ENGEB? A check by script, or one shared routine, would remove the risk.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: WEAKSCREENING (default 0.03) is the threshold below which weak screening is used for the reaction rates. The light-element cross sections (XSLI6, XSLI7, XSBE91–XSBE93, keV b, with the switches LXLI6–LXBE93) are turned into the factors SLI6…SBE93 by PARMIN.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. The constants are now double precision: the DATA values (masses, the coefficients Q1–Q7, the screening coefficients, C21) and the literals in the rate fits, which were single-precision constants; the exponent $2/3$ in $p_F$ and $\Lambda_0^{2/3}$ is now CC23 (it was 0.6666667 and 0.666667). The rates change by up to a few parts in a million, most where a constant sits in an exponent (Q7(2) = 76.6003 was off by $3.6\times10^{-6}$). The thresholds 1.E-2, 1.E-30 and 1.E-5 are still single precision. COMMON blocks to modules (/CTLIM/'s TCUT, SSTANDARD and WEAKSCREENING now in NMLPAR_MOD, /CTLIM/ and /WEAK/ dropped here), IMPLICIT NONE.

Changed since a8a5950. The file is unchanged; MIX no longer copies the rates into HCOMPM (the module BURN_MOD, removed since a8a5950), and ROTMIX computes its own with RATES.
