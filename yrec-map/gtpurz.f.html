Purpose

GTPURZ returns the opacity from the LAOL89 table for pure metals ($Z = 1$), with its derivatives, at a given density and temperature.

How it fits

Called only by GETOPAC, when $Z > 0.1$ and $\log T > 7.0$ (line 109↗, lowered from 7.7 by "JCZ 211125"; its error message still says "Z>0.10 T > 5 X 10^7 K"; it stops if no pure-$Z$ table was read, LPUREZ): GETOPAC then interpolates $\log\kappa$ and its derivatives linearly in $Z$ between a normal table (at ZIT) and this pure-$Z$ table ($Z = 1$). The table is in the modules ZLAOL_MOD (read by RDZLAOL) and ZSLAOL_MOD (its density splines, from ZSULAOL), COMMON blocks until rp_1; it takes TOLLAOL from NMLPAR_MOD (formerly /NWLAOL/) and the output unit from IOUNITS_MOD. Calls LOCATE, XRNG4, CSPLINE, SPLINT.

The method

As in GTLAOL (removed since rp_1) but without the composition step: a spline in $\log\rho$ at each of four temperatures (with linear extrapolation within the margin $\ln$ TOLLAOL), then a natural spline in $\log T$; chord slopes for the derivatives; $\log\kappa$ capped at 35.

Question 1 (lines 65–66↗; line 87↗; lines 95–96↗). Natural log for the margin. LOG(TOLLAOL) is a natural log, while the density it is compared with, DL, is $\log_{10}\rho$; is that intended? With the default TOLLAOL = 10 (NMLPAR_MOD; YREC's documentation does not describe it), the extrapolation reaches $\ln 10 = 2.30$ in $\log\rho$ beyond each temperature's first and last nonzero entries, where a factor 10 in $\rho$ would be 1.0. The comment says only that it "permits some extrapolation beyond table edge". It matters only past the densest entries of the four temperatures used, which in PURECO.DBGLAOL (the test suite's table) end between $2 \times 10^5$ and $3 \times 10^6$ g/cm³ near $\log T = 8$; beyond the margin the run stops. (The same question was raised for GTLAOL, removed since rp_1.)

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (c) A density beyond the table's margin is reported as a temperature. (lines 81–105↗, lines 120–126↗) At each of the four temperatures XRNG4 picks, a density inside the table or within TOLLOG of its edges adds a point; one beyond the margin adds none, silently. With one point missing, NUMTS < 4 and the run stops with "OUTSIDE Z OPACITY TABLE, IN TEMPERATURE", though the cause is the density.
