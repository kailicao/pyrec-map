Purpose

LLOT95E_MOD holds the OPAL95 opacity table interpolated to the surface composition (one $X$ and $Z$), and that $X$, shared by 3 routines. It replaces COMMON/LLOT95E/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), with its member XTAB renamed XOP95 (a name collision resolved before the conversion). The table dimensions NUMT = 70, NUMD = 19 are PRIVATE.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • CAPPAE(NUMT,NUMD): $\log\kappa$ on the ($\log T$, $\log R$) grid at $X$ = XOP95 and $Z$ = ZTAB (LLOT95_MOD), interpolated in $X$ (four points) by OP95XTAB, which SETUPOPAC and SURFOPAC call with the envelope's $X$. Read by OP952D (interpolation in $T$ and $R$).
  • XOP95: the $X$ of that table, set by OP95XTAB; read by GETOPAL95, which uses the 2D interpolation when the local $X$ and $Z$ match the table's to $10^{-4}$.

Design

A blanket USE LLOT95E_MOD. At rp_1, main.f declared the block with its own NUMTT (also 70); that declaration is gone.
