Purpose

Rescales a starting model before a run, as asked by a "kind card" (one entry of the run's sequence, number NK): the hydrogen abundance $X$, the metallicity $Z$, the abundance of one other species, the total mass and, for horizontal-branch stars, the core mass. It also applies the "ZRAMP" option, a metallicity that changes linearly with mass in the core.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: STARIN, once (the model is read and, if asked, its core extended first), when IRESCA(NK) is not 1.
  • Inputs: the model (HCOMP, HS = $\log_{10} m$ in g, HSTOT, HL, LC, M, SMASS in solar masses) and, through modules, the targets: RUNLOOP_MOD's RESCAL(1..4, NK) (new mass, $X$, $Z$, core mass; a negative or zero value means "no change"), and, in NMLPAR_MOD, XNEWCP, INEWCP, LNEWCP, LREL (one species to set) and RSCLZC, RSCLZM1, RSCLZM2, LZRAMP (the ramp), set by the namelist or by PARMIN.
  • What it calls: FINDSH, to locate the hydrogen-burning shell (JXBEG to JXEND) in a horizontal-branch model.
  • Outputs: HCOMP, HS, HSTOT, SMASS changed in place; in modules, XNEW, ZNEW, ZENV, STOTAL (COMP_MOD) and XENV0, ZENV0 (LABEL_MOD, which WRTHEAD and the output routines print).

The method

  1. $X$. Every shell's $X$ is multiplied by $X_\mathrm{new}/X_\mathrm{surf}$, capped at $X_\mathrm{new}$. XENV is deliberately not changed, since it fixes the mean molecular weight of the Yale equation of state from the first run (MHP 7/99). XENV and ZENV are the reference composition for which STARIN computes AMUENV (the sum of $X_i/A_i$ over the elements) and the number fractions FXENV of the elements used in the Saha ionization (EQSAHA); in each shell, MU and EQSTAT2 then correct these for the shell's $X - X_\mathrm{env}$ and $Z - Z_\mathrm{env}$ rather than recompute them, so changing XENV alone would make it disagree with AMUENV and FXENV.

  2. $Z$. The same amount is added in every shell, $Z_j \to \max(0, Z_j + \Delta Z)$ with $\Delta Z = Z_\mathrm{new} - Z_\mathrm{surf}$, and species 5–11 (C12 through O18) are multiplied by $Z_j^\mathrm{new}/Z_j^\mathrm{old}$.

    Question 1 (lines 123–125↗; lines 131–133↗). He3 and the $Z$ rescaling. The comment says "THE CNO CYCLE ELEMENTS AND HE3 ARE MULTIPLIED BY THE RATIO", but the loop covers species 5–11 only; He3 is species 4 and is not scaled. Which is meant?

  3. One species (LNEWCP, main sequence only): set to XNEWCP everywhere; with LREL, XNEWCP is first converted from the astronomers' scale, $X_\mathrm{sp} = 10^{\,\texttt{XNEWCP}-12}\,X_\mathrm{surf}$, into a local (XTARG), leaving XNEWCP as set.

    Question 2 (lines 156–171↗; lines 109–112↗). One XNEWCP for all kind cards. XNEWCP (and INEWCP, LREL) is one value for all kind cards, unlike the per-run RESCAL, so every rescaling card sets the same species to the same abundance. Is this intended?

  4. Mass, main sequence (when the central $X > 10^{-10}$): every mass point is multiplied by $M_\mathrm{new}/M_\mathrm{old}$ (in the log, $\log m \to \log m + \log(M_\mathrm{new}/M_\mathrm{old})$); all other variables are kept.

    Question 3 (lines 181–192↗). Mass rescaling without homology. Step 4 multiplies every $m$ by $M_\mathrm{new}/M_\mathrm{old}$ and keeps $P$, $T$, $r$, $L$ at each point, so after a large change of mass the rescaled model is far from equilibrium, and the relaxation (KINDRN = 2) must absorb the difference. A student would expect a homologous first guess (on the main sequence, for example, $R \propto M^{0.8}$, $L \propto M^{3.5}$, with $P$ and $T$ scaled to match). Was such a scaling tried, and is the lack of it why the User Guide advises several rescalings in turn for large changes? It matters for how large a change one rescaling can make.

  5. Mass, horizontal branch. Mass is added or removed outside the hydrogen shell, by stretching the mass coordinate above a fixed point $m_0$: $$m \to m_0 + s\,(m - m_0),\qquad s = \frac{M_\mathrm{new} - m_0}{M_\mathrm{old} - m_0}.$$ If more than $0.01\,M_\odot$ lies outside the core, $m_0$ is the top of the shell ("old method"); otherwise the base of the shell ("new method").

    Question 4 (line 240↗). The 0.01 solar-mass switch. The choice between the two horizontal-branch methods depends on whether the mass outside the core exceeds $0.01\,M_\odot$, a hard-coded value. Where does it come from?

  6. Core mass, horizontal branch. The core's points are multiplied by $M_\mathrm{core}^\mathrm{new}/M_\mathrm{core}^\mathrm{old}$, the shell's points shifted by the change $\Delta M_\mathrm{core}$ (so the shell's mass stays fixed), and the envelope squeezed or stretched so that the total mass is unchanged. The old and new masses are printed.

  7. $Y$ is reset everywhere to $1 - X - Z - X_\mathrm{He3}$.
  8. ZRAMP (if LZRAMP and the three parameters are positive): with $f = m/M_*$, $Z$ is set to $Z_c$ (RSCLZC) for $f <$ RSCLZM1, and rises linearly to the surface $Z$ between RSCLZM1 and RSCLZM2; $X$ absorbs the change, and species 5–11 are multiplied by $Z_\mathrm{new}/Z_\mathrm{old}$, as in step 2.

For users (from YREC's User Guide). The targets are the control namelist's RSCLM, RSCLX, RSCLZ and RSCLCM for each run (copied by PARMIN into RESCAL for KINDRN = 2 or 3); a negative value means no rescaling. A typical job rescales a library model to the wanted mass, composition and mixing length with a few zero-time-step models, then evolves it; a large change, such as a solar-metallicity template turned into a very metal-poor star, may need several rescalings in turn. The User Guide also advises care, or a starting model made by a script, for core-helium-burning stars, where RSCLM stretches only the envelope, RSCLCM sets the helium core's mass and RSCLX acts on the envelope (the core has no hydrogen), as steps 1, 5 and 6 do. It does not say that zero also means "no change".

Design

Several unconditional write(*,*) lines ("Entering old method", "SENV", "Envelope") print to the terminal in the horizontal-branch mass rescaling; they look like debugging left on. Many others are commented out.

Question 5 (lines 242–245↗; lines 288–291↗). Debug output. Can these write(*,*) lines be removed, or put behind a debug flag?

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (c) The error message for one species names no species. (lines 175–178↗; line 38↗) When the target abundance of the one species is 1 or more, format 1004 prints ICOMP, a local that is declared and never set (the species index is INEWCP), and ends "Z NOT RESCALED" whatever the species. Only the message is wrong: The abundance is left unchanged, as intended. Only with LNEWCP (a rescale card on the main sequence with a target $\ge 1$). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

  2. (d) The "new method" does not check the new total mass against the core. (lines 300–305↗; lines 310–311↗; lines 320–322↗) The old method stops when the new envelope above the shell would be negative (lines 250–258↗), which is exactly when its scale factor would be negative. The new method checks only the old model (CHKENV, the old total minus the core), not the target: If RESCAL(1,NK) is at or below the core mass COROLD, SFACTOR is zero or negative, and HS(I) for I $\ge$ JXBEG becomes the log of a mass at or below the core's, or of a negative number (NaN), with no message. Only for a horizontal-branch model with less than $0.01\,M_\odot$ outside the core and a target total mass below its core mass, which is not a sensible input; so this is fragile code rather than a wrong result in practice. Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (/CKIND/ to RUNLOOP_MOD, /COMP/ to COMP_MOD, /LABEL/ to LABEL_MOD, /NEWCMP/ and /ZRAMP/ to NMLPAR_MOD, /LUOUT/ to IOUNITS_MOD, the constants to CONSTANTS_MOD), the array size JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Changed since a8a5950. The ZRAMP block scales species 5–11 by $Z_\mathrm{new}/Z_\mathrm{old}$ instead of $(Z_\mathrm{old} - Z_\mathrm{new})/Z_\mathrm{old}$ (settles former question 1); with LREL, the converted target goes into a local, XTARG, instead of overwriting XNEWCP (settles the first half of former question 3, whose second half is now question 2), and STARIN's first of its two calls is gone, so a reused model is no longer rescaled twice; $Z$ is rescaled only for RESCAL(3,NK) > 0, as for the mass and $X$, since the default RSCLZ = 0 used to set $Z = 0$; and the three stops on an impossible mass are STOP 1 (KNOWN_ISSUES.md, issue 38).

28    sequence), the total mass (main sequence: every mass point scaled;
29    horizontal branch: the mass coordinate stretched above the hydrogen
30    shell or its base) and the core mass (horizontal branch); then reset Y
31    and apply ZRAMP. The targets are runloop_mod.rescal[1..4, nk], the
32    species nmlpar_mod.xnewcp, inewcp, and the ramp nmlpar_mod.rsclzc,

234                      - math.exp(c.cln * hs[jxbeg - 1])) / c.cmsun

257            hcomp[3, i] = zp
258            hcomp[1, i] = 1.0 - hcomp[3, i] - hcomp[4, i] - hcomp[2, i]
259            for j in range(5, 12):
260                hcomp[j, i] = hcomp[j, i] * zfact
