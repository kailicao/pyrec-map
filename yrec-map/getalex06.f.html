Purpose

GETALEX06 returns the low-temperature (molecular) opacity from the low-temperature opacities of Ferguson et al. (2005), called ALEX06 in the code (READALEX06; "Alexander low T opacities", per its header, MHP 3/09) for a given $\rho$, $T$, $X$, $Z$, with its logarithmic derivatives.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: only GETOPAC, when $\log T \le$ TMOLMAX and LALEX06 is set.
  • Inputs: DL, TL ($\log\rho$, $\log T$), X, Z.
  • What it calls: ALEX06TAB, which builds a table for the current $X$, $Z$ from the full set, and INTERP (four-point Lagrange weights and derivative weights).
  • Outputs: O ($\kappa$, cm$^2$ g$^{-1}$), OL ($\log\kappa$), QOD $=\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log\rho|_T$, QOT $=\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T|_\rho$.
  • Modules: GALOT06_MOD (the grids TGR06, R0GR06, XXG06, ZZG06) and ALOT06_MOD (the table CAPALEX06 at the composition XE, ZE, and the last indices IT, ID), shared with ALEX06TAB and READALEX06; $\ln 10$ from CONSTANTS_MOD.

The method

  1. If $X$ or $Z$ differs from the stored XE, ZE by more than $10^{-8}$, rebuild the table with ALEX06TAB (lines 26–33↗).

    Question 1 (lines 26–33↗). Table rebuilt per composition. Every change of $X$ or $Z$ beyond $10^{-8}$ rebuilds the whole table (85 × 19 values, from 16 tables). Where the points below TMOLMAX differ in composition (with settling, or with a gradient in the cool envelope), this happens at almost every call. Is that cost acceptable, or should the routine interpolate in $X$ and $Z$ directly? A profile of a run with diffusion would show the share of time spent in ALEX06TAB.

  2. The table is in $\log T$ and $\log R$, with $\log R = \log\rho - 3(\log T - 6)$ (that is, $R = \rho/T_6^3$).

  3. Find the 4×4 stencil from the last indices, get Lagrange weights in $\log T$ and $\log R$, and interpolate $\log\kappa$ and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log R$.

    Question 2 (lines 37–59↗; lines 66–88↗). No lower-edge handling. Below the smallest $\log R$ ($-8$), or outside the $\log T$ range (2.70 to 4.50), the stencil stays at the edge and the Lagrange weights extrapolate the cubic silently; only above the largest $\log R$ is the extrapolation made linear (step 4). Should there be a check, or a linear extrapolation at the other edges too? (Above $\log T$ = 4.50 is reached only if TMOLMAX is set above it; its default is 4.1.)

  4. Above the largest $\log R$ of the table, extrapolate $\log\kappa$ linearly from the last two columns (lines 90–96↗; lines 113–120↗); the derivative is not extrapolated (bug 1).

  5. Convert the temperature derivative from fixed $R$ to fixed $\rho$: $$\left.\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right|_\rho = \left.\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right|_R - 3\left.\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right|_T.$$

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (c) The derivative is not extrapolated with the value. (lines 90–96↗; lines 108–111↗; lines 113–120↗; lines 126–129↗) Above the largest $\log R$ of the table ($\log R > 1$), $\log\kappa$ is extrapolated linearly from the last two columns, but QOPR, and so QOD $=\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log\rho|_T$, is the derivative of the cubic at the table edge, not the slope of the extrapolation. QOT is then converted to fixed $\rho$ with this QOD (line 129). What follows: There, the returned derivatives do not match the returned opacity; where they are used (the linearized equations) was not traced. Only with LALEX06 (off by default in the code; recommended by YREC's User Guide) and at $\log T \le$ TMOLMAX. Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:
