Purpose

Writes a verbose stellar model to the .store file (ISTOR), "either at specified ages, every NPRTMOD models, or at the end of runs". Its header (G. Somers 11/14) calls it "mostly a clone of wrtlst.f and putmodel2.f, combined". Since JvS 09/25 it has two formats: the older one (a MODEL2-style model followed by optional per-shell physics and rotation columns) and, with LSTCH, the "stitched" format, in which STITCH writes interior, envelope and atmosphere points together.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the model as arguments (structure, composition, envelope data, luminosities TLUMX, rotation: OMEGA, ETA2, R0, FP, FT, HJM, HI), the mixing length CMIXL, XMSOL (passed on to WRTMOD) and the initial-mixture label COMPMIX (from STARIN, through the main program); the physics flags and the output switches (LSTORE, LSTATM, LSTENV, LSTMOD, LSTPHYS, LSTROT, LSTCH, LMILNE) from NMLPAR_MOD, and LPHHD from CCOUT_MOD; the per-shell physics in SCRTCH_MOD, SOUND_MOD (GAM1), TEMP2_MOD (the rotational mixing velocities) and ROTEN_MOD; the constants from CONSTANTS_MOD and the units from IOUNITS_MOD.
  • Who calls it: WRTOUT and the main program in main.f.
  • What it calls: STITCH (new format), PINDEX (which shells to print), WRTMIL (if also LMILNE) and WRTMOD (envelope and atmosphere, if LSTATM or LSTENV).
  • Output: the .store file, opened by PARMIN (which also writes the old format's header key). Its MOD2 blocks can serve as starting models (STARIN reads the MOD2 format).

The method

  1. Labels for the physics: atmosphere (EDD, KS, HRA, KUR, ALL from KTTAU; KTTAU = 5 gets none, bug 2), equation of state (SAHA, OPAL06, SCV, with +DH and OPAL 2006; SCV+O6 is never written, bug 1), low-temperature opacity (always NONE: the labels ALEX and KURZ went with the Alexander 1995 and Kurucz 1990 tables) and high-temperature opacity (OP95, or NONE). The message for the label ' ? ' (lines 176–180↗) is never written, since no branch sets that label (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

    Question 1 (lines 164–169↗). No low-temperature opacity label. With the Alexander 1995 and Kurucz 1990 tables removed, ALOK is always NONE: the Ferguson et al. (2005) tables (LALEX06), the only low-temperature tables left, were never labelled. Should LALEX06 set ALOK (for example to AL06)?

  2. Stitched format (LSTCH): on the first call (LPHHD) a header key, the flags line and the column titles; then STITCH (with CMIXL).

  3. Old format: the MOD2 header line, the flags, the TLUMX line (converted to erg s⁻¹ in place if the largest is $\le 10^{20}$, as in PUTMODEL2, whose in-place conversion is suspected bug 1), three ENV lines; then, with LSTMOD, one line per shell selected by PINDEX (whose burning-shell arguments are never set, bug 3): the basic columns, led by the model number; with LSTPHYS opacity, gravity $g = Gm/r^2$, $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, convective velocity, $\Gamma_1$, SBETA, SETA and the energy generation terms; with LSTROT (and LROT) the distortion parameter and the ratio of polar to equatorial radius, $$A = \frac{5}{3}\,\frac{\Omega^2 r_0^3}{G m\,(2+\eta_2)},\qquad \frac{R_p}{R_e} = \frac{1 - A}{1 + A/2},$$ where $A$ is the amplitude of the $P_2$ distortion of the level surface, $r(\theta) = r_0\,[1 - A\,P_2(\cos\theta)]$, in the Clairaut–Radau treatment of FPFT and SHAPE (Kippenhahn & Thomas 1970): $P_2 = 1$ at the pole and $-\tfrac12$ at the equator, hence $r_0(1 - A)$ and $r_0(1 + A/2)$; then FP, FT, $j$, $I$, the rotational kinetic energy change, and the mixing velocities $v_\mathrm{ES}$, $v_\mathrm{GSF}$, $v_\mathrm{SS}$ and their sum. Missing groups are written as zeros. The fifth energy column, SEG(5,·), is the triple-α term: COEFFT stores in it EG(5), ENGEB's E3AL (lines 286–296↗; lines 433–435↗; lines 1125–1127↗), as the stitched header's "TRIPLE_ALPHA" says; the old header's "3HE" is wrong (bug 4) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

    Question 2 (lines 307–312↗). Ionization fractions written as zeros. The columns HII, HEII, HEIII are written as 0.0 (the line with SFXION is commented out), although COEFFT fills SFXION for every shell (line 450↗) and STITCH writes it (lines 117–120↗). Is that intended?

    Question 3 (lines 133–174↗; lines 213–251↗). Duplicated code. The labels, the MOD2 header, and the TLUMX conversion repeat PUTMODEL2 and WRTLST, and the copies have already drifted (KTTAU = 5 is labelled in WRTLST, not here). Should one routine be shared?

For users (from YREC's Quick Start). This is the .store file (FSTOR): snapshots of the model's structure during the run. Which snapshots, and what they contain, the control namelist decides: LSTORE (NPRTMOD, every how many models; NPRTPT, every how many shells), LSTPCH (the final model), and LSTATM, LSTENV, LSTPHYS, LSTROT (the atmosphere, the envelope, the microphysics, the rotation), with LSTCH for the stitched format.

From YREC's documentation

Control Options (Output):

variable meaning (documentation)
LSTORE store model structures to the .store file at the chosen points (LSTMOD should be set the same; it may be redundant)
LSTCH write interior, envelope and atmosphere in one consistent format (recommended)
LSTATM, LSTENV add the atmosphere, the envelope; with both off nothing beyond the fitting point is written
LSTPHYS add the microphysics ($\epsilon$, $\kappa$, …)
LSTROT add the rotation data
LSTPCH write the final model's details

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (c) The SCV + OPAL 2006 label is lost. (lines 149–157↗) In the LSCV branch, IF (LOPALE06) EOS='SCV+O6' sits inside IF (LDH), where it is overwritten at once; without LDH, SCV with OPAL 2006 is labelled SCV in the flags line of both formats. Only with LSCV and LOPALE06 on and LDH off. The same lines are in WRTLST (suspected bug 1). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

  3. (c) PINDEX gets arguments that are never set. (lines 51–52↗; line 262↗) JXBEG, JXEND, and LSHELL are neither arguments nor in a module here, so they are undefined locals (zero and .FALSE. with a compiler that zero-initializes SAVEd locals). The burning-shell option of PINDEX, which prints every shell of the hydrogen-burning shell (lines 46–70↗), therefore never applies, and the old format prints only every NPRTPT-th shell there. Only in the old format (LSTCH off) with LSTMOD. Read from the code, not run. A possible fix: pass LSHELL, JXBEG, and JXEND to PUTSTORE as arguments, from WRTOUT (which has them as arguments) and from the main program (which sets them through FINDSH, lines 578–579↗), as they were passed to WRTMOD in the commented-out call (lines 344–346↗). The branch this reaches has two fragile points of its own (suspected bug 1; suspected bug 2).

  4. (c) The old header calls the triple-α column "3HE". (line 275↗; line 309↗) The fifth energy column, SEG(5,I), is the triple-α term (Explanation, step 3), as the stitched format's header says (line 208↗). What follows: a misleading column name in the old .store format. Only with LSTORE, LSTMOD, and LSTPHYS, and LSTCH off. Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:

81def putstore(hcomp: np.ndarray, hd: np.ndarray, hl: np.ndarray, hp: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hs: np.ndarray,
82             ht: np.ndarray, lc: np.ndarray, trit: np.ndarray, tril: np.ndarray, ps: np.ndarray, ts: np.ndarray,
83             rs: np.ndarray, cfenv: np.ndarray, ftri: float, tlumx: np.ndarray, jcore: int, jenv: int, model: int,
84             m: int, smass: float, teffl: float, bl: float, hstot: float, dage: float, ddage: float, omega: np.ndarray,
85             hs1: np.ndarray, eta2: np.ndarray, r0: np.ndarray, fp: np.ndarray, ft: np.ndarray, hjm: np.ndarray,
86             hi: np.ndarray, cmixl: float, xmsol: float, compmix: str) -> None:
87    """Write the model to the .store file (unit ISTOR): either the
88    "stitched" format through stitch (LSTCH), or the older one: a MOD2
89    header, the flags, TLUMX, the envelope lines and, with LSTMOD, a line
90    per selected shell with optional physics (LSTPHYS) and rotation
91    (LSTROT) columns; then, with LSTATM or LSTENV, the envelope and
92    atmosphere through wrtmil (LMILNE) and wrtmod.
93
94    Translates: putstore.f#PUTSTORE (7be4133)
95
96    Arguments: the model's arrays (hcomp ... lc, omega, hs1, eta2, r0, fp,
97    ft, hjm, hi; index 0 unused), the envelope triangle (trit, tril, ps,
98    ts, rs, cfenv, ftri), the luminosities tlumx, and the scalars; the
99    mixing length cmixl, xmsol (passed to wrtmod) and compmix, the initial
100    mixture's code. Arrays changed in place: tlumx (converted to erg/s if
101    given in solar units). Nothing is returned.
102
103    """
104
105    global atm
106    n = nmlpar_mod
107    c = constants_mod
108    istor = iounits_mod.istor
109    sc = scrtch_mod
110    t2 = temp2_mod
111
112    # Physics flags. Atmosphere flag ATM (Fortran lines 133–145).
113    # Suspected bug (see the page): KTTAU = 5 has no label.
114    if n.kttau in _ATM_LABELS:
115        atm = _ATM_LABELS[n.kttau]
116
117    # Equation of state flag EOS (Fortran lines 146–163).
118    eos = 'SAHA  '
119    if n.ldh:
120        eos = 'SAH+DH'
121    if n.lscv:
122        eos = 'SCV   '
123        if n.ldh:
124            # Suspected bug (see the page): overwritten on the next line.
125            if n.lopale06:
126                eos = 'SCV+O6'
127            eos = 'SCV+DH'
128            if n.lopale06:
129                eos = 'SCDHO6'
130    else:
131        if n.lopale06:
132            eos = 'OPAL06'
133            if n.ldh:
134                eos = 'OP6+DH'
135
136    # Low-temperature opacity flag, always NONE (Fortran lines 164–169).
137    # Question (see the page): no label for LALEX06.
138    alok = 'NONE'
139
140    # High-temperature opacity flag (Fortran lines 170–174).
141    hik = 'NONE'
142    if n.lopal95:
143        hik = 'OP95'
144
145    # Never true: no branch sets ' ? ' (Fortran lines 176–180).
146    if atm == ' ? ':
147        iounits_mod.write(iounits_mod.ishort,
148                          '*** YREC7 input file, flags, etc., have been '
149                          'defaulted.  ***')
150
151    # The stitched format, or the old one, not both (JvS 09/25) (Fortran
152    # lines 182–210).
153    if n.lstch:
154        if ccout_mod.lphhd:
155            iounits_mod.write(istor, _FMT1013)
156            iounits_mod.write(istor, '#' + _flags_line(
157                jcore, jenv, cmixl, eos, alok, hik, compmix))
158            iounits_mod.write(istor, _FMT1014)
159            ccout_mod.lphhd = False   # the physics header only once
160        stitch(hcomp, hr, hp, hd, hs, ht, hl, hs1, omega, eta2, hi, r0, hjm,
161               fp, ft, teffl, hstot, bl, m, lc, model, cmixl)
162        return
163
164    # Header record (FORMATs 10-13): the decimals depend on DAGE's size
165    # (Fortran lines 212–229).
166    if dage < 1.0e3:
167        head = "".join(f"{v:16.11f}" for v in (smass, teffl, bl, hstot, dage))
168    else:
169        if dage < 1.0e4:
170            nd = 10
171        elif dage < 1.0e5:
172            nd = 9
173        else:
174            nd = 8
175        head = ("".join(f"{v:16.12f}" for v in (smass, teffl, bl, hstot))
176                + f"{dage:16.{nd}f}")
177    iounits_mod.write(istor, f"MOD2 {model:8d}{m:8d}" + head
178                      + f"{ddage:18.10E}{hs[1]:16.12f}{hs[m]:16.12f}")
179
180    # Physics flags (FORMAT 30) (Fortran lines 231–237).
181    iounits_mod.write(istor, _flags_line(jcore, jenv, cmixl, eos, alok,
182                                         hik, compmix))
183
184    # Luminosities: if TLUMX are in solar units, convert to erg/s (decided
185    # by comparing with 1e20), in place (Fortran lines 239–251).
186    # Question (see the page): duplicated code.
187    cccmax = max(tlumx[1], tlumx[2], tlumx[3], tlumx[4], tlumx[5],
188                 abs(tlumx[6]), tlumx[7])
189    if cccmax <= 1.0e20:
190        for j in range(1, 8):
191            tlumx[j] = tlumx[j] * c.clsun
192    iounits_mod.write(istor, 'TLUMX     '
193                      + "".join(f"{tlumx[j]:17.9E}" for j in range(1, 8)))
194
195    # Envelope data (FORMAT 50) (Fortran lines 253–260).
196    for i in range(1, 4):
197        i0 = i
198        if ftri < 0.0:
199            i0 = -i
200        iounits_mod.write(
201            istor, f"ENV{i0:2d}{trit[i]:16.12f}{tril[i]:16.12f}"
202            f"{ps[i]:16.12f}{ts[i]:16.12f}{rs[i]:16.12f}"
203            + "".join(f"{cfenv[3 * i - 3 + j]:20.12E}" for j in range(1, 4)))
204
205    # Which shells to print (Fortran line 262). Suspected bug (see the
206    # page): jxbeg, jxend and lshell are never set.
207    id_ = np.zeros(len(hs), dtype=int)
208    idm = pindex(jxbeg, jxend, lshell, m, id_, 0)
209
210    # One line per selected shell (Fortran lines 264–339).
211    if n.lstmod:
212        iounits_mod.write(istor, _FMT55)
213        cg = math.exp(c.cln * c.cgl)
214        for ii in range(1, idm + 1):
215            i = id_[ii]
216
217            # The basic columns, led by MODEL (FORMAT 62) (Fortran lines
218            # 284–303).
219            line = (f"{model:6d} {i:6d}{hs[i]:18.14f}{hr[i]:18.14f}"
220                    f"{hl[i]:24.16E}{hp[i]:18.14f} {ht[i]:18.14f} "
221                    f"{hd[i]:18.14f}{omega[i]:24.16E} {_l(lc[i])}"
222                    + "".join(f"{hcomp[j, i]:12.9f}" for j in range(1, 4))
223                    + "".join(f"{hcomp[j, i]:16.8E}" for j in range(4, 16))
224                    + "  ")
225
226            # Additional physics (FORMAT 63) (Fortran lines 304–316).
227            if n.lstphys:
228                sg = math.exp(c.cln * (c.cgl - 2.0 * hr[i])) * hs1[i]
229                # Question (see the page): the ionization fractions are
230                # written as zeros.
231                vals = [sc.so[i], sg, sc.sdel[1, i], sc.sdel[2, i],
232                        sc.sdel[3, i], sc.svel[i], sound_mod.gam1[i],
233                        0.0, 0.0, 0.0, sc.sbeta[i], sc.seta[i]]
234                vals += [sc.seg[k, i] for k in range(1, 6)]
235                vals += [sc.sesum[i], sc.seg[6, i], sc.seg[7, i]]
236            else:
237                vals = [0.0] * 20
238            line += (f"{vals[0]:10.4E}{vals[1]:11.3E}{vals[2]:12.4E}"
239                     f"{vals[3]:12.4E}{vals[4]:12.4E}{vals[5]:12.4E}"
240                     f"{vals[6]:9.5f}"
241                     + "".join(f" {v:9.5f}" for v in vals[7:12])
242                     + "".join(f"{v:12.4E}" for v in vals[12:17])
243                     + "".join(f"{v:13.5E}" for v in vals[17:20]))
244
245            # Additional rotation info (FORMAT 64) (Fortran lines 317–328).
246            if n.lstrot and n.lrot:
247                fm = math.exp(c.cln * hs[i])
248                duma = c.cc13 * omega[i]**2 / (cg * fm) * 5.0 / (2.0 + eta2[i])
249                a = duma * r0[i]**3
250                rpoleq = (1.0 - a) / (1.0 + 0.5 * a)
251                vtot = t2.ves[i] + t2.vgsf[i] + t2.vss[i]
252                rot = [a, rpoleq, fp[i], ft[i], hjm[i], hi[i],
253                       roten_mod.derot[i], t2.ves[i], t2.vgsf[i], t2.vss[i],
254                       vtot]
255            else:
256                rot = [0.0] * 11
257            line += ("".join(f"{v:14.6E}" for v in rot[:7])
258                     + "".join(f"{v:11.3E}" for v in rot[7:]))
259            iounits_mod.write(istor, line)
260
261    # The envelope and atmosphere (Fortran lines 340–349).
262    if n.lstatm or n.lstenv:
263        if n.lmilne:
264            wrtmil(hcomp, hd, hl, hp, hr, hs1, m, model)
265        wrtmod(m, jenv, hcomp, hs1, hd, hl, hp, hr, ht, model, bl, teffl,
266               fp, ft, hs, dage, cmixl, xmsol)
267
268    # FORMAT 65, (/,/) (Fortran lines 351–352).
269    iounits_mod.write(istor, '\n\n')
