Explanation

Purpose. LAX_WENDROF2 is the second (full) step of the two-step Lax–Wendroff scheme for the settling term: from the coefficients at the grid midpoints (already updated with the half-step abundances) it adds the change over a full time step to the abundances at the grid points.

How it fits. Called by GRSETT and MICRODIFF_RUN, after LAX_WENDROF1. Arguments: DT, ECOD1_H (coefficients at the midpoints), EM_H (masses at the midpoints), EX (the abundances on the equal grid, updated in place), NPT, STOT, LDOLI. Modules as in LAX_WENDROF1: CONSTANTS_MOD, NMLPAR_MOD, GRAVEZ_MOD (here ECOD1Z_H in, EZ updated), and DIMENSIONS_MOD.

The method. With $F_{i+1/2}$ = ECOD1_H(I) and $m_{i+1/2}$ = EM_H(I):

$$X_1 \mathrel{+}= \Delta t\,4\pi\,\frac{F_{1/2}}{m_{1/2}},\qquad X_i \mathrel{+}= \Delta t\,4\pi\,\frac{F_{i+1/2}-F_{i-1/2}}{m_{i+1/2}-m_{i-1/2}}\ (i = 2..N-1),\qquad X_N \mathrel{+}= -\Delta t\,4\pi\,\frac{F_{N-1/2}}{M - m_{N-1/2}} .$$

The same for the metals (EZ) when LDIFZ is true and LDOLI is false.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (/CONST1/ to CONSTANTS_MOD, LDIFZ from /GRAVS3/ to NMLPAR_MOD, /GRAVEZ/ to GRAVEZ_MOD), JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. Surface mass. The surface update divides by $M - m_{N-1/2}$, the mass of half a cell plus whatever lies above point $N$; is the outermost point meant to hold that mass?

Second (full) step of the Lax-Wendroff scheme for settling.

Adds to X at the grid points the change over a full time step, from
the difference of the settling fluxes at the midpoints, with zero flux
at the centre and the surface; the same for Z (``gravez_mod.ez`` from
``gravez_mod.ecod1z_h``) when metal diffusion is on and ``ldoli`` is
false. Follows LAX_WENDROF1.
