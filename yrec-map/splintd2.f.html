Purpose

SPLINTD2 evaluates a cubic spline at one point; it is splint.f's algorithm and equation, with arrays of the fixed size JSON (5000, from DIMENSIONS_MOD), for the second derivatives made by SPLINC. Its header comment still names it "SUBROUTINE SPLINTD" (line 3↗).

Question 1 (line 31↗; line 28↗). Duplicates. SPLINTD2 and SPLINT differ only in their array declarations (and the comment's name SPLINTD). Could one routine serve both callers?

How it fits

  • Who calls it: only HPOINT, after SPLINC.
  • Modules: IOUNITS_MOD, for the unit ISHORT of its error message ("ERROR IN SPLINT ROUTINE: TWO ABSCISSAE COINCIDE", with the abscissa and $x$, also written to the terminal, then STOP 1).

10    """The cubic-spline value y at x, from the table (xa, ya) and its second
11    derivatives y2a (from SPLINC). Stops the run if two abscissas coincide;
12    outside the table it extrapolates the end interval.
13
14    Translates: splintd2.f#SPLINTD2 (7be4133)

16    Arguments: xa[1..n] (increasing), ya[1..n]; n (at most JSON);
17    y2a[1..n]; x; y, klo and khi, set only (klo and khi are reset on
18    entry). Returns (y, klo, khi), with klo, khi the bracketing indices.
19
20    """

29        else:
