Explanation

Purpose. This file gives the change of the surface convection zone's angular velocity over a time step due to magnetized-wind angular-momentum loss, with the older "Kawaler-type" wind law and a saturation threshold. It is used when the newer Matt et al. law (LMWIND) is off.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments BL ($\log L/L_\odot$), TEFFL, SMASS ($M/M_\odot$), DT (the time step), HICZ (the moment of inertia of the surface convection zone), OMEGAS (the surface $\Omega$ after the previous iteration), WOLD (at the start of the step), IT (the iteration); WMAX, EXMD, EXW, EXR, EXM, CONSTFACTOR in NMLPAR_MOD (set in PARMIN from the input index "ALFA", per the comment); TAUCZ (the convective overturn time) in OVRTRN_MOD; the solar constants in CONSTANTS_MOD.
  • Who calls it: MCOWIND, when LMWIND is false ("run old windlaw").
  • Outputs: WIND1, WIND2, the change of $\Omega$ over the step computed with $\Omega$ at its start and at its end. The comment says they enter the diffusion equations and are averaged: SECULR, which calls MCOWIND, passes them to DCOEFT or DADCOEFT, where the surface boundary takes $-\tfrac12(\mathtt{WIND1}+\mathtt{WIND2})$ times the moment of inertia.

The method.

  1. Saturation. If WMAX $> 1$, it is read as $\Omega_\mathrm{sat,\odot}\,\tau_\odot$ and $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = \mathtt{WMAX}/\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ (MHP 3/09; the run stops if $\tau_\mathrm{CZ}$ is not defined); otherwise $\Omega_\mathrm{sat} = \mathtt{WMAX}$.
  2. The radius from $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$: $\log R = \tfrac12(\log(L/L_\odot) + \log L_\odot - \log 4\pi - \log\sigma - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff})$, in cm.

  3. The comment's law, $dJ/dt \propto K\,(\dot M/10^{-14})^{a}\,\Omega^{n}\,(M/M_\odot)^{c}(R/R_\odot)^{b}$, gives $$C = \frac{\Delta t}{I_\mathrm{CZ}}\,K\left(\frac{\dot M}{10^{-14}\,M_\odot\,\mathrm{yr}^{-1}}\right)^{a}\left(\frac{R}{R_\odot}\right)^{b}\left(\frac{M}{M_\odot}\right)^{c},$$ $$\Delta\Omega = C\,\min(\Omega, \Omega_\mathrm{sat})^{\,n-1}\,\Omega,$$ with $K$ = CONSTFACTOR, $a$ = EXMD, $b$ = EXR, $c$ = EXM, $n$ = EXW. So $\Delta\Omega \propto \Omega^n$ below saturation and $\propto \Omega\,\Omega_\mathrm{sat}^{n-1}$ above.

  4. WIND1 uses WOLD. WIND2 uses OMEGAS; from the second iteration on, it is the mean of its previous value and the new one, which damps the iteration.

Design. The mass-loss rate is fixed: DMDOT = 2.0D-14 ($M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$), so the factor $(\dot M/10^{-14})^a$ is the constant $2^a$.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE; the unused /DEUTER/ is gone, and the wind parameters this file does not use (EXTAU, EXL, EXPR, EXCEN, C_2) are now namelist variables in NMLPAR_MOD, shared by all its users, which settles the former question 3 on unused inputs.

Questions for the experts

  1. A fixed mass-loss rate. $\dot M$ is hard-coded to $2\times10^{-14}\,M_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$, not taken from the model's mass-loss rate (DMDT0, now in NMLPAR_MOD). Intended as a calibration constant?
  2. The damping of WIND2. Averaging the previous WIND2 with the new value at each iteration means the converged WIND2 mixes values from several iterations. Is this only for stability, and does it converge to the right value?
  3. The test WMAX > 1. A real $\Omega_\mathrm{sat}$ is far below 1 rad s$^{-1}$, so the test works as a switch; is that documented for users?

11def cowind(bl, dt, hicz, it, omegas, smass, teffl, wold, wind1, wind2):
12    """The change of the surface convection zone's Omega over the step by
13    magnetized-wind angular-momentum loss, with a saturation threshold:
14    delta Omega = (dt/I_cz) K (Mdot/1e-14)**a (R/R_sun)**b (M/M_sun)**c
15    min(Omega, Omega_sat)**(n-1) Omega, for Omega at the start of the
16    step (wind1) and at its end (wind2).
17
18    Translates: cowind.f#COWIND (09c3b9b)
19
20    Arguments: bl, log L/L_sun; dt, the time step; hicz, the moment of
21    inertia of the surface convection zone; it, the iteration; omegas,
22    the surface Omega after the previous iteration; smass, M/M_sun;
23    teffl, log Teff; wold, Omega at the start of the step; wind1, passed
24    in (with any value); wind2, its value from the previous iteration.
25
26    Returns (wind1, wind2).
27    """
28    c = constants_mod
29    n = nmlpar_mod
30    taucz = ovrtrn_mod.taucz
31    # MHP 3/09: WMAX > 1 is read as WMAX(sun)*TAUCZ(sun), and the
32    # saturation threshold is WMAX/TAUCZ(star).
33    # Question (see the page): the test WMAX > 1 works as a switch.
34    if n.wmax > 1.0:
35        if taucz > 1.0:
36            wsat = n.wmax / taucz
37        else:
38            msg = (f"ERROR IN WIND - TAUCZ NOT DEFINED "
39                   f"{n.wmax:12.3E}{taucz:12.3E}STOPPED")
40            print(msg)
41            raise SystemExit(msg)
42    else:
43        wsat = n.wmax
44    # The total radius of the star (cm).
45    rl = 0.5 * (bl + c.clsunl - c.c4pil - c.csigl - 4.0 * teffl)
46    rtot = math.exp(c.cln * rl)
47    # The mass-loss rate, in solar masses per year.
48    # Question (see the page): fixed, not the model's.
49    dmdot = 2.0e-14
50    # cc is the Fortran's C (c here names constants_mod).
51    cc = (dt / hicz * n.constfactor * (dmdot / 1.0e-14) ** n.exmd
52          * (rtot / c.crsun) ** n.exr * smass ** n.exm)
53    wp = min(wold, wsat)
54    wn = min(omegas, wsat)
55    wind1 = cc * wp ** (n.exw - 1.0) * wold
56    temp = cc * wn ** (n.exw - 1.0) * omegas
57    # Question (see the page): the mean with the previous WIND2.
58    if it == 1:
59        wind2 = temp
60    else:
61        wind2 = 0.5 * (wind2 + temp)
62    return wind1, wind2
