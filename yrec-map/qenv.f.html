Purpose

The right-hand side of the envelope integration: with $\log P$ as the independent variable, it returns the derivatives of $\log m$ (relative to the total), $\log T$ and $\log r$, from hydrostatic equilibrium, mass conservation and the temperature gradient.

How it fits

  • Inputs: X0 $=\log P$; Y(1..3) $= \log(m/M_\ast)$, $\log T$, $\log r$; the luminosity B, the distortion factors FPL, FTL, TEFFL, $X$, $Z$; STOTAL ($\log M_\ast$) from COMP_MOD; the mixing length CMIXLQ from QENVMIX_MOD, set by ENVINT before every path that reaches QENV (the argument list must stay that of the EXTERNAL derivative routine, so CMIXL cannot be passed); LPUMOD from PULSE1_MOD.
  • Who calls it: ENVINT, directly and as the EXTERNAL derivative routine passed to BSSTEP and MMID.
  • What it calls: EQSTAT, GETOPAC, and TPGRAD (with CMIXLQ, and with IOVIM = -1 in DPMIX_MOD, so no overshoot point is flagged).
  • Outputs: DYDX(1..3); the counter KENV; the point's structure, gradients, $\beta$, convective velocity and ionization fractions in ENVPRT_MOD (always, since 07/02, 08/13 and 08/25), read by ENVINT, GETNEWENV and STARIN; and the quantities for pulsation output in PULSE2_MOD when saving. EFXION, QQDP, QQDT and QQCP are set always (lines 89–94↗) and again, to the same values, inside IF(LSAVE .OR. LPUMOD) (lines 107–109↗, line 120↗, line 125↗, line 127↗); the second assignments are redundant.

The method

With $f_P$ = FPL, $$\frac{d\log m}{d\log P} = -\frac{4\pi r^4 P}{G m^2 f_P},\qquad \frac{d\log T}{d\log P} = \nabla,\qquad \frac{d\log r}{d\log P} = -\frac{r P}{G m \rho}\,f_P,$$ with $\nabla$ from TPGRAD (lines 72–74↗; on $f_P$ in the last, suspected bug 1).

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) The distortion factor in $d\log r/d\log P$. (line 72↗; line 74↗) The interior equations in COEFFT have $d\log P/d\log m \propto f_P$ and $d\log r/d\log m$ without $f_P$ (lines 247–252↗); dividing gives $d\log r/d\log P \propto 1/f_P$. QENV divides by $f_P$ in $d\log m/d\log P$ (consistent) but multiplies by it in $d\log r/d\log P$. What follows: In a rotating model the slope $d\log r/d\log P$ is $f_P^2$ times the one COEFFT's form gives, so the fitting point's $\log r$ (RS) and the boundary plane of SURFBC are off; the effect is small where $f_P \approx 1$. Only with rotation (LROT, off by default; CRRECT passes $f_P$ of the outermost point, otherwise 1). Read from the code, not run. A possible fix:
