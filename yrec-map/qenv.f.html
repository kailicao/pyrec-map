Explanation

Purpose. The right-hand side of the envelope integration: with $\log P$ as the independent variable, it returns the derivatives of $\log m$ (relative to the total), $\log T$ and $\log r$, from hydrostatic equilibrium, mass conservation and the temperature gradient.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: X0 $=\log P$; Y(1..3) $= \log(m/M_\ast)$, $\log T$, $\log r$; the luminosity B, the distortion factors FPL, FTL, TEFFL, $X$, $Z$; STOTAL ($\log M_\ast$) from COMP_MOD; the mixing length CMIXLQ from QENVMIX_MOD, set by ENVINT before every path that reaches QENV (the argument list must stay that of the EXTERNAL derivative routine, so CMIXL cannot be passed); LPUMOD from PULSE1_MOD.
  • Who calls it: ENVINT, directly and as the EXTERNAL derivative routine passed to BSSTEP and MMID.
  • What it calls: EQSTAT, GETOPAC, and TPGRAD (with CMIXLQ, and with IOVIM = -1 in DPMIX_MOD, so no overshoot point is flagged).
  • Outputs: DYDX(1..3); the counter KENV; the point's structure, gradients, $\beta$, convective velocity and ionization fractions in ENVPRT_MOD (always, since 07/02, 08/13 and 08/25), read by ENVINT, GETNEWENV and STARIN; and the quantities for pulsation output in PULSE2_MOD when saving.

The method. With $f_P$ = FPL, $$\frac{d\log m}{d\log P} = -\frac{4\pi r^4 P}{G m^2 f_P},\qquad \frac{d\log T}{d\log P} = \nabla,\qquad \frac{d\log r}{d\log P} = -\frac{r P}{G m \rho}\,f_P,$$ with $\nabla$ from TPGRAD.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: unused arguments removed from the call to MEQOS (that call is gone since rp_1); a never-executed PRINT keeps GL and LOCOND referenced, "for external procedure compatibility".

Changed since rp_1. The MHD equation of state removed: EQSTAT is always called (no LMHD, no MEQOS); TPGRAD takes the mixing length as a new last argument, CMIXLQ from QENVMIX_MOD; COMMON blocks to modules (the unused /PULSE/, /FLAG/ and /MHD/ dropped), IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. The distortion factor in $d\log r/d\log P$. The interior equations in COEFFT have $d\log P/d\log m \propto f_P$ and $d\log r/d\log m$ without $f_P$; dividing gives $d\log r/d\log P \propto 1/f_P$. QENV divides by $f_P$ in $d\log m/d\log P$ (consistent) but multiplies by it in $d\log r/d\log P$. Is this a sign slip in the exponent of $f_P$, or does the envelope use a different form on purpose? (The effect is small where $f_P \approx 1$.)
  2. Repeated assignments. EFXION, QQDP, QQDT and QQCP are now set both always and again inside IF(LSAVE .OR. LPUMOD). The second set can go.
