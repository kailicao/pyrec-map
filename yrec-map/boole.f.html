boole.f

Purpose

This file integrates tabulated data with Boole's rule (5-point Newton–Cotes), after interpolating the data onto an even grid with a cubic spline. Its only use is the acoustic depth in CALCAD, $\int dr / c_s$.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments X(N) and Y(N), and NN, the number of points of the even grid (it should be $1 + 4p$).
  • Who calls it: CALCAD, twice: over the whole star and from the base of the convection zone to the surface.
  • What it calls: SPLINJ (the spline's second derivatives) and SPLINT (spline evaluation).
  • Output: the integral in S.

The method

  1. Rescale: $x' = 10^{-11} x$, $y' = 10^{7} y$ (SCALEX, SCALEY; the comment says this brings the radius and the sound-speed vector to values near 1).

    Question 1 (lines 34–35↗; lines 41–45↗; line 65↗). Fixed scales. The factors $10^{-11}$ and $10^{7}$ suit radii in cm and $1/c_s$ in s cm$^{-1}$ of a star like the Sun. Since the result is scaled back, they only affect rounding; are they needed?

  2. Fit a natural cubic spline to $(x', y')$ and evaluate it at NN evenly spaced points $x'_k = x'_1 + (k-1)h$, $h = (x'_N - x'_1)/(NN-1)$.

  3. Apply Boole's rule to each group of 5 points: $$\int_{x_0}^{x_4} f\,dx \approx \frac{2h}{45}\left(7f_0 + 32f_1 + 12f_2 + 32f_3 + 7f_4\right),$$ over $p = \lfloor (NN-1)/4 \rfloor$ groups. If $NN - 1$ is not a multiple of 4, the last points would be left out without a message; CALCAD, the only caller, rounds $NN - 1$ up to a multiple of 4 in both calls (lines 310–316↗, lines 375–381↗), except for a profile of at most 3 points (suspected bug 7) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

  4. Undo the scaling: $S = S'/(10^{-11}\cdot 10^{7})$.

The local arrays XI, YI, XD, YD, D2X have sizes set by the arguments N and NN (automatic arrays, standard in Fortran 90), and H and S are arrays of length 1 used as scalars (lines 36–37↗).
