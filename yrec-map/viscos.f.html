Purpose

VISCOS computes, for every shell, the kinematic viscosity (radiative plus molecular) and the radiative thermal diffusivity, which the rotational mixing routines use (for example the Prandtl number $\nu/K$ in vcirc.f). It also computes the Endal & Sofia versions "for comparison only" (VISCR2, VISMO2, VISC2, line 67↗, lines 93–103↗; unused?: they are neither stored nor returned).

How it fits

  • Inputs: the composition HCOMP (its first 11 species), $\log\rho$ (HD), $\log T$ (HT), and through modules the opacity SO (SCRTCH_MOD) and $c_P$ CP (TEMP_MOD), filled by PHYSIC.
  • Who calls it: only PHYSIC.
  • Outputs: in TEMP_MOD (formerly COMMON/TEMP/), VISC (cm²/s) and THDIF (cm²/s), shared with GETW, HPOINT and MIDMOD.

The method

With HCOMP's species $i$ of mass $A_i$ (WEIGHT) and charge $Z_i$ (Z), $n_i = X_i/A_i$, the mean ion charge $\bar Z = \sum n_i Z_i/\sum n_i$ and ELECN = $\bar Z\rho/m_u$ (used as $n_e$ below, which it is not: see Bugs, by priority, 1):

  1. Radiative viscosity (Ledoux 1958, electron scattering only, by the comment): $$\nu_\mathrm{rad} = 6.728\times10^{-26}\,\frac{T^4}{\kappa\,\rho^2}$$ (the constant is $4a/15c$).
  2. Molecular viscosity (Spitzer 1962). With the Coulomb logarithm $$\ln\Lambda = 9.4245 + \tfrac12\ln\!\left(\frac{T^3\,\min(1,\,4.2\times10^5/T)}{n_e\,\bar Z^2}\right),$$ each species contributes $$\eta_i = \frac{3.125\times10^{-15}\,T^{5/2}\,n_i\sqrt{A_i}}{(\ln\Lambda - \ln Z_i)\,Z_i^2\,\sum_j n_j Z_j^2\sqrt{(A_i + A_j)/A_j}},$$ and $\nu_\mathrm{mol} = \sum_i \eta_i/\rho$ (species 3, the total $Z$, has $Z_3 = 0$ and is skipped).
  3. VISC $= \nu_\mathrm{rad} + \nu_\mathrm{mol}$.
  4. Thermal diffusivity (radiation only; conduction neglected, by the comment): $$K = \frac{16\,\sigma\,T^3}{3\,\kappa\,\rho^2\,c_P},$$ with $\sigma = 5.669\times10^{-5}$ written in the code.

Question 1 (lines 45–46↗). "Skip convective zones". The comment above the loop says so, but every shell is computed; the argument LC that could have marked them was never read in the loop and has been removed (line 31↗). Is the comment stale, or should convective shells be skipped?

Question 2 (lines 48–57↗, lines 38–42↗). The total $Z$ as a species. Species 3 (the total metal fraction $Z$) has weight 1.0 and charge 0; it is skipped in the sums over $Z_i^2$, but it adds $Z/1.0$ to COMSUM, and so lowers $\bar Z$, as a neutral particle of mass 1 would. Should it be left out of COMSUM (or given the mean mass and charge of the metals not among species 5–11)?

Question 3 (lines 37–40↗, line 107↗). Hard-coded constants. The atomic mass unit, $\sigma$ and the isotope masses are written in the code, not taken from CONSTANTS_MOD; some masses differ from standard values ($^{15}$N 15.0011, $^{17}$O 16.99491 and $^{18}$O 17.99491, against about 15.0001, 16.9991 and 17.9992). Are the three masses typing slips, and should the constants come from CONSTANTS_MOD?

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) ELECN is not the electron density. (line 48↗; line 57↗; line 61↗; line 73↗) The comment says the loop finds "MEAN CHARGE PER ION(ZF) AND NE", and ELECN enters the Coulomb logarithm LAMBD1 as the electron density. But ELECN = ZF*RHO/AMU uses the mean charge per ion, $\bar Z\rho/m_u$, while the electron density is $n_e = (\rho/m_u)\sum_i X_i Z_i/A_i = \bar Z\,\rho/m_u\,\sum_i n_i$: ELECN is too large by $1/\sum_i n_i$, the mean ion mass (about 1.3 in the Sun), so $\ln\Lambda$ is too small by about $\tfrac12\ln 1.3 \approx 0.13$. This changes the molecular viscosity, VISC, which reaches the rotational mixing (VCIRC, through MIDMOD's VISCM); only in rotating runs (LROT, off by default). Read from the code, not run; how much the mixing changes was not traced. A possible fix:
