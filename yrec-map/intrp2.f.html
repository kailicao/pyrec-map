Python version: intrp2.py, 47 lines, on the right (switch to Python above the code).

Purpose

INTRP2 returns the weights of 4-point Lagrange interpolation at a point $x$, without the derivative weights: the same as INTERP minus its C output.

How it fits

Called by the opacity-table routines ALEX06TAB, GETOPAL95, OP95XTAB and OP95ZTAB (ALX8TH, ALXZTAB and YALO3D also called it; removed since rp_1), by the diffusion regridding EQUAL_TO_MODEL and MICRODIFF_ETM, and by PHYSIC. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

Question 1 (line 4↗; lines 15–17↗). Header typo and stale comment. The banner says "INTEP2", and the comment, copied from INTERP, still speaks of the VandenBerg opacity tables only. Comments to update?

The method

$B_i(x) = \prod_{j\ne i}(x-a_j)/(a_i-a_j)$ for $i = 1..4$.
