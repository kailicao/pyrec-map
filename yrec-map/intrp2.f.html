Explanation

Purpose. INTRP2 returns the weights of 4-point Lagrange interpolation at a point $x$, without the derivative weights: the same as INTERP minus its C output.

How it fits. Called by the opacity-table routines ALEX06TAB, GETOPAL95, OP95XTAB and OP95ZTAB (ALX8TH, ALXZTAB and YALO3D also called it; removed since rp_1), by the diffusion regridding EQUAL_TO_MODEL and MICRODIFF_ETM, and by PHYSIC. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

The method. $B_i(x) = \prod_{j\ne i}(x-a_j)/(a_i-a_j)$ for $i = 1..4$.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: IMPLICIT NONE with explicit declarations. Three of its callers (ALX8TH, ALXZTAB, YALO3D) are removed since rp_1.
