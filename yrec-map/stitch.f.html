Purpose

STITCH writes one model's full profile to the .store file, in an alternative format (JvS 08/25): the interior shells, then, if asked, the envelope and atmosphere "stitched" onto them, one line per point with 61 columns. Its comment lists the columns: model, shell, $\log m$ [g], $\log r$ [cm], $L/L_\odot$, $\log P$, $\log T$, $\log\rho$, $\Omega$ [rad/s], three flags for the point's kind, the 15 mass fractions, the opacity, gravity, the three temperature gradients, the convective velocity, $\Gamma_1$, ionisation fractions, $\beta$, $\eta$, the energy rates of each burning chain, neutrino and gravitational energy, $c_P$, $d\ln\rho/d\ln T$, and the rotation columns (51–61).

How it fits

  • Inputs: the model through the arguments (and the mixing length CMIXL, passed on to ENVINT), and the per-shell physics left in modules by earlier routines: SCRTCH_MOD (opacity SO, gradients SDEL, SVEL, ionisation SFXION, $\eta$ SETA, energy rates SEG and SESUM, SCP; filled by the physics routines such as PHYSIC), SOUND_MOD (GAM1), PULSE1_MOD (PQDT), ROTEN_MOD (DEROT) and TEMP2_MOD (the circulation velocities VES, VGSF, VSS, written by VCIRC among others).
  • Who calls it: PUTSTORE, when LSTCH is set; PUTSTORE is called by WRTOUT every NPRTMOD models and by the main program.
  • What it calls: ENVINT, to integrate the envelope (and atmosphere) again for output.
  • Output: lines on the unit ISTOR (IOUNITS_MOD).
  • Modules: besides those above, ENVSTRUCT_MOD and ATMSTRUCT_MOD (the envelope's and atmosphere's points, filled by ENVINT), NMLPAR_MOD (the output steps ATMSTP, ENVSTP, the integration steps ATMBEG … ENVMAX, the switches LSTENV, LSTATM, the spot parameters and LROT), CONSTANTS_MOD, JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

The method

  1. Interior (shells 1 to $M$): the gravity $g = G m / r^2$, and, with rotation, the shape quantities $$A = \frac{\Omega^2 r_0^3}{3\,G m}\,\frac{5}{2 + \eta_2},\qquad \frac{R_p}{R_e} = \frac{1 - A}{1 + A/2},$$ with $r_0$ (R0) and $\eta_2$ (ETA2) from the rotation routines, and the total circulation velocity VTOT = VES + VGSF + VSS. Without rotation the 11 rotation columns are zeros. Column 39 ($\beta$) is read from the envelope's array (bug 2).

  2. Envelope (LSTENV): the integration steps are set to the fixed output steps ATMSTP, ENVSTP ("dropping a sinkline"; they are not restored, bug 1), and ENVINT integrates from the surface, at $\log R = \tfrac12(\log L + \log L_\odot - 4\log T_\mathrm{eff} - \log 4\pi - \log\sigma)$ and $\log g = \log G + \log M - 2\log R$, down to the pressure of the last interior shell; for a spotted star at the ambient temperature (the formula in starin.f). The envelope points get shell numbers $M+1, \dots$; $\Omega$ and the composition are those of the last interior shell; the burning and rotation columns are zero. LPULPT, passed to ENVINT, is never set (bug 5).

    Question 1 (lines 167–173↗). Spot test. The spotted-star branch tests LC(M) (the last shell convective), while STARIN and CALCAD test JENV.EQ.M. Same intent?

  3. Atmosphere (LSTATM): the atmosphere's points, outermost last, with $r = R_\mathrm{env} + $ ATMOR, the total mass and the surface luminosity; $R_\mathrm{env}$ is the envelope's last point, ENVR(NUMENV), or the outer shell's HR(M) when no envelope point is stored (NUMENV = 0, as for the starting model). The gravity and luminosity columns are not computed for these points (bugs 3 and 4).

    Question 2 (line 182↗, line 215↗). Two points with one number. The envelope's points are numbered M+1 to M+NUMENV, outermost last, and the atmosphere's from NUMATM-I+M+NUMENV with I = NUMATM first, that is M+NUMENV again. If the atmosphere's first point is the envelope's last (both at the base of the atmosphere, RBASE = ENVR(NUMENV)), the shared number is right and the point is written twice; if not, should the atmosphere start at M+NUMENV+1?

    Question 3 (lines 202–217↗). Atmosphere without envelope. With LSTATM but not LSTENV, ENVINT is not called here, so the atmosphere's points, their numbering, and their base radius come from whatever envelope integration ran last (NUMENV, ENVR(NUMENV); HR(M) only when NUMENV is 0). Should the atmosphere require the envelope?

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) The integration steps are not restored. (lines 140–151↗) STITCH saves ATMBEG, ATMMIN, ATMMAX, ENVBEG, ENVMIN, ENVMAX into ABEG0 … EMAX0 and sets them to the output steps, but never restores them (ABEG0 … EMAX0 are never read), so every later envelope integration of the run, those of the surface boundary condition included, starts and steps with the output steps (lines 376–377↗; lines 556–558↗): with the defaults, a fixed step of 0.5 in place of steps between 0.1 and 0.5 (lines 92–98↗; lines 223–224↗). WRTMOD (lines 237–242↗) and GETNEWENV restore the steps they change; CALCAD does not either. What follows: the models after the first stored one are computed with a coarser envelope integration; how much that changes them was not traced. Only with LSTCH (off by default) and LSTENV. Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE (the unused OVRTRN dropped); a new argument CMIXL, passed on to ENVINT; the formats of the lines (62, 63) now put a blank between the $\log P$, $\log T$, $\log\rho$ columns and between the six F9.5 columns ($\Gamma_1$ to $\eta$), so that neighbouring values no longer run together.

Changed since a8a5950. The atmosphere's base radius is the outer shell's HR(M) when NUMENV is 0; for the starting model it was read from ENVR(0), which put meaningless radii in the .store file. The question on the atmosphere without envelope stays, for a stale NUMENV above 0.

34def _e0(x, w, d):
35    """Fortran's 0PEw.d: a mantissa in [0.1, 1), e.g. 0.12345678E+01."""
36
37    if x == 0.0:
38        return f"{'0.' + '0' * d + 'E+00':>{w}}"
39    e = math.floor(math.log10(abs(x))) + 1
40    m = x / 10.0**e
41    if round(abs(m), d) >= 1.0:  # rounding carried into the next decade
42        m /= 10.0
43        e += 1
44    s = f"{abs(m):.{d}f}"[1:]  # drop the leading zero, as Fortran does
45    sign = "-" if x < 0 else ""
46    return f"{sign + '0' + s + f'E{e:+03d}':>{w}}"

53def _fmt62(model, i, hs, hr, hl, hp, ht, hd, om, l1, l2, l3, l4, comp):
54    # I6,1X,I6,0P2F18.14,1PE24.16,0PF18.14,1X,F18.14,1X,F18.14,1PE24.16,
55    # 1X,L1,1X,L1,1X,L1,1X,L1,3(0PF12.9),12(0PE16.8),2X
56    s = (f"{model:6d} {i:6d}{hs:18.14f}{hr:18.14f}{hl:24.16E}{hp:18.14f}"
57         f" {ht:18.14f} {hd:18.14f}{om:24.16E}"
58         f" {_l1(l1)} {_l1(l2)} {_l1(l3)} {_l1(l4)}")
59    s += "".join(f"{comp[j]:12.9f}" for j in range(1, 4))
60    s += "".join(_e0(comp[j], 16, 8) for j in range(4, 16))
61    return s + "  "

64def _fmt63(v):
65    # 1PE10.4,1PE11.3,E12.4,E12.4,E12.4,1PE12.4,0PF9.5,1X,F9.5,1X,F9.5,
66    # 1X,F9.5,1X,F9.5,1X,F9.5,5E12.4,8E13.5 (the last E's with 0P); the
67    # 22 values end the record at the fifth E13.5.
68    s = f"{v[0]:10.4E}{v[1]:11.3E}{v[2]:12.4E}{v[3]:12.4E}{v[4]:12.4E}"
69    s += f"{v[5]:12.4E}{v[6]:9.5f}"
70    s += "".join(f" {v[k]:9.5f}" for k in range(7, 12))
71    s += "".join(_e0(v[k], 12, 4) for k in range(12, 17))
72    s += "".join(_e0(v[k], 13, 5) for k in range(17, 22))
73    return s

76def _fmt64(v):
77    # 7E14.6, 4E11.3 (0P)
78    return ("".join(_e0(x, 14, 6) for x in v[:7])
79            + "".join(_e0(x, 11, 3) for x in v[7:]))

82def stitch(hcomp: np.ndarray, hr: np.ndarray, hp: np.ndarray, hd: np.ndarray, hs: np.ndarray, ht: np.ndarray,
83           hl: np.ndarray, hs1: np.ndarray, omega: np.ndarray, eta2: np.ndarray, hi: np.ndarray, r0: np.ndarray,
84           hjm: np.ndarray, fp: np.ndarray, ft: np.ndarray, teffl: float, hstot: float, bl: float, m: int,
85           lc: np.ndarray, model: int, cmixl: float) -> None:
86    """Write one model's full profile to the .store unit ISTOR, one line of
87    61 columns per point: the interior shells, then, if asked (LSTENV,
88    LSTATM), the envelope integrated again with ENVINT at fixed output
89    steps, and the atmosphere.
90
91    Translates: stitch.f#STITCH (7be4133)
92
93    Arguments: the model (hcomp[species, shell], hr, hp, hd, hs, ht, hl,
94    hs1, omega, lc, index 0 unused), the rotation arrays eta2, hi, r0, hjm,
95    fp, ft, log Teff, log M (hstot), log L/Lsun (bl), m, the model number,
96    and cmixl (the mixing length, passed to envint). Nothing is changed.
97
98    Returns None.
99
100    """
101
102    global b
103    c = constants_mod
104    nml = nmlpar_mod
105    sc = scrtch_mod
106    es = envstruct_mod
107    at = atmstruct_mod
108    unit = iounits_mod.istor
109    zeros11 = [0.0] * 11
110
111    # The interior shells (Fortran lines 108–133).
112    cg = math.exp(c.cln * c.cgl)
113    sg = 0.0
114    for i in range(1, m + 1):
115        sg = math.exp(c.cln * (c.cgl - 2.0 * hr[i])) * hs1[i]
116        line = _fmt62(model, i, hs[i], hr[i], hl[i], hp[i], ht[i], hd[i],
117                      omega[i], lc[i], True, False, False, hcomp[:, i])
118        # Suspected bug (see the page): beta of an interior shell is read
119        # from the envelope's EBETAS, not SBETA.
120        line += _fmt63([sc.so[i], sg, sc.sdel[1, i], sc.sdel[2, i],
121                        sc.sdel[3, i], sc.svel[i], sound_mod.gam1[i],
122                        sc.sfxion[1, i], sc.sfxion[2, i], sc.sfxion[3, i],
123                        es.ebetas[i], sc.seta[i], sc.seg[1, i], sc.seg[2, i],
124                        sc.seg[3, i], sc.seg[4, i], sc.seg[5, i],
125                        sc.sesum[i], sc.seg[6, i], sc.seg[7, i], sc.scp[i],
126                        pulse1_mod.pqdt[i]])
127        if nml.lrot:
128            fm = math.exp(c.cln * hs[i])
129            duma = c.cc13 * omega[i]**2 / (cg * fm) * 5.0 / (2.0 + eta2[i])
130            a = duma * r0[i]**3
131            rpoleq = (1.0 - a) / (1.0 + 0.5 * a)
132            vtot = temp2_mod.ves[i] + temp2_mod.vgsf[i] + temp2_mod.vss[i]
133            line += _fmt64([a, rpoleq, fp[i], ft[i], hjm[i], hi[i],
134                            roten_mod.derot[i], temp2_mod.ves[i],
135                            temp2_mod.vgsf[i], temp2_mod.vss[i], vtot])
136        else:
137            line += _fmt64(zeros11)
138        iounits_mod.write(unit, line)
139
140    if nml.lstenv:
141        # Only provide an envelope if asked to do so: fixed output steps
142        # ("dropping a sinkline") (Fortran lines 136–151).
143        # Suspected bug (see the page): the saved steps are never restored.
144        abeg0 = nml.atmbeg  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
145        amin0 = nml.atmmin  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
146        amax0 = nml.atmmax  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
147        ebeg0 = nml.envbeg  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
148        emin0 = nml.envmin  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
149        emax0 = nml.envmax  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
150        nml.atmbeg = nml.atmstp
151        nml.atmmin = nml.atmstp
152        nml.atmmax = nml.atmstp
153        nml.envbeg = nml.envstp
154        nml.envmin = nml.envstp
155        nml.envmax = nml.envstp
156
157        # The envelope's surface values, and the integration (Fortran lines
158        # 152–176).
159        idum = 0
160        b = math.exp(c.cln * bl)
161        fpl = fp[m]
162        ftl = ft[m]
163        katm = 0
164        kenv = 0
165        ksaha = 0
166        ixx = 0
167        lprt = True
168        lsbc0 = False
169        x = hcomp[1, m]
170        z = hcomp[3, m]
171        rl = 0.5 * (bl + c.clsunl - 4.0 * teffl - c.c4pil - c.csigl)
172        gl = c.cgl + hstot - rl - rl
173        plim = hp[m]
174        # Question (see the page): tests lc[m], where STARIN tests jenv == m.
175        if lc[m] and nml.spotf != 0.0 and nml.spotx != 1.0:
176            ateffl = teffl - 0.25 * math.log10(
177                nml.spotf * nml.spotx**4.0 + 1.0 - nml.spotf)
178        else:
179            ateffl = teffl
180        dum1 = np.zeros(5)
181        dum2 = np.zeros(4)
182        dum3 = np.zeros(4)
183        dum4 = np.zeros(4)
184        rl, idum, katm, kenv, ksaha = envint(
185            b, fpl, ftl, gl, hstot, ixx, lprt, lsbc0, plim, rl, ateffl, x, z,
186            dum1, idum, katm, kenv, ksaha, dum2, dum3, dum4, lpulpt, cmixl)
187
188        # The envelope's masses, then one line per point (Fortran lines
189        # 178–195).
190        for i in range(1, es.numenv + 1):
191            envs1[i] = math.exp(c.cln * (es.envs[i] + hstot))
192        for i in range(m + 1, m + es.numenv + 1):
193            k = i - m
194            sg = math.exp(c.cln * (c.cgl - 2.0 * es.envr[k])) * envs1[k]
195            line = _fmt62(model, i, es.envs[k] + hstot, es.envr[k],
196                          es.envl[k], es.envp[k], es.envt[k], es.envd[k],
197                          omega[m], es.lcenv[k], False, True, False,
198                          hcomp[:, m])
199            line += _fmt63([es.envo[k], sg, es.edels[1, k], es.edels[2, k],
200                            es.edels[3, k], es.evels[k], es.egam1[k],
201                            es.efxions[1, k], es.efxions[2, k],
202                            es.efxions[3, k], es.ebetas[k]]
203                           + [0.0] * 9 + [es.eqcp[k], es.eqdt[k]])
204            line += _fmt64(zeros11)
205            iounits_mod.write(unit, line)
206
207    if nml.lstatm:
208        # The atmosphere (Fortran lines 200–227). Question (see the page):
209        # without LSTENV, numenv and envr are those of an earlier
210        # integration. Suspected bugs (see the page): b may be from an
211        # earlier call, and sg is the last point's. The base radius is the
212        # outer shell's when no envelope point is stored (MHP 10/30/26).
213        if es.numenv > 0:
214            rbase = es.envr[es.numenv]
215        else:
216            rbase = hr[m]
217        for i in range(at.numatm, 0, -1):
218            rad = math.log10(math.exp(c.cln * rbase) + at.atmor[i])
219            line = _fmt62(model, at.numatm - i + m + es.numenv, hstot, rad,
220                          b, at.atmop[i], at.atmot[i], at.atmod[i], omega[m],
221                          False, False, False, True, hcomp[:, m])
222            line += _fmt63([at.atmoo[i], sg, at.adels[1, i], at.adels[2, i],
223                            at.adels[3, i], 0.0, at.agam1[i],
224                            at.afxions[1, i], at.afxions[2, i],
225                            at.afxions[3, i], at.abetas[i]]
226                           + [0.0] * 9 + [at.atmocp[i], at.aqdt[i]])
227            line += _fmt64(zeros11)
228            iounits_mod.write(unit, line)
