Purpose

Extends each convection zone by convective overshoot. Its comment: it "computes the local pressure scale height at both edges of a given convective region and locates the boundaries of overshoot regions based on the user-specified extent."

How it fits

  • Inputs: the model (HCOMP, HD, HP, HR, HS, HT, M), the convection zones MXZONE and MXZON0 (without overshoot) and NZONE; from NMLPAR_MOD the switches LOVSTC, LOVSTE, LOVSTM (core, envelope, intermediate zones), the extents ALPHAC, ALPHAE, ALPHAM (in pressure scale heights), LOVMAX with BETAC, and the flags LROT and LINSTB.
  • Who calls it: only CONVEC.
  • What it calls: HSUBP, the pressure scale height at a zone's edge.
  • Outputs: MXZONE with the new edges (CONVEC then merges zones that overshoot made overlap); IOV1, IOV2 in DPMIX_MOD ("store position of overshoot for adiabatic extension," DBG 8/94): the new and the formal lower edge of a zone overshooting downward. With LADOV, TPGRAD gives every radiative point with index IOVIM between them the adiabatic gradient, $\nabla = \nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ (IOVIM is the point being computed, set by COEFFT and PHYSIC). The zones with and without overshoot are printed to ISHORT, four to a line; more zones continue on new lines without the label, by Fortran's reuse of the format (lines 170–176↗).

Question 1 (lines 61–62↗; lines 143–145↗). IOV1, IOV2 for one zone only. They are reset to −1 and then overwritten by every zone with downward overshoot, so they keep only the last such zone (usually the envelope). With an intermediate zone and an envelope, the intermediate zone's downward overshoot does not get the adiabatic gradient of LADOV; nor does any upward overshoot (above a core or an intermediate zone), since only the downward branch sets them. Is this intended (LADOV meant for the envelope's base only)?

The method

For each zone, by type:

  • Core (starts at the centre): overshoot upward by $d = \alpha_c H_p$; with LOVMAX, $d = \min(\alpha_c H_p,\ \beta_c\, r_\mathrm{edge})$, which limits overshoot above small convective cores "as per Woo & Demarque 2001." A fully convective star gets no overshoot: OVERSH has an exit for it that prints the first zone and returns (lines 70–76↗), but it is never reached, since CONVEC, its only caller, returns before calling OVERSH when one zone covers the whole star (convec.f, lines 131–138↗) (unused?).
  • Envelope (reaches the surface): downward by $\alpha_e H_p$.
  • Intermediate: both ways by $\alpha_m H_p$, each with the scale height at that edge.

Here $H_p = P r^2/(\rho\, G m)$ is the pressure scale height at the zone's formal (Schwarzschild) edge, from HSUBP, in cm, like $d$ and $r$. LOVMAX (off by default), which the documentation calls a test "to avoid pathologies in overshoot of small cores," caps the distance at $\beta_c r_\mathrm{edge}$ (BETAC, "maximum overshoot in fraction of physical size of core"). The new edge is the last model point within $d$ in radius of the formal edge: downward, the innermost point with $r \ge r_\mathrm{edge} - d$; upward, the outermost point with $r \le r_\mathrm{edge} + d$; or the centre or surface if $d$ reaches beyond. For rotating models with LINSTB, the zone is widened by one more point if overshoot moved no edge (MHP 11/91), so that each overshoot region has at least one radiative point.

Question 2 (lines 82–92↗). Step overshoot, and its default near small cores. Overshoot here is a step (full mixing over $\alpha H_p$, $H_p$ at the formal edge), with no exponential or diffusive option, as the YREC paper (§2.3.3) says ("a step overshoot model"). Near the centre $H_p$ should grow without bound as $m \to 0$ (with $m \propto r^3$, $H_p \propto 1/r$), so that $\alpha_c H_p$ above a small core could exceed the core's radius unless LOVMAX is set: Are these the "pathologies" the documentation means? LOVMAX is off by default. Is the uncapped step meant to be the default for stars with small convective cores?

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Convective mixing & overshoot): LOVSTC with ALPHAC overshoots convective cores, by ALPHAC pressure scale heights; unless LADOV is on, this is overmixing only and does not change the thermal structure. LOVMAX (checked only with LOVSTC) is an alternative test that avoids pathologies above small cores, limiting overshoot to the fraction BETAC (default 0.15) of the core's size. LOVSTE with ALPHAE overshoots the envelope; LOVSTM with ALPHAM overshoots intermediate convection zones above and below, which the documentation recommends leaving off ("needs to be checked"). All extents default to 0. LADOV is listed among the experimental options.

14def _zone_lines(label: str, mx: np.ndarray, nzone: int) -> str:
15    """FORMAT(1X,label,4('[',I4,'-',I4,' ]')): four zones a line; further
16    lines restart at the repeated group, without the label (Fortran's
17    format reversion; Fortran lines 170–176)."""

19    groups = [f"[{mx[i, 1]:4d}-{mx[i, 2]:4d} ]" for i in range(1, nzone + 1)]

21    for n in range(4, len(groups), 4):
