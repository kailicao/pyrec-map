Purpose

OLDMOD_MOD holds the model at the start of the step (the "old" model): its structure, composition, effective temperature, and surface luminosity, shared by 17 routines. It replaces COMMON/OLDMOD/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • HPO, HTO, HRO, HLO, HDO (JSON each): $\log P$, $\log T$, $\log r$, $L$ and $\log\rho$ of the old model. Saved by HPOINT (when it remaps the model onto a new grid, interpolating them with OSPLIN), by the main program ("save old model for PTIME") and by STARIN. Read by PTIME (the time step from the structure changes), MIDMOD (interpolation within the step), LIRATE88 (the old rates) and LIBURN, LIBURN2.
  • HCOMPP(15,JSON): the old composition, saved by the main program, HPOINT, GETW and BURSMIX; read by the burning routines (KEMCOM, EQBURN, DBURN, DBURNM, LIRATE88), MIDMOD, CHECKC and CRRECT.
  • HSS(JSON): the old mass coordinates, against which HPOINT interpolates (and the new grid while HPOINT builds it).
  • LCO, LCZO (JSON each): the old convective flags, saved by HPOINT (LCZO also by STARIN); LCO is read by MIDMOD, LCZO by CRRECT, which passes it to GETROT.
  • TEFFLO, HLSURO: the old $\log T_\mathrm{eff}$ ("JVS 04/14") and surface luminosity, saved by the main program, HPOINT (before it remaps the model) and STARIN; read by ENTIME, which compares them with the new model.

Design

A blanket USE OLDMOD_MOD. HPOINT also uses HPO, HTO and HDO as scratch arrays ("note HDO is being used as a dummy array here"), so between HPOINT and the next save they do not hold the old model. At rp_1, MIX and SECULR also declared the block without using it; they no longer do.

Changed since a8a5950. The point count MO is replaced by HLSURO, the old model's surface luminosity, which is what ENTIME needs: HLO(MO) was not the surface once HPOINT had changed the number of points.
