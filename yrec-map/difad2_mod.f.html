Explanation

Purpose. DIFAD2_MOD holds the advective and diffusive parts of the meridional circulation velocity at each shell, for the diffusion-plus-advection treatment of angular momentum (LDIFAD), shared by 3 routines. It replaces COMMON/DIFAD2/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • VESA(JSON), VESD(JSON): the advective (signed) and diffusive parts, set by VCIRC (and rescaled there by the μ-gradient factor); read by ROTGRID, which interpolates them into ECOD3, ECOD4 (DIFAD_MOD), and printed by CHECKJ.
  • VESA0(JSON), VESD0(JSON): their values from the previous call, saved and read by VCIRC alone.

Design. A blanket USE DIFAD2_MOD.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/DIFAD2/ as a module.
