Purpose

SOLID makes a range of shells, JSTART to JEND, rotate as a solid body while conserving their total angular momentum. Given each shell's angular momentum per unit mass, it returns the common rotation rate $\Omega$ and the shells' moments of inertia. Its comment adds that it also gives $r_0$ and $\eta_2$ (R0, ETA2), which FPFT needs.

How it fits

  • Inputs: arguments only: the shells' masses (HS2, probably in grams, since the angular momentum is the sum of HS2*HJM), specific angular momenta HJM, the previous rates OMEGA, and the structure (HD, HR, HS), passed on to the shape routines.
  • Who calls it: the rotation routines that impose solid-body rotation on a zone: CHECKJ, GETROT, MIDMOD, SECULR and WCZIMP (for convection zones, by its name; WCZ, removed since rp_1, was another).
  • What it calls: SHAPE (the distortion of the shells, ETA2 and R0) and MOMI (each shell's moment of inertia HI and QIW, which the comments call $dI/d\Omega$ but which is $\tfrac12\,\Omega\,dI/d\Omega$: suspected bug 2).
  • Outputs: OMEGA, HI, QIW, ETA2, R0 and an updated HJM, all through the arguments.
  • Modules: ERRMOM_MOD (the tolerance TOLERI, $10^{-12}$, used only here and in CHECKJ: CHECKJ saves it (TOLER0 = TOLERI), relaxes it to MAX(DJOK*1.0D-2,TOLER0) around its call to GETROT, and restores it); DISK_MOD (the age SAGE, shared with GETW, SECULR, MDOT and others); NMLPAR_MOD (the disk-locking parameters: the locking time TDISK, the locked rate PDISK and the switch LDISK, namelist parameters read by PARMIN); DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

Let $m_j$ be HS2(J) and $j_j$ be HJM(J). The target is the zone's total angular momentum,

$$J = \sum_{j=J_\mathrm{start}}^{J_\mathrm{end}} m_j\, j_j .$$

The first guess for $\Omega$ is the mean of the previous rates. With every shell at $\Omega$, SHAPE and MOMI give the moments of inertia $I_j(\Omega)$ and $I'_j$, taken as $dI_j/d\Omega$ (QIW, which is in fact $\tfrac12\,\Omega\,dI_j/d\Omega$; suspected bug 2). With $I = \sum I_j$ (CZI) and $I' = \sum I'_j$ (CZQIW), a Newton step solves $\Omega\, I(\Omega) = J$:

$$\Delta\Omega = \frac{J - \Omega I}{I + \Omega I'} ,$$

repeated until $|J - \Omega I|/J \le$ TOLERI, or 20 times (lines 68–95↗). Finally each shell's specific angular momentum is reset to $j_j = I_j\,\Omega / m_j$ (lines 97–99↗).

Question 1 (lines 88–95↗). No warning after 20 steps. When the Newton iteration has not converged after 20 steps, SOLID keeps the last guess silently. Should it report this?

Disk locking. For the surface convection zone (JEND = M, more than one shell), when LDISK is set and the age SAGE is at most TDISK, $\Omega$ is fixed to PDISK with no iteration, and the angular momentum follows from it ("MHP 9/94," lines 58–67↗).

PDISK is an angular rate. Under disk locking, WGUESS = PDISK (line 66↗), which is used as $\Omega$, and NMLPAR_MOD's initial value 7.2722D-6 (nmlpar_mod.f, line 210↗) is $2\pi/(10\ \mathrm{d})$ in rad/s, a rate for a period of 10 days. The comment calls it the "PERIOD AT WHICH LOCKING IS SET" (lines 29–30↗), and the Physics namelist page gives the unit rad/s but calls it a "Disk rotation period," so both could say "rate."

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options (Initializing rotation): LDISK enforces disk locking until the age TDISK (Gyr; $10^{-4}$, i.e. $10^5$ yr, is the deuterium birthline, and $10^{-4}$–$10^{-2}$ the recommended range), at the disk's rate PDISK. The documentation calls PDISK the "disk rotation period (rad/s)": it is an angular velocity, used as such here; the default 7.2722D-6 is a 10-day period, while the documentation suggests 8 days (9.0902D-6), the median for disked solar-mass stars in star-forming regions.

1. (never occurred) A zone without angular momentum divides by zero.

(lines 49–57↗; lines 86–88↗) The convergence test DABS(DELJ/CZJ) divides by the zone's total angular momentum CZJ, which is zero for shells that do not rotate (HJM = 0). What follows: In the development build, which traps invalid operations and division by zero (-ffpe-trap=invalid,zero,overflow in src/Makefile), the run stops; in the release build the test gives NaN (when DELJ is 0 too, and the test is false) or infinity (the iteration then drives $\Omega$ towards 0 for its 20 steps). Only with rotation (LROT, off by default), for a zone with $j = 0$.

2. (harmless in the runs) QIW in the Newton step.

(line 91↗; momi.f, lines 84–86↗; intmom.f, lines 28–31↗) The step DELW = DELJ/(CZI + WGUESS*CZQIW) treats QIW as $dI/d\Omega$, as the comments say (line 78↗; momi.f, line 50↗), but MOMI returns $A\,dI/dA = \tfrac12\,\Omega\,dI/d\Omega$ (since $A \propto \Omega^2$), so the denominator adds a term with units of $I$ s$^{-1}$; Newton's step for $\Omega I(\Omega) = J$ needs $I + \Omega\,dI/d\Omega = I + 2\,$QIW. What follows: The result is unchanged when the loop converges, since the test is on $J$; the missing term is of order $A$ relative to $I$, so the iteration, in effect a fixed-point one, still converges almost as fast as Newton's (its error shrinks by about $2\,$QIW$/I \approx 0.18(\eta_2+5)A$ per step, from INTMOM's leading term) for any $A$ in the method's range, but may need more of its 20 steps for a fast rotator. Only with rotation (LROT, off by default) and WALPCZ = 0 (the default; otherwise MOMI sets QIW to 0).
