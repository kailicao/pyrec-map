Explanation

Purpose. SOLID makes a range of shells, JSTART to JEND, rotate as a solid body while conserving their total angular momentum. Given each shell's angular momentum per unit mass, it returns the common rotation rate $\Omega$ and the shells' moments of inertia. Its comment adds that it also gives $r_0$ and $\eta_2$ (R0, ETA2), which FPFT needs.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: arguments only: the shells' masses (HS2, probably in grams, since the angular momentum is the sum of HS2*HJM), specific angular momenta HJM, the previous rates OMEGA, and the structure (HD, HR, HS), passed on to the shape routines.
  • Who calls it: the rotation routines that impose solid-body rotation on a zone: CHECKJ, GETROT, MIDMOD, SECULR and WCZIMP (for convection zones, by its name; WCZ, removed since rp_1, was another).
  • What it calls: SHAPE (the distortion of the shells, ETA2 and R0) and MOMI (each shell's moment of inertia HI and $dI/d\Omega$, QIW).
  • Outputs: OMEGA, HI, QIW, ETA2, R0 and an updated HJM, all through the arguments.
  • Modules: ERRMOM_MOD (the tolerance TOLERI, used only here and in CHECKJ; no routine or initializer gives it a value: CHECKJ saves it (TOLER0 = TOLERI), relaxes it to MAX(DJOK*1.0D-2,TOLER0) around its call to GETROT, and restores it); DISK_MOD (the age SAGE, shared with GETW, SECULR, MDOT and others); NMLPAR_MOD (the disk-locking parameters: the locking time TDISK, the locked rate PDISK and the switch LDISK, namelist parameters read by PARMIN); DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method. Let $m_j$ be HS2(J) and $j_j$ be HJM(J). The target is the zone's total angular momentum,

$$J = \sum_{j=J_\mathrm{start}}^{J_\mathrm{end}} m_j\, j_j .$$

The first guess for $\Omega$ is the mean of the previous rates. With every shell at $\Omega$, SHAPE and MOMI give the moments of inertia $I_j(\Omega)$ and $I'_j = dI_j/d\Omega$. With $I = \sum I_j$ (CZI) and $I' = \sum I'_j$ (CZQIW), a Newton step solves $\Omega\, I(\Omega) = J$:

$$\Delta\Omega = \frac{J - \Omega I}{I + \Omega I'} ,$$

repeated until $|J - \Omega I|/J \le$ TOLERI, or 20 times. Finally each shell's specific angular momentum is reset to $j_j = I_j\,\Omega / m_j$.

Disk locking. For the surface convection zone (JEND = M, more than one shell), when LDISK is set and the age SAGE is at most TDISK, $\Omega$ is fixed to PDISK with no iteration, and the angular momentum follows from it ("MHP 9/94").

KC's changes. The call to MOMI drops the arguments HD and M (2025-05-31), matching MOMI's new argument list.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (/ERRMOM/ to ERRMOM_MOD; /DISK/ split between DISK_MOD (SAGE) and NMLPAR_MOD (TDISK, PDISK, LDISK); the unused /LUOUT/ dropped), the array size JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE. One caller, WCZ, was removed with its file.

Questions for the experts

  1. The tolerance TOLERI. Nothing initializes it: CHECKJ only relaxes it around its call to GETROT and then restores the old value. Outside those calls TOLERI is zero (where the compiler zero-initializes module variables; ERRMOM_MOD gives it no initial value), so the test DABS(DELJ/CZJ).GT.TOLERI always holds and the Newton loop always runs its 20 steps. Is a tolerance missing from the setup?
  2. No warning after 20 steps. When the Newton iteration has not converged after 20 steps, SOLID keeps the last guess silently. Should it report this?
  3. PDISK as a rate. Under disk locking, WGUESS = PDISK, which is used as $\Omega$. NMLPAR_MOD gives PDISK the initial value 7.2722D-6, which is $2\pi/(10\ \mathrm{d})$ in rad/s: an angular rate, for a period of 10 days. The comment calls it the "period at which locking is set". Should the comment say "rate", and do users who set it in the namelist know it is a rate?
  4. A zone of zero angular momentum. The test DABS(DELJ/CZJ) divides by the total angular momentum, which is zero for a non-rotating zone.

Review (round 1)
  1. Wrong. "ERRMOM (the tolerance TOLERI, shared only with CHECKJ, which sets it)". CHECKJ never gives TOLERI a value of its own: it saves it (TOLER0 = TOLERI), relaxes it to MAX(DJOK*1.0D-2,TOLER0) around its call to GETROT, and restores it. No routine, DATA or BLOCK DATA initializes it. Fix: say so, and rewrite question 1: outside CHECKJ's relaxed calls TOLERI is zero (where the compiler zero-initializes COMMON), so the Newton loop always runs its 20 steps.
  2. Missing. Question 3 (PDISK as a rate): PARMIN's DATA gives PDISK = 7.2722D-6, which is $2\pi/(10\ \mathrm{d})$ in rad/s, so it is an angular rate, and the comment's "period" is the misleading part. Fix: reword the question with this fact.
