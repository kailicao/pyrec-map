Purpose

This file drives the solar calibration: after each solar model, it checks whether the model has the solar luminosity and radius (and, optionally, the solar surface $Z/X$) at the solar age; if not, it computes new guesses for the hydrogen abundance $X$ and the mixing length $\alpha$ (and $Z$) for the next model, by a Newton step.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments BL ($\log L/L_\odot$ of the model), RL ($\log R/R_\odot$, computed by the caller from $L$ and $T_\mathrm{eff}$), N (the current kind card) and SOLZX (the model's surface $Z/X$); the tolerances TOLL, TOLR, TOLZ, the switch LCALSOLZX and the target CALSOLZX in NMLPAR_MOD, set in PARMIN and SETCAL.

  • Who calls it: the main program (main.f), when solar calibration is on (LCALS), at every third kind card (MOD(NK,3).EQ.0, main.f, lines 1147–1152↗): "solar models in 3 kind cards" (MHP 5/96).

    The header predates the three-card cycle. It says "ONLY CALLED FOR EVEN NK, ASSUMES RESCALING ON ODD NK AND EVOLVING ON EVEN NK" (lines 22–23↗), but main.f calls it when NK is a multiple of 3 (main.f, line 1147↗), which matches the routine's use of N-2 and N+1..N+3 (lines 93–97↗; lines 153–163↗).

  • Outputs: the arguments LSOL and the derivatives DLDX, DRDX, DLDA, DRDA, which the main program keeps (they were in /CALSUN/, also used by WRTMONTE, removed since rp_1); the new $X$ (RESCAL(2,N+1), and XENV0A for cards $N+1$ to $N+3$), $\alpha$ (CMIXLA, cards $N+1$ to $N+3$) and $Z$ (RESCAL(3,N+1), ZENV0A) in RUNLOOP_MOD (RESCAL) and NMLPAR_MOD (the others); lines to unit IOWR.

  • Kept between calls: the previous model's BLP, RLP and the steps DX, DA, now local variables kept by SAVE.

The method

  1. Converged if $|\log L/L_\odot| <$ TOLL and $|\log R/R_\odot| <$ TOLR and, with the $Z/X$ option, $|\log_{10}(Z/X) - \log_{10}(Z/X)_\odot| <$ TOLZ: set LSOL and return. The target is thus exactly $L_\odot$ and $R_\odot$. These are the code's CLSUN and CRSUN (in CONSTANTS_MOD, settable in the PHYSICS namelist), $3.8515\times10^{33}$ erg s⁻¹ and $6.9598\times10^{10}$ cm by default; BL and RL are measured in them (how these compare with other adopted solar values is the question below).

    Question 1 (line 59↗; constants_mod.f, lines 44–49↗). The solar values targeted. Convergence means $|\log L/L_\odot| <$ TOLL with the default $L_\odot$ = CLSUN = $3.8515\times10^{33}$ erg s⁻¹ and $R_\odot$ = CRSUN = $6.9598\times10^{10}$ cm, while the IAU 2015 nominal values are $3.828\times10^{33}$ erg s⁻¹ and $6.957\times10^{10}$ cm. With TOLL = $10^{-5}$, the 0.6% difference in $L_\odot$ ($0.0027$ in $\log L$) is far larger than the tolerance, so the calibrated $X$ and $\alpha$ depend on which value is used. Are the defaults kept for continuity with published YREC solar models, and should the documentation of TOLL, CLSUN and CRSUN say that a calibration matches whatever $L_\odot$, $R_\odot$ the namelist sets?

    Question 2 (lines 59–72↗). $Z/X$ not converged. If $L$ and $R$ are within tolerance but $Z/X$ is not, the routine falls through to the Newton step and also changes $X$ and $\alpha$ slightly. Is this intended? (A target CALSOLZX of 0 is bug 2.)

  2. Newton step with fixed partial derivatives ("empirical," LLP 6/28/09): $\partial\log L/\partial X = -3.78$, $\partial\log R/\partial X = -0.89$, $\partial\log L/\partial\alpha = 0.0139$, $\partial\log R/\partial\alpha = -0.050$. It solves $$\log L + L_X\,\Delta X + L_\alpha\,\Delta\alpha = 0, \qquad \log R + R_X\,\Delta X + R_\alpha\,\Delta\alpha = 0,$$ that is $$\Delta\alpha = \frac{\log L\,R_X/L_X - \log R}{R_\alpha - L_\alpha R_X/L_X}, \qquad \Delta X = -\frac{\log L + L_\alpha\,\Delta\alpha}{L_X}.$$

    Question 3 (lines 74–84↗; main.f, line 1163↗). Fixed derivatives. Why fixed "empirical" derivatives rather than secants from the previous cycles (BLP, RLP are already saved)? A user changing the physics (another opacity, say) would want to know whether convergence slows, or fails within the 15 cycles main.f allows, when the true derivatives differ from these.

  3. The new values go to kind cards $N+1$ to $N+3$, starting from those of card $N-2$ (the start of the current cycle). With the $Z/X$ option, $Z$ is scaled: $Z_\mathrm{new} = Z_\mathrm{old}\,(Z/X)_\odot / (Z/X)_\mathrm{model}$.

Design

An older method, which measured the derivatives with extra runs at kind cards 3, 6 and 9, is still in the file but skipped by GOTO 1234 (lines 85–146↗; unused?; on that path DX and DA are never set, their assignments being commented out, line 91↗, line 110↗). The local ZLP is set (line 181↗) and never read (unused?).

From YREC's documentation

Control Options (Calibration): TOLL, TOLR are the tolerances of the solar calibration in $L$ and $R$; LCALSOLZX enforces the mixture's $Z/X$, CALSOLZX (0.02292 for GS98), and without it $Z$ is held constant in the calibration loop.

(lines 167–170↗; lines 179–180↗) BLP and RLP are saved locals with no DATA and are set only at the end of the Newton step, so on the first call (N = 3) the lines "New BL, Old BL, Delta BL" and "New RL, Old RL, Delta RL" print whatever they hold before any assignment (zero under most compilers, not promised by the standard).
