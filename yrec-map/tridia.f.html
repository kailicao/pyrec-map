Purpose

TRIDIA solves the tridiagonal system of the implicit angular-momentum diffusion step (the Thomas algorithm), and returns the change of angular momentum at each point.

How it fits

  • Inputs: TRIDI_MOD, filled by DCOEFT just before the call: the three diagonals A, B, C, the right-hand side D (by DCOEFT's comment, the angular velocities at the start of the time step, the outermost one already reduced by the wind), and GAMA(N), the wind term at the surface. GAMA is also the elimination's work array (loop 1 sets GAMA(2..N), line 29↗), so TRIDIA reads the wind term first (line 22↗). The argument EI holds the points' moments of inertia (by SECULR's use, probably).
  • Who calls it: only SECULR.
  • Outputs: U in TRIDI_MOD, the angular velocities at the end of the step; DJ, the change of angular momentum at each point; and SUMDJ, their sum. SECULR passes DJ to EQ2MOD to return to the model's grid.
  • Modules: TRIDI_MOD (formerly COMMON/TRIDI/), shared with DCOEFT, CCOEFT, CTRIDI, GETQUA and MIXCOM.

The method

Forward elimination and back substitution for $a_j u_{j-1} + b_j u_j + c_j u_{j+1} = d_j$; then $$\Delta J_j = (\Omega_j^\mathrm{new} - \Omega_j^\mathrm{old})\,I_j,$$ with, at the surface, the wind term added: $\Delta J_N = \gamma_N + (\Omega_N^\mathrm{new} - \Omega_N^\mathrm{old})\,I_N$. A zero pivot stops the run ("#TRIDIA:SINGULAR MATRIX").

Question 1 (lines 36–37↗). A no-op. FJ = 1 + (U(N)-U0(N))/U0(N) and U(N) = U0(N)*FJ give back U(N) unchanged (up to rounding; and a NaN if U0(N) = 0, a surface that does not rotate). Was something else meant, such as a scaling of U(N) that conserves angular momentum?
