Explanation

Purpose. TRIDIA solves the tridiagonal system of the implicit angular-momentum diffusion step (the Thomas algorithm), and returns the change of angular momentum at each point.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: TRIDI_MOD, filled by DCOEFT just before the call: the three diagonals A, B, C, the right-hand side D (by DCOEFT's comment, the angular velocities at the start of the time step, the outermost one already reduced by the wind), and GAMA(N), the wind term at the surface. The argument EI holds the points' moments of inertia (by SECULR's use, probably).
  • Who calls it: only SECULR.
  • Outputs: U in TRIDI_MOD, the angular velocities at the end of the step; DJ, the change of angular momentum at each point; and SUMDJ, their sum. SECULR passes DJ to EQ2MOD to return to the model's grid.
  • Modules: TRIDI_MOD (formerly COMMON/TRIDI/), shared with DCOEFT, CCOEFT, CTRIDI, GETQUA and MIXCOM.

The method. Forward elimination and back substitution for $a_j u_{j-1} + b_j u_j + c_j u_{j+1} = d_j$; then $$\Delta J_j = (\Omega_j^\mathrm{new} - \Omega_j^\mathrm{old})\,I_j,$$ with, at the surface, the wind term added: $\Delta J_N = \gamma_N + (\Omega_N^\mathrm{new} - \Omega_N^\mathrm{old})\,I_N$. A zero pivot stops the run ("#TRIDIA:SINGULAR MATRIX").

KC's changes. The unused argument EJ was removed (2025-05-31).

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON/TRIDI/ to TRIDI_MOD, JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. A no-op. FJ = 1 + (U(N)-U0(N))/U0(N) and U(N) = U0(N)*FJ give back U(N) unchanged (up to rounding). Was something else meant?
  2. The wind term through GAMA. The surface's wind loss reaches TRIDIA in GAMA(N), the same array the elimination uses for its work, so it must be read first (as it is). A separate variable would be clearer.
