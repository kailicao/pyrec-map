Explanation

Purpose. DIFAD_MOD holds the advective and diffusive coefficients of angular momentum transport on the even grid, used when transport is treated as diffusion plus advection (LDIFAD), shared by 4 routines. It replaces COMMON/DIFAD/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.

  • ECOD3(JSON), ECOD4(JSON): the advective and diffusive coefficients. ROTGRID interpolates them onto the even grid from VESA, VESD (DIFAD2_MOD), adds the composition diffusion coefficient to ECOD4 and rescales both; DADCOEFT recomputes them in its iterations and builds its band matrix from them; DCOEFT reads them; CHECKJ prints them.

Design. A blanket USE DIFAD_MOD.

Changed since rp_1. New file: COMMON/DIFAD/ as a module.
