Purpose

KSPLINE computes the second derivatives of a natural cubic spline through exactly 4 points (the size NM = 4 is fixed), as in Numerical Recipes' SPLINE. KSPLINT evaluates the spline.

How it fits

Called by the surface-pressure routines KCSURFP and SURFP, the SCV equation of state EQSCVE, and TAUINTNEW. No COMMON blocks, no calls.

The method

The tridiagonal system for the second derivatives $y''_i$ of a natural spline ($y''_1 = y''_4 = 0$), solved by forward elimination and back-substitution.
