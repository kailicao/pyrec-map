Explanation

Purpose. INTERP returns the weights of 4-point Lagrange interpolation, and of its first derivative, at a point $x$.

How it fits. Called by EQSCVE and EQSCVG (the SCV equation of state), GETALEX06 and GETOPAL95 (opacity tables; YALO3D is removed since rp_1), and MICRODIFF_MTE and MODEL_TO_EQUAL (regridding for diffusion). No modules, no calls. The version without derivatives is INTRP2; the 3-point one is INTER3.

The method. For nodes $a_1,\dots,a_4$,

$$B_i(x) = \prod_{j\ne i}\frac{x-a_j}{a_i-a_j},\qquad C_i(x) = \frac{dB_i}{dx},$$

returned in B and C; then $f(x)\approx\sum_i B_i f(a_i)$ and $f'(x)\approx\sum_i C_i f(a_i)$.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: IMPLICIT NONE. One caller, YALO3D, is removed with the obsolete opacity options.
