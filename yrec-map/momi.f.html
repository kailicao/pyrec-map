Purpose

Computes the moment of inertia HI of each shell of a rotating, distorted model, and its derivative with respect to $\ln A$, $A$ being the distortion parameter below, QIW $= dI/d\ln A$, which at fixed shape ($\eta_2$, $r_0$) is $\tfrac12\,\Omega\,\partial I/\partial\Omega$ (since $A \propto \Omega^2$; the comments name it a derivative in $\Omega$, line 50↗, and SOLID uses it as one, a suspected bug: solid.f, suspected bug 2). The comment: "Moment of inertia is calculated by the method used in Wai-Yuen Law's thesis (Yale, 1980) p.61," and "MOMI assumes that SHAPE has already been called."

How it fits

  • Inputs: the shape parameters ETA2 (Radau's $\eta_2$) and R0 (the characteristic radius $r_0$ of the equipotential, $r = r_0(1 - AP_2)$) per shell, from SHAPE; $\log r$ (HR), $\log m$ (HS), the shell masses HS2, the angular velocities OMEGA, and the shell range JSTART..JEND.
  • Who calls it: HPOINT, SOLID and STARIN.
  • What it calls: INTMOM, which evaluates the integral at each shell.
  • Outputs: HI and QIW per shell.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (WALPCZ), CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN, CC13, CC23, and $\log G$, CGL), DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON). The unused /CCOUT/ and /LUOUT/ of rp_1 are no longer declared.

The method

  • Spherical shortcut. If WALPCZ $\ne 0$ (WALPCZ is the exponent of the rotation law $\Omega\, r^{\mathrm{WALPCZ}} =$ constant in the surface convection zone, as GETNEWENV's comment says; PARMIN keeps it between −2 and 0, so 0 is solid-body rotation), each shell is a thin spherical shell, $I = \tfrac23\,\Delta m\, r^2$, with $dI/d\Omega = 0$; the central shell is a sphere, $I = 0.4\,\Delta m\, r^2$, at the mean of the first two $\log r$ (lines 62–72↗).

    Question 1 (lines 62–72↗). WALPCZ as a switch. The spherical shortcut is taken for every shell in JSTART..JEND, not only those in the convection zone, whenever the convection zone's rotation law is not solid-body (WALPCZ $\ne 0$). Why should that law switch off the distortion of all shells? - Distorted shells. Otherwise, for each shell, with $r_0$ = R0, the parameter $$A = \frac{5}{3}\,\frac{\Omega^2\, r_0^3}{G\,m\,(2 + \eta_2)}$$ (CON $=5/(3G)$), the ratio R0R $= r_0^5/r^3$, and QR0R $= d\ln r_0/d\ln r$ set to 1, go to INTMOM, which returns $I$ and QIWM per unit mass; these are multiplied by HS2. INTMOM sums a series to $A^5$ (Law 1980's coefficients $c_j$): $I = H_1\bigl(1 + 0.15\sum_j c_j(j\eta_2+5)A^j\bigr)$ and QIWM $= H_1\cdot0.15\sum_j j\,c_j(j\eta_2+5)A^j = A\,dI/dA$ at fixed $\eta_2$ and $r_0$ (lines 73–89↗). R0P and RPHIP (the previous shell's $\ln r_0$ and $\ln r$, lines 54–60↗, lines 87–88↗) and H0 (the thin-shell moment of inertia, line 77↗) are computed and never read (unused?), and QR0R is fixed at 1 (line 76↗).

    Question 2 (lines 76–86↗; intmom.f, lines 26–31↗). A series to $A^5$ on a first-order shape. INTMOM expands $I$ to $A^5$, while SHAPE keeps the equipotentials to first order in $\Omega^2$ (only the $P_2$ term). Is the expansion the exact moment of a body $r = r_0(1-AP_2)$, kept for accuracy at large $A$, or should it be truncated consistently with the shape?
