Purpose

WCZIMP sets the rotation of a convection zone (shells ISTART–IEND) at fixed total angular momentum, as wcz.f (removed since rp_1) did, and adds options to impose a rotation profile on the radiative interior below the surface convection zone (JNT, 2015/05/14, copied into this code 2025/09/03–04). IMPJMOD ("impose J model", PARMIN's comment) chooses: 0, no effect; 1, solid-body rotation; 2, a solid-body core decoupled from the surface convection zone; 3, a solid-body core coupled to it.

How it fits

  • Who calls it: GETROT and GETW (in place of WCZ since 09/25).
  • What it calls: SOLID, for the zone, single shells, or the interior.
  • Inputs and outputs: the arguments, as in solid.f: HJM, OMEGA, HI, QIW, ETA2, R0 are updated.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist parameters LSOLID, IMPJMOD and WALPCZ, read by PARMIN; formerly the COMMON blocks SBROT and ROT), CONSTANTS_MOD (CLN; formerly CONST1) and DIMENSIONS_MOD (JSON).

The method

With $m_j$ = HS2, $j_j$ = HJM, $I_j$ = HI, $\alpha$ = WALPCZ, $r_j = 10^{\texttt{HR}(j)}$:

  • Solid body for the zone if $\alpha \ge 0$, LSOLID, IMPJMOD = 1, or the zone is not the surface one (IEND < M). Since this test comes first, IMPJMOD = 2 and 3 take effect only with $\alpha < 0$; with the default WALPCZ = 0 they act as solid body (line 48↗).

    Question 2 (lines 47–51↗). IMPJMOD and $\alpha \ge 0$. Since the solid-body test comes first, IMPJMOD = 2 and 3 have no effect unless WALPCZ < 0, and with the default WALPCZ = 0 the interior is not coupled or decoupled as PARMIN's comment describes. Is that intended, or should IMPJMOD be tested first?

  • IMPJMOD = 2: the zone follows $\Omega_j = \Omega_0 r_j^{\alpha}$ with $\Omega_0 = \sum_\mathrm{cz} m_j j_j / \sum_\mathrm{cz} r_j^{\alpha} I_j$; the interior (shells 1 to ISTART-1) is then made a solid body on its own, keeping its own angular momentum.

  • IMPJMOD = 3: the interior and the zone share one angular momentum budget; the interior rotates at the rate of the zone's base: $$\Omega_0 = \frac{\sum_{j \le N} m_j j_j}{r_\mathrm{s}^{\alpha}\sum_{j \le \mathrm{s}} I_j + \sum_{\mathrm{s} < j \le N} r_j^{\alpha} I_j},$$ with s = ISTART and $N$ = IEND; then $\Omega_j = \Omega_0 r_j^{\alpha}$ in the zone, $\Omega_j = \Omega_\mathrm{s}$ below it (lines 80–111↗).

    Question 3 (lines 56–61↗; lines 81–92↗). The interior's moments of inertia. In IMPJMOD = 2 and 3, the $I_j$ of the budget (the zone's, and with IMPJMOD = 3 the interior's too) are those in HI on entry (from the previous rotation), and only the SOLID calls that follow the budget update them. Is one pass enough, or should the budget be iterated?

    Question 4 (lines 104–110↗). IMPJMOD = 3 and SOLID. After the interior is set to $\Omega_\mathrm{s}$, SOLID is called on shells 1 to ISTART-1, which conserves their total angular momentum and may change $\Omega$ again. Does the interior stay at the base's rate?

  • Constant specific angular momentum if $\alpha \le -2$ (and IMPJMOD = 0), as in WCZ.

  • Otherwise the power law for the zone alone, as in WCZ. After each assignment, SOLID is called on single shells to make $\Omega$, $I$ and $j$ consistent.

From YREC's documentation

Physics Options: IMPJMOD sets the global coupling of angular momentum (0 no effect, 1 solid body everywhere, 2 solid-body core with a decoupled convection zone, 3 solid-body core coupled at the zone's base), and WALPCZ the profile in the convection zone, $\Omega \propto r^{\mathrm{WALPCZ}}$ (0 solid body, −2 uniform specific angular momentum).
