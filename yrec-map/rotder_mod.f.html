Purpose

ROTDER_MOD passes three quantities at each shell from the structure equations to the setup of rotational mixing. Shared by 2 routines. It replaces COMMON/ROTDER/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), less its arrays SQOD and SQED, which COEFFT stored and no routine read; JSON comes from DIMENSIONS_MOD.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All three are stored by COEFFT at each shell when LROT is set, and read by SETUPV for the circulation's coefficients.

  • SQOT(JSON): the opacity's $\partial\ln\kappa/\partial\ln T$ (QOT); SETUPV forms $\partial\ln\chi/\partial\ln T = 3 - \partial\ln\kappa/\partial\ln T$ and its radial derivative.
  • SQET(JSON): the energy generation's $\partial\ln\epsilon/\partial\ln T$ (QET).
  • FRACNUC(JSON): the share of the energy generation that is nuclear, $(\epsilon_\mathrm{tot} - \epsilon_\nu)/\epsilon_\mathrm{tot}$ from SESUM and SEG(6), SEG(7) (SCRTCH_MOD), or 0 when $\epsilon_\mathrm{tot} \le 0$.

Design

A blanket USE ROTDER_MOD in each routine.
