## Explanation

**Purpose.** `SCRTCH_MOD` holds the local physics of the interior at each shell, as computed in the last pass of the structure equations: energy generation, opacity, temperature gradients, ionization, convective velocity. Many routines read it later in the step (mixing, burning, regridding, output). Shared by 16 routines. It replaces `COMMON/SCRTCH/` (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); `JSON` comes from `DIMENSIONS_MOD`.

**What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.** [`COEFFT`](coefft.f.md#coefft) stores all of them at each shell `IM`; [`PHYSIC`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/physic.f#L3-L191) stores `SO`, `SDEL`, and `SVEL` again when it recomputes the physics.
- `SESUM(JSON)`: the sum of the nuclear energy generation's five terms `EG(1)`…`EG(5)` (0 below the cutoff temperature `TCUT(1)`); `SEG(7,JSON)`: those five as fractions of `SESUM`, then the neutrino term `EG(6)` and the gravitational term `HHC` (`EGRAV`) (`SEG(6)`, `SEG(7)`). Read by [`HPOINT`](hpoint.f.md#hpoint) (which interpolates them, through [`OSPLIN`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/osplin.f#L12-L50), to the grid at the start of the step), [`MIDMOD`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/midmod.f#L2-L523), [`WRTMIL`](wrtmil.f.md#wrtmil), the setup of rotation (`FRACNUC` in `ROTDER_MOD`), and the output ([`PUTSTORE`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/putstore.f#L13-L360), [`STITCH`](stitch.f.md#stitch), [`WRTMOD`](wrtmod.f.md#wrtmod)).
- `SO(JSON)`: the opacity. Read by `HPOINT`, `MIDMOD`, [`VISCOS`](viscos.f.md#viscos), and the output (`PUTSTORE`, `STITCH`, `WRTMOD`, [`WRTOUT`](wrtout.f.md#wrtout)).
- `SDEL(3,JSON)`: $`\nabla_\mathrm{rad}`$, $`\nabla`$, $`\nabla_\mathrm{ad}`$. [`ROTMIX`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotmix.f#L5-L251) replaces `SDEL(2,·)` for a while and restores it. Read by the burning and mixing routines ([`LIBURN`](liburn.f.md#liburn), [`LIBURN2`](liburn2.f.md#liburn2), [`MICRODIFF_MTE`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/microdiff_mte.f#L4-L294), [`SETUP_GRSETT`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setup_grsett.f#L2-L330), `ROTMIX`), [`GETTAU`](gettau.f.md#gettau), `HPOINT`, `MIDMOD`, `PHYSIC`, and the output.
- `SVEL(JSON)`: the convective velocity (cm/s); read by `GETTAU`, `HPOINT`, `MIDMOD`, and the output.
- `SBETA(JSON)`, `SETA(JSON)`, `SFXION(3,JSON)`, `SCP(JSON)`: $`\beta`$ (gas over total pressure), the degeneracy parameter $`\eta`$, the three ionization fractions, and $`c_P`$, for the stored model (`PUTSTORE`, `STITCH`).
- `LOCONS(JSON)`: meant to say whether conduction dominates the opacity; set by `COEFFT` from its `LOCOND`, which it always sets to true ([coefft.f, line 161](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/coefft.f#L161); [coefft.f, line 444](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/coefft.f#L444)), and read by no routine (unused?).

**Design.** A blanket `USE SCRTCH_MOD` in each routine.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** New file: `COMMON/SCRTCH/` as a module.
