## Explanation

**Purpose.** Prepares, once per call of the rotational-mixing step, the structural factors that the stability criteria and circulation velocities need at each interface between shells. Its comment explains the scheme: stability is computed for the interface $`i/i-1`$ and stored in element $`i`$; each criterion is "a combination of variables stored at each shell multiplied by a gradient between the shells," which must stay below a critical number. `SETUPV` computes the parts that "do not change during a diffusion timestep"; [`VCIRC`](vcirc.f.md#vcirc) multiplies them by the current $`\Omega`$ gradients.

**How it fits.**
- **Who calls it:** [`SECULR`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/seculr.f#L6-L532), once per time step, before the iterations.
- **Inputs:** the model's $`\log\rho`$, $`g`$ (`HG`), $`L`$ (solar units), $`\log P`$, $`\log r`$, enclosed mass ($`\mathrm{g}`$), $`\log T`$, and the range `IMIN`–`IMAX`; from `MDPHY_MOD` the shell values of $`\mu`$ (`AMUM`), $`c_P`$ (`CPM`), $`\nabla`$, $`\nabla_\mathrm{ad}`$, $`\nabla_\mathrm{rad}`$ (`DELM`, `DELAM`, `DELRM`), $`\epsilon`$ (`ESUMM`), $`\kappa`$ (`OM`), $`\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T`$ (`QDTM`); from `ROTDER_MOD` the derivatives of $`\kappa`$ and $`\epsilon`$ (`SQOT`, `SQET`) and the nuclear fraction `FRACNUC`, set in [`COEFFT`](coefft.f.md#coefft); the namelist switch `LDIFAD` from `NMLPAR_MOD`.
- **Outputs:** `HRU` (radii), `QWRMAX` (the largest stable $`d\Omega/d\ln r`$ for the dynamical shear), the interpolation weights `FACI` (an argument owned by `SECULR`, which passes it on to `VCIRC`), and, through modules, read by `VCIRC`, [`CODIFF`](codiff.f.md#codiff), [`GETFC`](getfc.f.md#getfc) and [`DADCOEFT`](dadcoeft.f.md#dadcoeft): the interface values in `INTVAR_MOD` (`RM`, `PM`, `TM`, `DM`, `DELMI`, `DELAMI`, `QDTMI`, `HS3`, `EPSILM`, `ALM`, `HGM`), the velocity factors `FACT1`…`FACT5`, `FACT7`, `FACT8` (`VFACT_MOD`), `FACT6`, `FES1`–`FES3`, `FGSFJ`, `FGSFK` (`TAUKH_MOD`), `DDELM`, `FESTIME`, `THM` (`OLDROT2_MOD`), and with `LDIFAD` the higher-order terms in `DIFAD3_MOD` and `EGRIDDER_MOD`.

**The method.**
1. **Interface values.** At the midpoint $`r_{m,i} = \tfrac12(r_i + r_{i-1})`$, each quantity is found by 4-point Lagrange interpolation in $`r`$ over shells $`i-2 \dots i+1`$ (the first and last interfaces use the first or last four shells); $`P`$, $`T`$, $`\rho`$ are interpolated in the log. The actual gradient is $`\nabla = \min(\nabla_\mathrm{rad}, \nabla_\mathrm{ad})`$.
2. **Scales.** With $`G`$, $`m`$ the mass inside the interface, $`H_p = P r^2/(\rho G m)`$, $`H_T = H_p/\nabla`$, $`\eta = \tfrac43\,r^3/(G m)`$, and $`\Delta\nabla = \max(\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla,\ \nabla_\mathrm{min})`$ with a floor of $`10^{-3}`$ (larger at the ends of the range, including a $`\mu`$-gradient term).

    > **Question** ([line 340](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L340)). **The definition of `ETA`.** `ETA = 2.0D0*CC23*RM(I)**3/CG/HS3(I)`, that is $`2\cdot\tfrac23\,r^3/(G m)`$. Is the factor 2 part of the physics or a leftover (the comment block gives no formula)?

    > **Question** ([lines 320–335](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L320-L335); [lines 358–361](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L358-L361); [line 392](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L392)). **Two floors for $`\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla`$.** `FACT1` and `QWRMAX` use a floor of $`10^{-3}`$; `FESTIME` and `FGSFK` use `DDEL` with the end-dependent floor `DMIN`; `DDELM` adds the $`\mu`$ gradient. The comment says $`1/(\nabla_\mathrm{ad} - \nabla)`$ "is set to a maximum of 10^6," but the floor $`10^{-3}`$ gives a maximum of $`10^3`$. Which is intended?

3. **Eddington–Sweet circulation** (Endal and Sofia, paper II, as cited): $`v_\mathrm{ES} =`$ `FACT1`$`\cdot`$`FACT2`$`\cdot\Omega^2`$, with
   
   $$\displaystyle \texttt{FACT1} = \frac{1}{(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T)\\,\max(\nabla\_\mathrm{ad}-\nabla,\\,10^{-3})},\qquad \texttt{FACT2} = \frac{\nabla\_\mathrm{ad}}{g}\\,\eta\\,\Bigl(\epsilon - \frac{L}{m}\Bigr).$$
4. **GSF and $`\mu`$ currents.** `FACT3` $`= |H_T r|`$ for the Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke velocity; `FACT4` $`= \frac{r^2}{G m\rho}\,(P - aT^4/3)/\sqrt g`$ for the "fictitious $`\mu`$ currents" (Kippenhahn, IAU 66).
5. **Dynamical shear.** Stable when the Richardson number $`\frac{\rho}{P}(\nabla_\mathrm{ad}-\nabla)\,g^2/(d\Omega/d\ln r)^2 \gt{} \tfrac14`$, that is when
   
   $$\displaystyle \left\|\frac{d\Omega}{d\ln r}\right\| \lt{} \texttt{QWRMAX} = 2g\sqrt{\frac{\rho}{P}\max(\nabla\_\mathrm{ad}-\nabla,\\,10^{-3})}.$$
6. **Secular shear with $`\mu`$ gradients:** `FACT5` $`= \frac{\rho g^2}{P\,\Delta\ln P}\cdot\frac{p_r - 1}{1 + 3p_r}`$, where $`p_r = aT^4/(3P)`$ is the radiation-pressure fraction ($`1 - p_r`$ is the comment's "BETA") and the last factor is the comment's $`-d\ln T/d\ln\mu`$.
7. **Zahn-type terms** (`FES1`, `FES2`, `FES3`, `FV*`, and, with `LDIFAD`, `FACT7`, `FACD2`, `FACD3`, following Zahn and Maeder [1998](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1998A%26A...334.1000M) as the comments say), built from $`F = P/(g\,\Delta\nabla\,c_P\,\rho\,T)`$, $`L/m`$ and $`\epsilon`$; and `THM` $`= r_i^2 r_{i-1}^2/(G\sqrt{m_i m_{i-1}}) \approx r^2/g`$ for the $`d\theta/dt`$ term ([lines 304–309](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L304-L309)). With `LDIFAD`, the block starts at shell `IMIN-1` ([lines 408–410](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setupv.f#L408-L410)); `IMIN` is at least 2 ([midmod.f, lines 375–383](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/midmod.f#L375-L383)), so no index 0 is read (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).
