## Explanation

**Purpose.** `ATMOS2_MOD` holds the surface-pressure table read when `KTTAU` is 3 or 4 (used only with 3; see below): $`\log P`$ on a grid of 57 values of $`\log T_\mathrm{eff}`$ and 11 of $`\log g`$. It replaces `COMMON/ATMOS2/` (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26); the sizes `NG`, `NT` are private.

**What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them.** [`SETUPS`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setups.f#L16-L396) reads them all from the file `FATM`: `ATMZ`, the table's metallicity; `ATMTL(NT)`, the $`\log T_\mathrm{eff}`$ grid; `ATMGL(NG)`, the $`\log g`$ grid; `ATMPL(NT,NG)`, $`\log P`$. [`SURFP`](surfp.f.md#surfp) interpolates in them. `ATMZ` is read by no routine; `SETUPS` also stores in it the metallicity of the `KTTAU` = 5 table ([`ATMOS2C_MOD`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/atmos2c_mod.f#L23-L35)).

**Why `KTTAU` = 4 loads it.** With `KTTAU` = 4 (Allard atmospheres, [`ALFILEIN`](alfilein.f.md#alfilein), [`ALSURFP`](alsurfp.f.md#alsurfp)) `SETUPS` reads this Kurucz table too, but no routine then uses it: above the Allard table's top $`T_\mathrm{eff}`$ (`AL_TEFFLmax`, [`ALATM05_MOD`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/alatm05_mod.f#L19-L27)), or off its gravities, [`SURFBC`](surfbc.f.md#surfbc) and [`ENVINT`](envint.f.md#envint) switch to the gray atmosphere (`KTTAU` = 0), not to Kurucz, although `ALSURFP`'s header names Kurucz (`KTTAU` = 3) as a fallback the caller could choose.

> **Question** ([setups.f, lines 251–268](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/setups.f#L251-L268); [surfbc.f, lines 96–108](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/surfbc.f#L96-L108); [envint.f, lines 241–254](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/envint.f#L241-L254); [alsurfp.f, lines 32–34](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/alsurfp.f#L32-L34)). **The Kurucz table with Allard atmospheres.** `SETUPS` reads the Kurucz table `FATM` whenever `KTTAU` is 3 or 4, but with `KTTAU` = 4 nothing reads it: When $`T_\mathrm{eff}`$ leaves the Allard table, `SURFBC` and `ENVINT` fall back to the gray atmosphere. `ALSURFP`'s header, though, says the caller could set `KTTAU` to 3 for a Kurucz atmosphere instead. Is the read a remnant of an older fallback to Kurucz, and should a hot Allard run switch to Kurucz rather than gray? It matters for the boundary condition of stars that heat past the Allard table.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** New file: `COMMON/ATMOS2/` as a module.
