## Explanation

**Purpose.** For the transport of angular momentum and the mixing it drives, maps one radiative region (shells `IBEG` to `IEND`) onto an auxiliary grid of points equally spaced in a mesh coordinate $\chi$, and computes there what the diffusion solver needs: each zone's mass, moment of inertia, angular momentum and angular velocity at the zone centers, and the diffusion coefficients, multiplied by the Jacobian of the change of variables, at the zone edges.

**How it fits.**
- **Who calls it:** [[seculr.f#SECULR|`SECULR`]] (the secular transport of angular momentum and composition), for each region it treats.
- **What it calls:** [[getgrid.f#GETGRID|`GETGRID`]], which defines the coordinate $\chi$ (`CHI` at the model's shells, `ECHI` at the new points, spacing `DCHI`, `NTOT` points, and their masses `ES1`; all in `EGRID_MOD`), and [[osplin.f#OSPLIN|`OSPLIN`]] for every interpolation, through the work arrays of `SPLIN_MOD`.
- **Inputs (arguments):** the model's log density `HD`, log radius `HR` and radius `HRU`, log pressure `HP`, luminosity `HL`, log mass `HS`, masses `HS1` (enclosed) and `HS2` (of each shell), moments of inertia `HI`, specific angular momenta `HJMSAV`, angular velocities `WSAV`, the convection flags `LCZ`, and the diffusion coefficients `COD` (angular momentum) and `COD2` (composition). Also, from modules: the mesh tolerances `HPTTOL`, the switch `LDIFAD` and the factor `FW` (all in `NMLPAR_MOD`, formerly `/CTOL/`, `/VARFC/` and `/VMULT/`), the energy generation `EPSM` (`MDPHY_MOD`), with `LDIFAD` the advective and diffusive velocities `VESA`, `VESD` (`DIFAD2_MOD`), and the constants (`CONSTANTS_MOD`).
- **Outputs:** on the new grid, `EM` (zone masses), `EI` (moments of inertia), `EJ` (angular momenta), `EW` (angular velocities), `ECOD`, `ECOD2` (the two diffusion coefficients with their factors), and with `LDIFAD` `ECOD3`, `ECOD4` in `DIFAD_MOD`; `DR` is set to `DCHI`. `SECULR` solves the diffusion on this grid and maps the result back. `LDUM2` returns true, and nothing else is done, when the region has only one interface (`IEND - IBEG <= 1`).

**The method.**
1. **Zone centers.** $\log r$, $I/(m r^2)$ (`EI0`), $j = J/m$ and $\Omega$ are interpolated from the shells onto the new points. Then, for the interior zones,
   $$\Delta m_k = \tfrac12\,(m_{k+1} - m_{k-1}),\qquad I_k = \frac{I}{m r^2}\Big|_k\,\Delta m_k\,r_k^2,\qquad J_k = j_k\,\Delta m_k .$$
2. **The two end zones** run from the half-way point to the next equally spaced point out to the half-way point to the next original shell (or the center, or the surface, where the mass is $10^{\texttt{HSTOT}}$). If the region borders a convection zone, all of that zone's shells are added to the end zone, so a convection zone moves as one block. The end zones take $r^2$ from the shells `IBEG` and `IEND` (`HRU`, [[#L138]], [[#L165]]) rather than from the interpolated $\log r$; it is the same value, since the first and last grid points lie at those shells (`ECHI(1) = CHI(1)`, and `ECHI(NTOT) = CHI(NTAB)` up to rounding, since here `NTOT` = `NTAB`; [[getgrid.f#L62]], [[getgrid.f#L72-L81]]), where the spline passes through the shells' values (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). The loops that add a convection zone also set `I0 = I + 1` and `I1 = I - 1` ([[#L143]], [[#L170]]), with `I` left over from the zone-center loop rather than the loop variable `II`; `I0` and `I1` are read nowhere (unused?).
3. **Zone edges.** The diffusion coefficients are interpolated at $\chi_k - \tfrac12\Delta\chi$, assumed constant beyond the region's ends. With `LDIFAD`, the advective and diffusive terms $0.2\cdot4\pi f_W\,v$ are interpolated too, and the composition coefficient is added to the diffusive one (following Zahn 1992, as the comment says). These `ECOD3`, `ECOD4` ([[#L213-L238]], [[#L275-L280]]) are unused?: with `LDIFAD`, SECULR calls [[dadcoeft.f#DADCOEFT|DADCOEFT]], which sets `ECOD3` and `ECOD4` afresh at every iteration ([[dadcoeft.f#L268-L270]]) before it reads them, and [[dcoeft.f#DCOEFT|DCOEFT]], which also reads them, runs only without `LDIFAD` ([[checkj.f#CHECKJ|CHECKJ]] prints them after DADCOEFT); so the composition coefficient added here does not reach the solver. The factor 0.2 in `FAC` is the same as DADCOEFT's (a question on that page).
4. **The Jacobian.** The mesh coordinate is defined by the comment as
   $$\chi = \frac{\log m}{\delta_m} + \frac{L}{\delta_L} - \frac{\log P}{\delta_P},$$
   with $\delta_m$ = `HPTTOL(2)`, $\delta_L$ = `HPTTOL(9)` times the surface luminosity, $\delta_P$ = `HPTTOL(11)`, so that
   $$\frac{d\chi}{dr} = \frac{4\pi\rho r^2}{\ln 10\;\delta_m\,m} + \frac{4\pi\rho r^2\,\epsilon}{\delta_L} + \frac{G m \rho}{\ln 10\;\delta_P\,P\,r^2}.$$
   The composition coefficient is multiplied by $\rho r^2\,d\chi/dr$, and the angular-momentum coefficients by $\rho r^2\,(I/m)\,d\chi/dr$ (for a thin spherical shell, $I/m = \tfrac23 r^2$, the comment notes), each interpolated in $\log_{10}$.

    > **Question** ([[#L248]], [[#L251-L252]]). **The comment's $d\chi/dr$ omits $P$.** The comment writes the third term as `RHO*GM/(LN10*DP*R**2)`; the code computes $G m\rho/(P r^2)$ (with `-HP(II)` in the exponent), which is the correct $d\log P/dr$ term. Should the comment read `RHO*GM/(LN10*DP*P*R**2)`?

**Changed since `rp_1`.** Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (`/EGRID/`, `/SPLIN/`, `/MDPHY/`, `/DIFAD/`, `/DIFAD2/` to their `_MOD` modules; the settings of `/CTOL/`, `/VARFC/` and `/VMULT/` to `NMLPAR_MOD`; `/CONST/`–`/CONST2/` to `CONSTANTS_MOD`), `JSON` from `DIMENSIONS_MOD`, `IMPLICIT NONE`.
