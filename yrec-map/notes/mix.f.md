## Explanation

**Purpose.** `MIX` updates the composition of a model over one time step: nuclear burning, mixing of convection zones, semiconvection, gravitational settling and deuterium burning. Its header gives the outline: (1) find the convection zones and the mixed regions with overshoot; (2) compute the burning rates for the given composition, density and temperature; (3) solve implicitly for the abundances in radiative regions; (4) solve for burning in each convection zone; (5) semiconvection if desired; (6) microscopic diffusion if desired. The header also says that `MIX` is called "prior to rezoning and on the third level of iteration in the Henyey portion." So it runs as a predictor and a corrector: the main program saves the start-of-step composition in `HCOMPP` and calls `MIX` with `ITLVL = 1` on the old structure, before rezoning, to predict the composition (with the explicit equilibrium burning of step 4); then, in every Henyey iteration from level 3 on, [CRRECT](crrect.f.md#crrect) restores `HCOMPP` and calls `MIX` again with the current $`T`$ and $`\rho`$. The level-1 result is only a starting guess.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the model (`HD`, `HL`, `HP`, `HR`, `HS` = $`\log m`$, `HS1` = $`m`$, `HS2` = shell masses, `HSTOT`, `HT`, `LC`, `M`), the composition `HCOMP(15,M)`, the step `DELTS` ($`\mathrm{s}`$), the iteration level `ITLVL`, `TEFFL` and the mixing length `CMIXL`, passed on to `SCONVEC`. Parameters come from `NMLPAR_MOD`: `DPENV`, `LSEMIC`, the settling switches and limits (`LDIFY`, `XMIN`, `YMIN`, `DT_GS`, `LNEWDIF`), `TCUT`, the accretion settings (`LMDOT`, `DMDT0`, `COMPACC`) and `LEXCOM`, all set by [PARMIN](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/parmin.f#L16-L1489).
- **Who calls it:** [CRRECT](crrect.f.md#crrect) and the main program in [main.f](main.f.md).
- **What it calls:** [CONVEC](convec.f.md#convec) (zones), [RATES](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L6-L677) and [DEUTRATE](deutrate.f.md#deutrate) (rates), [KEMCOM](kemcom.f.md#kemcom) (implicit burning), [EQBURN](eqburn.f.md#eqburn) (explicit burning at `ITLVL = 1`), [SCONVEC](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/sconvec.f#L4-L302) (semiconvection), [MICRODIFF](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/microdiff.f#L4-L245) or [GRSETT](grsett.f.md#grsett) (settling), and [DBURN](dburn.f.md#dburn) (deuterium).
- **Outputs:** `HCOMP` in place; the time step in years `DDAGE`; the zone edges `JCORE`, `JENV`, `MXZONE`, `MXZON0`. The deuterium rate goes to `DRATE` in `DEUTER_MOD`. The mixed zones are printed to `ISHORT`.
- **Modules:** besides those above, `CONSTANTS_MOD` (`CLN`, `CGL`, `CSECYR`), `IOUNITS_MOD` (`ISHORT`) and `DIMENSIONS_MOD` (`JSON`).

**The method.**
1. **Deep mixing.** If `DPENV < 1` (and `ITLVL > 1`), every shell with $`m \le \mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast`$ is flagged convective in a copy of the flags (`LCC`) before `CONVEC` finds the zones; a commented-out variant used a fraction of the peak luminosity instead (MHP 1/95). Otherwise `LCC = LC`.

    **`DPENV` mixes from the center.** The first comment says "DPENV MIXES THE STAR FROM THE SURFACE TO MASS FRACTION DPENV, E.G. DPENV = 0.7 MEANS THE OUTER .3 OF THE STAR IS MIXED" ([lines 127–128](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L127-L128)), but the code flags the shells inside $`\mathrm{DPENV}\,M_\ast`$ ([lines 129–141](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L129-L141)), as the next comment ("MIX FROM CENTER TO A FIXED MASS FRACTION," [line 130](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L130)) and the documentation ("Full mixing from center to fractional mass depth," `docs/source/namelist_physics.md`, line 296) say, so the first comment is stale.
2. **Rates.** For every shell hotter than `TCUT(1)`, `RATES` gives the rates `HR1`–`HR13`, `HF1`, `HF2`, and `DEUTRATE` the deuterium rate if the surface (or accreted) deuterium exceeds $`10^{-11}`$. Cooler shells get zero rates.
3. **Implicit burning.** `KEMCOM` solves shell by shell in radiative zones (down to the `TCUT(1)` cutoff) and zone by zone in convective ones, which also homogenizes them.
4. **Explicit burning** (only `ITLVL = 1`): `EQBURN` gives hydrogen and helium burning rates assuming equilibrium $`^3\mathrm{He}`$ and CN abundances, which overwrite $`X`$, $`Z`$, $`^{12}\mathrm{C}`$ and $`^{16}\mathrm{O}`$: $`X \to X + \dot X\,\Delta t`$, $`Z \to Z - \dot Y\,\Delta t`$, with $`\Delta t`$ = `DDAGE`$`\times10^{-9}`$ ($`\mathrm{Gyr}`$). Here $`\dot Y \lt{} 0`$ (`DYDT = -4.0D0*(R412 + 3.0D0*R3A)`, from the $`^{12}\mathrm{C} + \alpha`$ and triple-$`\alpha`$ rates), so $`Z`$ rises: the helium burnt becomes carbon and oxygen, counted in $`Z`$. `EQBURN` starts from the start-of-step composition `HCOMPP`, not from `KEMCOM`'s result, and overwrites only those four (and only where its rates are nonzero); what `KEMCOM` gave for $`^3\mathrm{He}`$, the other CNO isotopes ($`^{13}\mathrm{C}`$, $`^{14}\mathrm{N}`$, $`^{15}\mathrm{N}`$, $`^{17}\mathrm{O}`$, $`^{18}\mathrm{O}`$) and the light elements is kept.

    > **Question** ([lines 279–304](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L279-L304)). **The equilibrium predictor.** At `ITLVL = 1` the burning of $`X`$, $`Z`$, $`^{12}\mathrm{C}`$ and $`^{16}\mathrm{O}`$ comes from `EQBURN`, which assumes equilibrium $`^3\mathrm{He}`$ and CN abundances, although the network (`KEMCOM`) has just been run. Before and after the main sequence $`^3\mathrm{He}`$ and CN can be far from equilibrium, so the predictor may start the Henyey iteration further from the answer. Is the `EQBURN` predictor kept for speed, and is it known to cost iterations where $`^3\mathrm{He}`$ or CN are out of equilibrium?
5. **Semiconvection** (`LSEMIC`, `ITLVL > 1`): `SCONVEC`. Then, if `LSEMIC` or `ITLVL = 1`, each convection zone is mixed to its mass-weighted mean, $`\bar X_k = \sum_j X_{k,j}\,\Delta m_j / \sum_j \Delta m_j`$.
6. **Settling** (`LDIFY`): with $`d\ln P/dr`$ from hydrostatic equilibrium,
   
   $$\displaystyle \frac{d\ln P}{dr} = -\frac{G\\,m\\,\rho}{r^2\\,P}$$
   
   (`HQPR`, from the logs and $`\log G`$ = `CGL`), and the helium settling time at the outer boundary (the first shell with $`Y \gt{}`$ `YMIN`, searching inward from `JENV`, the base of the surface convection zone), from Michaud et al. ([1984](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1984ApJ...282..206M)) as the comment cites,
   
   $$\displaystyle \tau = 4.348\times10^{21}\ \mathrm{yr}\\;\frac{\Delta M/M\_\ast}{T^{3/2}},$$
   
   where $`\Delta M/M_\ast`$ is the mass fraction above that shell, the step is split into `NSTEP` substeps of at most `DT_GS`$`\times\tau`$ (`NSTEP = INT(DELTS/DTMAX)`, plus one if `MOD(DELTS,DTMAX)` is not zero or `NSTEP` is 0), each running `MICRODIFF` (if `LNEWDIF`) or `GRSETT`. Both diffuse only between the convective core (if any) and the base of the surface convection zone, and apply the change found at that base to every shell of the zone ([EQUAL_TO_MODEL](equal_to_model.f.md#equal_to_model), [MICRODIFF_ETM](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/microdiff_etm.f#L2-L174)): the zone stays homogeneous, and the flux through its base sets the surface $`Y`$ and $`Z`$.

    > **Question** ([lines 459–461](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L459-L461); [rotmix.f, lines 228–230](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotmix.f#L228-L230)). **Convection flags for settling.** `MIX` passes `LCC` (the flags before overshoot, or the deep-mixing flags) to `MICRODIFF`, while `ROTMIX` passes flags built from the mixed zones, overshoot included. Should the two agree?

    > **Question** ([lines 434–438](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L434-L438)). **`YMIN`.** The documentation says diffusion is disabled "once surface helium `Y < YMIN`"; here `YMIN` only locates the outer boundary used for the settling time (the first shell inward from the convection zone's base with $`Y \gt{}`$ `YMIN`), and settling is skipped only when $`Y \lt{}`$ `YMIN` in every shell below that base. Which is intended?

    > **Question** ([lines 444–454](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L444-L454)). **The settling substep bound.** The substeps are bounded by `DT_GS` times the helium settling time at the base of the surface convection zone only, $`\tau \propto (\Delta M/M_\ast)/T^{3/2}`$. Metals and light elements, or deeper layers with steep gradients (near a burning core), may need shorter substeps, and the explicit Lax–Wendroff step of [GRSETT](grsett.f.md#grsett) on its equally spaced grid has its own stability (Courant) limit. Is `DT_GS`$`\times\tau`$ known to satisfy that limit?
7. **Clean-up.** Every abundance is clipped to $`[0,1]`$, $`Z \le 1 - X - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}`$, and $`Y = 1 - X - Z - X_{^3\mathrm{He}}`$.
8. **Deep mixing again:** `CONVEC` is called with the true flags `LC` to reset `JCORE` and `JENV`.
9. **Deuterium** (`LEXCOM`): `DBURN`, shell by shell in radiative zones and per convection zone.

    **`DBURN`'s rate is per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$, like `DT`.** The explicit and deuterium burning use `DT = DDAGE*1.0D-9`, in $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$ ([line 293](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L293); [lines 485–503](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L485-L503)), which matches `EQBURN`'s rates, "per gigayear per amu" (KEMCOM's comment, [kemcom.f, lines 85–88](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/kemcom.f#L85-L88)). `DBURN`'s rate `DRATE` = `X*RDEUT*C21` ([deutrate.f, line 81](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/deutrate.f#L81)) states no units, but `RDEUT` is per second, with a factor $`N_A`$/`AMDEUT` ([deutrate.f, line 62](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/deutrate.f#L62)), and `C21` = 5.240358D-8 ([deutrate.f, line 30](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/deutrate.f#L30)) is $`3.1558 \times 10^{16}\,\mathrm{s/Gyr}`$ divided by $`N_A`$, so `DRATE` is per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$ per $`\mathrm{amu}`$ too.

**Design.** `MIX` switches settling off for the rest of the run (it sets `LDIFY = .FALSE.` in `NMLPAR_MOD`) once the central hydrogen falls below `XMIN`. A model without a surface convection zone gets only a warning ("diffusion not meaningful") and no settling.

> **Question** ([lines 412–416](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L412-L416)). **Settling switched off for good.** `LDIFY = .FALSE.` in `NMLPAR_MOD` when $`X_c \lt{}`$ `XMIN` stops all settling, including that of metals and light elements, for the rest of the run, and `MICRODIFF` is reached only when `LDIFY` is true. Is metal or lithium settling without helium settling meant to be possible?

**From YREC's documentation.** [Physics Options](https://yrec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/namelist_physics.html): `LSEMIC` turns on semiconvection; `DPENV` (experimental) mixes fully "from center to fractional mass depth"; for settling, `DT_GS` (default 0.1) splits the step into several when the global step exceeds `DT_GS` times the settling time at the base of the convection zone, `XMIN` (1.0D-3) turns diffusion off once the central $`X`$ is below it, and `YMIN` (1.0D-3), in the documentation's words, "once surface helium Y < YMIN"; `LNEWDIF` (legacy) turns on the updated settling routines.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** `CMIXL` is a new last argument (formerly `/CONST3/`), passed on to `SCONVEC`; COMMON blocks to modules, `IMPLICIT NONE`; the unused `/OLDMOD/`, `/COMP/`, `/THEAGE/`, `/NEWENG/` (and `/CTLIM/`, whose `TCUT` now comes from `NMLPAR_MOD`) are gone, which settles the former question 6.

**Changed since `a8a5950`.** The burning rates are no longer copied into `HCOMPM`, which nothing had read since [ROTMIX](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotmix.f#L5-L251) computes its own rates, and `BURN_MOD` (removed since `a8a5950`) is no longer used; the settling substeps are counted with `MOD(DELTS,DTMAX)` instead of the swapped `MOD(DTMAX,DELTS)`, and a count of 0 is raised to 1 (settles former question 1).
