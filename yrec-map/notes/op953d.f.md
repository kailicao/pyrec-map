## Explanation

**Purpose.** Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the model's $Z$ (MHP 7/98: "at fixed Z"): a three-dimensional interpolation in $X$, $\log T$ and $\log R$ ($R = \rho/T_6^3$). The same as [[op952d.f#OP952D|OP952D]], with one more interpolation in $X$.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the table `CAPPA(10,70,19)` of $\log\kappa$ at the model's $Z$ in `LLOT95_MOD`, built by [[op95ztab.f#OP95ZTAB|OP95ZTAB]]; the indices and weights in `OP95INTERP_MOD` (`BX(1,·)` for $X$; formerly `/OP95INDX/`, `/OP95FACT/` and `/OP95EXT/`), from [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]].
- **Who calls it:** [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]], when the point's $Z$ matches the table's `ZTAB` (to a relative $10^{-4}$) but its $X$ does not match the surface table's, and the acoustic depth is not being computed.
- **Outputs:** $\kappa$, $\log\kappa$, `QOD` and `QOT`, as in `OP952D`.

**The method.** For each of the four nearest $X$ tables, the interpolation of `OP952D` in $\log R$ (with linear extrapolation beyond the table) and in $\log T$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its two derivatives; these three are then interpolated in $X$ with four-point weights `BX(1,J)`, and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ is converted to constant $\rho$ as in `OP952D`.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the three interpolation blocks merged into `OP95INTERP_MOD`, with `RL`, `RLL` renamed `RHO_T63L`, `RHO_T63LL`), the dimension parameters moved into `LLOT95_MOD`, `IMPLICIT NONE`.
