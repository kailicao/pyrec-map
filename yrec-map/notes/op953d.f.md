## Explanation

**Purpose.** Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the model's $`Z`$ (MHP 7/98: "at fixed Z"): a three-dimensional interpolation in $`X`$, $`\log T`$ and $`\log R`$ ($`R = \rho/T_6^3`$). The same as [OP952D](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/op952d.f#L6-L76), with one more interpolation in $`X`$.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the table `CAPPA(10,70,19)` of $`\log\kappa`$ at the model's $`Z`$ in `LLOT95_MOD`, built by [OP95ZTAB](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/op95ztab.f#L6-L126); the indices and weights in `OP95INTERP_MOD` (`BX(1,·)` for $`X`$; formerly `/OP95INDX/`, `/OP95FACT/` and `/OP95EXT/`), from [GETOPAL95](getopal95.f.md#getopal95).
- **Who calls it:** [GETOPAL95](getopal95.f.md#getopal95), when the point's $`Z`$ matches the table's `ZTAB` (to a relative $`10^{-4}`$) but its $`X`$ does not match the surface table's, and the acoustic depth is not being computed.
- **Outputs:** $`\kappa`$, $`\log\kappa`$, `QOD` and `QOT`, as in `OP952D`.

**The method.** For each of the four nearest $`X`$ tables, the interpolation of `OP952D` in $`\log R`$ (with linear extrapolation beyond the table) and in $`\log T`$ gives $`\log\kappa`$ and its two derivatives; these three are then interpolated in $`X`$ with four-point weights `BX(1,J)`, and $`\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T`$ is converted to constant $`\rho`$ as in `OP952D`.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the three interpolation blocks merged into `OP95INTERP_MOD`, with `RL`, `RLL` renamed `RHO_T63L`, `RHO_T63LL`), the dimension parameters moved into `LLOT95_MOD`, `IMPLICIT NONE`.
