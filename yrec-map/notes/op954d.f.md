## Explanation

**Purpose.** Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the full set of tables (MHP 7/98): a four-dimensional interpolation in $Z$, $X$, $\log T$ and $\log R$ ($R = \rho/T_6^3$). It is the general case, used when the point's composition matches neither pre-interpolated table.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the full set in `LLOT95A_MOD`: the grids `TGR`, `XXG`, `R0GR`, `ZZG`, the opacities `CAPPA2(126,70,19)`, and for each $Z$ the offset of its first table `NZ` (its number of $X$ tables `NUMXM` is in the module too, but unused? by this or any routine) (loaded by [[ll95tbl.f#LL95TBL|LL95TBL]]); the indices, weights and extrapolation data from [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]] in `OP95INTERP_MOD` (formerly `/OP95INDX/`, `/OP95FACT/` and `/OP95EXT/`; `BZ`, `BX(K,·)`, and $\log R$ as `RHO_T63L`).
- **Who calls it:** [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]], when the point's $Z$ differs from the table's `ZTAB`, and always when the acoustic depth is computed (`LADON` and `LACOUT`; GETOPAL95's comment, JVS 04/11).
- **Outputs:** $\kappa$, $\log\kappa$, `QOD`, `QOT`, as in [[op952d.f#OP952D|OP952D]].

**The method.** For each of the four nearest $Z$ (table offset `NZ(JZ+K-1)`) and, within it, the four nearest $X$ (`JX(K,J)`, which may differ between $Z$ values since high-$Z$ sets lack some $X$ tables), the $\log R$ and $\log T$ interpolation of `OP952D`; then four-point interpolation in $X$ (weights `BX(K,·)`) and in $Z$ (weights `BZ`), and the conversion of $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ to constant $\rho$.

**Table layout.** The header explains it: "tables are incremented in sets of NZ*NX", so the tables of the third metal abundance ($3\times10^{-4}$) are numbers 21–30; at high $Z$ some $X$ tables are missing ($X < 0.9$ for $Z = 0.1$). The offsets `NZ` (0, 10, …, 100, 109, 118, in [[ll95tbl.f#LL95TBL|LL95TBL]]) give $Z = 0.06$ and $0.08$ nine slots each and $Z = 0.1$ eight. `LL95TBL` stores the tables at $X = 1 - Z$ for $Z$ = 0.06 and 0.08 at `NZ(IZ)+10`, over the $X = 0$ tables of the next $Z$; this routine reads them with the same indexing, so it finds $X = 1 - Z$ where it was stored, but takes $X = 0$ at $Z$ = 0.08 and 0.1 from the wrong tables ([[#L45-L48]]; a suspected bug, listed on [[ll95tbl.f#LL95TBL|LL95TBL]]'s page, with a fix).

**Changed since `rp_1`.** Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the three interpolation blocks merged into `OP95INTERP_MOD`), `IMPLICIT NONE`; the extrapolation's $\log R$ and its boundary value are renamed `RHO_T63L` and `RHO_T63LL` (formerly `RL`, `RLL`, names that clashed with the radius elsewhere). No question is settled.
