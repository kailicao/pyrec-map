## Explanation

**Purpose.** Computes the nuclear reaction rates at one shell, for the abundance updates. Its header calls it "a stripped-down version of ENGEB": the same reactions, screening and constants as [`ENGEB`](engeb.f.md#engeb), but without the energy generation. The rates are those of Bahcall's *Neutrino Astrophysics* (1989), with the $`\alpha`$ captures from Caughlan and Fowler ([1988](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1988ADNDT..40..283C)), as the comments say.

**How it fits.**
- **Who calls it:** [`MIX`](mix.f.md#mix) (for each shell, before the composition is mixed and burned), [`ROTMIX`](rotmix.f.md#rotmix) (for each shell of its substeps, since `7be4133`), and [`XTIME`](xtime.f.md#xtime) (at the shell `IEND`, for the time step).
- **Inputs:** $`\log_{10}\rho`$ (`DL`), $`\log_{10}T`$ (`TL`), the mass fractions `X`, `Y`, `XHE3`, `XC12`, `XC13`, `XN14`, `XO16`, `XO18`, and the shell index `IU`.
- **Outputs:** the arrays `HR1`…`HR13` (the rates of the 13 reactions) and `HF1`, `HF2` (two branching fractions) at element `IU`. [`KEMCOM`](kemcom.f.md#kemcom) reads them (all but `HR9`, `HR13` and `HF2`) to update the abundances; no routine computes with `HF2`, the Be7 branching $`F_1`$ (unused?). [`ROTMIX`](rotmix.f.md#rotmix) calls `RATES` itself, shell by shell, for its own substeps.
- **Modules:** [`CROSS_MOD`](cross_mod.f.md#cross_mod) (the derivative scalings `QS0E`, `QQS0EE` and the N15+p branching factors `FO16`, `FC12`, set in [`REMAP`](remap.f.md#remap), shared with `ENGEB`); [`NMLPAR_MOD`](nmlpar_mod.f.md#nmlpar_mod) (the cross-section factors `SSTANDARD(17)`, set in [`PARMIN`](parmin.f.md#parmin) or by `REMAP`; `WEAKSCREENING`, 0.03 by default in `PARMIN`; the temperature cuts `TCUT`); [`CONSTANTS_MOD`](constants_mod.f.md#constants_mod) (`CLN` $`=\ln 10`$, `CC13` $`=1/3`$, `CC23` $`=2/3`$).

**The method.**

1. **Composition sums.** Over the 13 isotopes (n, H1, D, H3, He3, He4, C12, C13, N14, O16, O18, Ne20, Mg24; those of n, D, H3, Ne20, Mg24 set to zero) with mass fractions $`X_i`$, bare nuclear masses $`A_i`$ (`ANUC`) and charges $`Z_i`$:
   
   $$\displaystyle \mu\_I^{-1} = \sum\_i \frac{X\_i}{A\_i}\ (\texttt{AMU}),\quad \mu\_e^{-1} = \sum\_i \frac{Z\_iX\_i}{A\_i}\ (\texttt{EMU}),\quad \texttt{ZET} = \sum\_i\frac{Z\_i^2X\_i}{A\_i},\quad \texttt{XTR} = \sum\_i\frac{Z\_i^{1.58}X\_i}{A\_i}.$$
2. **Below the temperature cut** $`\log T \le`$ `TCUT(1)`, all rates are zero (with the default 6.5 this stops pp burning and $`^3\mathrm{He}`$ production too, not only deuterium burning; see [engeb.f, question 2](engeb.f.md#q2)). On that path ([lines 346–351](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L346-L351)) `F1` and `F3` are not set, so `HF1(IU)`, `HF2(IU)` would get the values kept (by `SAVE`) from the previous call; no caller takes it: [`MIX`](mix.f.md#mix) and [`ROTMIX`](rotmix.f.md#rotmix) call `RATES` only above `TCUT(1)` and zero the rates and `HF1`, `HF2` of the colder shells themselves ([mix.f, line 181](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L181), [mix.f, lines 219–237](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mix.f#L219-L237); [rotmix.f, lines 88–103](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotmix.f#L88-L103)), and `XTIME`'s [`EQBURN`](eqburn.f.md#eqburn) does not read `HF1` (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).
3. **Electron degeneracy** (for Salpeter's screening): with $`\rho/\mu_e`$ (`RWE`), $`p_F^2/(m_ec)^2 = 1.017677\times10^{-4}(\rho/\mu_e)^{2/3}`$ and $`E_F/kT = 5.92986\,T_9^{-1}\bigl(\sqrt{1+p_F^2/m_e^2c^2}-1\bigr)`$. The factor $`f'/f`$ (`FPRF`) is 1 for $`E_F/kT \le 0.01`$, 0 for $`\log_{10}(E_F/kT) \ge 1.5`$, and a sixth-order polynomial in $`\log_{10}(E_F/kT)`$ between.
4. **Screening.** $`\Lambda_0 = 5.9426\times10^{-6}\,T_9^{-3/2}\sqrt{\rho/\mu_I}`$ (`XXL`), $`\tilde\zeta = \sqrt{(\texttt{ZET} + f'/f\cdot\mu_e^{-1})\,\mu_I}`$ (`ZCURL`), $`\bar Z = \mu_I/\mu_e`$ (`ZBAR`). For reaction $`j`$ with charge product $`Z_1Z_2`$ (`ZPRD`):
   
   $$\displaystyle U\_\mathrm{wk} = Z\_1Z\_2\\,\Lambda\_0\\,\tilde\zeta .$$
   
   If $`U_\mathrm{wk} \le`$ `WEAKSCREENING`, weak screening is used, $`U = U_\mathrm{wk}`$. Otherwise the intermediate form
   
   $$\displaystyle U\_\mathrm{int} = 0.38\\,\Lambda\_0^{0.86}\\,\frac{\texttt{XTR}\\;\zeta\_{86,j}}{\mu\_I^{-1}\\,\tilde\zeta^{0.58}\\,\bar Z^{0.28}}$$
   
   (Graboske et al. [1973](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1973ApJ...181..457G), with their coefficients `Z86`), or, for $`U_\mathrm{wk} \gt{} 2`$, the strong form
   
   $$\displaystyle U\_\mathrm{str} = 0.624\\,\bar Z^{1/3}\Lambda\_0^{2/3}\Bigl\[\zeta\_{53,j} + 0.316\\,\bar Z^{1/3}\zeta\_{43,j} + \frac{0.737\\,\zeta\_{23,j}}{\bar Z\\,\Lambda\_0^{2/3}}\Bigr\]$$
   
   (`USTR`, with the coefficients `Z53`, `Z43`, `Z23`) when it is smaller than $`U_\mathrm{int}`$ or when $`U_\mathrm{wk} \ge 5`$. The rate is multiplied by $`e^{U}`$.
5. **Reactions 1–7** (pp, He3+He3, He3+He4, p+C12, p+C13, p+N14, p+O16), with $`T_9 = T/10^9\,\mathrm{K}`$:
   
   $$\displaystyle r\_j = \rho\\,\Bigl\[T\_9^{-2/3} + q\_{1}T\_9^{-1/3} + s'\_j\\,(q\_{2} + q\_{3}T\_9^{1/3}) + s''\_j\\,(q\_{4}T\_9^{2/3} + q\_{5}T\_9)\Bigr\]\exp\bigl(q\_{6}T\_9^{-1/3} + q\_{7} + (q\_{8}T\_9)^2 + U\_j\bigr)\\;S\_j,$$
   
   with $`q_{k} =`$ `Qk(j)` (the terms of eq. 3.14 of *Neutrino Astrophysics*, as the comments explain), $`s'_j`$, $`s''_j`$ = `QS0E(j)`, `QQS0EE(j)` (user scalings of the S-factor's first and second derivatives) and $`S_j`$ = `SSTANDARD(j)`. Rates below $`10^{-30}`$ are set to zero. All are skipped when $`X = 0`$.
6. **Be7.** Electron capture: $`\lambda_e = 1.752\times10^{-10}\,T_9^{-1/2}\,[1 + 0.004(1000\,T_9 - 16)]\,\mu_e^{-1}\,S_{15}`$. Proton capture: $`\lambda_p = 3.128813\times10^5\,X\,S_{16}\,e^{-10.2625\,T_9^{-1/3}}`$ times the bracket of step 5 with `Q(8)`, and its own weak or intermediate screening. Both are multiplied by $`\rho\cdot 8.584981\times10^{22}`$, and $`F_1 = \lambda_e/(\lambda_e+\lambda_p)`$ is the fraction of Be7 that captures an electron.

    > **Question** ([lines 541–549](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L541-L549)). **Hard-coded Be7 constants.** The Be7 screening uses its own `ZPRDBE7P = 4.0` and `Z86BE7P = 5.7790`, and no strong-screening branch. Fine for the Sun, but is this routine used for denser cores, where strong screening would matter?
7. **N15+p branching.** The $`(p,\alpha)`$ and $`(p,\gamma)`$ channels have their own brackets, times $`67500\,f_{C12}`$ and $`64\,f_{O16}`$ (`FC12`, `FO16`); $`F_3 = \mathrm{C12ALPHA}/(\mathrm{C12ALPHA}+\mathrm{O16GAMMA})`$. The comment says the S-factor of $`^{15}\mathrm{N}(p,\alpha)`$ "has been changed from 78000. to 675000. on 9/25/97" ([lines 580–581](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L580-L581)), but the code multiplies by 67500 ([line 597](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L597)), the value [`REMAP`](remap.f.md#remap) gives as the reference of Adelberger et al. ([1998](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1998RvMP...70.1265A)), `S0_1_15_C12ALP_REF` = 6.75D4, beside 6.4D1 for the $`(p,\gamma)`$ channel, the code's 64 ([remap.f, lines 77–78](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/remap.f#L77-L78)); the comment's 675000 is a typo (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

    **Three comments disagree with the code.** "F3 IS THE FRACTION OF THE N14 THAT IS BURNED BY P, ALPHA" ([line 516](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L516)) should read N15: `F3 = C12ALPHA/(C12ALPHA + O16GAMMA)` ([line 598](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L598)) splits the two branches of N15 + p. The comment above the screening loop says `WEAKSCREENING` is passed "in the FLUX COMMON block" ([lines 433–434](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L433-L434)), but it comes from `NMLPAR_MOD` ([nmlpar_mod.f, line 151](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/nmlpar_mod.f#L151); `/WEAK/` at `rp_1`). And "DL AND DT" ([line 331](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L331)) are `DL` and `TL`, the routine's arguments ([line 6](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L6)).
8. **$`\alpha`$ captures** (only when $`\log T \ge`$ `TCUT(4)`): $`^{13}\mathrm{C}(\alpha,n)^{16}\mathrm{O}`$ (`RATE(8)`), $`^{12}\mathrm{C}(\alpha,\gamma)^{16}\mathrm{O}`$ (`RATE(10)`), $`^{14}\mathrm{N}(\alpha,\gamma)^{18}\mathrm{F}`$ (`RATE(11)`) and the triple-$`\alpha`$ (`RATE(12)`, $`\propto\rho^2 T_9^{-3} e^{-4.4027/T_9}`$), as fits of the Caughlan and Fowler form. `RATE(9)` and `RATE(13)` are always zero, and every rate at or below $`10^{-5}`$ is set to zero.

    > **Question** ([line 494](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L494); [lines 646–648](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L646-L648)). **Two thresholds for zero.** Reactions 1–7 are zeroed below $`10^{-30}`$, but at the end every rate at or below $`10^{-5}`$ (per second per gram, before `C21`) is set to zero ([`ENGEB`](engeb.f.md#engeb) has the same cuts). At low density or temperature the second cut could switch off slow but relevant rates. Is it intended?
9. **Output units.** Each rate (per second per gram, mass fractions left out) is multiplied by `C21` $`= 5.240358\times10^{-8}`$, "(10^9 years/1 second)·(1 atomic mass unit/1 gram)," which gives `HRj(IU)`. `HF1(IU)` $`= F_3`$ and `HF2(IU)` $`= F_1`$. Before `C21`, $`r_jX_aX_b`$ is the number of reactions per gram per second (the comment: `RATE(I)` is "the rate of the different reactions per second per gram, except that the mass fractions are omitted"), and by the comment on `Q7` its logarithmic normalization carries $`-\ln(A_0A_1)`$, $`-\ln S_0`$ and $`-\ln(1+\delta_{01})`$, where $`\delta_{01} = 1`$ "only when the two reacting nuclei … are identical" (pp, $`^3\mathrm{He}+{}^3\mathrm{He}`$); the full form, with Avogadro's number and the triple-$`\alpha`$ prefactor, is the question below. Multiplying by the $`\mathrm{amu}`$ in grams turns a number of reactions into a mass, and by the seconds in a $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$ gives the rate per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$. [`KEMCOM`](kemcom.f.md#kemcom) then writes $`dX_k/dt = A_k\sum_j \pm\,\mathrm{HR}_j X_aX_b`$, with $`X`$ mass fractions and $`t`$ in $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$. (A check of the units against the Sun is the second question below.)

    > **Question** ([lines 193–200](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L193-L200); [lines 640–641](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L640-L641)). **The rates' normalization.** From the comment on `Q7`, $`r_jX_aX_b = \rho N_A^2\langle\sigma v\rangle_j (X_a/A_a)(X_b/A_b)/(1+\delta_{ab})`$ reactions per gram per second, so that $`r_j = \rho N_A^2\langle\sigma v\rangle_j/[A_aA_b(1+\delta_{ab})]`$, with $`N_A`$ inside the constant 70.62860: is that the intended form? Two numbers seem to bear it out, though neither is in the code: $`e^{\mathtt{Q7(1)}} \approx 1.19\times10^9`$ is about half of $`N_A`$ times Caughlan & Fowler's pp coefficient, $`4.01\times10^{-15}`$; and triple $`\alpha`$'s prefactor, $`1.565315\times10^{21}`$ (`RATE(12)`, with $`Y^3`$), equals $`N_A/(3!\,A_\alpha^3)`$ with the code's `CAVOG` and $`A_\alpha = 4.0026`$ (the atomic weight in gmass06.f, not `ANUC(6)` = 4.001506). Does that prefactor carry the $`1/3!`$ for three identical reactants, and which $`A_\alpha`$ is meant?

    > **Question** ([line 658](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L658)). **A check against the Sun.** Taking the Sun's center at $`\rho \approx 150\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}`$ and $`T \approx 1.57\times10^7\,\mathrm{K}`$ (values not in the code), `HR1` comes out about 0.23 per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$, and $`3\,\mathrm{HR}_1X^2`$ with $`X \approx 0.35`$ gives $`dX/dt \approx -0.08`$ per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$, close to a mean loss of central hydrogen from about 0.70 to 0.35 in $`4.6\,\mathrm{Gyr}`$. Does a calibrated solar model from YREC show that rate, so that `C21`'s units (per $`\mathrm{Gyr}`$, mass fractions) are confirmed?

**Design.** All constants are `DATA` statements (double precision since `rp_1`), with earlier sets kept as comments (BP00, 7/21/03). The routine duplicates much of `ENGEB`, so a change to one must be copied to the other.

> **Question** ([lines 149–280](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rates.f#L149-L280); [engeb.f, lines 259–461](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/engeb.f#L259-L461)). **Duplication with ENGEB.** Are the constants here guaranteed to match those in `ENGEB`? A check by script, or one shared routine, would remove the risk.

**From YREC's documentation.** [Physics Options](https://yrec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/namelist_physics.html): `WEAKSCREENING` (default 0.03) is the threshold below which weak screening is used for the reaction rates. The light-element cross sections (`XSLI6`, `XSLI7`, `XSBE91`–`XSBE93`, $`\mathrm{keV\,b}`$, with the switches `LXLI6`–`LXBE93`) are turned into the factors `SLI6`…`SBE93` by [`PARMIN`](parmin.f.md#parmin).

**Changed since `rp_1`.** The constants are now double precision: the `DATA` values (masses, the coefficients `Q1`–`Q7`, the screening coefficients, `C21`) and the literals in the rate fits, which were single-precision constants; the exponent $`2/3`$ in $`p_F`$ and $`\Lambda_0^{2/3}`$ is now `CC23` (it was `0.6666667` and `0.666667`). The rates change by up to a few parts in a million, most where a constant sits in an exponent (`Q7(2)` = 76.6003 was off by $`3.6\times10^{-6}`$). The thresholds `1.E-2`, `1.E-30` and `1.E-5` are still single precision. COMMON blocks to modules (`/CTLIM/`'s `TCUT`, `SSTANDARD` and `WEAKSCREENING` now in `NMLPAR_MOD`, `/CTLIM/` and `/WEAK/` dropped here), `IMPLICIT NONE`.

**Changed since `a8a5950`.** The file is unchanged; `MIX` no longer copies the rates into `HCOMPM` (the module `BURN_MOD`, removed since `a8a5950`), and `ROTMIX` computes its own with `RATES`.
