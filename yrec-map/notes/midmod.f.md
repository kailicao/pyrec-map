## Explanation

**Purpose.** In the rotational-mixing and angular-momentum transport loop, a model time step is divided into shorter "diffusion" steps. `MIDMOD` builds the structure at an intermediate time of the model step by linear interpolation between the old model (start of the step) and the new one (end of the step). Its comments say it also accounts for nuclear burning, recomputes the angular velocity from the conserved specific angular momentum, and finds the edges of the central and surface convection zones and the moment of inertia of the surface convection zone.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the new model in the arguments (`HD`, `HL`, `HP`, `HR`, `HT`, `HG`, the convection flags `LC`); the old model in `OLDMOD_MOD`, `OLDPHY_MOD` and `OLDROT_MOD` (`HGO`); the new auxiliary physics in `SCRTCH_MOD` (`SDEL`, `SESUM`, `SEG`, `SO`, `SVEL`) and `TEMP_MOD` (`CP`, `HAMU`, `SQDT`, `THDIF`, `VISC`); the fraction of the step `FX` and the diffusion step `DT` (of the model step `DELTS`); the specific angular momentum `HJM`.
- **Who calls it:** only [[getw.f#GETW|GETW]], once per diffusion step, with `FX = SUMDT/DELTS`.
- **What it calls:** [[ovrot.f#OVROT|OVROT]] (convective overshoot for the mixing zones), [[rotmix.f#ROTMIX|ROTMIX]] (burning and mixing over the short step), [[dburnm.f#DBURNM|DBURNM]] (deuterium burning), and [[getrot.f#GETROT|GETROT]] or [[solid.f#SOLID|SOLID]] (the rotation variables).
- **Outputs:** the intermediate structure (`HDM`, `HLM`, `HPM`, `HRM`, `HTM`, `HGM`, `LCM`, `LCZM`), the rotation variables (`OMEGAM`, `HIM`, `ETA2M`, `QIWM`, `R0M`), `IMIN`, `IMAX`, `HSTOP`, `HSBOT`, `HICZ`, `LCZSUR`, `LALLCZ`, the mixing zones (`MRZONE`, `MXZONE`, `NRZONE`, `NZONE`), and the interpolated physics in `MDPHY_MOD` (`DELM`, `DELAM`, `DELRM`, `VELM`, `VISCM`, `THDIFM`, `CPM`, `QDTM`, `OM`, `AMUM`, `EPSM`, `ESUMM`), which GETW and the transport routines ([[codiff.f#CODIFF|CODIFF]], [[mixgrid.f#MIXGRID|MIXGRID]], [[mixcz.f#MIXCZ|MIXCZ]] and others) read. It saves the composition after mixing in the argument `HCOMPS` (formerly `/OLDAB/`), an array GETW owns and passes on to [[seculr.f#SECULR|SECULR]].
- **Modules:** `NMLPAR_MOD` (`LSOLID`, `IMPJMOD`, `TCUT`, `LEXCOM`), `DEUTER_MOD` (`DRATE`, `DRATE0`), `CONSTANTS_MOD` (`CLN`, `CC23`, `CMSUN`), `OLDROT_MOD` (the old model's `HGO`) and the modules above. The values of the previous intermediate step (`DDELP`, `DDELN`, `HRP`, `LCP`, formerly `/PREVMID/`) are local saved arrays.

**The method.**
0. **The first diffusion step** (`LFIRSTIT`): `HCOMP` (rows 1–11, or 1–15 with `LEXCOM`) is reset to the old model's composition `HCOMPP`, and `AMUM` to `AMUO`, so that burning and mixing start from the start of the model step.
1. **Interpolation in time.** For each variable $q$ (logarithmic structure variables and the physics),
$$q_\mathrm{mid} = q_\mathrm{old} + f\,(q_\mathrm{new} - q_\mathrm{old}),\qquad f = \texttt{FX}.$$
The mean molecular weight `AMUM` is instead incremented: $\mu_\mathrm{mid} \mathrel{+}= (\Delta t/\Delta t_\mathrm{model})(\mu_\mathrm{new}-\mu_\mathrm{old})$ ([[#L165]]). This is the same as interpolating: it starts from `AMUO` at the first substep ([[#L130]]), GETW's substeps add up to the model step ([[getw.f#L262-L268]]), and a substep that is redone is subtracted again ([[getw.f#L355-L359]]) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). The total energy generation `EPSM` interpolates `SESUM + SEG(6) + SEG(7)`. The interpolation is at fixed shell because the two models share a mass grid: when [[hpoint.f#HPOINT|HPOINT]] rezones at the start of the step it interpolates the old model onto the new points (`HPO`, `HTO`, `HRO`, `HLO`, `HDO` in `OLDMOD_MOD`), and otherwise the main program copies the old model into them unchanged.
2. **Convection.** A shell keeps its convective state if it is the same at both ends of the step; otherwise it is convective if $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} < \nabla_\mathrm{rad}$ at the intermediate time, with the larger of the two convective velocities.

    > **Question** ([[#L183]]). **The criterion for shells that change state.** A shell whose convective state differs at the two ends of the step is called convective if $\nabla_\mathrm{ad} < \nabla_\mathrm{rad}$ at the intermediate time (`DELAM(I).LT.DELRM(I)`), the Schwarzschild criterion. Does this match the criterion the structure uses for that model (with any $\mu$-gradient or semiconvection treatment)? Near a receding convective core with a $\mu$ gradient the two can disagree, and a shell could be mixed here that the structure calls radiative.

3. **Receding convection zones** (MHP 06/02): a block meant to correct the specific angular momentum of shells that leave a convection zone during the step. It is unused?: `LNEWCZ`, set by loop 50 when any shell changes state, is reset to `.FALSE.` at [[#L192]], just before `IF(LNEWCZ)` ([[#L197-L318]]). As written it could not be revived unchanged: It overwrites the argument `FX`, which GETW uses ([[#L253]], [[#L292]]); `RR = HJM2/HJM(J)` comes after `HJM(J) = HJM2`, so it is always 1 ([[#L268-L269]]); the zone's moment of inertia uses `HR(II)-HRO(II)` but the shell's `HR(J)-HRP(J)` ([[#L257]], [[#L264]]); the envelope branch sums $J$ over `J+1..ICZ1` but rescales `J+1..M` ([[#L295-L299]], [[#L310-L312]]); and `DDELN(I0-1)` is read at index 0 when the first shell changes state ([[#L214-L215]], [[#L220]]).
4. **Mixing and burning.** `OVROT` sets the mixed zones with overshoot; `ROTMIX` burns and mixes over `DT`, using the intermediate temperature `HTM` "for thermodynamic consistency" (MHP 6/00). If the surface deuterium abundance at the start of the step (`HCOMPP(12,M)`) exceeds $10^{-14}$, the deuterium rate is interpolated and burnt by `DBURNM`, shell by shell in radiative zones (down to the burning cutoff `TCUT(1)`) and zone by zone in convective ones. This is where the header's "nuclear burning is accounted for here" ([[#L112]]) is done: The update by `HCOMPM` is commented out ([[#L151-L154]]), and `ROTMIX` and `DBURNM` burn instead ([[#L326-L328]], [[#L335-L371]]) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). The radiative loop leaves both loops at the first shell at or below `TCUT(1)` ([[#L353]]); this skips no hotter shell, since [[convec.f#CONVEC|CONVEC]] lists the radiative zones from the center outward ([[convec.f#L184-L205]]) and the temperature falls outward (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026; [[convec.f#b1]] aside).
5. **Convection zone edges.** `IMIN` $= \max(2, I-1)$, where $I$ is the first shell that is not convective, so it is the outermost shell of a central convection zone, or 2; `IMAX` the lowest shell of the surface convection zone (or `M`). `HSTOP` is the total mass, `HSBOT` the mass at the base of the surface zone.

    > **Question** ([[#L372-L383]]). **`IMIN`.** The comment says `IMIN` "is the first zone above a central convection zone," but `IMIN = MAX(2,I-1)` with `I` the first non-convective shell is the last convective one. Which is meant, given that `IMIN` is the first zone tested for rotational instabilities?

    > **Question** ([[#L403-L408]]). **`LCZSUR`.** The test `(HSTOP-HSBOT)/CMSUN .GT. 0` is true for any surface convection zone; the comment speaks of one "deep enough for angular momentum loss." Was a threshold intended?

6. **Rotation.** Unless rotation is forced to be solid (`LSOLID`, or `IMPJMOD = 1`), the first guess of each shell's moment of inertia per unit mass is that of a thin spherical shell, $i = \tfrac23 r^2$, so $\Omega = j/i$; `GETROT` then solves for the distorted shells. `HICZ` sums the moments of inertia of the surface convection zone. For solid rotation, `SOLID` gives the run, and `HICZ` sums all shells.

    > **Question** ([[#L515-L520]]). **`HICZ` for solid rotation.** With solid rotation, `HICZ` is the moment of inertia of the whole star, not of the surface convection zone. Intended, since a solid body couples everything?

**Design.** A long block for a local convective turnover time (MHP 3/09) is commented out; [[getw.f#GETW|GETW]] and other routines handle `TAUCZ` through `OVRTRN_MOD`. The routine prints to the screen (`WRITE(*,*)`) inside the dead block only.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** `HCOMPS` is a new last argument (formerly `/OLDAB/`), and `LFIRST` is renamed `LFIRSTIT`; COMMON blocks to modules (the unused `/BURN/`, `/CWIND/`, `/DWMAX/`, `/OVRTRN/` and `/CTLIM/` are gone, `TCUT` now from `NMLPAR_MOD`; `/PREVMID/` to local saved arrays), `IMPLICIT NONE`. No question is settled.
