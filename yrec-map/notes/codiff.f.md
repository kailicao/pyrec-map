## Explanation

**Purpose.** This file computes the diffusion coefficients for rotational mixing: one for angular-momentum transport (`COD`) and one for composition transport (`COD2`), from the characteristic velocities of the rotational instabilities, at each interface between shells.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the arguments `HRU` (the radius of each shell's midpoint, not logged) and `M`; the velocities `VES` (meridional circulation), `VGSF` (Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke instability) and `VSS` (secular shear) in [`TEMP2_MOD`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/temp2_mod.f#L19-L37), computed by [VCIRC](vcirc.f.md#vcirc); the variable $`f_c`$, the argument `VFC` (SECULR's array, filled by [GETFC](getfc.f.md#getfc)); in [`NMLPAR_MOD`](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/nmlpar_mod.f#L32-L279), the multipliers `FW`, `FC`, `FESC`, `FGSFC`, `FSSC`, the switch `LVFC`, the constant background coefficient `CODM`, `LCODM`, and `LNOJ`. All options are set in [PARMIN](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/parmin.f#L16-L1489).
- **Who calls it:** [SECULR](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/seculr.f#L6-L532).
- **Outputs:** `COD`, `COD2` and `RMID` (arguments), from element 2 on: no routine sets `COD(1)` or `COD2(1)`, and their readers, [ROTGRID](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotgrid.f#L2-L285) and [MIXGRID](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mixgrid.f#L2-L167), start at element `IBEG+1` $`\ge 2`$ ([rotgrid.f, lines 189–197](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotgrid.f#L189-L197), [rotgrid.f, lines 205–210](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotgrid.f#L205-L210), [mixgrid.f, lines 133–138](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/mixgrid.f#L133-L138); settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). `HLE` in `TEMP2_MOD`, set to zero ([lines 101–106](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/codiff.f#L101-L106); the line `HLE(I)=RMID(I)` is commented out): unused?, since its only reader is the column "LENGTH" of [CHECKJ](checkj.f.md#checkj)'s detailed print, which is switched off ([checkj.f, line 326](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/checkj.f#L326), [checkj.f, line 363](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/checkj.f#L363)); the header's "characteristic velocity length scales" ([lines 70–71](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/codiff.f#L70-L71)) are thus not computed.
- **Unused?:** the local arrays `FADV`, `FADV0` (formerly `/ADVEC/`, declared only here, [line 57](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/codiff.f#L57)).

**The method.** Element $`i`$ refers to the interface between shells $`i-1`$ and $`i`$, at $`r_{\mathrm{mid},i} = \tfrac12(r_i + r_{i-1})`$. With $`v = v_\mathrm{ES} + v_\mathrm{GSF} + v_\mathrm{SS}`$:
```math
D_J = 4\pi f_w\, v\, r_\mathrm{mid}, \qquad D_X = 4\pi f_w f_c \left(f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}\,v_\mathrm{ES} + f_{c,\mathrm{GSF}}\,v_\mathrm{GSF} + f_{c,\mathrm{SS}}\,v_\mathrm{SS}\right) r_\mathrm{mid},
```
with $`f_{c,\mathrm{ES}}`$, $`f_{c,\mathrm{GSF}}`$, $`f_{c,\mathrm{SS}}`$ = `FESC`, `FGSFC`, `FSSC`, as on VCIRC's page; or, with a variable $`f_c`$ (`LVFC`), $`D_X = D_J\,f_c(r)`$. The two sets of factors differ: `FES`, `FGSF`, `FSS` scale the velocities in VCIRC; `FESC`, `FGSFC`, `FSSC` weight each mechanism's share of composition mixing only. With `LCODM`, a constant `CODM` is added to $`D_J`$ ($`4\pi f_w\,\mathrm{CODM}`$; "MHP 9/14," "MHP 8/13"). It is added to $`D_J`$ only, never to $`D_X`$, in both branches ([lines 129–131](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/codiff.f#L129-L131), [lines 135–138](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/codiff.f#L135-L138)): the Physics namelist page describes `CODM` as a magnetic diffusion term for angular-momentum transport (below), and `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` issue 1 records the same (settled from YREC's documentation, Nov 9, 2026). With `LNOJ`, $`D_J = 0`$: composition is mixed but angular momentum is not transported (MHP 9/93). So the coefficient is a velocity times a length, the radius: $`v\,r`$ is in $`\mathrm{cm^2\,s^{-1}}`$, and the factor $`4\pi`$ belongs to the form in which the equation is solved: [ROTGRID](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/rotgrid.f#L2-L285) multiplies the coefficients by $`\rho r^4\,d\chi/dr`$ and $`I/(mr^2)`$ (its comment: "a factor of 4PI has already been included in CODIFF"), giving the $`D`$ of [DCOEFT](dcoeft.f.md#dcoeft)'s equation. The $`\mu`$-gradient enters only through the velocities: with `IMU` = 3, [VCIRC](vcirc.f.md#vcirc) subtracts the $`\mu`$ currents (`VMU`, scaled by `FMU`) from `VES` and `VGSF` before they reach CODIFF ([vcirc.f, lines 540–566](https://github.com/yreclab/yrec/blob/7be4133/src/vcirc.f#L540-L566)), and CODIFF does not read `VMU` (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

**From YREC's documentation.** [Physics Options](https://yrec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/namelist_physics.html) (Angular momentum transport): `LCODM` adds a magnetic diffusion term `CODM` to angular-momentum transport (0 is pure hydrodynamics; 9e4 matches the constraints on the solar core's rotation; the code's default is 2.5e4); `FW` scales all angular-momentum diffusion coefficients, and `FC` the mixing coefficients relative to them, usually calibrated on lithium depletion (in clusters or the Sun). `LVFC`, experimental, is the variable-`FC` model of Zahn ([1991](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1991A%26A...252..179Z)).

**Changed since `rp_1`.** `VFC` is now an argument (`/VARFC/` retired); COMMON blocks to modules (the unused `/INTVAR/` and `/MDPHY/` dropped, `/ADVEC/` made local), `IMPLICIT NONE`.

### CODIFF

Diffusion coefficients for angular momentum, $`D_J = 4\pi f_w (v_\mathrm{ES} + v_\mathrm{GSF} + v_\mathrm{SS})\,r`$, and for composition, $`D_X`$ (with the extra factors $`f_c`$ and per-mechanism weights), at the interfaces; options for a constant background and for no angular-momentum transport.
