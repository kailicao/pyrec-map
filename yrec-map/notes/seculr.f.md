## Explanation

**Purpose.** The secular transport of angular momentum, and of the composition it carries, by rotational instabilities, over one time step. Its comment: "SECULR CALCULATES THE TRANSFER OF ANGULAR MOMENTUM (AND THE ASSOCIATED TRANSPORT OF COMPOSITION) DUE TO SECULAR ROTATIONAL INSTABILITIES." The mechanisms considered are "secular and dynamical shear, Eddington circulation, and the GSF instability." It is the central routine of rotational mixing: it sets up, iterates and checks the diffusion of $\Omega$ and of the abundances.

**How it fits.**
- **Who calls it:** [[getw.f#GETW|`GETW`]], once per time step, for a rotating model.
- **Inputs (arguments):** the step `DT`; the model (`HD`, `HG`, `HI`, `HL`, `HP`, `HR`, `HS`, `HS1`, `HS2`, `HT`, `HCOMP`, `M`); the rotation (`OMEGA`, specific angular momentum `HJM` and its saved copy `HJMSAV`, `ETA2`, `QIW`, `R0`); the composition at the start of the step, `HCOMPS` (filled by [[midmod.f#MIDMOD|`MIDMOD`]]; [[getw.f#GETW|`GETW`]] owns the array); the convection flags `LCZ` and the range of radiative interfaces `IMIN`–`IMAX`; and for the wind, `BL`, `HSTOT`, `SMASS`, `TEFFL`, `HSBOT`, `HSTOP`, `LCZSUR` (the star has a surface convection zone).
- **Outputs:** `OMEGA`, `HJM`, `HCOMP` updated; `HV` (the circulation velocities), `COD2` (composition diffusion coefficients), `HICZ`, `OMEGAS`; the flags `LOK` (converged) and `LREDO`, `IREDO` (the step must be cut, set by the checks).
- **What it calls, in order:** [[setupv.f#SETUPV|`SETUPV`]] (factors for the velocities), [[getqua.f#GETQUA|`GETQUA`]], [[vcirc.f#VCIRC|`VCIRC`]] (stability tests and velocities; sets `LDO`), [[getfc.f#GETFC|`GETFC`]] (with `LVFC`), [[mixcz.f#MIXCZ|`MIXCZ`]], the wind routines [[mwind.f#MWIND|`MWIND`]] and [[mcowind.f#MCOWIND|`MCOWIND`]], [[solid.f#SOLID|`SOLID`]] (solid-body rotation), [[codiff.f#CODIFF|`CODIFF`]] (diffusion coefficients), [[rotgrid.f#ROTGRID|`ROTGRID`]] (the equally spaced grid), [[dcoeft.f#DCOEFT|`DCOEFT`]] and [[tridia.f#TRIDIA|`TRIDIA`]] (the diffusion equation for $\Omega$ and its tridiagonal solution) or [[dadcoeft.f#DADCOEFT|`DADCOEFT`]] (diffusion plus advection, `LDIFAD`), [[eq2mod.f#EQ2MOD|`EQ2MOD`]] (back to the model's grid), [[ndifcom.f#NDIFCOM|`NDIFCOM`]] (composition diffusion), [[checkj.f#CHECKJ|`CHECKJ`]] and [[checkc.f#CHECKC|`CHECKC`]] (convergence and step-cutting checks).
- **Modules, main ones:** [[nmlpar_mod.f|NMLPAR_MOD]] (namelist inputs: `ITDIF2`, the maximum number of iterations; `LDISK`, `TDISK`, disk locking; `LSOLID`, `IMPJMOD`; `LVFC`, `LDIFAD`; `LJDOT0`, the wind; `LCODM`, `CODM`, the constant diffusion coefficient; `LNOJ`; `LEXCOM`), [[disk_mod.f|DISK_MOD]] (`SAGE`, the age), [[intvar_mod.f|INTVAR_MOD]] (`RM`, the mid-shell radii), [[oldrot2_mod.f|OLDROT2_MOD]] (`WMST`), [[egrid_mod.f|EGRID_MOD]] (`NTOT`), [[constants_mod.f|CONSTANTS_MOD]], [[iounits_mod.f|IOUNITS_MOD]] (`IMODPT`) and [[dimensions_mod.f|DIMENSIONS_MOD]] (`JSON`).
- **Arrays it owns:** `FACI` (factors for the velocities; formerly `/INTFAC/`), filled by `SETUPV` and read by `VCIRC`; `VFC` (formerly `/VARFC/`), cleared here at each call, filled by `GETFC` and read by `CODIFF`.

**The method.**
1. **Disk locking:** if `LDISK` and the age is below `TDISK`, the surface rate is held fixed (`LOCK`).
2. **Shear.** At each interface the logarithmic gradient of $\Omega$ is centered on the mid-shell radius $r_m$:
   $$\frac{d\Omega}{d\ln r}\bigg|_i \approx \frac{\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1}}{4}\left[\frac{1}{\ln r_i - \ln r_m} + \frac{1}{\ln r_m - \ln r_{i-1}}\right],$$
   which is $(\Omega_i - \Omega_{i-1})/(\ln r_i - \ln r_{i-1})$ when $r_m$ is the geometric mean. Here $r_m$ = `RM` is the arithmetic mean $\tfrac12(r_i + r_{i-1})$ ([[setupv.f#SETUPV|SETUPV]]), and since $\tfrac14(1/x + 1/y) \ge 1/(x+y)$, the gradient comes out larger than the simple difference, by a fraction of about $(\ln r_i - \ln r_{i-1})^2/16$.

    > **Question** ([[#L173-L179]]). **The shear stencil.** The centred formula uses `RM`, the arithmetic mean of the shell radii (SETUPV), rather than the geometric mean for which it reduces to the simple difference; so $d\Omega/d\ln r$, and with it the shear and the onset of the shear instability, is slightly overestimated (by about $(\Delta\ln r)^2/16$, small on a fine grid). Is that intended? Why not the simple difference kept as a comment, `QWLNR(I) = (OMEGA(I)-OMEGA(I-1))/CLN/(HR(I)-HR(I-1))` ([[#L178]])?

3. **Iteration** `IT` = 1 … `ITDIF2`. From the second iteration on, $\Omega$ is replaced by the average of the start and the latest solution, $\Omega \leftarrow \tfrac12(\Omega + \Omega_\mathrm{start})$, to get, in the comment's words, "a corrected V and thus a more accurate run of diffusion coefficients"; the velocities are recomputed, $j$ and the first four abundances are reset to their starting values, and convection zones are remixed (`MIXCZ`; the flag `LIMIX` set before it, [[#L253]], is no longer passed to it: unused?).

    > **Question** ([[#L203-L216]]). **The averaged iteration.** From the second iteration on, $\Omega \leftarrow \tfrac12(\Omega + \Omega_\mathrm{start})$ before the velocities are recomputed (the comment speaks of averaging the velocities themselves). Is the aim to evaluate the velocities, and so the diffusion coefficients, at the middle of the substep (time-centred, as in a Crank–Nicolson scheme)?

4. **Wind.** For an isolated surface convection zone (no diffusion across its base, `HV(IMAX)` = 0) the wind torque is applied to that zone (`MWIND`); for solid-body models (`LSOLID` or `IMPJMOD` = 1) to the whole star, followed by `SOLID`. Otherwise the wind enters the diffusion equation as a boundary term (`MCOWIND`, giving `WIND1`, `WIND2`), or not at all when locked.
5. **Unstable regions.** If `VCIRC` found no instability (`LDO` false), the routine ends. Otherwise `CODIFF` gives the coefficients, and each run of interfaces with $v > 0$ (`HV`) is solved on its own (or, with `LCODM` and `CODM` > 0, the whole domain `IMIN-1`…`IMAX` as one region, "MHP 8/13," [[#L308-L314]]; that this also runs for solid-body models, where the solution for $\Omega$, the only place `CODM` acts, is skipped, is `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`'s issue 1, open, with a proposed gate): `ROTGRID` maps it to an equally spaced grid, the diffusion equation for $\Omega$ is solved implicitly (`DCOEFT` + `TRIDIA`, or `DADCOEFT`; the equation, from [[dcoeft.f#DCOEFT|DCOEFT]]'s header, is $\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{4\pi\rho r^2}\frac{1}{I/M}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\bigl(D\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial r}\bigr)$, "the diffusion equation we are solving," $D$ being [[codiff.f#CODIFF|CODIFF]]'s $D_J = 4\pi f_w v r$ multiplied in [[rotgrid.f#ROTGRID|ROTGRID]] by "rho r^4 by d chi/dr" and by "I/MR^2" (`HI/HS2`, the shell's mass), for its equally spaced coordinate $\chi$; the composition obeys a diffusion equation of the same kind with $D_X$), `EQ2MOD` maps the change in $j$ back, and `NDIFCOM` diffuses species 1–4 ($X$, $Y$, $Z$, He3: those that change the mean molecular weight; the comment names only H, He3, and He4, [[#L413-L415]], but the call covers $Z$ too, [[#L419-L424]]). A region that `ROTGRID` flags (`LDUM2`: a single radiative interface between two convection zones) is skipped. For solid-body models (`LSOLID` or `IMPJMOD` = 1) the solution for $\Omega$ is skipped ("MHP 3/09"), but `NDIFCOM` still diffuses the composition. The center and the surface shells are treated as convective during this.

    > **Question** ([[#L345-L347]]; [[rotgrid.f#L262-L263]]). **The equation's coordinate.** The transport equation above is written in $r$, but the coefficient $D$ that ROTGRID builds includes $d\chi/dr$, which belongs to the equation written in the equally spaced coordinate $\chi$ (with $\partial/\partial r = (d\chi/dr)\,\partial/\partial\chi$). Where does the second factor of $d\chi/dr$ enter (ROTGRID or DCOEFT), and what are the units of each factor? A comment stating the equation in $\chi$ would settle it.

6. **Checks.** `CHECKJ` checks the solution and updates $\Omega$; `CHECKC` checks the composition. On convergence (`LOK`), or after `ITDIF2` iterations, species 5–11 (or 5–15 with `LEXCOM`) are diffused with `NDIFCOM`. This call uses neither the last region's `IBEG`, `IEND` nor its grid: With `LOK` true, as it always is here ([[#L448]], [[#L452]]), [[ndifcom.f#NDIFCOM|NDIFCOM]] finds every unstable region itself and builds each one's grid ([[ndifcom.f#L64-L121]]) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026).

    > **Question** ([[#L451-L452]]). **Non-convergence counts as success.** If the loop ends after `ITDIF2` iterations without `LOK`, the code sets `LOK = .TRUE.` and goes on, with no message. Should it warn, or cut the step?


**Design.** Printing is switched off (`LPRT = .FALSE.`, "G Somers 11/14"), so the block that writes the composition changes to `IMODPT` never runs. Large commented-out blocks remain (the old `EQUAL`, `EQUAL2`, `DIFCOM`, `BSCOMP` routines, and a coupling-factor diagnostic).

**From YREC's documentation.** [Physics Options](https://yrec.readthedocs.io/en/latest/namelist_physics.html) (Angular momentum transport): `LINSTB` includes rotationally induced mixing and hydrodynamic angular-momentum transport; `LSOLID` enforces solid-body rotation everywhere (for strongly coupled magnetic models); `WALPCZ` makes $\Omega \propto r^{\mathrm{WALPCZ}}$ in the convection zone (0 solid body, −2 uniform specific angular momentum); `IMPJMOD` couples globally: 0 no effect, 1 solid body everywhere, 2 solid-body core with a decoupled convection zone, 3 solid-body core coupled at the zone's base. `ITDIF2` is the number of iterations between nuclear burning and rotational mixing (1 = none), `DJOK` the tolerance of rotational mixing.

**Changed since `rp_1`.** `LCODM` and `CODM` now arrive from `NMLPAR_MOD`, so the constant-diffusion option (whole domain treated as unstable) takes effect for the first time (bug fix; question 1 of `rp_1` is settled and removed; the solid-body case that follows from it is `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`'s issue 1); `HCOMPS` is a new last argument (formerly `/OLDAB/`); `FACI` and `VFC` are local arrays passed to `SETUPV`, `VCIRC`, `GETFC` and `CODIFF` (formerly `/INTFAC/` and `/VARFC/`); other COMMON blocks to modules, unused ones dropped (`/CCOUT/`, `/CCOUT1/`, `/CWIND/`, `/OLDMOD/`, `/TEMP2/`, `/ROTPRT/`); `IMPLICIT NONE`.

**Changed since `a8a5950`.** `LREDO` is cleared on entry: the stable-model exit returns before [[checkj.f#CHECKJ|CHECKJ]] or [[checkc.f#CHECKC|CHECKC]] can set it, so after a redo a retried sub-step that was stable returned the failed attempt's `.TRUE.`, and [[getw.f#GETW|GETW]] rewound the clock again although nothing had failed.
