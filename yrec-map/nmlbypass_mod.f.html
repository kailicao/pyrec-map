Purpose

NMLBYPASS_MOD holds the 83 namelist variables that serve only to set other quantities, or are kept for later development: none is read by the physics routines. It is used by PARMIN, REMAP and STARIN. It replaces COMMON/NMLBYPASS/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), which had gathered COMMON/NEWPARAM/, /NEWCROSS/ and /XSECT/ and 18 derivation-only members of other blocks (/PMMWIND/, /NEWMX/, /VMULT/, /CCOUT1/, /ENVGEN/, and LNEWNUC of /CROSS/), so that NMLPAR_MOD holds only what the physics reads.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. All are in PARMIN's PHYSICS namelist, except NPENV, ISETMIX, ISETISO, R14_15, R16_17, ZXMIX, XB10_INI and XB11_INI, which are in CONTROL. All but NPENV carry initial values, moved from PARMIN's DATA statements.

  • LNEWVARS and the tolerances TOL_DM_MIN, TOL_DM_MAX, TOL_DL_MAX, TOL_DP_*, TOL_DX_MAX, TOL_DZ_MAX, TOL_CZBASE_FINE_WIDTH, FLAG_DX, FLAG_DZ, FLAG_DW, TIME_* and LSTRUCT_TIME: "more intuitive variable names" for the mesh and time-step tolerances; with LNEWVARS, REMAP copies them into HPTTOL, ATIME, LPTIME, DTWIND and DTDIF (in NMLPAR_MOD).
  • LNEWNUC and the S-factors S0_*, with their first and second derivatives S0P_*, S0PP_* (pp chain, CNO cycle, Be7, hep, and the N15+p branches): with LNEWNUC, REMAP turns them into the rate factors SSTANDARD (in NMLPAR_MOD) and QS0E, QQS0EE, FC12, FO16 (CROSS_MOD).
  • XSLI6, XSLI7, XSBE91–XSBE93 with their switches LXLI6–LXBE93: the light-element cross sections (keV b); PARMIN turns each one switched on into the factor SLI6, …, SBE93 relative to the default.
  • ISETMIX, ISETISO, ZXMIX, R14_15, R16_17, XB10_INI, XB11_INI: the heavy-element mixture and isotope ratios that PARMIN applies to the starting model ("MHP 10/24 added heavy element mixture controls"); PARMIN sets ZXMIX from the chosen mixture. R16_17's default (471.4) looks swapped with R16_18's (suspected bug 3).
  • PMMA–PMMM, PMMJD: the exponents and constant of the wind law (reset by PARMIN for the presets of AWIND), from which PARMIN derives the factors of NMLPAR_MOD (EXMD, EXW, CONSTFACTOR, …); FO: written to the .short file only (lines 1008–1009↗; unused?); NPENV: a print interval, 9999 when not positive.
  • LENVG and PMMMD are read nowhere (line 101↗, line 115↗; unused?). LENVG is KNOWN_ISSUES.md's issue 15 (lines 926–985): Every namelist file has dropped it, and its removal from the code waits for the other developers (settled from the documentation, Nov 9, 2026).

Design

A blanket USE NMLBYPASS_MOD in the three routines; STARIN uses none of the members, and declares the module so that its isotope code can reach them later (the conversion's commit message says so).

From YREC's documentation

Where Namelist Variables Live explains the split by consumer: NMLPAR_MOD holds what the physics routines read, this module what only PARMIN reads to derive something else, so that USE NMLPAR_MOD imports only names a physics routine could use; a new namelist variable goes in one or the other by that test. CONSTANTS_MOD keeps CLSUN, CRSUN beside their logarithms, and INTPAR_MOD keeps the three settings of the one integrator that uses them; the 32 CHARACTER variables are in no module and travel as arguments. The page counts 110 CONTROL, 237 PHYSICS and 36 DERIVED variables, 234 of them in NMLPAR_MOD and 83 here. Two points are out of date at 7be4133 (namelist_modules.md, lines 27 and 49): REMAP and STARIN also use this module (line 13↗, line 52↗), and NMLPAR_MOD now has 235 members, with L_ZERODT_PREMS (lines 29–30↗).
