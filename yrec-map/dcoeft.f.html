Purpose

This file sets up the tridiagonal system for one implicit step of angular-momentum transport by diffusion (and, with LDIFAD, a simple advective term), on the even grid in the coordinate $\chi$ (DR is DCHI, set by ROTGRID). It is the solver setup used when the band solver of DADCOEFT is not.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments ECOD (the diffusion coefficients at the grid points), DR, DT, EI (moments of inertia), EJ (angular momenta), EW ($\Omega$), NTOT, WIND1, WIND2 (the wind's change of $\Omega$, explicit and implicit), LFIX (fixed surface $\Omega$); with LDIFAD (a setting in NMLPAR_MOD, formerly /VARFC/), the advective and diffusive coefficients ECOD3, ECOD4 from DIFAD_MOD. ROTGRID has already multiplied ECOD by $\rho r^2\,(d\chi/dr)$ and by the shell's $I/\Delta m$ ($4\pi$ is in CODIFF), and with LDIFAD added the composition coefficient ECOD2 to ECOD4.
  • Who calls it: SECULR.
  • Outputs: A, B, C, D in TRIDI_MOD, solved next by TRIDIA; also GAMA(NTOT), set to the wind's loss of angular momentum, $\Delta J = -\tfrac12(W_1 + W_2)\,I_N$, which TRIDIA reads before overwriting it (line 82↗; line 22↗). GAMA is the solver's work array, so this is a message passed through work space, which an explicit argument would make clearer.

The method

The header gives $$\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{4\pi\rho r^2}\,\frac{1}{I/M}\,\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(D\frac{\partial\Omega}{\partial r}\right),$$ differenced implicitly with $f_i = \Delta t/(\Delta r\,I_i)$: $$a_i = -f_i D_i, \qquad b_i = 1 + f_i(D_i + D_{i+1}), \qquad c_i = -f_i D_{i+1}, \qquad d_i = \Omega_i^n,$$ with no flux at the centre and at the surface (or a fixed surface $\Omega$ if LFIX). The wind enters the surface: $d_N = \Omega_N(1 + \Delta J/J_N)$. With LDIFAD, $D$ is ECOD4 and centred advective terms $\pm\tfrac12\Delta t\,\mathtt{ECOD3}/I_i$ are added, in conservative form: each interface's flux, $\tfrac12\mathtt{ECOD3}_i(\Omega_{i-1}+\Omega_i)$, enters both neighbouring rows, and none crosses the centre or the surface. This LDIFAD branch (lines 108–139↗) is unused?: SECULR calls DCOEFT only when LDIFAD is off and calls DADCOEFT otherwise (lines 390–404↗; settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). Its advective factor, FACTA = 0.5D0*DT/EI(I) (line 116↗, line 122↗, line 130↗), has no $1/\Delta r$, unlike DADCOEFT's 0.5D0*DT/EI(1)/DR (line 305↗).
