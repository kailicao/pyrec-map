Purpose

Builds the equation-of-state table for the envelope's mixture from the SCV (Saumon–Chabrier–Van Horn, by the module's name SCVEOS_MOD, "MHP 5/97") tables of pure hydrogen, pure helium and metals, which SETUPS has read. It adds the entropy of mixing, and computes by finite differences the derivatives the structure equations need. (The header comment, "evaluate the EOS with the new tables and call eqstat … and compare", describes an earlier purpose; the routine calls neither.)

How it fits

  • Who calls it: STARIN, once the starting model's envelope composition is known.
  • Inputs, through modules: SCVEOS_MOD (the tables TABLEX (hydrogen), TABLEY (helium), TABLEZ (metals) on a grid of temperatures TLOGX(63) and up to 76 pressures NPTSX(I)), and COMP_MOD (XENV, ZENV).
  • What it calls: INTER3, three-point Lagrange interpolation weights (FT) and their derivatives (FTD).
  • Outputs, through SCVEOS_MOD: column 12 of TABLEX and TABLEY, SMIX (the entropy of mixing), and TABLENV(63, 76, 12), the mixture's table; also the interpolation indices IDTSCV (named IDT at rp_1) and IDP left from the last point. It is read by EQSCVE and EQSCVG, through EQSTAT, when LSCV is true.

The method

The table columns are used, in the code's notation, as $\log P$ (1), the ionization fractions (2, 3), $\log\rho$ (4), $\log S$ (5), $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ (7), $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$ (8) and $\partial\ln S/\partial\ln T$ (9); we read this from the formulas.

  1. $dU/dT$ of each pure table (column 12; the loop's comment, "READ IN EQUATION OF STATE TABLES FOR HYDROGEN AND HELIUM" (line 37↗), is left from the reading, now done in SETUPS, lines 368–393↗), from the two expressions for $c_P$ in the comments, $$c_P = S\left(\frac{\partial\ln S}{\partial\ln T}\right)_P = \frac{dU}{dT} + \frac{P}{\rho T}\,\frac{(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T)^2}{\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P}.$$

  2. Entropy of mixing. With $Y = 1 - X$ (XENV), the mixture's density $\rho_\mathrm{mix} = 1/((1-Y)/\rho_X + Y/\rho_Y)$, the number densities of hydrogen and helium particles and of their electrons from the ionization fractions, and $\beta = (m_H/m_{He})\,Y/(1-Y)$, $\gamma$ the ratio of particles per nucleus, the code computes $$S_\mathrm{mix} = \frac{k}{m_H}\,\frac{2(1-Y)}{1+x_2+3x_1}\left[\ln(1+\beta\gamma) - \frac{n_{e,H}}{n}\ln(1+\delta) + \beta\gamma\ln\Bigl(1+\frac{1}{\beta\gamma}\Bigr) - \frac{n_{e,He}}{n}\ln\Bigl(1+\frac1\delta\Bigr)\right],$$ with $n$ = TNUM the sum of the hydrogen and helium number densities (HNUM + HENUM), $n_{e,H}$, $n_{e,He}$ their electron number densities (HNUME, HENUME, each floored at 1), $\delta$ (DE) the ratio of electrons from helium to those from hydrogen, scaled by $\beta\gamma$ (the last term uses DEI, which is $1/\delta$ when both are positive); $x_1$, $x_2$ are TABLEX(I,J,2) and (I,J,3). Constants: $m_H$ = CMH, $m_{He}$ = CMHE (g), $k$ = CBOLTZ (erg K$^{-1}$). This is the code's formula; its source is not cited.

Here $Y = 1 - $ XENV, so the ratio of helium to hydrogen counts the metals as helium, while step 3 uses $Y = 1 - X - Z$.   3. The mixture at each $(T, P)$, with $X$, $Y = 1 - X - Z$, $Z$ from COMP_MOD: densities add as volumes, $$\frac1\rho = \frac{X}{\rho_X} + \frac{Y}{\rho_Y} + \frac{Z}{\rho_Z},\qquad \frac{\partial\ln\rho}{\partial\ln P} = \sum_k X_k\frac{\rho}{\rho_k}\left(\frac{\partial\ln\rho}{\partial\ln P}\right)_k,$$ and the same for $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$; the entropy is $S = X S_X + Y S_Y + S_\mathrm{mix}$, and $c_P$ follows, with the metals' term added. The radiation pressure PRAD $= aT^4/3$ is added to P and PL (lines 122–126↗), but neither is used afterwards (unused?), nor is QPT0 (line 144↗, unused?): The table stays on the gas-pressure grid (TABLENV(I,J,1) is TABLEX(I,J,1)), and EQSCVE adds radiation itself, looking the table up at the gas pressure $\beta P$ (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 52–53↗, lines 159–160↗). Stored: TABLENV(·,·,1..6) = $\log P$, $\log\rho$, $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$, $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$, $c_P$, $dU/dT$.

  4. Derivatives, by three-point Lagrange differentiation in $\ln T$ and $\ln P$: of $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ in $\ln P$ and $\ln T$, of $\partial\ln P/\partial\ln\rho$ in $\ln\rho$, of $\ln c_P$ in $\ln T$, and of $dU/dT$ in $\ln P$ and $\ln T$; stored in columns 7–12. The derivative of $\ln c_P$ in $\ln P$, QCPP, is computed but not stored (line 218↗, unused?). Of the table, EQSCVE reads columns 1–6 and EQSCVG column 1; columns 7–12 are read nowhere at 7be4133.

Suspected bugs, by priority

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

  1. (a) QSMIXT reads the entropy of mixing beyond the lower row's pressures. (lines 150–151↗) The temperature derivative of SMIX at row I, point J takes SMIX(IDTSCV..IDTSCV+2, J), but the first loop sets SMIX(I',J) only for J up to NPTSX(I') (lines 38–39↗). In the shipped tables (input/eos/scv/h_tab_i.dat) NPTSX grows with temperature (30 points at $\log T = 2.10$, 38 at $3.54$, 49 at $3.62$, and 76 from $5.14$), so at the high-pressure end of a row with more points than the row below, the lower neighbor's value is one that was never set (zero in practice, as a module array). QSMIXT enters $\partial\ln S/\partial\ln T$ and so $c_P$, column 5 (lines 153–154↗, lines 159–160↗), which EQSCVE interpolates, and EQSCVE uses such points: At its lowest row IDTSCV it reads pressures up to NPTSX(IDTSCV) (lines 102–128↗), and that row's derivative used row IDTSCV-1. Only with LSCV (off by default, line 258↗; on in several test-suite cases, such as Test_m0p3feh+0p0GS98_scv_ZAMS). Read from the code, not run; how far the wrong $c_P$ reaches a model was not traced. A possible fix, with the protection the code already uses for QCPT (lines 219–223↗), which takes the neighbor's last point at a lower pressure:

248                       TABLENV(I,J,10) = QCPT
249                       TABLENV(I,J,11) = QAP
250                       TABLENV(I,J,12) = QAT
251                 END DO
252            END DO

254            RETURN
255            END

27def setscv() -> None:
28    """Build the envelope mixture's table tablenv in scveos_mod: dU/dT of
29    the pure H and He tables (column 12), the entropy of mixing smix, the
30    mixture's density, d ln rho / d ln T and d ln P, c_P and dU/dT
31    (additive volumes), then their derivatives by three-point Lagrange
32    differentiation (columns 7-12).
33
34    Translates: setscv.f#SETSCV (7be4133)
35
36    No arguments; works on scveos_mod (and reads comp_mod.xenv, zenv).
37
38    """
39
40    c = constants_mod
41    s = scveos_mod
42    tlogx = s.tlogx
43    tablex = s.tablex
44    tabley = s.tabley
45    tablez = s.tablez
46    tablenv = s.tablenv
47    smix = s.smix
48    nptsx = s.nptsx
49    xenv = comp_mod.xenv
50    zenv = comp_mod.zenv
51
52    # dU/dT of each pure table and the entropy of mixing (Fortran lines
53    # 37–97).
54    for i in range(1, NTS + 1):
55        for j in range(1, nptsx[i] + 1):
56
57            # dU/dT from c_P = S (d ln S/d ln T)_P
58            #   = dU/dT + P (d ln rho/d ln T)^2 / (rho T d ln rho/d ln P)
59            # (Fortran lines 40–50).
60            tablex[i, j, 12] = (
61                math.exp(c.cln * tablex[i, j, 5]) * tablex[i, j, 9]
62                - math.exp(c.cln * (tablex[i, j, 1] - tablex[i, j, 4]
63                                    - tlogx[i]))
64                * tablex[i, j, 7]**2 / tablex[i, j, 8])
65            tabley[i, j, 12] = (
66                math.exp(c.cln * tabley[i, j, 5]) * tabley[i, j, 9]
67                - math.exp(c.cln * (tabley[i, j, 1] - tabley[i, j, 4]
68                                    - tlogx[i]))
69                * tabley[i, j, 7]**2 / tabley[i, j, 8])
70
71            # Entropy of mixing: number densities of H and He (Fortran
72            # lines 51–61).
73            # Question (see the page): Y = 1 - X here, ignoring Z.
74            y = 1.0 - xenv
75            ym1 = 1.0 - y
76            denx = math.exp(c.cln * tablex[i, j, 4])
77            deny = math.exp(c.cln * tabley[i, j, 4])
78            dmix = 1.0 / (ym1 / denx + y / deny)
79            hnum = (2.0 * ym1 * dmix / CMH
80                    / (1.0 + 3.0 * tablex[i, j, 2] + tablex[i, j, 3]))
81            henum = (3.0 * y * dmix / CMHE
82                     / (1.0 + 2.0 * tabley[i, j, 2] + tabley[i, j, 3]))
83
84            # Electron number densities from H and He (Fortran lines
85            # 62–69).
86            hnume = 0.5 * hnum * (1.0 - tablex[i, j, 2] - tablex[i, j, 3])
87            henume = c.cc13 * henum * (2.0 - 2.0 * tabley[i, j, 2]
88                                       - tabley[i, j, 3])
89            tote = hnume + henume
90            hnume = max(hnume, 1.0)
91            henume = max(henume, 1.0)
92            tote = max(tote, 1.0)  # unused in the Fortran too
93
94            # The entropy of mixing (Fortran lines 70–95).
95            if hnum == 0.0 or henum == 0.0:
96                smix[i, j] = 0.0
97            else:
98                be = (CMH / CMHE) * (y / ym1)
99                ga = (1.5 * (1.0 + tablex[i, j, 3] + 3.0 * tablex[i, j, 2])
100                      / (1.0 + 2.0 * tabley[i, j, 2] + tabley[i, j, 3]))
101                xd = 1.0 - tablex[i, j, 2] - tablex[i, j, 3]
102                yd = 2.0 - 2.0 * tabley[i, j, 2] - tabley[i, j, 3]
103                if xd > 0.0:
104                    de = 1.5 * yd * be * ga / xd
105                else:
106                    de = 0.0
107                if yd > 0.0:
108                    dei = xd / (1.5 * yd * be * ga)
109                else:
110                    dei = 0.0
111                tnum = hnum + henum
112                smix[i, j] = (ym1 / CMH * 2.0
113                              / (1.0 + tablex[i, j, 3] + 3.0 * tablex[i, j, 2])
114                              * (math.log(1.0 + be * ga)
115                                 - hnume / tnum * math.log(1.0 + de)
116                                 + be * ga * math.log(1.0 + 1.0 / be / ga)
117                                 - henume / tnum * math.log(1.0 + dei)))
118                smix[i, j] = CBOLTZ * smix[i, j]
119
120    # The equation of state of the surface mixture (Fortran lines 98–172).
121    for i in range(1, NTS + 1):
122
123        # Temperature interpolation factors (Fortran lines 100–114).
124        tl = tlogx[i]
125        t = 10.0**tl
126        s.idtscv = _three_point_start(i, NTS)
127        idt = s.idtscv
128        for k in range(1, 4):
129            qr[k] = c.cln * tlogx[k + idt - 1]
130        tln = c.cln * tl
131        inter3(qr, ft, ftd, tln)
132
133        for j in range(1, nptsx[i] + 1):
134
135            # Fortran lines 116–121.
136            pl = tablex[i, j, 1]
137            dl = tablex[i, j, 4]
138            x = xenv
139            z = zenv
140            y = 1.0 - x - z
141            p = 10.0**pl
142
143            # Radiation pressure; P and PL are not used afterwards
144            # (Fortran lines 122–126).
145            prad = c.ca3 * (t**2)**2
146            pgas = p
147            p = p + prad
148            pl = math.log10(p)
149
150            # Densities add inversely: 1/rho = X/rho_X + Y/rho_Y + Z/rho_Z
151            # (Fortran lines 127–132).
152            denx = math.exp(c.cln * tablex[i, j, 4])
153            deny = math.exp(c.cln * tabley[i, j, 4])
154            denz = math.exp(c.cln * tablez[i, j, 4])
155            d = 1.0 / (x / denx + y / deny + z / denz)
156            dl = math.log10(d)
157
158            # d ln rho / d ln P (Fortran lines 133–137).
159            qdp0 = (tablex[i, j, 8] * x * (d / denx)
160                    + tabley[i, j, 8] * y * (d / deny)
161                    + tablez[i, j, 13] * z * (d / denz))
162
163            # d ln rho / d ln T (Fortran lines 138–144).
164            qdt0 = (x * (d / denx) * tablex[i, j, 7]
165                    + y * (d / deny) * tabley[i, j, 7]
166                    + z * (d / denz) * tablez[i, j, 10])
167            qpt0 = -qdt0 / qdp0  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
168
169            # Entropy: additive, plus the entropy of mixing (Fortran lines
170            # 145–151).
171            sx = math.exp(c.cln * tablex[i, j, 5])
172            sy = math.exp(c.cln * tabley[i, j, 5])
173            s0 = x * sx + y * sy + smix[i, j]
174            # Suspected bug (see the page): row J of the neighbor
175            # temperatures may lie beyond their NPTSX, where SMIX is unset.
176            qsmixt = (ftd[1] * smix[idt, j] + ftd[2] * smix[idt + 1, j]
177                      + ftd[3] * smix[idt + 2, j])
178
179            # d ln S / d ln T (Fortran lines 152–154).
180            qst0 = (x * sx * tablex[i, j, 9] + y * sy * tabley[i, j, 9]
181                    + qsmixt) / s0
182
183            # c_P (gas pressure only), with the metals' term (Fortran lines
184            # 155–160).
185            qcp0 = s0 * qst0 + z * (
186                math.exp(c.cln * tablez[i, j, 7])
187                + pgas * tablez[i, j, 10]**2 / tablez[i, j, 13] / denz / t)
188
189            # dU/dT (Fortran lines 161–162).
190            qut = qcp0 - pgas * qdt0**2 / qdp0 / d / t
191
192            # Store the envelope mixture's table (Fortran lines 163–170).
193            tablenv[i, j, 1] = tablex[i, j, 1]
194            tablenv[i, j, 2] = dl
195            tablenv[i, j, 3] = qdt0
196            tablenv[i, j, 4] = qdp0
197            tablenv[i, j, 5] = qcp0
198            tablenv[i, j, 6] = qut
199
200    # The derivatives, numerically (Fortran lines 173–252).
201    # Question (see the page): the comment names DEL(AD), the code dU/dT.
202    for i in range(1, NTS + 1):
203
204        # Temperature interpolation factors (Fortran lines 181–194).
205        s.idtscv = _three_point_start(i, NTS)
206        idt = s.idtscv
207        for k in range(1, 4):
208            qr[k] = c.cln * tlogx[k + idt - 1]
209        tl = c.cln * tlogx[i]
210        inter3(qr, ft, ftd, tl)
211        t = math.exp(tl)
212
213        for j in range(1, nptsx[i] + 1):
214
215            # Pressure interpolation factors (Fortran lines 196–208).
216            s.idp = _three_point_start(j, nptsx[i])
217            idp = s.idp
218            for k in range(1, 4):
219                qr[k] = c.cln * tablenv[i, idp + k - 1, 1]
220            pl = c.cln * tablenv[i, j, 1]
221            inter3(qr, fp, fpd, pl)
222
223            # Derivatives of d ln rho / d ln T (Fortran lines 209–213).
224            qdtp = (tablenv[i, idp, 3] * fpd[1]
225                    + tablenv[i, idp + 1, 3] * fpd[2]
226                    + tablenv[i, idp + 2, 3] * fpd[3])
227            # Suspected bug (see the page): no MIN(NPTSX, J) protection.
228            qdtt = (tablenv[idt, j, 3] * ftd[1]
229                    + tablenv[idt + 1, j, 3] * ftd[2]
230                    + tablenv[idt + 2, j, 3] * ftd[3])
231
232            # Derivatives of ln c_P; QCPP is not stored (Fortran lines
233            # 214–224).
234            for k in range(1, 4):
235                qr[k] = math.log(tablenv[i, idp + k - 1, 5])
236            qcpp = qr[1] * fpd[1] + qr[2] * fpd[2] + qr[3] * fpd[3]  # noqa: F841  (unused in the Fortran too)
237            for k in range(1, 4):
238                ii = idt + k - 1
239                jj = min(nptsx[ii], j)
240                qr[k] = math.log(tablenv[ii, jj, 5])
241            qcpt = qr[1] * ftd[1] + qr[2] * ftd[2] + qr[3] * ftd[3]
242
243            # Derivatives of dU/dT (Fortran lines 225–236).
244            for k in range(1, 4):
245                ii = idt + k - 1
246                jj = min(nptsx[ii], j)
247                qr[k] = tablenv[ii, jj, 6]
248                # Suspected bug (see the page): this line undoes the one
249                # above.
250                qr[k] = tablenv[idt + k - 1, j, 6]
251            qat = qr[1] * ftd[1] + qr[2] * ftd[2] + qr[3] * ftd[3]
252            for k in range(1, 4):
253                qr[k] = tablenv[i, idp + k - 1, 6]
254            qap = qr[1] * fpd[1] + qr[2] * fpd[2] + qr[3] * fpd[3]
255
256            # d/d ln rho (d ln P / d ln rho) (Fortran lines 237–244).
257            for k in range(1, 4):
258                qr[k] = c.cln * tablenv[i, idp + k - 1, 2]
259            dln = c.cln * tablenv[i, j, 2]
260            inter3(qr, fp, fpd, dln)
261            qpdd = (fpd[1] / tablenv[i, idp, 4]
262                    + fpd[2] / tablenv[i, idp + 1, 4]
263                    + fpd[3] / tablenv[i, idp + 2, 4])
264
265            # Store the derivatives (Fortran lines 245–250).
266            tablenv[i, j, 7] = qdtp
267            tablenv[i, j, 8] = qdtt
268            tablenv[i, j, 9] = qpdd
269            tablenv[i, j, 10] = qcpt
270            tablenv[i, j, 11] = qap
271            tablenv[i, j, 12] = qat

274def _three_point_start(i: int, n: int) -> int:
275    """The first of three grid points centered on i, kept inside 1..n
276    (the IF block the Fortran repeats for IDTSCV and IDP, Fortran lines
277    103–109, 182–188, and 197–203).
278
279    """
280
281    if i == 1:
282        return 1
283    elif i == n:
284        return i - 2
285    else:
286        return i - 1
