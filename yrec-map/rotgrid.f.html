Explanation

Purpose. For the transport of angular momentum and the mixing it drives, maps one radiative region (shells IBEG to IEND) onto an auxiliary grid of points equally spaced in a mesh coordinate $\chi$, and computes there what the diffusion solver needs: each zone's mass, moment of inertia, angular momentum and angular velocity at the zone centers, and the diffusion coefficients, multiplied by the Jacobian of the change of variables, at the zone edges.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: SECULR (the secular transport of angular momentum and composition), for each region it treats.
  • What it calls: GETGRID, which defines the coordinate $\chi$ (CHI at the model's shells, ECHI at the new points, spacing DCHI, NTOT points, and their masses ES1; all in EGRID_MOD), and OSPLIN for every interpolation, through the work arrays of SPLIN_MOD.
  • Inputs (arguments): the model's log density HD, log radius HR and radius HRU, log pressure HP, luminosity HL, log mass HS, masses HS1 (enclosed) and HS2 (of each shell), moments of inertia HI, specific angular momenta HJMSAV, angular velocities WSAV, the convection flags LCZ, and the diffusion coefficients COD (angular momentum) and COD2 (composition). Also, from modules: the mesh tolerances HPTTOL, the switch LDIFAD and the factor FW (all in NMLPAR_MOD, formerly /CTOL/, /VARFC/ and /VMULT/), the energy generation EPSM (MDPHY_MOD), with LDIFAD the advective and diffusive velocities VESA, VESD (DIFAD2_MOD), and the constants (CONSTANTS_MOD).
  • Outputs: on the new grid, EM (zone masses), EI (moments of inertia), EJ (angular momenta), EW (angular velocities), ECOD, ECOD2 (the two diffusion coefficients with their factors), and with LDIFAD ECOD3, ECOD4 in DIFAD_MOD; DR is set to DCHI. SECULR solves the diffusion on this grid and maps the result back. LDUM2 returns true, and nothing else is done, when the region has only one interface (IEND - IBEG <= 1).

The method.

  1. Zone centers. $\log r$, $I/(m r^2)$ (EI0), $j = J/m$ and $\Omega$ are interpolated from the shells onto the new points. Then, for the interior zones, $$\Delta m_k = \tfrac12\,(m_{k+1} - m_{k-1}),\qquad I_k = \frac{I}{m r^2}\Big|_k\,\Delta m_k\,r_k^2,\qquad J_k = j_k\,\Delta m_k .$$
  2. The two end zones run from the half-way point to the next equally spaced point out to the half-way point to the next original shell (or the center, or the surface, where the mass is $10^{\texttt{HSTOT}}$). If the region borders a convection zone, all of that zone's shells are added to the end zone, so a convection zone moves as one block.
  3. Zone edges. The diffusion coefficients are interpolated at $\chi_k - \tfrac12\Delta\chi$, assumed constant beyond the region's ends. With LDIFAD, the advective and diffusive terms $0.2\cdot4\pi f_W\,v$ are interpolated too, and the composition coefficient is added to the diffusive one (following Zahn 1992, as the comment says).
  4. The Jacobian. The mesh coordinate is defined by the comment as $$\chi = \frac{\log m}{\delta_m} + \frac{L}{\delta_L} - \frac{\log P}{\delta_P},$$ with $\delta_m$ = HPTTOL(2), $\delta_L$ = HPTTOL(9) times the surface luminosity, $\delta_P$ = HPTTOL(11), so that $$\frac{d\chi}{dr} = \frac{4\pi\rho r^2}{\ln 10\;\delta_m\,m} + \frac{4\pi\rho r^2\,\varepsilon}{\delta_L} + \frac{G m \rho}{\ln 10\;\delta_P\,P\,r^2}.$$ The composition coefficient is multiplied by $\rho r^2\,d\chi/dr$, and the angular-momentum coefficients by $\rho r^2\,(I/m)\,d\chi/dr$ (for a thin spherical shell, $I/m = \tfrac23 r^2$, the comment notes), each interpolated in $\log_{10}$.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (/EGRID/, /SPLIN/, /MDPHY/, /DIFAD/, /DIFAD2/ to their _MOD modules; the settings of /CTOL/, /VARFC/ and /VMULT/ to NMLPAR_MOD; /CONST/–/CONST2/ to CONSTANTS_MOD), JSON from DIMENSIONS_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. Stale loop variable at the region's ends. In the loops that add a neighboring convection zone, I0 = I + 1 and I1 = I - 1 use I, which is left over from an earlier loop (the zone-center loop), not the loop variable II. I0 and I1 are not used afterwards, so there is no effect today, but they look like a remnant of an index meant to be II. Remove them?
  2. The comment's $d\chi/dr$ omits $P$. The comment writes the third term as RHO*GM/(LN10*DP*R**2); the code computes $G m\rho/(P r^2)$ (with -HP(II) in the exponent), which is the correct $d\log P/dr$ term. The comment should include $1/P$.
  3. Hard-coded 0.2. In the advection–diffusion option, FAC = 0.2D0*C4PI*FW. Where does the 1/5 come from (probably the $\frac15$ of Zahn's 1992 transport equation)? A comment would help.
  4. End zones use HRU but interior zones use interpolated $\log r$. EI(1) and EI(NTOT) take $r^2$ at the original shells IBEG and IEND, while the interior zones use $r$ interpolated at their centers. Intended?
