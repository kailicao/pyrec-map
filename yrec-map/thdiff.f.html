Explanation

Purpose. THDIFF solves Burgers' equations for the diffusion of the species in a fully ionised plasma, and returns the diffusion coefficients. Its header: the routine was written by Anne A. Thoul (Institute for Advanced Study), see Thoul et al. (1994, ApJ 421, 828); LUDCMP and LUBKSB are from Numerical Recipes.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: arguments: the number of species M (fluid 1 hydrogen, 2 helium, 3 to M-1 heavy elements, M the electrons), their atomic masses A (with A(M) = $m_e/m_u$), charges Z (Z(M) = $-1$), mass fractions X, and the Coulomb logarithms CL(M,M).
  • Who calls it: MICRODIFF_COD and SETUP_GRSETT (which, for the gravitational settling of hydrogen, uses -AP(1) and -SDEL(2,I)*AT(1), or else a fitted polynomial in $X$).
  • What it calls: LUDCMP (LU decomposition) and LUBKSB (back substitution).
  • Outputs: AP(M), AT(M), AX(M,M), the coefficients of the pressure, temperature and concentration gradients in each species' diffusion velocity; also X(M), the electrons' mass fraction, is overwritten.
  • COMMON blocks: none.

The method.

  1. Concentrations from mass fractions, normalised to the electrons ($C_M = 1$, by charge neutrality): $$C_s = \frac{X_s/A_s}{\sum_{t<M} Z_t X_t/A_t},\qquad C = \sum_s C_s,\qquad \rho \propto \sum_s A_s C_s ,$$ and the electrons' mass fraction $X_M = A_M / \sum_s A_s C_s$.
  2. The collision coefficients, with $\ln\Lambda_{st}$ = CL: $$K_{st} = \ln\Lambda_{st}\,\sqrt{\frac{A_s A_t}{A_s + A_t}}\;C_s C_t\,Z_s^2 Z_t^2,$$ and the mass ratios $x_{st} = A_t/(A_s + A_t)$, $y_{st} = A_s/(A_s + A_t)$, $z_{st} = 3y_{st} + 1.3\,x_{st}A_t/A_s$ (XX, Y, YY).
  3. The $N = 2M + 2$ equations ($M$ momentum equations, $M$ heat-flow equations, current neutrality and zero mass flow), written as the code's comment does, $$\alpha_s\,\frac{d\ln p}{dr} + \nu_s\,\frac{d\ln T}{dr} + \sum_{t \ne 2, M} \gamma_{st}\,\frac{d\ln C_t}{dr} = \sum_t \delta_{st}\,w_t ,$$ in the $N$ unknowns: $M$ diffusion velocities, $M$ heat fluxes, the electric field and the gravitational term. The matrix $\delta$ is assembled with the coefficients 0.6, 1.5, 1.6, 0.8 and 2.7 of Burgers' equations.
  4. LUDCMP factorises $\delta$ once; LUBKSB solves it for the right-hand sides $\alpha$, $\nu$ and each column of $\gamma$, which gives the coefficients $$w_s = A^{(p)}_s\,\frac{d\ln p}{dr} + A^{(T)}_s\,\frac{d\ln T}{dr} + \sum_t A^{(C)}_{st}\,\frac{d\ln C_t}{dr},$$ multiplied by KO*AC*CC $= K_0 \left(\sum_s A_s C_s\right)\left(\sum_s C_s\right)$ with $K_0 = 2$; $\sum_s A_s C_s$ is only proportional to $\rho$ (the comment: "multiplied by $p/K_0$").

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. No check of the array sizes. The work arrays hold at most MMAX = 20 species (NMAX = 42 equations), with no test that M ≤ 20.
  2. X(M) is overwritten. The electrons' mass fraction is written into the caller's array. Do the callers expect it?
  3. The normalisation comment. The comment says the results "must be multiplied by p/K_0", while the code multiplies by KO*AC*CC ($K_0 \sum A_s C_s \sum C_s$). Which units do the callers assume?
