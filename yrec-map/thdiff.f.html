Purpose

THDIFF solves Burgers' equations for the diffusion of the species in a fully ionised plasma, and returns the diffusion coefficients. Its header: the routine was written by Anne A. Thoul (Institute for Advanced Study), see Thoul et al. (1994, ApJ 421, 828); LUDCMP and LUBKSB are from Numerical Recipes.

How it fits

  • Inputs: arguments: the number of species M (fluid 1 hydrogen, 2 helium, 3 to M-1 heavy elements, M the electrons), their atomic masses A (with A(M) = $m_e/m_u$), charges Z (Z(M) = $-1$), mass fractions X, and the Coulomb logarithms CL(M,M).

  • Who calls it: MICRODIFF_COD and SETUP_GRSETT (which, for the gravitational settling of hydrogen, uses -AP(1) and -SDEL(2,I)*AT(1), or else a fitted polynomial in $X$).

    Question 1 (lines 275–285↗; line 306↗). A mixed treatment. In SETUP_GRSETT the concentration coefficient AC = 1.15 - 1.42X + 0.647X^2, the metals' AH and the derivatives QAPX, QATX always come from the fits, even when $A_P$ and $A_T$ come from THDIFF. Is this intended?

  • What it calls: LUDCMP (LU decomposition) and LUBKSB (back substitution).

  • Outputs: AP(M), AT(M), AX(M,M), the coefficients of the pressure, temperature and concentration gradients in each species' diffusion velocity; also X(M), the electrons' mass fraction, is overwritten (line 99↗). Harmless with both callers: they pass a local array, XFRAC, refill its first three entries at every point and never read XFRAC(4) (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026: lines 79–81↗; lines 224–226↗).
  • COMMON blocks: none.

The method

  1. Concentrations from mass fractions, normalised to the electrons ($C_M = 1$, by charge neutrality): $$C_s = \frac{X_s/A_s}{\sum_{t<M} Z_t X_t/A_t},\qquad C = \sum_s C_s,\qquad \rho \propto \sum_s A_s C_s ,$$ and the electrons' mass fraction $X_M = A_M / \sum_s A_s C_s$.
  2. The collision coefficients, with $\ln\Lambda_{st}$ = CL: $$K_{st} = \ln\Lambda_{st}\,\sqrt{\frac{A_s A_t}{A_s + A_t}}\;C_s C_t\,Z_s^2 Z_t^2,$$ and the mass ratios $x_{st} = A_t/(A_s + A_t)$, $y_{st} = A_s/(A_s + A_t)$, $z_{st} = 3y_{st} + 1.3\,x_{st}A_t/A_s$ (XX, Y, YY).
  3. The $N = 2M + 2$ equations ($M$ momentum equations, $M$ heat-flow equations, current neutrality and zero mass flow), written as the code's comment does, $$\alpha_s\,\frac{d\ln p}{dr} + \nu_s\,\frac{d\ln T}{dr} + \sum_{t \ne 2, M} \gamma_{st}\,\frac{d\ln C_t}{dr} = \sum_t \delta_{st}\,w_t ,$$ in the $N$ unknowns: $M$ diffusion velocities, $M$ heat fluxes, the electric field and the gravitational term. The matrix $\delta$ is assembled with the coefficients 0.6, 1.5, 1.6, 0.8 and 2.7 of Burgers' equations. The sum leaves out the electrons and helium because their gradients are not independent: the concentrations are normalised to the electrons, so $C_M = 1$ and $d\ln C_M/dr = 0$, and charge neutrality, $\sum_{t<M} Z_t C_t = 1$, gives helium's gradient in terms of the others, which is how its terms enter $\gamma_{st}$ (GAMMA(I,J) = -C(J)/CC + C(2)/CC*Z(J)*C(J)/Z(2)/C(2), then 1 added when $t = s$, a term subtracted when $s = 2$, and the whole multiplied by $C_s/C$). In full, for $t \ne 2, M$, $$\gamma_{st} = \frac{C_s}{C}\Bigl[\mathbb{1}_{s=t} - \frac{C_t}{C} + \frac{Z_tC_t}{Z_2C_2}\Bigl(\frac{C_2}{C} - \mathbb{1}_{s=2}\Bigr)\Bigr],\qquad \alpha_s = \frac{C_s}{C},\qquad \nu_s = 0,$$ where $C = \sum_t C_t$ (CC) and $\mathbb{1}_{s=t}$ is 1 if $s = t$ and 0 otherwise (written so, to keep it apart from the matrix $\delta$).

  4. LUDCMP factorises $\delta$ once; LUBKSB solves it for the right-hand sides $\alpha$, $\nu$ and each column of $\gamma$, which gives the coefficients $$w_s = A^{(p)}_s\,\frac{d\ln p}{dr} + A^{(T)}_s\,\frac{d\ln T}{dr} + \sum_t A^{(C)}_{st}\,\frac{d\ln C_t}{dr},$$ multiplied by KO*AC*CC $= K_0 \left(\sum_s A_s C_s\right)\left(\sum_s C_s\right)$ with $K_0 = 2$; $\sum_s A_s C_s$ is only proportional to $\rho$ (the comment: "multiplied by $p/K_0$"). The callers then multiply the coefficients by $r^2T^{5/2}/\ln\Lambda$ ($r$ in $R_\odot$, $T$ in $10^7$ K; FAC in SETUP_GRSETT and MICRODIFF_COD), which gives the flux in the units of the settling equation on GRSETT's page.

    Question 2 (lines 278–286↗). The normalisation comment. The comment says the results "must be multiplied by p/K_0", while the code multiplies by KO*AC*CC ($K_0 \sum A_s C_s \sum C_s$). Which units do the callers assume?

    Question 3 (lines 112–114↗; lines 130–154↗; lines 261–275↗). Is the Coulomb logarithm counted twice? THDIFF puts the Coulomb logarithms CL into $K_{st}$, so its coefficients already scale as $1/\ln\Lambda$. MICRODIFF_COD computes TCL per pair (a fit in the Debye length or interionic distance), passes it to THDIFF, and then multiplies by FAC, divided by a fixed LN_LAMBDA = 2.2D0; SETUP_GRSETT with LTHOUL (not LTHOULFIT) passes TCL = LN_LAMBDA (or the fitted TCL with ILAMBDA = 4) and again divides FAC by LN_LAMBDA; and the time unit of both callers "includes factor of 2.2 from ln lambda". Only the fitted branch (LTHOULFIT) is clearly free of $\ln\Lambda$. Do THDIFF's outputs need the division by $\ln\Lambda$ again, or does KO*AC*CC normalise them to $\ln\Lambda = 1$? If not, settling with the full Thoul solution would be too slow by a factor of about $\ln\Lambda$ (to be checked against Thoul et al. 1994 before calling it an error).
