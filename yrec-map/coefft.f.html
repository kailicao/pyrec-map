Purpose

This file evaluates, at every point of the interior model, the four equations of stellar structure and their partial derivatives, and passes them to REDUCE, which builds the linearized (Henyey) system that CRRECT solves for the corrections. On the way it calls the physics (equation of state, opacity, temperature gradient, nuclear energy), totals the luminosity by source, and stores many per-point quantities for output and for other routines.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments, the current model HS ($\log m$), HR ($\log r$), HP ($\log P$), HT ($\log T$), HL ($L/L_\odot$), HD ($\log\rho$), HCOMP; HS1, HS2 (the mass $m$ at each point and the shell's mass $\Delta m$, in g; STARIN, step 7); the changes over the step HTT, HPP ($\Delta\log T$, $\Delta\log P$) and DELTS (the time step, s); the rotational factors FP, FT and kinetic energies HKEROT, HKEROTO; JENV, TEFFL, CMIXL (the mixing length, passed on to TPGRAD). NMLPAR_MOD brings the options LDH, LROT, LSNU, LPULSE, the accretion switches LMDOT, DMDT0 and the nuclear cut-off TCUT(1); MASSCHG2_MOD the accretion terms DLOGT, DLOGP.
  • Who calls it: CRRECT, at each iteration of the structure solution.
  • What it calls: for each point, EQSTAT (the MHD equation of state, MEQOS, removed since rp_1); GETOPAC; TPGRAD (the temperature gradient and convection); ENGEB (nuclear energy) above TCUT(1), with IU = IM so that its per-point rates HR1… land at the right point, which also returns EALPCA, ENU and the $^7$Be mass fraction (unused here); and REDUCE for each pair of points.
  • Outputs:
  • the matrices HHA, HHB, HMAX (through REDUCE and the central conditions), HHC (the gravothermal energy per point), LC (convective or not), HD (density from the EOS);
  • TLUMX(1:8), the luminosity by source in $L_\odot$: 1–5 the nuclear terms from ENGEB, 6 the neutrino term ENU, 7 gravothermal, 8 alpha captures (EALPCA); CRRECT renormalizes them;
  • per point, in SCRTCH_MOD: the nuclear total SESUM (the five ENGEB terms) and SEG (SEG(1:5) the fractions of SESUM, SEG(6) the neutrino term and SEG(7) the gravothermal term, both in erg g$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$), $\beta$, $\eta$, opacity, the gradients $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, ionization fractions, convective velocity, $c_P$; read by 21 routines (output, mixing, diffusion);
  • GAM1 in SOUND_MOD ($\Gamma_1$); with rotation, SQOT, SQET, FRACNUC ($(\mathtt{SESUM} - \mathtt{SEG(6)} - \mathtt{SEG(7)})/\mathtt{SESUM}$) in ROTDER_MOD (for SETUPV) and DEROT in ROTEN_MOD; for pulsation output, PULSE1_MOD; the solar neutrino fluxes FLUXTOT in FLUXES_MOD.

The method

With $\ln m$ as the independent variable and the code's variables in $\log_{10}$, the structure equations at each point are (the code's names in parentheses): $$\frac{d\ln r}{d\ln m} = \frac{m}{4\pi r^3\rho} \;(\mathtt{QR}), \qquad \frac{d\ln P}{d\ln m} = -\frac{G m^2}{4\pi r^4 P}\,f_P \;(\mathtt{QP}), \qquad \frac{d\ln T}{d\ln m} = \nabla\,\frac{d\ln P}{d\ln m} \;(\mathtt{QT}),$$ $$\frac{d(L/L_\odot)}{d\log m} = \frac{\ln 10\; m}{L_\odot}\left(\epsilon_\mathrm{nuc} - \epsilon_\nu + \epsilon_\mathrm{grav} - \epsilon_\mathrm{rot}\right) \;(\mathtt{QL}),$$ where $f_P$ is the rotational correction (FP) and $\nabla$ the temperature gradient from TPGRAD. $f_T$ (FT) enters through $\nabla$: TPGRAD multiplies $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$ by $f_T/f_P$ (lines 80–81↗), so the radiative $d\ln T/d\ln m$ carries $f_T$ (settled from the code, Nov 9, 2026). The nuclear term is ENGEB's SUM1, which is net of the neutrino losses: above TCUT(5) ENGEB adds ENU $= -\epsilon_\nu$ to it (from the Itoh et al. 1996 fits with LNULOS1, otherwise from older fitting formulae), the same ENU that goes to TLUMX(6) and SEG(6). Their derivatives with respect to $\ln r$, $\ln P$, $\ln T$ and $L$ follow; for example $\partial\mathtt{QR}/\partial\ln r = -3\,\mathtt{QR}$, $\partial\mathtt{QP}/\partial\ln r = -4\,\mathtt{QP}$, and $\partial\mathtt{QR}/\partial\ln P = -\mathtt{QR}\,(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P)_T$. For the temperature gradient:

  • radiative: $\partial\mathtt{QT}/\partial\ln T = \mathtt{QT}\,(-4 + \kappa_T + \kappa_\rho\,\rho_T)$, $\partial\mathtt{QT}/\partial\ln P = \mathtt{QT}\,\kappa_\rho\,\rho_P$, and a term in $L$, $\mathtt{QT}/(L \ln 10)$;
  • convective: from TPGRAD's derivatives of $\nabla$ (QACP, QACT, QACR), with no dependence on $L$.

Here $\kappa_T$, $\kappa_\rho$ are QOT, QOD and $\rho_T$, $\rho_P$ are QDT, QDP ($\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ and $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$), locals that EQSTAT sets at each point.

Question 1 (lines 156–160↗; lines 226–246↗; lines 253–254↗). Rotation in $d\ln P/dr$. The terms in $d\ln f_P/d\ln r$ and $d\ln f_T/d\ln r$ (QFPR, QFTR) are commented out ("MHP 10/02 not used"), so the derivatives treat $f_P$ and $f_T$ as constants. Is that accurate enough for fast rotators?

Gravothermal energy. With $\Delta\log T$, $\Delta\log P$ over the step: $$\epsilon_\mathrm{grav} = \frac{\ln 10}{\Delta t}\,\frac{P\rho_T}{\rho}\left(\frac{\Delta\log T}{\nabla_\mathrm{ad}} - \Delta\log P\right),$$ which, with $\rho_T = -\delta$ and $c_P = P\delta/(\rho T\nabla_\mathrm{ad})$, is the usual $-c_P\,dT/dt + (\delta/\rho)\,dP/dt$, without a term in $d\mu/dt$ (the question below). $\Delta\log T$, $\Delta\log P$ (HTT, HPP) are changes at fixed mass: HPOINT moves the mass points before the structure is solved (interpolating the model and the previous step's rates of change, ENTROP_MOD, onto the new points), main.f then starts HTT, HPP from zero (or from the extrapolated guess), CRRECT adds each correction to them, and no routine moves the points during the iteration. During accretion (LMDOT, DMDT0 > 0), points at and above JENV add DLOGT, DLOGP to the changes; JENV comes from CRRECT, which has it from MIX only at levels 3 and 4 (CRRECT's suspected bug 1, suspected bug 1). With rotation, the change of rotational kinetic energy per gram per second is subtracted.

Question 2 (lines 338–341↗). $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ without the composition term. The formula used, $-c_P\,dT/dt + (\delta/\rho)\,dP/dt$, omits the term in $d\mu/dt$ of the full $-T\,ds/dt$ (Kippenhahn, Weigert & Weiss, §4.4), which matters where burning or settling changes $\mu$ quickly. A student would expect it in cores near hydrogen exhaustion. Is the neglect deliberate (for example because HTT, HPP are measured after MIX has changed the composition), and has its effect on solar models been checked?

Question 3 (lines 165–170↗; line 337↗). A one-year floor on the time step. IF(HCOMP(1,IM).GT.0.01D0 .AND. DELTS.LT.TIME) DT = TIMEI: where hydrogen is present and the step is shorter than a year (3.1558e7 s), $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ uses $1/(1\,\mathrm{yr})$ instead of $1/\Delta t$. So for short steps $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ is underestimated by the factor $\Delta t/1\,\mathrm{yr}$. What is this for? The year here is also hard-coded rather than taken from CSECYR.

Question 4 (lines 360–367↗). Accretion entropy. A term for the entropy of accreted material (QACC) is commented out. Is accretion meant to change the energy equation only through DLOGT, DLOGP?

The centre. Point 1 gets the central boundary conditions: $r^3 = 3m/(4\pi\rho)$ in log form, and $L = \epsilon\, m$.

$\Gamma_1$. Always computed (JVS 01/11), by Cox and Giuli's relations: $\chi_\rho = 1/\rho_P$, $\chi_T = -\chi_\rho\rho_T$, $c_V = c_P - (P/\rho T)\,\chi_T^2/\chi_\rho$, $\Gamma_1 = \chi_\rho c_P/c_V$.

Design

The routine holds many options and outputs that grew over decades (the comments are dated from 1990 to 2012). It sets LOCOND, LDERIV, LATMO, LMIX (arguments) at every call.

(a) may give wrong results; (b) may crash; (c) misbehaves silently (a wrong message, an ignored setting); (d) fragile: correct today, but breaks on a change or an unusual input.

Change log

The changes since rp_1

Changed since rp_1. The MHD equation of state removed (EQSTAT always), which settles the former question on the Debye–Hückel composition being set only without it; the new argument CMIXL, passed on to TPGRAD; EALPCA, ENU and the $^7$Be mass fraction returned by ENGEB (/NEWEPS/ retired); the write-only SQOD, SQED and the write-only opacity indices IDT, IDD (/OPTAB/) no longer set; COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE.

Changed since a8a5950. QDT and QDP are locals instead of arguments, since EQSTAT sets them at each point before they are read; the derivatives of $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ with respect to $\log P$ and $\log T$ (QLP, QLT) divide QDTP and QDTT by QDT, since $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ carries the factor $\rho_T$ (QDT) and QDTP, QDTT are derivatives of $\rho_T$, not of its logarithm (only the Jacobian changes, not the residual); the derivatives of the nuclear energy QED, QET are zeroed below TCUT(1), where they used to keep the last burning shell's values, which reached SQET and the pulsation arrays; the never-read $^3$He luminosities UMHE33, UMHEAL and the copies VALFMLT, VPHMLT, VCMXMLT of TPGRAD's always-zero ALFMLT, PHMLT, CMXMLT are no longer set, so GRAB_MOD (removed since a8a5950) and PUALPHA_MOD are no longer used.
