Explanation

Purpose. This file evaluates, at every point of the interior model, the four equations of stellar structure and their partial derivatives, and passes them to REDUCE, which builds the linearized (Henyey) system that CRRECT solves for the corrections. On the way it calls the physics (equation of state, opacity, temperature gradient, nuclear energy), totals the luminosity by source, and stores many per-point quantities for output and for other routines.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the arguments, the current model HS ($\log m$), HR ($\log r$), HP ($\log P$), HT ($\log T$), HL ($L/L_\odot$), HD ($\log\rho$), HCOMP; HS1, HS2 (the mass at each point and in each shell, in g, probably); the changes over the step HTT, HPP ($\Delta\log T$, $\Delta\log P$) and DELTS (the time step, s); the rotational factors FP, FT and kinetic energies HKEROT, HKEROTO; JENV, TEFFL, CMIXL (the mixing length, passed on to TPGRAD). NMLPAR_MOD brings the options LDH, LROT, LSNU, LPULSE, the accretion switches LMDOT, DMDT0 and the nuclear cut-off TCUT(1); MASSCHG2_MOD the accretion terms DLOGT, DLOGP.
  • Who calls it: CRRECT, at each iteration of the structure solution.
  • What it calls: for each point, EQSTAT (the MHD equation of state, MEQOS, removed since rp_1); GETOPAC; TPGRAD (the temperature gradient and convection); ENGEB (nuclear energy) above TCUT(1), with IU = IM so that its per-point rates HR1… land at the right point, which also returns EALPCA, ENU and the $^7$Be mass fraction (unused here); and REDUCE for each pair of points.
  • Outputs:
  • the matrices HHA, HHB, HMAX (through REDUCE and the central conditions), HHC (the gravothermal energy per point), LC (convective or not), HD (density from the EOS), QDT, QDP;
  • TLUMX(1:8), the luminosity by source in $L_\odot$: 1–5 the nuclear terms from ENGEB, 6 the neutrino term ENU, 7 gravothermal, 8 alpha captures (EALPCA); CRRECT renormalizes them;
  • per point, in SCRTCH_MOD: the nuclear total SESUM (the five ENGEB terms) and SEG (SEG(1:5) the fractions of SESUM, SEG(6) the neutrino term and SEG(7) the gravothermal term, both in erg g$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$), $\beta$, $\eta$, opacity, the gradients $\nabla_\mathrm{rad}$, $\nabla$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$, ionization fractions, convective velocity, $c_P$; read by 21 routines (output, mixing, diffusion);
  • GAM1 in SOUND_MOD ($\Gamma_1$); with rotation, SQOT, SQET, FRACNUC ($(\mathtt{SESUM} - \mathtt{SEG(6)} - \mathtt{SEG(7)})/\mathtt{SESUM}$) in ROTDER_MOD (for SETUPV) and DEROT in ROTEN_MOD; for pulsation output, PULSE1_MOD and PUALPHA_MOD; the solar neutrino fluxes FLUXTOT in FLUXES_MOD; the $^3$He luminosities UMHE33, UMHEAL in GRAB_MOD (JVS 10/11).

The method. With $\ln m$ as the independent variable and the code's variables in $\log_{10}$, the structure equations at each point are (the code's names in parentheses): $$\frac{d\ln r}{d\ln m} = \frac{m}{4\pi r^3\rho} \;(\mathtt{QR}), \qquad \frac{d\ln P}{d\ln m} = -\frac{G m^2}{4\pi r^4 P}\,f_P \;(\mathtt{QP}), \qquad \frac{d\ln T}{d\ln m} = \nabla\,\frac{d\ln P}{d\ln m} \;(\mathtt{QT}),$$ $$\frac{d(L/L_\odot)}{d\log m} = \frac{\ln 10\; m}{L_\odot}\left(\epsilon_\mathrm{nuc} + \epsilon_\mathrm{grav} - \epsilon_\mathrm{rot}\right) \;(\mathtt{QL}),$$ where $f_P$ is the rotational correction (FP) and $\nabla$ the temperature gradient from TPGRAD. Their derivatives with respect to $\ln r$, $\ln P$, $\ln T$ and $L$ follow; for example $\partial\mathtt{QR}/\partial\ln r = -3\,\mathtt{QR}$, $\partial\mathtt{QP}/\partial\ln r = -4\,\mathtt{QP}$, and $\partial\mathtt{QR}/\partial\ln P = -\mathtt{QR}\,(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P)_T$. For the temperature gradient:

  • radiative: $\partial\mathtt{QT}/\partial\ln T = \mathtt{QT}\,(-4 + \kappa_T + \kappa_\rho\,\rho_T)$, $\partial\mathtt{QT}/\partial\ln P = \mathtt{QT}\,\kappa_\rho\,\rho_P$, and a term in $L$, $\mathtt{QT}/(L \ln 10)$;
  • convective: from TPGRAD's derivatives of $\nabla$ (QACP, QACT, QACR), with no dependence on $L$.

Here $\kappa_T$, $\kappa_\rho$ are QOT, QOD and $\rho_T$, $\rho_P$ are QDT, QDP ($\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln T$ and $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$).

Gravothermal energy. With $\Delta\log T$, $\Delta\log P$ over the step: $$\epsilon_\mathrm{grav} = \frac{\ln 10}{\Delta t}\,\frac{P\rho_T}{\rho}\left(\frac{\Delta\log T}{\nabla_\mathrm{ad}} - \Delta\log P\right),$$ which, with $\rho_T = -\delta$ and $c_P = P\delta/(\rho T\nabla_\mathrm{ad})$, is the usual $-c_P\,dT/dt + (\delta/\rho)\,dP/dt$. During accretion (LMDOT, DMDT0 > 0), points at and above JENV add DLOGT, DLOGP to the changes; JENV comes from CRRECT, which has it from MIX only at levels 3 and 4 (CRRECT's question 5). With rotation, the change of rotational kinetic energy per gram per second is subtracted.

The centre. Point 1 gets the central boundary conditions: $r^3 = 3m/(4\pi\rho)$ in log form, and $L = \epsilon\, m$.

$\Gamma_1$. Always computed (JVS 01/11), by Cox and Giuli's relations: $\chi_\rho = 1/\rho_P$, $\chi_T = -\chi_\rho\rho_T$, $c_V = c_P - (P/\rho T)\,\chi_T^2/\chi_\rho$, $\Gamma_1 = \chi_\rho c_P/c_V$.

Design. The routine holds many options and outputs that grew over decades (the comments are dated from 1990 to 2012). It sets LOCOND, LDERIV, LATMO, LMIX (arguments) at every call.

KC's changes. 2025-05-31: the unused argument MODEL removed; the call to MEQOS no longer passes LDERIV, LATMO, KSAHA; the call to ENGEB drops the unused arguments (Z, XN15, XO17, XLI6, XLI7, XBE9 and others).

Changed since rp_1. The MHD equation of state removed (EQSTAT always), which settles the former question on the Debye–Hückel composition being set only without it; the new argument CMIXL, passed on to TPGRAD; EALPCA, ENU and the $^7$Be mass fraction returned by ENGEB (/NEWEPS/ retired); the write-only SQOD, SQED and the write-only opacity indices IDT, IDD (/OPTAB/) no longer set; COMMON blocks to modules, IMPLICIT NONE.

Questions for the experts

  1. A one-year floor on the time step. IF(HCOMP(1,IM).GT.0.01D0 .AND. DELTS.LT.TIME) DT = TIMEI: where hydrogen is present and the step is shorter than a year (3.1558e7 s), $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ uses $1/(1\,\mathrm{yr})$ instead of $1/\Delta t$. So for short steps $\epsilon_\mathrm{grav}$ is underestimated by the factor $\Delta t/1\,\mathrm{yr}$. What is this for? The year here is also hard-coded rather than taken from CSECYR.
  2. Rotation in $d\ln P/dr$. The terms in $d\ln f_P/d\ln r$ and $d\ln f_T/d\ln r$ (QFPR, QFTR) are commented out ("MHP 10/02 not used"), so the derivatives treat $f_P$ and $f_T$ as constants. Is that accurate enough for fast rotators?
  3. $f_T$ unused. $f_P$ multiplies QP, but $f_T$ enters only through TPGRAD; is that the intended form of the rotating structure equations?
  4. Accretion entropy. A term for the entropy of accreted material (QACC) is commented out. Is accretion meant to change the energy equation only through DLOGT, DLOGP?
