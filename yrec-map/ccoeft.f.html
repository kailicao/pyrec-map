Purpose

This file sets up the tridiagonal system for one implicit time step of composition transport by rotationally induced mixing, on an evenly spaced grid in the coordinate $\chi$ built by GETGRID and MIXGRID (the comments' "radius" and "cm" are older than that grid).

Question 1 (lines 25–36↗; lines 61–63↗). Outdated comments. The comments speak of an even grid in radius, a spacing in cm, coefficients "computed in SR COEFFT" and "angular momentum" at the boundary, while the routine works on the grid in $\chi$ with coefficients from CODIFF and MIXGRID, for composition. How it fits

  • Inputs: the arguments COD (diffusion coefficients, "computed in SR COEFFT" per the comment), DR (grid spacing: MIXCOM passes DCHI, the spacing in $\chi$), DT (time step, s), ECOMP (mass fractions at the start of the step on the even grid), EM (the masses of the grid's shells, g), NTOT (number of points).
  • Who calls it: MIXCOM.
  • Outputs: the coefficients A, B, C and right-hand side D in TRIDI_MOD, which CTRIDI then solves (MIXCOM calls only that one); TRIDI_MOD is shared with these and with DCOEFT and GETQUA.

The method

The comment gives the equation as $$\frac{\partial X}{\partial t} = \frac{1}{4\pi\rho r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(D\,\frac{\partial X}{\partial r}\right),$$ differenced fully implicitly on the grid in $\chi$ ($\Delta r$ below is DR, that is, $\Delta\chi$; $m_i$ is the shell mass EM): $$X_i^{n+1} - X_i^{n} = \frac{\Delta t}{\Delta r\,m_i}\left[D_{i+1}\left(X_{i+1}^{n+1} - X_i^{n+1}\right) - D_i\left(X_i^{n+1} - X_{i-1}^{n+1}\right)\right],$$ where COD(I) is $D$ at the interface between points $i-1$ and $i$ (written $D_{i-1/2}$ in the comment). So, with $f_i = \Delta t/(\Delta r\, m_i)$: $$a_i = -f_i D_i, \qquad b_i = 1 + f_i (D_i + D_{i+1}), \qquad c_i = -f_i D_{i+1}, \qquad d_i = X_i^n.$$ At both ends there is no flux across the boundary: $a_1 = 0$, $b_1 = 1 + f_1 D_2$; $c_N = 0$, $b_N = 1 + f_N D_N$.

The scheme conserves mass: CODIFF includes $4\pi$ in COD2, and MIXGRID multiplies the coefficient by $\rho r^2\,d\chi/dr$, so COD is $4\pi\rho r^2 D\,d\chi/dr$, and the flux between shells is COD times the difference in $X$ over $\Delta\chi$.
