Explanation

Purpose. Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the model's $Z$ (MHP 7/98: "at fixed Z"): a three-dimensional interpolation in $X$, $\log T$ and $\log R$ ($R = \rho/T_6^3$). The same as OP952D, with one more interpolation in $X$.

How it fits.

  • Inputs: the table CAPPA(10,70,19) of $\log\kappa$ at the model's $Z$ in LLOT95_MOD, built by OP95ZTAB; the indices and weights in OP95INTERP_MOD (BX(1,·) for $X$; formerly /OP95INDX/, /OP95FACT/ and /OP95EXT/), from GETOPAL95.
  • Who calls it: GETOPAL95, when the point's $Z$ matches the table's ZTAB (to a relative $10^{-4}$) but its $X$ does not match the surface table's, and the acoustic depth is not being computed.
  • Outputs: $\kappa$, $\log\kappa$, QOD and QOT, as in OP952D.

The method. For each of the four nearest $X$ tables, the interpolation of OP952D in $\log R$ (with linear extrapolation beyond the table) and in $\log T$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its two derivatives; these three are then interpolated in $X$ with four-point weights BX(1,J), and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ is converted to constant $\rho$ as in OP952D.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: COMMON blocks to modules (the three interpolation blocks merged into OP95INTERP_MOD, with RL, RLL renamed RHO_T63L, RHO_T63LL), the dimension parameters moved into LLOT95_MOD, IMPLICIT NONE.

18    """Get the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the
19    model's Z (MHP 7/98): as OP952D (log R, then log T) for each of the four
20    nearest X tables, then four-point interpolation in X, with the indices
21    and weights GETOPAL95 left in op95interp_mod.
22
23    Translates: op953d.f#OP953D (09c3b9b)
24
25    Arguments: o, ol, qod, qot, passed in with any value.
26
27    Returns (o, ol, qod, qot): kappa, log kappa, dlog kappa/dlog rho and
28    dlog kappa/dlog T (at constant rho).
29    """
30    im = op95interp_mod
31    cappa = llot95_mod.cappa
32    for j in range(1, 5):
33        ix = im.jx[1, j]
34        # For each X, log kappa and its slope in log R at four values of T.
35        for i in range(1, 5):
36            it = im.jt + i - 1
37            id_ = im.jd[i]
38            c[i] = (im.bd[i, 1] * cappa[ix, it, id_]
39                    + im.bd[i, 2] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 1]
40                    + im.bd[i, 3] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 2]
41                    + im.bd[i, 4] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 3])
42            qc[i] = (im.bdd[i, 1] * cappa[ix, it, id_]
43                     + im.bdd[i, 2] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 1]
44                     + im.bdd[i, 3] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 2]
45                     + im.bdd[i, 4] * cappa[ix, it, id_ + 3])
46        # Outside the table in rho: extrapolate with the slope at the edge.
47        if im.lextl:
48            deltar = im.rho_t63l - im.rho_t63ll
49            for i in range(1, 5):
50                c[i] = c[i] + deltar * qc[i]
51        elif im.lexth:
52            for i in range(1, 5):
53                if im.lexh[i]:
54                    deltar = im.rho_t63l - im.rlh[i]
55                    c[i] = c[i] + deltar * qc[i]
56        # Interpolate in T.
57        cc[j] = (im.bt[1] * c[1] + im.bt[2] * c[2] + im.bt[3] * c[3]
58                 + im.bt[4] * c[4])
59        cct[j] = (im.btt[1] * c[1] + im.btt[2] * c[2] + im.btt[3] * c[3]
60                  + im.btt[4] * c[4])
61        ccd[j] = (im.bt[1] * qc[1] + im.bt[2] * qc[2] + im.bt[3] * qc[3]
62                  + im.bt[4] * qc[4])
63    # Interpolate in X.
64    bx = im.bx
65    ol = bx[1, 1] * cc[1] + bx[1, 2] * cc[2] + bx[1, 3] * cc[3] + bx[1, 4] * cc[4]
66    qot = (bx[1, 1] * cct[1] + bx[1, 2] * cct[2] + bx[1, 3] * cct[3]
67           + bx[1, 4] * cct[4])
68    qod = (bx[1, 1] * ccd[1] + bx[1, 2] * ccd[2] + bx[1, 3] * ccd[3]
69           + bx[1, 4] * ccd[4])
70    # From the derivative at fixed R to the derivative at fixed rho.
71    qot = qot - 3.0 * qod
72    o = 10.0**ol
73    return o, ol, qod, qot
