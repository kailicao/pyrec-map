Purpose

Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the model's $Z$ (MHP 7/98: "at fixed Z"): a three-dimensional interpolation in $X$, $\log T$ and $\log R$ ($R = \rho/T_6^3$). The same as OP952D, with one more interpolation in $X$.

How it fits

  • Inputs: the table CAPPA(10,70,19) of $\log\kappa$ at the model's $Z$ in LLOT95_MOD, built by OP95ZTAB; the indices and weights in OP95INTERP_MOD (BX(1,·) for $X$; formerly /OP95INDX/, /OP95FACT/ and /OP95EXT/), from GETOPAL95.
  • Who calls it: GETOPAL95, when the point's $Z$ matches the table's ZTAB (to a relative $10^{-4}$) but its $X$ does not match the surface table's, and the acoustic depth is not being computed.
  • Outputs: $\kappa$, $\log\kappa$, QOD and QOT, as in OP952D.

The method

For each of the four nearest $X$ tables, the interpolation of OP952D in $\log R$ (with linear extrapolation beyond the table) and in $\log T$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its two derivatives; these three are then interpolated in $X$ with four-point weights BX(1,J), and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$ is converted to constant $\rho$ as in OP952D.
