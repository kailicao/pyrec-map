Explanation

Purpose. EQSTAT2 is the core of YREC's equation of state: it chooses among, and blends, the Yale Saha solver, the relativistic degenerate gas, the SCV tables and the OPAL 2006 table, depending on temperature, density and the run's settings. Its header calls it "the old EOS routine"; since 2006 the code calls it through EQSTAT.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: EQSTAT (once or five times per call, see there) and CALCAD directly.
  • Inputs: TL, PL, X, Z, LDERIV, LATMO; DL as a starting guess.
  • What it calls: EQSAHA (Saha ionization), EQSCVE (SCV tables), EQRELV (fully ionized, degenerate gas), and for OPAL OEQOS06 with EQBOUND06 (the OPAL 1995 and 2001 tables, with OEQOS, EQBOUND, OEQOS01 and EQBOUND01, removed since rp_1).
  • Outputs: as listed in EQSTAT: $T$, $P$, $\rho$, $\beta$, the ionization fractions, $\mu$ terms, $\eta$, $Q_{\rho T}$, $Q_{\rho P}$, $c_P$, $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$ and their derivatives.
  • Modules: NMLPAR_MOD (the namelist settings: the flag LOPALE06, LSCV, TSCUT, the temperature below which Saha is solved, and LDIFZ, metal diffusion); COMP_MOD (the envelope composition XENV, ZENV, AMUENV, FXENV, set in STARIN); CONSTANTS_MOD (constants: $\ln 10$, $R$ as CGAS, $a/3$ as CA3); IOUNITS_MOD (ISHORT).

The method.

  1. Composition. AMU $=\sum_i X_i/A_i$ (ions per unit mass, in units of $1/m_\mathrm{H}$) and the number fractions FX of 12 species (Na, Al, Mg, Fe, Si, C, H, O, N, Ar, Ne, He; atomic weights in ATOMW2) are rescaled from the envelope values. Without metal diffusion, any change in $Z$ is taken as carbon ("ASSUME EXCESS Z(METALS) IS IN THE FORM OF CARBON(12)"); with it, all metals scale together by $Z/Z_\mathrm{env}$.
  2. Radiation. $1-\beta = aT^4/(3P)$; then BETA14 is redefined as $4(1-\beta)/\beta$ (with $1-\beta$ capped at 1).
  3. Fully ionized gas ($\log T \ge$ TSCUT): EQRELV alone, with $\mu_e^{-1} = 0.9921X + 0.4995(1-X)$ and $R/\mu = R(\mu_\mathrm{ion}^{-1} + \mu_e^{-1})$.
  4. Partial ionization ($\log T <$ TSCUT): EQSAHA, and if LSCV is set, EQSCVE, which overwrites $\rho$ and the first derivatives where its table covers the point.

  5. Transition band (TSCUT − 0.5 < $\log T$ < TSCUT): EQRELV is also called, and the two are blended with a smooth step. With $w = 2(\mathrm{TSCUT} - \log T)$, between 0 and 1, $$W(w) = w^3(6w^2 - 15w + 10),\qquad q = q_\mathrm{rel} + W\,(q_\mathrm{Saha} - q_\mathrm{rel})$$ for $\log\rho$, the ionization fractions and the derivatives (but see question 7 for QDTT and QDTP); then $R/\mu = \beta P/(\rho T)$ and $\mu_e^{-1}$ follows.

  6. OPAL. If LOPALE06 is set and the point is in the table's rough range ($1870 \le T \le 2\times10^8$ K, $\log\rho \le 7$), OPAL's values replace the above, or are blended with them by the weight $f$ from EQBOUND06: $$q = q_\mathrm{Yale} + f\,(q_\mathrm{OPAL} - q_\mathrm{Yale})$$ for $\log\rho$, $\beta$, the $\mu$ terms, $Q_{\rho T}$, $Q_{\rho P}$, $c_P$ and $\nabla_\mathrm{ad}$. OPAL gives no second derivatives, so those stay from the Yale/SCV result. If OPAL fails (alternate return) or the point is out of its table, the routine exits with the Yale/SCV result.

Questions for the experts

  1. Two meanings of BETA14. In the Yale branch BETA14 is $4(1-\beta)/\beta$ (step 2), but after an OPAL result it is set to $1-\beta$. Callers receive one or the other depending on the regime. Which do they expect?
  2. Dead SCV derivative block. LSCVD is set to false just before the block that would compute SCV derivatives numerically, so the block never runs. It also has a bug: both temperature calls write QDT1, QCP1, DELA1, so QDT2, QCP2, DELA2 are never set, and DL1, D1 are uninitialized. Remove the block, or fix it?
  3. Negative $\beta$. When radiation pressure exceeds $P$, the routine writes a message and continues with $\beta < 0$. Should it stop?
  4. Mixed outputs with SCV. RMU, EMU and the second derivatives come from EQSAHA even when SCV supplies $\rho$; see question 2 in eqscve.f.
  5. Hard-coded OPAL range. The rough range in step 6 is hard-coded; the real edges are checked in EQBOUND06. Is this range still needed, and is it right?
  6. Z restored. The routine saves Z and restores it at the end (Z = Z0), but no statement in it changes Z; only OEQOS06 and EQSCVE receive it as an argument. Do they change it, or is the restore a leftover?
  7. Second derivatives in the transition band. The differences Saha − relativistic are formed for DL0, QDT0, QDP0, QCP0, DELA0, QCPT0, QCPP0, QAT0, QAP0, but not for QDTT0 and QDTP0. So QDTT = QDTT + WT0*QDTT0 adds $W$ times the full Saha value to the relativistic one, instead of $W$ times the difference; the same for QDTP. Also, the blended derivatives leave out $dW/d\log T$ (the line for it, WT1, is commented out). Are these bugs?
