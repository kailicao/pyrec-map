Purpose

NEWOPAC_MOD holds two switches of the opacity setup, shared by 4 routines. It replaces what is left of COMMON/NEWOPAC/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26), into which /MISCOPAC/ and /NWLAOL/ had been merged on 9/24/26: the opacity choices and their $Z$ and temperature limits (ZOPAL951, TMOLMIN, TMOLMAX, LALEX06, LOPAL95) are namelist variables now in NMLPAR_MOD, and the members of obsolete tables (ZLAOL1, ZOPAL1, ZALEX1, ZKUR1, LLAOL89, LOPAL92, LKUR90, LALEX95 and their pairs) were removed with those options.

What its variables hold, who sets them, who reads them. Both are LOGICAL*4.

  • L2Z: whether two opacity tables in $Z$ are needed, LZRAMP .OR. LDIFZ, set by SETUPOPAC; read by GETOPAC in its test of whether the local $Z$ is covered by the tables (a $Z$ far from ZENV is accepted only with L2Z or LOPAL95).
  • LLAOL: the switch of the obsolete LAOL opacities, initially .FALSE. (moved from PARMIN's DATA statement) and set nowhere, so STARIN always takes the element fractions from VNEW rather than from the LAOL tables (its comment: "to maintain backward compatibility", lines 910–916↗); unused? (a switch that cannot be turned on, for tables that were removed).

Design

A blanket USE NEWOPAC_MOD; PARMIN still uses the module but reads neither member. The module header calls LLAOL a namelist variable, but it is in no namelist (so also at rp_1).
