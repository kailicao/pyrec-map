Explanation

Purpose. The modified midpoint method of Numerical Recipes ("this SR from Numerical Recipes, p.562"): it advances three variables $y$ from $x$ to $x + H$ in $n$ substeps. It is the inner step of the Bulirsch–Stoer integrator BSSTEP, which ENVINT uses to integrate the envelope.

How it fits.

  • Who calls it: only BSSTEP, with a sequence of $n$ (NSEQ).
  • What it calls: the derivative routine passed in as DERIV (an EXTERNAL argument; the site cannot resolve it statically). All the arguments after DERIV (B, FPL, FTL, GL, LATMO, …, KSAHA) are only passed through to it.
  • Output: YOUT, the values at $x+H$.

The method. With $h = H/n$, $z_0 = y(x)$, $z_1 = z_0 + h\,y'(x)$, $$z_{m+1} = z_{m-1} + 2h\,f(x + m h, z_m),\quad m = 1,\dots,n-1,\qquad y(x+H) \approx \tfrac12\left[z_n + z_{n-1} + h\,f(x+H, z_n)\right].$$ YOUT holds the derivatives in between, to save an array.

Design. The size is fixed at three variables (Y(3)), whatever NVAR is.

Changed since rp_1. Declarations only: IMPLICIT NONE.

Advance y from xs to xs + htot in nstep substeps by the modified
midpoint method; the result goes to yout.

Translates: mmid.f#MMID (09c3b9b)

Arguments: y and dydx at xs (three variables, index 0 unused; nvar of
them used); the step htot and the number of substeps nstep; yout, set
in place; deriv, the derivative routine (qenv or qatm); the arguments
from b to ksaha are only passed on to deriv.

Returns (rl, kount, ksaha), as deriv leaves them.
