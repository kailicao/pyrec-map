Explanation

Purpose. CTOL_MOD holds FCORR, the fraction of the Henyey correction applied in the current iteration, shared by the main program and CRRECT. It replaces what is left of COMMON/CTOL/ (MHP via Claude, 10/14/26): the block's tolerances and iteration limits (HTOLER, FCORR0, FCORRI, HPTTOL, NITER1–NITER3) are namelist variables now in NMLPAR_MOD.

What its variable holds, who sets it, who reads it.

  • FCORR (REAL*8): the main program sets it to $|\mathtt{FCORR0}| - \mathtt{FCORRI}$ at the start of each model; CRRECT raises it by FCORRI per iteration, up to 1 (when FCORR0 > 0), and scales the corrections by it.

Design. A blanket USE CTOL_MOD in both routines.

Changed since rp_1. New file: FCORR of COMMON/CTOL/ as a module; the block's other members moved to NMLPAR_MOD.
