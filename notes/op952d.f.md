## Explanation

**Purpose.** Gets the OPAL95 opacity from the table pre-interpolated to the surface composition (MHP 7/98: "get OPAL95 opacity from table at surface X,Z"): a two-dimensional interpolation in $\log T$ and $\log R$, where $R = \rho/T_6^3$. It is the fastest of three routines; [[op953d.f#OP953D|OP953D]] adds $X$ and [[op954d.f#OP954D|OP954D]] adds $X$ and $Z$.

**How it fits.**
- **Inputs:** the surface table `CAPPAE(70,19)` of $\log\kappa$ in `/LLOT95E/`, built by [[op95xtab.f#OP95XTAB|OP95XTAB]]; the indices and the Lagrange weights prepared by [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]] in `/OP95INDX/`, `/OP95FACT/` and `/OP95EXT/`.
- **Who calls it:** [[getopal95.f#GETOPAL95|GETOPAL95]], when the point's $Z$ matches the table's `ZTAB` (to a relative $10^{-4}$) and its $X$ the surface table's `XTAB` (to $10^{-4}$), and the acoustic depth is not being computed (`LADON` and `LACOUT`, which force [[op954d.f#OP954D|OP954D]]).
- **Outputs:** $\kappa$ (`O`), $\log\kappa$ (`OL`), `QOD` $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log\rho$ and `QOT` $= \partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T$.

**The method.** At each of the four nearest temperatures $T_i$, four-point Lagrange interpolation in $\log R$ gives $\log\kappa$ and its slope:
$$C_i = \sum_k b^{(D)}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k},\qquad C'_i = \sum_k b^{(D')}_{ik}\,\log\kappa_{i,k}.$$
If $\log R$ lies below the table (`LEXTL`) or above it at some $T_i$ (`LEXH(I)`), the value at the edge is extrapolated linearly, $C_i \to C_i + (\log R - \log R_\mathrm{edge})\,C'_i$. Then, in $\log T$, $\log\kappa = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C_i$, $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log T|_R = \sum_i b^{(T')}_i C_i$ and $\partial\log\kappa/\partial\log R|_T = \sum_i b^{(T)}_i C'_i$. Since $\log R = \log\rho - 3\log T_6$,
$$\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log\rho}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T,\qquad \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_\rho = \left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log T}\right)_R - 3\left(\frac{\partial\log\kappa}{\partial\log R}\right)_T .$$

**Design.** The table dimensions are fixed as parameters (70 temperatures, 19 values of $\log R$, 10 of $X$, 13 of $Z$, 126 compositions), repeated in each of the five `OP95…` files.
